## 1. A shape is a constraint, not a filler

A shape is the narrowed form of an answer you can state before you can name it. It constrains the class of acceptable answers without occupying the slot. For this question, the shape is:

> what crosses into a substantially different situation must be a reapplicable generator (a method or a head start with its typed relation intact), must be describable in effect terms, must enter a habitat whose required contact has been reproduced, and must be settled again by a ground that does not update on the learner's own report.

That statement rules out recordings, ingredient lists, pooled scores and agent-only capability claims without naming a cognitive mechanism. The mechanism slot stays marked `???`.

From *The Library of Babel*, opened directly: *"This means that you can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."* And the stopping paradox: *"You will know that you are done because the finish line will also be the starting place."*

## 2. How a shape is obtained

- *Library of Babel*: use the vocabulary you already hold that is closest to what you are trying to grasp, then *"With each new term gained, you narrow the space and deepen your vocabulary."* This is a bootstrapping convergence, not a one-shot guess.
- Fig 6 :30: **"outward: does another frame already hold it? The description travels. Fails by being sharpened into terms only we can read. Cast it in effect terms."** Fig 9 :30-31: **"your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect rather than the cause."**
- *Decoding Tacit Knowledge*, opened directly: *"When contact isn't possible, the best you can send is a plain description of what you'd expect to see, because anyone can check that against the world."* This is why the outreach artifact leads with an effect-level refuter rather than a mechanism name.
- *Library of Babel* on analogy itself, opened directly and especially relevant here: *"The reason why analogies work so well to explain concepts: they are translating structural relationships and problems through language and scenarios that you are already familiar with, so that the knowledge transfers."* The essay is describing the transfer of meaning between people, but the structure it names is the same one Fig 12 names for composition: relations travel, attributes do not.

## 3. What licenses a name

Only two things, both outside the shape:

1. **The ground settles it.** Formula step 5: *"SETTLE — the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays. A gate is not the ground."* For this question that means a source-discriminating observation in a substituted habitat, per slot.
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs** (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8 :50-52), with a source gate and a shadow test. If a cognitive mechanism name arrives from one literature, it must say what gap it explains and where it came from.

*They're Scanning Books When They Should* (carried from the exemplar's canonical mapping): *"The real import is not a new term in the existing space. It is a new dimension of the space."* And: *"A minimum viable set of six terms is a human's careful guess at reality's compression axes; another sensorium's minimum set need not be those six."* The six are a visible guess, not nature's own list. Naming one as *the* mechanism turns a visible choice into an invisible one.

**Answer to the shape question for REA-0012:** nothing in the framework licenses a specific cognitive mechanism for far transfer. A mechanism is licensed only by a ground settlement or a provenanced import with a shadow test. Until then it is the searcher's own contribution and must be recorded as such. The slot stays `???`, and that is a `gap` verdict, not a silence.

## 4. The ground and the source problem

*The Hard Problem is About Source*, opened directly, gives the mechanism for why a mechanism name cannot be read off a success score. Metamerism: an instrument reads green and *"cannot comment on the source."* The source might be one green light, or yellow plus blue, or RGB, or CMYK, or a scheme not yet discovered. The essay states: *"If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information."* And: *"The answer is unreached until a better instrument exists."*

Applied here: a learner succeeds in a new situation. That reading does not determine whether the cause was a method crossing, a reproduced habitat, an imported atom from a peer, or a lucky match of exemplars. Several paths create the same behaviour. What is owed is a discriminating instrument, not a name. This is also why a benchmark score cannot license "transfer happened for reason X."

*Decoding Tacit Knowledge* adds the human-side constraint: *"Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down."* The history behind a compressed message does not travel with it, so each learner rebuilds part of it through contact. A transfer claim that ignores this will mistake a shared notation for shared capability.

## 5. Correction to my own section 3

I initially wrote "the framework returns a shape, not a name" as though it forbade naming any held item. That overstates the rule. The canonical wording governs a **missing** primitive, one not held in Γ. Method, head start, bond, arrangement, habitat and ground are not missing primitives; they are already in the framework's vocabulary. Naming them is a binding from the framework or from a world frame onto held terms, not the invention of a seventh term. What remains forbidden is treating such a binding as a ground settlement or as an import that moves the wall. Both the original claim and its narrowing are kept visible here so neither is quietly lost.

## 6. Consequences for contact and for this solution

**Prestige is not action.** *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*, opened directly: *"Prestige must never be allowed to validate an action directly... Someone let prestige stand in for proof... Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it. The Ground Maintainer must be blind to prestige."* A list of famous transfer researchers selected for likely agreement is the named collapse, not corroboration. The legitimate ask gives the shape claim a budget to be tried and lets a ground settle it.

**Simulation is not a ground reading.** Same essay, opened directly: *"LLMs can quickly simulate an approximate answer/output with the formula found in the architecture post. It will often be better than the generic outputs that you will usually get otherwise too. However, the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself."* So `01_figure_grounded_audit.md`, `06_isomers_and_order.md` and `07_the_tether_answer.md` are simulated derivations checked for internal consistency against the figures. They are not tests of the claim and must be labelled as such. They cannot settle anything they report on.

**What can be honestly offered.** A shape any candidate answer must have, six failure conditions written in advance, and the instruments that would close the slot. Not a mechanism, not a score, not a verdict.
