## What is deliberately not here

- No personal emails of named individuals.
- No ranking of people by how likely they are to believe the claim.
- No bulk-send plan.

Three reasons, in the figures' own words. First, a room is not an anchor: *"Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you."* (Fig 2 :35). Second, selecting for receptiveness manufactures the correlation that disqualifies a verdict: *"agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool"* (Fig 2 :40). Third, asking for trust before a check spends the only thing an outside frame contributes: *"A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it."* (Fig 10 :42). Legally, scraping personal emails for unsolicited outreach breaches GDPR and CAN-SPAM style rules in many jurisdictions. That is a practical failure on top of the structural one.
