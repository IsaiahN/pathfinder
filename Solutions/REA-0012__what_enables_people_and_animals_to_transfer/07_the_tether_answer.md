# The Tether's answer: what enables transfer to substantially different situations?

**Mode: `general` on the structural shape, `specified` on the per-domain verdicts, not `grounded`.** This is a shape and a route, not a result. Simulated derivation checked against the figures (`05_what_shape_means.md` section 6), not a ground reading.

## 1. The residual it answers

A person or animal predicts well in the situation it learned in and fails in a substantially different one. The residual is not a global score. Fig 1 :19: **"The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated."** The failure is a slice, and a global near-zero with one live slot is a legal state, so the question "does it transfer?" has to be re-asked as "which slot fails, and what crossed the boundary to it?"

The question is a **union compound**, not one hard problem with opposite answers. Fig 9 :57: **"What remains is smaller and it is also categorically different: a case in two conditions is not a harder case of either."** Verdicts are kept per domain and never averaged.

## 2. The shape, one loop per domain

**D1, the individual learner.** The enablement is not a faculty inside the learner. Fig 11 :39: **"capability is a property of agent-and-habitat, never of the agent alone."** Fig 11 :40: **"A system cannot be made more capable by improving the agent in isolation. An improvement that does not change contact changes nothing, however much it improves."** What the learner carries must be reapplicable, and the weighting between private history and inherited prior is learnable from outcomes rather than fixed. Verdict: `specified`, triangulated with the meta-learning literature.

**D2, the carried item.** Fig 4 :23: **"upward: a method that can be reapplied. Never the recording of a particular success."** Fig 12 :47: **"recipe that names only its ingredients has recorded half of itself."** What carries is a typed relation, not an ingredient list, and it travels as an effect description. Fig 6 :30: **"The description travels. Fails by being sharpened into terms only we can read. Cast it in effect terms."** Verdict: `specified`, triangulated across Fig 4 and Fig 12, with Gentner's systematicity principle as a low-overlap world witness.

**D3, the habitat entered.** A substantially different situation is not a larger distance on a similarity scale. Fig 11 :66: **"Isolation is not removal of the habitat. It is substitution of one habitat for another."** Fig 11 :68: **"what you failed to reproduce is invisible until the goal fails"** and Fig 11 :69: **"what you unintentionally introduced is invisible until it acts."** Verdict: `specified`, single-source on the figure, corroborated in shape by the comparative-cognition requirement that transfer stimuli be novel and dissimilar.

**D4, the social or population frame.** Where a frame exists, only methods cross up and only head starts come down. Fig 4 :22: **"Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down."** A peer whose closure differs can supply an atom the learner cannot derive, but the import needs provenance and a shadow test. Fig 8 :53: **"Residual first, frame second."** Where a species has no such frame, this verdict does not apply. Verdict: `specified` for people, conditional for animals.

**Across all domains.** Fig 2 :35: **"Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you."** The target situation's ground settles the claim, separately for each frame. The abstraction loses information on the way up, so the round trip has to be measured. Fig 4 :30: **"So take the round trip and measure the gap. Without it you are navigating by dead reckoning: confident about a position nobody checked."** Verdict: `general` structural, not `grounded`.

## 3. What it explicitly does not say

- Not a mechanism name. The slot stays `???`. Fig 9 :32: **"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself."**
- Not that similarity causes transfer. Overlap is a filter for where to look, not a witness for the verdict (Fig 9 :60).
- Not that one score decides far transfer. Barnett and Ceci 2002 reject a single effect size across their nine dimensions.
- Not that more trials in the same closure supply what a new situation needs. Fig 8 :32: **"Closure is idempotent. Composing inside a closed set never leaves it, so no search, however long, adds an atom."**
- Not that a benchmark solve demonstrates capability. Fig 11 :70: **"A test harness is a substituted habitat. Which is why a synthetic solve proves wiring and never capability."**
- Not `grounded`. No ground reading was taken in this derivation.

## 4. What would settle it, the ground

A discriminating instrument, not a further argument. *The Hard Problem is About Source*, opened 2026-11-23, states that when several paths create the same behaviour, *"behavior is a projection with lossy information,"* and that *"the answer is unreached until a better instrument exists."* Here the discriminating observations are:

1. **Method or recording?** In a substituted habitat with unchosen actors present, does the learner generate a novel success, or only repeat a source occasion?
2. **Relation or attributes?** Does transfer survive when shared object attributes are held constant and only the typed relation is varied?
3. **Reproduced contact?** Are the conditions the goal required actually reproduced, and were unintended actors enumerated before scoring?
4. **Per-slot residual.** Which slot fails, and does the failure persist and refuse to trend down under more trials?
5. **Round trip.** What is R_T, the gap between the effect description sent up and the behaviour concretised in the target?
6. **Standing falsifiers from section 3 of `01_figure_grounded_audit.md`.** Six conditions, written before the run.

## 5. The honest stop

**Unreached, not unreachable.** The specific cognitive mechanism that enables far transfer is a declared `gap`. No figure line governs it. Fig 13 :134: **"It can say the answer is out of reach with the library as it stands, so something has to be imported before it is asked again."** Fig 8 :39: **"So a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence."** The answer stops at the limit of the available instruments and records the lineage so the stop is checkable. The canonical README: *"Indiscernibility is not identity."* A search that returns nothing is a verdict, not a silence, and it is not an invitation to invent a mechanism name.

## 6. One-paragraph version for outreach

> The Tether does not name the mental trick that lets a person or animal generalise far from experience. It fixes the shape any answer must have. Transfer is a union of conditions, not one faculty: what crosses into a new situation must be a reapplicable method or a head start with its typed relation intact, never a recording of one occasion; the description must be cast in effect terms so another closure can read it; the new situation must be treated as a substituted habitat where what you failed to reproduce and what you unintentionally introduced stay invisible until they act; and the ground there, which does not update on the learner's own report, must settle the result per slot. Capability belongs to learner and habitat together, so improving the learner alone with no change of contact changes nothing. The compression from method to effect description loses information, so the round-trip gap is the honest measure of what the abstraction cannot hold. The specific cognitive mechanism is left as `???`, with six failure conditions written in advance and the instruments that would close the slot.
