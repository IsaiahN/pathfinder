# Expert verification packet - the Tether on cross-modal abstractions

**What this is:** a claim in a form a representation-learning researcher or cognitive scientist can check. Mode, derivation and refuters are stated before the conclusion, so a reader knows what to look for and what would settle it.

**Mode: `general` on the structural shape, `specified` on the per-domain verdicts, `gap` on the concrete representation. Not `grounded`.** No experiment is claimed to have settled this. It is a shape and a route, not a result. This packet is a simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures, not a ground reading.

## 1. The claim, in one paragraph

An abstraction that remains useful across a change of sensory input, language or task is not a stored context-free object. What persists is a reapplicable generator with its typed bonds and provenance, carried into the new frame and re-settled against a non-updating ground in that habitat; the new level remakes whatever lands in it, so the recording of one success does not persist. The residual stays indexed per modality, because pooling hides the live channel. A shared atom held by two frames is corroboration only if the record shows convergence from different evidence; the same atom from one corpus is one witness. Which concrete representation does this is a declared gap. The packet offers the shape, four refuters written in advance, and the instruments owed to close it.

## 2. Three checkable sub-claims

### C1 - framework-internal; checkable by reading Figures 4 and 10

- **Statement:** the transferable thing is a generator, not an instance; a head start crosses, a recording does not.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 4 :22 - *"Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down."* Fig 4 :23 - *"upward: a method that can be reapplied. Never the recording of a particular success."* Fig 10 :68 - *"Different actors, a different currency, a different ground, and a lossy transform between. That is why there is no top rung and no regress: a generator crosses, an instance does not, and the level it lands on remakes it."*
- **Refuter:** a recording of one occasion that crosses into a new modality unchanged and yields a novel correct result there without re-derivation or re-settling against that modality's ground.

### C2 - machine-learning facing; checkable against a transfer experiment

- **Statement:** capability is a property of agent-and-habitat, and a synthetic benchmark is a substituted habitat. A cross-modal score therefore proves wiring until the term pays in a real habitat, with the residual read per modality.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 11 :39 - *"So the answerable question is not what intelligence is, but what makes a system capable. And capability is a property of agent-and-habitat, never of the agent alone."* Fig 11 :70 - *"A test harness is a substituted habitat. Which is why a synthetic solve proves wiring and never capability."* Fig 1 :19 - *"The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated."*
- **What to check:** in the experiment you know best, is the gain a new correct result in a habitat the model had not been tuned to, or a better score on a drawn-from-the-same-distribution harness? Is the pooled score hiding a live residual in one channel?
- **Refuter:** an agent-only change that alters contact with the new habitat's ground and pays there while habitat and channel are held fixed and measured; or a pooled residual near zero while a per-modality slot stays live.

### C3 - framework-internal plus literature-facing; checkable by reading Figure 8 and the provenance of a model

- **Statement:** convergence of representations is not by itself corroboration. Convergent derivation and adopted import are identical in contents and separable only by the record.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 8 :50-52 - *"Convergent derivation and adopted import look identical in the contents. Only the record of where each came from separates them, and the surplus lives only where the closures differ."* Fig 2 :31 - *"Independence, between two frames, is how little they draw on the same evidence."*
- **What to check:** did the two models reach the shared atom from demonstrably different data or objectives (source gate plus shadow test), or did they train on a common corpus or benchmark?
- **Refuter:** two frames demonstrably drawing on the same evidence that nevertheless produce a surplus neither could derive alone, with no differing closure. That breaks the independence condition and returns the case to the census.

## 3. What is explicitly not claimed

- Not that a shared embedding is useless. It is a head start and a vocabulary; its generator status must be shown.
- Not that convergence is worthless. Independent convergence is corroboration at maximum independence.
- Not that a harness result is false. It is a wiring result in a substituted habitat.
- Not that the framework names the winning representation. Fig 9 :32 - *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself."* The concrete representation is a `gap`.
- Not that convergence results in the literature (Radford et al. 2021, Huh et al. 2024) have been re-derived here. They were opened and used as secondary corroboration only.
- Not `grounded`. Nothing here is asserted as settled by experiment.
- Not a uniqueness claim over all theories of abstraction. No such census was run.

## 5. What would settle it

A source-discriminating instrument, not a further argument. From *The Hard Problem is About Source*: an instrument that reads green *"cannot comment on the source"*; the spectrometer resolved it. Here:

1. **Generator-versus-replay:** a novel correct result in a habitat not used for tuning, with habitat and channel held fixed and measured.
2. **Per-slot discriminator:** a pooled score near zero with a live per-modality residual.
3. **Provenance test:** the same atom reached from demonstrably different evidence, with source gate and shadow test reported.
4. **Round trip R_T:** carry the shared abstraction up and back down and measure which bond type, which per-slot residual, which provenance and which anchor were dropped.
