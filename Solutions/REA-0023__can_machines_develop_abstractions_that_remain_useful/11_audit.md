### 2.1 Arity, symmetry, scale

- **Arity:** a relation among a term φ, a bet/action (b,a), an observation o′ from a named channel, and a transform between two frames. At least three operands plus a directed transform. A two-place reading (representation, benchmark score) loses the channel and the transform, so it is rejected. An ordered bond is required because transfer has a direction; a comparison bond is required because "stays small" is a threshold (Fig 12 :87).
- **Symmetry:** not symmetric. The frames have different currencies and grounds. Treating them as interchangeable is the common-cause collapse, where agreement is the pool talking (Fig 2 :18-21). "Useful across" is a threshold, not a conjunction.
- **Scale:** the slot, the single-frame loop, the membrane between frames, and the lineage across tasks. The residual is intrinsically cross-scale. The frame transform F (Symbols table :81) runs between frames at one scale, but is defined for allocentric/egocentric, so it raises a strain rather than settling the cross-modal case.

## 4. Round-trip gap R_T

Fig 4 :30: *"So take the round trip and measure the gap. Without it you are navigating by dead reckoning: confident about a position nobody checked."*

- **T_A, going up:** carry the primary's shape to one sentence. Result: *a transferable abstraction is a method that keeps contact with a ground across a frame change.*
- **T_E, coming back:** put it into a specific modality. Result: *reweight the input features against the object term and the task objective in the new modality.*

**R_T = gap(x, (T_E ∘ T_A)(x)).** The gap is what the abstraction cannot hold:

1. **Bond type.** "Method" does not say whether the link is conjunction, sequence or production. Coming down, that decision decides whether a tactile sequence and a visual conjunction are the same substance (Fig 12 :117).
2. **Per-slot indexing.** "Keeps contact" does not say which channel is failing; coming down it licenses more of the same training, which is effort above the break (Fig 3 :29-30).
3. **Provenance.** "Method" does not say whether the shared atom was reached by convergence or import, so the one-sentence form cannot enforce the source gate (Fig 8 :50-52).
4. **The gap itself.** The abstraction cannot say which instrument would discriminate a generator from a replay, which is the actual `gap` in the answer.

This gap is not an error to eliminate; it is the measurement of what the coarser description cannot hold (Fig 4 :31). It is why the packet sends the generator and the refuter rather than the one-paragraph version. That one-paragraph version in `07` is a deliberately lossy artefact and is labelled as such.
