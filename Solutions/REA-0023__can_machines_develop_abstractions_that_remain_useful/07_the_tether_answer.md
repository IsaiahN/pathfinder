# The Tether's answer on cross-modal abstractions

**Mode: `general` on the structural shape, `specified` on the per-domain verdicts, `gap` on the concrete representation. Not `grounded`.** This is a shape and a route, not a result. Simulated derivation checked against the figures (`05_what_shape_means.md` section 7), not a ground reading.

## 1. The residual it answers

A term settled in one frame fails when the observation channel changes. R = |Γ(b,a) - o′| rises in the new modality, language or task. The question "can machines develop abstractions that remain useful across..." is a **union compound**, not one hard problem with a single lever: a reapplicable method must cross a membrane (D2) AND the term must keep contact with a non-updating ground in the new habitat (D3). Their overlap is a compound with its own loop (Fig 9 :55-57). Verdicts are kept per domain and never averaged.

## 2. The shape

1. **Yes, with a re-grounding of "remain".** What persists is not invariance of content but a reapplicable generator. Fig 4 :22: *"Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down."* Fig 10 :68: *"a generator crosses, an instance does not, and the level it lands on remakes it."* A stored context-free object (X1) has no nameable anchor and is not a domain yet.
2. **The kernel is invariant; the actors, currency and anchor change.** Fig 4 :19-20: *"The kernel is the part that does not change with size: the same loop and the same rule for naming what worked, whether one agent is running it or a thousand. Only the actors, the currency and the anchor change."* Applied across modalities, this is general on the structure and specified on the machine case.
3. **A second modality can add an atom only through contact with a differing closure.** Fig 8 :32: *"Closure is idempotent. Composing inside a closed set never leaves it, so no search, however long, adds an atom."* Fig 6 :23: *"No frame escapes its own closure. The bound moves only on contact with a frame whose closure differs."* Training longer in one sensorium cannot manufacture the other sensorium's atom.
4. **The import needs a record.** Fig 8 :50-52: *"Convergent derivation and adopted import look identical in the contents. Only the record of where each came from separates them, and the surplus lives only where the closures differ."* Convergence between independently trained models is corroboration at maximum independence; convergence from a shared corpus is one evidence pool talking (Fig 2 :18-21). The contents cannot decide it.
5. **Capability is agent-and-habitat.** Fig 11 :39: *"capability is a property of agent-and-habitat, never of the agent alone."* Fig 11 :70: *"A test harness is a substituted habitat. Which is why a synthetic solve proves wiring and never capability."* A cross-modal benchmark proves the wiring; only a real habitat pays for the abstraction.
6. **The residual stays per slot.** Fig 1 :19: *"The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated."* A pooled score is how a live failure in one channel disappears (Fig 1 :22). Modalities are not interchangeable actors with a fixed valence (Fig 11 :49-51).
7. **The carrier is a bond, not a feature list.** Fig 12 :5: *"A set of ingredients is not one. The bond is the rest of the information, and it is the half nothing was recording."* Fig 13 :129: *"A term carries the atoms it was built from and the bonds that joined them. That record is not documentation of the term. It is the term."*
8. **No figure names the representation.** Fig 9 :32: *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself."* Fig 13 :134: *"Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence,"* This is a declared `gap`, not a hidden answer and not an invitation to invent a name.

## 4. What it explicitly does not say

- Not that a stored, context-free representation is impossible in engineering terms. It says such an object cannot be the thing the figures identify as the transferable abstraction, because it has no nameable anchor in the new habitat.
- Not that a shared embedding is useless. It is a head start and a vocabulary; its status as a generator must be shown by provenance and by payment in a real habitat.
- Not that convergence is worthless. Convergent derivation at maximum independence is corroboration; convergence from a shared pool is one witness.
- Not that a harness result is false. It is a result about wiring in a substituted habitat (Fig 11 :66-67).
- Not `grounded`. No measurement here settles `left(R,φ)` for a real cross-modal transfer.
- Not that the framework is the only account of abstraction. That would be a verdict stated in `general` mode over an unrun census (Seat Map :281).

## 5. What would settle it - the ground

A source-discriminating instrument, not a further argument. From *The Hard Problem is About Source*: an instrument that reads green *"cannot comment on the source"*; the spectrometer resolved it. Here:

- **Generator-versus-replay test:** does the term produce a novel correct result in a new habitat that was not in the training channel, with the habitat and channel held fixed and measured? If yes, a generator crossed. If it only reproduces a stored occasion, it is a replay.
- **Per-slot discriminator:** a benchmark where the pooled score is near zero and a per-modality slot stays live. That is the residual the figures say is the only signal that says where to look.
- **Provenance test:** two frames that reach the same atom from demonstrably different evidence (source gate plus shadow test). That is convergence at maximum independence. The same atom from one corpus is one witness.
- **Round trip R_T:** carry the shared abstraction up and back down; measure what it cannot hold (bond type, per-slot residual, provenance, anchor). The gap is the report, not an error to remove.
- **Standing refuters:** the six pre-registered conditions in `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` section 4.

## 6. The honest stop

**Unreached, not unreachable.** The six-term set is *"a human's careful guess at reality's compression axes"* (carried from *They're Scanning Books*, marked as carried in `05`). Another sensorium's minimum set need not be these six. The answer stops where the instruments can no longer distinguish a generator from a replay, and records the lineage so the stop is checkable. Fig 13 :134: *"Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence,"* and Fig 8 :39: *"So a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence."* One live strain remains: the F frame transform (Symbols table :81), narrowed to the shape "an alignment is required" and excluded from the verdict.

## 7. One-paragraph version for outreach

> The Tether does not name a shared embedding as the abstract machine abstraction. It says that what remains useful across sensory inputs, languages and tasks is a generator with its bonds and provenance intact, carried across the membrane and re-settled against a ground that does not update on the model's own report; the recording of one success does not remain useful, because the level it lands on remakes it. The residual is read per modality, not averaged, and a synthetic benchmark proves wiring, not capability. Convergence between models is corroboration only when the record shows they drew on different evidence. The framework returns that shape, four refuters written in advance, and the instruments owed to close it. Which concrete representation occupies the slot stays open until the ground pays.
