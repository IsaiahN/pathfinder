# WATSON answer, REA-0023

**Mode: `general` on the structural shape, `specified` on the per-domain verdicts, `gap` on the concrete representation, not `grounded`.** Figures are primary and decide. World sources are secondary, corroborate but never overrule, and a conflict returns the derivation to the domain census. This is a simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures, not a ground reading.

## QUESTION

Can machines develop abstractions that remain useful across different sensory inputs, languages, and tasks?

Restated as an effect: a term φ settled against a ground in frame A keeps reducing R = |Γ(b,a) - o′| when the observation channel changes to frame B. R is indexed per slot, never averaged (Fig 1 :19). The anchor is a task ground that does not update on the model's own report (Fig 2 :29). The sought-for shape, bound before the run, is *a term that still pays after the channel changes because a generator crossed, not because a recording was replayed*.

## DOMAIN CENSUS

Arity: a relation among term φ, action (b,a), observation o′ from a named channel, and a directed transform between two frames. It carries an ordered bond and a comparison bond (Fig 12 :87). Symmetry: modalities are not interchangeable; treating them as such is the common-cause collapse (Fig 2 :18-21). Scale: slot, loop, membrane, lineage.

## TETHER (primary, verbatim, per domain)

**D1 Single-frame loop, status triangulated (Fig 8 and Fig 6, idempotence versus wall rule).**

> Fig 8 :32 "Closure is idempotent. Composing inside a closed set never leaves it, so no search, however long, adds an atom."
> Fig 6 :23 "No frame escapes its own closure. The bound moves only on contact with a frame whose closure differs."

Reading: more training inside one modality cannot manufacture the other modality's atom; a differing closure is required. Mode `general` on closure, `specified` on the model. Refuter: a new atom added by internal composition alone that pays at the ground.

**D2 The membrane, status triangulated (Fig 4 and Fig 10, membrane rule versus level rule).**

> Fig 4 :22 "Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down."
> Fig 4 :23 "upward: a method that can be reapplied. Never the recording of a particular success."
> Fig 10 :68 "Different actors, a different currency, a different ground, and a lossy transform between. That is why there is no top rung and no regress: a generator crosses, an instance does not, and the level it lands on remakes it."

Reading: what persists is a generator with its bonds, re-made at the new level; a recording is a description of one occasion. Mode `general` on the crossing rule, `specified` on the application. Refuter: a recording that transfers unchanged and yields a novel correct result in the new modality without re-derivation.

Reading: run inward and outward as two tracks; convergence is corroboration only with provenance of different evidence. Mode `general` on the rules, `specified` on the ML case. Refuter: same-evidence frames producing a surplus with no differing closure.

## VERDICT (from the figures)

**The figure-derived verdict:** yes, with a re-grounding of "remain". What stays useful across sensory inputs, languages and tasks is a reapplicable generator with its typed bonds and provenance, carried across a membrane and re-settled against a non-updating ground in each new habitat; the stored instance does not stay useful, because the level it lands on remakes it (Fig 4 :22-23, Fig 10 :68). A second frame can add an atom only through contact with a differing closure (Fig 8 :32, Fig 6 :23), the residual stays per slot (Fig 1 :19), capability is agent-and-habitat (Fig 11 :39), and convergence is corroboration only with a record of different evidence (Fig 8 :50-52). Verdicts are kept separate per domain and never averaged. Mode `general` on the structure, `specified` on the domains. The ML pool corroborates at the wiring level and is one witness; the animal and human fields corroborate the mechanism shape at low overlap. Which concrete representation occupies the slot is a declared `gap`, which is a verdict and not silence. One live strain (the F frame transform) is named and excluded.

- **cost if wrong:** building and shipping abstractions that are recordings of one occasion, then mistaking a harness score for capability. A team would tune a shared embedding for the source distribution, report cross-modal generality, and miss the live residual in the target channel. In outreach terms, an expert's twenty minutes would be spent refuting a shape that was never the right shape. The cost is bounded and largely reversible, but a wrong atom imported against a budget limit costs more than starting from primitives (Fig 6 :24).
- **undoable:** yes. Nothing is deleted if the claim fails; a refutation is added and the reach falls because *"something was added rather than because anything was deleted"* (Fig 6 :42-43). The shape and its six refuters are recorded with provenance so a later reader can locate the error (Fig 10 :62-64).
- **reversed by:** any of the six pre-registered refuters, in particular (1) a new atom added by internal composition alone that pays at the ground, (2) a recording that transfers unchanged and produces a novel correct result without re-derivation, (3) an agent-only change that changes contact and pays in the new habitat, (4) a pooled residual near zero with a live per-modality slot, (5) transfer with the bond lost and the same result, or (6) a figure line naming the concrete representation. A genuinely low-overlap source showing a fixed stored object transfers unchanged and pays in a new habitat would also reverse it.
