## What the wider literature contributes

1. **The question's apparent target is a `gap`, and the field knows it.** SEP *Information* reports that Shannon, Kolmogorov and Floridi have "next to nothing in common", quotes Shannon's warning that "It is hardly to be expected that a single concept of information would satisfactorily account for the numerous possible applications of the general field", and labels the resulting position "informational pluralism". The DIKW page records 130 definitions from 45 scholars and no consensus. These are one evidence pool, not two independent witnesses, but they agree with the figure-derived `gap` at Fig 9 :32 and Fig 13 :134.
2. **The information/knowledge boundary has an independent analogue.** SEP *Semantic Conceptions of Information*: Floridi's network theory of account makes semantic information count as knowledge when it is embedded in a network of questions and answers that account for it correctly. That is the same shape as the figure claim that a term becomes knowledge when it is settled and can be composed over (Fig 5 :69-72), reached from a different field. Corroboration, not authority.
3. **The factive/non-factive split is the same separator under another name.** SEP *Semantic Conceptions*: Bar-Hillel and Carnap's semantic information is explicitly non-factive ("semantic information is here not meant as implying truth"), while Floridi adds the veridicality thesis, "well-formed, meaningful, and truthful data". The figures do not adjudicate that split; they explain why a reading does not determine its source. Fig 6 :26: *"instruments extend what can be represented at all."*
4. **Evidence is relational in the literature too.** Wikipedia *Evidence*: the evidential relation is three-place (evidence, hypothesis, auxiliary assumptions) and is threatened by underdetermination and theory-ladenness. That matches Fig 9 :51, where admissibility depends on bearing on the residual rather than on the content of the reading.
5. **The DIKW ladder's own sources doubt the ladder.** The DIKW page records "occasional doubt being cast over any simple, linear, unidirectional model" and that even the processes transforming one component into the next are little described. That is the same doubt the figures raise about the bond type (Fig 12 :72/:77/:87).

## Distribution: offer, do not push

Figure-grounded reasons:

- **Fig 10 :52** *"Bring a characterised residual, never the source."* and **Fig 10 :42** *"A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it."* Lead with the four-role separator and the refuter. Show the framework only after a verdict on stage 1 is recorded.
- **Fig 9 :27-28** *"describe it before you go looking, or any frame you pick will seem to fit"*. Ask a reader what they would count as a failure of the separator before handing them the articles.
- **Fig 2 :40** *"disqualifying for a verdict: agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool"*. Do not select recipients for likely agreement, and do not pool later agreement as corroboration.
- **Fig 4 :22** *"Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down."* Stage 3 sends the formula as a method, with each user writing their own refuter and stating their own mode.

**Recommended order in a reply:** (1) the four-role separator and the five refuters; (2) ask what they would count as a failure and record the answer; (3) only then the links, with the note that the figures, not this summary, are the authority.

## The two-stage offer

1. **Stage 1.** Send the residual, the four-role separator and the pre-registered refuter. Ask for a failure condition. No theory, no request for trust, no request for agreement.
2. **Stage 2, opt-in only.** If they are curious and have recorded a stage-1 verdict, offer the three source links above. Their later agreement is never pooled as corroboration.
3. **Stage 3, opt-in only.** Offer the formula as a method to reuse, with the instruction that each user writes a refuter before the run and states the mode of the result.

**Hard precondition:** stages 1 to 3 only work if the stage-1 output survives. The separator's `general`/`specified` mode labels and its declared `gap` are part of the output, not optional framing. Remove them and the claim becomes a `general`-mode verdict about definitions, which is the output-layer failure named in the Seat Map.
