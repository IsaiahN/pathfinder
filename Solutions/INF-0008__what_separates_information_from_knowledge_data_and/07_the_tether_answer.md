# The Tether's answer: what separates information from knowledge, data, and evidence

**Mode: `general` on the structural separator, `specified` on the per-domain separators, `gap` on the request for one settled definition. Not `grounded`.** This is a shape and a route, not a result. It is a simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures (`05_what_shape_means.md` §6), not a ground reading.

## 1. The residual it answers

A single reading is called data by one frame, information by another, evidence by a third and knowledge by a fourth, and nothing in the ordinary vocabulary says which sorting is right. The popular answer is a value ladder. The question therefore asks for two things at once: a separator and a definition. The separator is reachable; the definition is not, and reporting that honestly is part of the answer.

## 2. The separator, per role

1. **Data is the recorded reading, the ingredient list.** Fig 12 :14: *"the ingredients are recorded and the arrangement is not"*. It is frame-internal to the instrument and the channel can degrade it without the ground changing (Fig 10 :24: *"the ground does not decay, the channel does"*).
2. **Information is the difference the reading makes.** Fig 12 :5: *"A set of ingredients is not one. The bond is the rest of the information, and it is the half nothing was recording."* Fig 12 :46: *"A bond is the typed relation between two operands."* At the loop scale it is the gap between prediction and observation (Symbols table :36: *"The gap between prediction and observation. The central quantity"*). With no prediction and no residual there is no information for that frame. A bit count cannot score the frame that produced it (Fig 1 :27: *"They are frame-internal. A frame cannot score itself with a quantity it also produces."*).
3. **Knowledge is a settled term that can be composed over.** Fig 5 :70: *"A term is settled when the ground pays for it. A term is usable when something can compose over it."* Fig 4 :25: *"A recording carried upward looks like knowledge and is a description of one occasion."* A library can grow while the reach does not (Fig 5 :72: *"and a library that grows is not a reach that grows."*).
4. **Evidence is a reading ranged against a non-updating anchor on a live residual.** Fig 2 :29: *"An anchor is whatever settles the question and does not change its answer because of what any frame thinks."* Fig 9 :51: *"evidence bearing on the residual is admissible; evidence bearing only on its maker is a stake-inducing channel, and the verifier is denied it"*. The same physical reading is data in one setting and evidence in another. What separates them is the role, not the content.

All four are **roles in one loop**, not members of the six-term substrate. Fig 13 :116: *"six members, and no amount of arranging produces a seventh"*. The data-to-wisdom ladder is a convention being used as a verdict: the bond type is undetermined (Fig 12 :72/:77/:87), and *"a convention that decides an outcome has stopped being one"* (Fig 10 :47).

## 3. What it explicitly does not say

- Not that any one of the four is a seventh primitive, or that the framework names one as such.
- Not that there is a single correct measure of information. SEP *Information* records Shannon, Kolmogorov and Floridi as having "next to nothing in common" and calls the resulting position informational pluralism.
- Not that the DIKW ladder is figure-derived, and not that value increases monotonically along it.
- Not that a reading determines its source. *The Hard Problem is About Source*: *"If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information"*.
- Not a `grounded` definition. No instrument settles a definition, and this whole answer is a simulated derivation.

## 4. The gap, stated plainly

The question asks for one separator that yields one definition. The figures return a separator by anchor and loop position, and then stop. Fig 9 :32: *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself"*. Fig 13 :134: *"Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence"*. So the unique definition is a declared `gap` of the shape-not-a-name kind, not an omission and not a failure. What would close it is a verbatim figure line naming one of the four as a substrate member or ranking them by value, or a source-discriminating instrument that collapses two roles at the ground.

## 5. What would settle it, the ground

An instrument or a figure re-census, not more agreement.

- A source-discriminating instrument showing whether a reading carries information independently of a frame's prediction would break the data/information separator (refuter 2).
- A demonstration that a recording of one occasion can be composed over as knowledge without settlement would break the information/knowledge separator (refuter 3).
- A claim settled by frames sharing one evidence pool with no non-updating anchor would break the evidence separator (refuter 4).
- A figure line naming any of the four as a seventh substrate member would break the role claim and close the gap (refuter 1).
- Standing falsifiers from `01` §3: all five are written before the conclusion and are repeated in `08` and `09`.

## 6. The honest stop

**Unreached, not unreachable in principle.** The figures report a shape, keep the lineage and stop where the available instruments stop. The six-term set is *"a human's careful guess at reality's compression axes"* (*They're Scanning Books*); declaring it makes the choice visible, not neutral. Fig 2 :40: *"disqualifying for a verdict: agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool"*. The essays, the SEP entries and the encyclopedia pages partly share an evidence pool, so their agreement is one witness talking, not corroboration. *"Indiscernibility is not identity."*

## 7. One-paragraph version for outreach

> The four words do not name four stages of a ladder. They name four roles in one loop, and what separates them is the anchor each is ranged against and what happens to a reading at each point. Data is the recorded ingredient list, the reading the instrument handed you. Information is the difference that reading makes: what the typed bond carries beyond its operands, and, at the loop scale, the gap between what you predicted and what happened. Knowledge is the narrower class of a settled term that something can be composed over; a recording of one occasion is not knowledge however much it looks like it. Evidence is not a stock of facts but a reading pressed into service on a live question and ranged against an anchor that does not move when a frame pushes on it. The same reading changes category when the residual or the anchor changes. None of the four is a member of the six-term substrate, the data-to-wisdom ladder has no figure-derived bond, and the single definition the question appears to ask for is a declared gap, reported as a verdict rather than as silence. What is offered is the separator, the refuters written in advance, and the instruments that would close it. The stop is honest because the lineage is kept.
