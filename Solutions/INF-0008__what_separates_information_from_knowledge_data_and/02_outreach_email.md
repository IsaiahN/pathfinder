**Use:** one-to-one, or as a cover letter attached to a submission through the public venues in `03_public_contact_points.csv`. **Not** for bulk send.

**Hard precondition:** do not send until the mode labels and the `gap` in `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` are intact in the text below. The offer collapses on contact if the separator is presented as a settled definition.

**Subject:** Four words, one residual, and a separator you can check in twenty minutes

> Hi [name],
>
> I have one specific output I would like you to check against what you already know. I am not asking you to read the framework first, and I am not asking you to agree. If the output does not survive, there is nothing else to look at.
>
> **The output (`general` on the shape, `specified` per domain, not `grounded`):** "information", "knowledge", "data" and "evidence" are not four stages of one ladder. They are four roles in one loop, and what separates them is the anchor each is ranged against and the position each occupies relative to the bond and the ground.
>
> - **Data** is the recorded reading, the list of ingredients. The figures say the ingredients are recorded and the arrangement is not, and that nothing downstream can tell the difference.
> - **Information** is the difference the reading makes. It is what the typed bond carries that the operands do not, and, at the loop scale, the gap between prediction and observation. With no prediction there is no information for that frame. A bit count cannot be used to score the frame that produced it.
> - **Knowledge** is a settled term something can be composed over. A recording of one occasion is not knowledge; a reapplicable method is. A library can grow while the reach does not.
> - **Evidence** is a reading pressed into service on a live residual and ranged against an anchor that does not move when a frame pushes on it. The same physical reading is data in one setting and evidence in another. What counts is whether it bears on the residual, not whether it bears on the person who made the claim.
>
> **What I am not claiming:** that any one of the four is a member of the six-term substrate (it is not, and no figure line names one); that the data-to-wisdom ladder is derived (the bond type is undetermined, so it is a convention being used as a verdict); or that this returns one settled definition of information. That last request is a `gap`, reported as a verdict and not as silence. I also cannot name which single measure of information is correct; the field's own sources disagree about that.
>
> **What would refute the separator:**
> 1. a verbatim figure line naming any of the four as a seventh substrate member, or ranking them by value;
> 2. a reading that carries information for a frame with no prediction and no residual;
> 3. a recording of one occasion that can be composed over as knowledge without settlement;
> 4. a claim settled by agreement among frames sharing one evidence pool with no non-updating anchor;
> 5. a source-discriminating instrument that makes one of the four collapse into another at the ground.
>
> I wrote these down before sending this. If any one holds, I will retract the shape claim. A one-line "this fails because..." is more useful to me than agreement. If you would rather not engage, no reply is needed.
>
> **If it survives, and only then:** the sources are below. The figures are the authority, not my summary of them.
> - Canonical figures with line numbers (local extract): `FRAMEWORK/reference/03-FIGURES_TEXT.md`, cited as `Fig N :line`

> - "A Mathematical Theory of Communication", Shannon 1948 (record and reprint links): https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/A_Mathematical_Theory_of_Communication
>

Write down what would refute your result *before* you run it and state the mode of what you get. A reported failure is more useful to me than a reported success.
