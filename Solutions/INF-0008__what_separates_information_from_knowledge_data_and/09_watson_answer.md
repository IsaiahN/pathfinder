## DOMAIN CENSUS

R by arity, symmetry and scale, then one line per domain with actors, currency and anchor; partition or union; order by dependency.

- **R (arity / symmetry / scale).** A reading is stably sortable into one of four bins called data, information, knowledge and evidence. Arity: at least binary, a reading related to a frame and, for two of the four, to a ground. Symmetry: the four are ordered by settlement and by ranging, so a value-ladder's symmetric ranking is not the relation at issue. Scale: carrier scale, agent and library scale, verification scale. The question bundles four conditions, so it is a union, not a partition (Fig 9 :54-57).
- **Domain 1, carrier frame.** Actors: instrument, channel, signal, reading `o'`. Currency: readings, bits, entropy, description length. Anchor: the code and the distribution, a convention; the ground is borrowed through a channel that decays. Overlap with D3 through the same physical reading.
- **Domain 2, agent and library frame.** Actors: an agent with `Γ`, `b`, `w`. Currency: settled terms that can be composed over, methods versus recordings. Anchor: the ground that paid for a term.
- **Domain 3, verification frame.** Actors: claim, verifier, ground. Currency: residuals and settlement; admissible evidence bears on the residual, not on the maker. Anchor: the non-updating anchor.
- **D4, semantic and source frame.** Actors: propositions, interpretations, instruments. Currency: truth, meaning, source discrimination. Anchor: the ground as read through a source-discriminating instrument. This is an overlap of D1 and D3, not a fifth domain.
- **Order by dependency.** Reading, then prediction and residual, then settlement, then verifier ranging (Fig 3 :20). Describe the residual before choosing a frame (Fig 9 :27-28). In this simulated run all domains draw on one evidence pool, so their agreement is one witness talking, not corroboration.

## VERDICT (from the figures)

Data is the recorded ingredient list; information is the difference the bond and the residual make; knowledge is a settled term something can compose over; evidence is a reading ranged against a non-updating anchor on a live residual. The same reading changes category with the residual and the anchor, so the four are roles in one loop, not stages of a value ladder and not members of the six-term substrate. The DIKW ordering has no figure-licensed bond, so it is a convention used as a verdict. The single definition the question appears to ask for is a declared `gap`, of the shape-not-a-name kind, reported as unreachable rather than as silence. Mode: `general` on the structural separator, `specified` per domain, `gap` on the unique definition, none of it `grounded`.

- **cost if wrong:** bounded and locatable. A mis-sort treats a recording as knowledge, a self-produced compression score as information and an agreeing evidence pool as evidence against the world. Those are the named collapses at Fig 4 :25, Fig 1 :27 and Fig 2 :40, and each is silent from inside the frame that commits it. The avoidable cost is spending an outside reader's independence by sending a finished definition instead of a separator and a refuter, which Fig 2 :42 prices: "Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong."
- **undoable:** yes. The separators are relational roles, so re-sorting a reading when the residual or the anchor changes requires no new primitive, no retraction of a definition and no deletion from the record. A sent claim can be superseded by a corrected packet. Nothing has to be withdrawn from the substrate.
- **reversed by:** a ground reading or a figure re-census, not agreement. A source-discriminating instrument that shows whether a reading carries information independently of a frame's prediction would break refuter 2; a recording composable over as knowledge without settlement would break refuter 3; a claim settled by agreeing frames with no non-updating anchor would break refuter 4; a verbatim figure line naming one of the four as a seventh substrate member would break the role claim and close the gap. Until one of these fires, the verdict stands as `candidate`.
