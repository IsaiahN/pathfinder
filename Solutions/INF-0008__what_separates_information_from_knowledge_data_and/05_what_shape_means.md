## 1. A shape is a constraint you can state before you can name the answer

The canonical limit: the framework returns *"what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)"*. Fig 9 :32: *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself"*. Fig 13 :134: *"Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence"*.

From *The Library of Babel*: *"You can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."*

So a **shape** here is the four-role separator stated as constraints, not a definition of "information" handed down as a name. The constraints are: which frame assigns the term, what currency moves, which anchor is ranged against, and which bond carries the composition. That is enough to sort a reading and to notice a mis-sort. It is not enough to choose Shannon over Kolmogorov over Floridi, and the framework explicitly says so.

For this question the shape reads: *data* is the recorded ingredient list; *information* is the difference the bond and the residual make; *knowledge* is settled and composable; *evidence* bears on a live residual against an anchor that does not move. The sought-for single definition is the filler we refuse to supply.

## 2. How the shape is obtained

- *Library of Babel*: use the vocabulary closest to the thing, then look for new terms that overlap or correlate with it. *"With each new term gained, you narrow the space and deepen your vocabulary."* Here the closest vocabulary is the field's own: Shannon entropy, DIKW levels, semantic information, evidential relation. Those terms overlap; the figure census narrows them by anchor rather than adopting any one of them.
- Fig 6 :30: *"The description travels. Fails by being sharpened into terms only we can read. Cast it in effect terms."* Fig 9 :30-31: *"your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect rather than the cause"*. So the residual was described as an effect (a reading cannot be stably sorted), not as a cause (nobody agrees on definitions).
- *Decoding Tacit Knowledge*: *"When contact isn't possible, the best you can send is a plain description of what you'd expect to see, because anyone can check that against the world."* That is exactly the stage-1 offer in `02_outreach_email.md`.
- *Library of Babel* on stopping: *"You can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough..."*, and the paradox, *"you enter as a researcher looking for a book, and if successful, you will leave as the author"*. The recursive narrowing of this audit produced a role assignment, not a dictionary entry, which is the right kind of stop.
- *Library of Babel* on bundles: an exhaustive search that fails leaves two possibilities, the question was unanswerable or *"malformed"*, possibly *"a bundle or conflation of several problems masquerading as one"*. That is the figure-derived union verdict (Fig 9 :54-57) stated in essay vocabulary.

## 3. What licenses a name

Only two things, both outside the shape itself:

1. **The ground settles it.** `THE_FORMULA.md` step 5: *"the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays. A gate is not the ground."* The canonical README: the framework *"stops at the limit of what the available instruments can distinguish, and records the lineage so the stop is honest."*
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs** (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8 :50-52), with a source gate and a shadow test.

SEP *Semantic Conceptions of Information* gives an unusually clean instance of (1) from outside the framework: Floridi's network theory of account makes strongly semantic information count as knowledge when it is embedded in a network of questions and answers that account for it correctly. That is an independent field arriving at "settled and answerable for" as the knowledge condition. It corroborates the figure shape; it does not replace it.

*They're Scanning Books* sharpens the boundary between a stored record and the capacity that produced it: *"A corpus is the compressed output of human minds; the capacity that produced it is a generator, and outputs do not contain their generator."* This is the same asymmetry as Fig 4 :25 between a recording and a method, reached from machine learning. And: *"Declaring the primitives does not make them neutral. It makes the choice visible"*, which is why the six-term substrate cannot be used to smuggle in a definition of any of the four names.

## 4. Why the source question governs here

*The Hard Problem is About Source* supplies the mechanism the figures state only structurally: *"If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information"*; *"An instrument that can't distinguish these cases demonstrates its own inadequacy"*; and the answer *"is unreached until a better instrument exists"*. A reading does not determine which source produced it. That is why "a reading is data" and "a reading is evidence" can both be true, and why the separator cannot be a property of the reading alone.

*How Intelligence Actually Works* adds the distinction horizon and the union horizon: the point at which an observer, or all observers together with any instrument any of them can build, cannot discriminate origin. Being below the union horizon *"is never a settled verdict, only a report that nobody has reached it yet"*. That is the honest status of the `gap` in this folder: not "information is undefinable", but "no figure line and no available instrument returns the single definition the question appears to ask for".

Fig 8 :39: *"So a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence."* The gap is a verdict about reach, not about the world.

## 5. Q: does "shape, not a name" forbid naming any of the four?

**No, with a qualification.** The canonical phrase governs a *missing* primitive, one not held in the library. Fig 13 :116: *"six members, and no amount of arranging produces a seventh"*. Data, information, knowledge and evidence are not missing primitives; they are roles in the loop. What the framework refuses is not the words but the claim that a figure line names one of them as a member of the substrate or returns a unique definition. The words are ordinary terms we bind to roles, and binding is allowed.

**Correction to my own first pass, kept visible.** In the first draft of §1 I wrote that the framework "forbids naming information". That overstated the rule. The correct reading, triangulated across the canonical README, Fig 13 :116 and the exemplar's §5b, is: naming a role is binding; claiming the framework derived a definition is not. The overstatement is recorded here rather than erased. The exemplar's `05` section 5b makes the same correction for naming Gradient in the QMGR case.

## 6. Two consequences for outreach

**Prestige is not action.** *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*: *"Prestige must never be allowed to validate an action directly"*; *"Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it."* A list of people selected for likely agreement with a definition is the named collapse, not corroboration. The legitimate ask is a budget to be tried and a failure condition.

**My own audit is a simulation, not a ground reading.** Same essay: *"LLMs can quickly simulate an approximate answer/output with the formula found in the architecture post"*, and *"the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself"*. *Arrival of the Fittest* repeats the caveat: outputs produced by simulating the framework with an LLM *"could never be verified if the solutions presented were not contrived to fit the formula"* and must be seen as *"simulacra"*. So this folder's derivation is a simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures. It is not a test of the separator and must be labelled as such. It cannot settle the question it reports on.

## 7. Where the eight essays agree with the figures, and where the overlap is too high to count

- *How a Society of Agents Would Work* carries the Figure 4 rule into a concrete setting: *"Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down."* That is a restatement of the figure, not an independent witness.
- *Arrival of the Fittest* carries the formula, the symbols and the operators; it is the canonical article, not world corroboration.
- *The Library of Babel* and *Decoding Tacit Knowledge* are the two essays that genuinely sharpen the shape (stop at a descriptive set; check effects against the world). They still share an author and an evidence pool with the figures, so their agreement is expected and carries no independent witness weight. Fig 2 :40: *"disqualifying for a verdict: agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool"*.

The genuinely low-overlap corroboration in this solution comes from the SEP entries, the DIKW and Evidence encyclopedia records, and Shannon's publication record, all listed in `10_source_ledger.csv`. Even those philosophy-of-information sources overlap with each other and are counted as one pool.
