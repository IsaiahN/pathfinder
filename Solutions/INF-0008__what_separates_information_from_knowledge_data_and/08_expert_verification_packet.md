# Expert verification packet: the Tether on information, knowledge, data and evidence

**What this is:** a claim in a form an information scientist, philosopher of information or epistemologist can check. Mode, derivation and refuters are stated before the conclusion, so a reader knows what to look for and what would settle it.

**Mode: `general` on the structural separator, `specified` per domain, `gap` on the single definition. Not `grounded`.** No measurement settles a definition. This is a shape and a route, not a result, and this packet cannot settle the question it reports on.

## 1. The claim, in one paragraph

Four roles in one loop are separated by anchor and by position relative to the bond and the ground. Data is the recorded reading or ingredient list, frame-internal to the instrument and degradable through the channel. Information is the difference the reading makes, the typed bond's contribution beyond its operands and the residual between prediction and observation; a frame cannot score itself with the amount it produces. Knowledge is a settled term something can be composed over, a reapplicable method rather than a recording of one occasion. Evidence is a reading ranged against a non-updating anchor on a live residual; admissibility depends on bearing on the residual, not on the maker of the claim. All four are roles, not members of the six-term substrate; the data-to-wisdom ladder has no figure-derived bond; and the unique definition the question appears to ask for is a declared `gap`, which is a verdict and not silence.

## 2. Three checkable sub-claims

### C1 - framework-internal and composition-facing; checkable by reading Figures 12, 1 and the Symbols table

- **Statement:** data is the recorded ingredients; information is what the bond carries beyond the operands and the residual between prediction and observation. A self-produced compression score is disqualified as a ground metric.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 12 :14 *"the ingredients are recorded and the arrangement is not"*; Fig 12 :5 *"A set of ingredients is not one. The bond is the rest of the information, and it is the half nothing was recording."*; Fig 12 :46 *"A bond is the typed relation between two operands."*; Fig 1 :27 *"They are frame-internal. A frame cannot score itself with a quantity it also produces."*
- **Refuter:** a reading that carries information for a frame with no prediction and no residual, or a bond that contributes nothing beyond its operands, or a demonstration that a frame's own bit count legitimately settles what that frame knows.

### C2 - framework-internal; checkable by reading Figures 4, 5 and 12

- **Statement:** knowledge is the intersection of settled and composable; a recording is not knowledge even when it looks like it, and a library can grow while the reach does not.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 4 :25 *"A recording carried upward looks like knowledge and is a description of one occasion."*; Fig 5 :70 *"A term is settled when the ground pays for it. A term is usable when something can compose over it."*; Fig 5 :72 *"and a library that grows is not a reach that grows."*; Fig 12 :104 *"A settled molecule becomes an atom for whatever composes over it."*
- **Refuter:** a recording of one occasion that can be composed over as a method without settlement, or a settled term with no composability and no figure term for the difference. The independent analogue to check against is Floridi's network theory of account in SEP *Semantic Conceptions of Information*, which makes information count as knowledge when embedded in a network of questions and answers that account for it.

### C3 - verification-facing; checkable by reading Figures 2, 9 and 10

- **Statement:** evidence is a role rather than a stock, defined by bearing on a live residual and by being ranged against an anchor that does not move when a frame pushes on it.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 2 :29 *"An anchor is whatever settles the question and does not change its answer because of what any frame thinks."*; Fig 9 :51 *"evidence bearing on the residual is admissible; evidence bearing only on its maker is a stake-inducing channel, and the verifier is denied it"*; Fig 10 :42 *"A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it."*
- **Refuter:** a claim settled by agreement among frames drawing on one evidence pool with no non-updating anchor, with no way for the framework to call it a mirror chain; or evidence that settles a claim without bearing on any residual. The independent analogue to check against is the three-place evidential relation in the Wikipedia *Evidence* article.

## 3. What is explicitly not claimed

- Not that any of the four is a seventh substrate member or a primitive. Fig 13 :116 *"six members, and no amount of arranging produces a seventh"*.
- Not that the DIKW ladder is derived. The bond type is undetermined among sequence, production, subtraction and comparison (Fig 12 :72/:77/:82/:87), and Fig 10 :47 *"a convention that decides an outcome has stopped being one"*.
- Not that there is one correct measure of information, nor that this packet chooses between Shannon, Kolmogorov and Floridi. That is the declared `gap` (Fig 9 :32, Fig 13 :134).
- Not that a reading determines its source. A source-discriminating instrument is owed.
- Not `grounded`, and not a ground reading. This is a simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures.

## 4. Five refuters, written before the run

1. A verbatim figure line naming any of the four as a seventh substrate member, or ranking them by value.
2. A reading that carries information for a frame with no prediction and no residual.
3. A recording of one occasion composable over as knowledge without settlement.
4. A claim settled by agreement among frames sharing one evidence pool with no non-updating anchor.
5. A source-discriminating instrument that makes one of the four collapse into another at the ground.
