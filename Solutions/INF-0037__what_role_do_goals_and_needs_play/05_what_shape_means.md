## 1. A shape is a constraint, not a filler

The figures README states the limit: the framework returns *"what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)"*. Fig 9 :32: *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself."* Fig 13 :134: *"Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence"* and the figure adds that the framework cannot name the missing primitive because naming it would mean already holding it.

From *The Library of Babel*, the essay closest to this question: *"You can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."*

So a **shape** is the narrowed form of an answer you can state before you can name an instance. It constrains; it does not fill. For this question:

- **Shape:** an item is useful to the degree that it reduces a live per-slot residual under a stated comparison and subtraction.
- **Name:** "this document is the useful one for that analyst on Tuesday at 09:00." That is an instance, and the framework does not produce it from inside the frame.

## 2. How a shape is obtained

- *Library of Babel* gives the steering rule in terms that are almost this question verbatim: *"The problem you have determines what makes a book relevant to you or not."* And: *"The rest of the books that are not relevant to you might as well be incoherent noise no matter whether they are gibberish or not."* The question supplies the relevance relation, exactly as the residual does in Fig 11.
- *Library of Babel* on vocabulary: *"With each new term gained, you narrow the space and deepen your vocabulary."* Bootstrapping by proximity is the search method.
- Fig 6 :30: *"The description travels. Fails by being sharpened into terms only we can read. Cast it in effect terms."* Fig 9 :30-31: *"your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect rather than the cause."* So "goal" must be described as the effect it produces (a comparison that fixes a target), not as a mentalistic cause imported from a preferred psychology.
- *Decoding Tacit Knowledge*: *"When contact isn't possible, the best you can send is a plain description of what you'd expect to see, because anyone can check that against the world."* The outreach packet therefore states what would be observed if the shape claim were true.
- *Library of Babel* on stopping: *"you will know that you are done because the finish line will also be the starting place"*, and the paradox is that by then you have *"composed the answer through the process of locating it."*

## 3. What licenses a name

Only two things, both outside the shape itself:

1. **The ground settles it.** Formula step 5: *"the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays. A gate is not the ground."* For usefulness, the ground is a decision outcome: did the item change what the system did, and did that action pay? That outcome is not in this derivation.
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs** (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8 :50-52), with a source gate and a shadow test. A relevance researcher's finding can be imported as a corroborating witness, but it must be recorded as coming from that frame, not derived from the figures.

*They're Scanning Books* sharpens the limit: *"A minimum viable set of six terms is a human's careful guess at reality's compression axes; another sensorium's minimum set need not be those six."* And: *"Declaring the primitives does not make them neutral. It makes the choice visible, which is worth a great deal, because a visible choice can be revised and an emergent one cannot. But visibility is not neutrality, and revision is still bounded by what can be conceived to revise toward."*

So stating that goals set residuals is a visible choice within this framework, not a neutral fact about all possible systems. A system whose primitives do not include a comparison operator may not have goals in this sense at all. That is a live limit, not a footnote.

## 4. The ground and the source problem

*The Hard Problem is About Source* gives the mechanism: an instrument reads green and *"cannot comment on the source"*. Several sources can produce one reading, and *"If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information"*. It was the spectrometer, not an argument, that resolved the case. An instrument that cannot discriminate *"demonstrates its own inadequacy, it cannot claim anything substantial about what is being measured or what causes it."*

Applied here: an item's being attended to is a reading, not a source. It can be produced by a goal-driven comparison, by a stimulus-driven salience spike, or by a shared habit. Calling it "useful" from the reading alone is the metamerism error. What is owed is an instrument that discriminates among the sources, not a label.

The world sources corroborate this from outside. Relevance theory and value of information both define usefulness relationally, with the decision maker's goals and effort in the definition. Optimal foraging defines the currency by the forager's problem. But all of them are secondary witnesses. The figures decide.

## 5. Answer to the shape question

**What shape must an answer to INF-0037 have?** It must be a relation with at least three places: an item, a live per-slot residual, and a goal or need that fixes the comparison and the subtraction. It must not be a property of the item alone, because Fig 11 :49 says valence is relative to the residual. It must not be a global average, because Fig 1 :22 says aggregation destroys the live signal. It must not be a frame-internal score, because Fig 1 :27 disqualifies a frame scoring itself with a quantity it also produces. And it must be bindable as an ordinary term so it can be refused, because Fig 5 :66 requires the held shape to become refusable.

**Does the framework return a name here?** No. It returns the form of a useful item, not the item. The Library of Babel essay's wording is the cleanest statement: the problem you have determines what makes a book relevant. The framework can tell you the problem is doing the selecting; it cannot tell you which book you need.

## 5b. Correction to my own §3 (see `06_isomers_and_order.md` Q1)

I first wrote that the "shape, not a name" rule forbids naming a goal or need at all. That was too broad. The README's phrase governs a *missing* primitive, one not held in Γ. Comparison `⋛`, subtraction `−` and sequence `→` are all already in the operator set. Naming the bond that a goal uses is a **binding onto an operator already held**, not the invention of a new primitive. What remains forbidden is treating a goal as a seventh atom, or claiming that naming it moves the wall (Fig 6 :22 *"IMPORT adds an atom, and the added atom is what expands the closure"*: this one adds none). Both readings are recorded so the correction is visible.

## 6. Consequences for contact

**Prestige is not action.** From *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*: *"Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it."* A list of famous relevance researchers who agree with the shape claim would be a convention, not corroboration.

**Agreement is disqualifying when the pool is shared.** *How a Society of Agents Would Work* puts the same point for a group: a group is worth having only while its members stay different, and the group's agreement is never the verdict. Fig 2 :40: *"disqualifying for a verdict: agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool"*.

**My own audit is a simulation, not a ground reading.** *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*: *"LLMs can quickly simulate an approximate answer/output with the formula found in the architecture post... However, the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself."* The Arrival article adds that outputs *"could never be verified if the solutions presented were not contrived to fit the formula."* So `01` is a simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures. It is not a test of the claim and must be labelled as such.
