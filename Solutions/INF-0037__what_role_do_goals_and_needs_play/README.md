## What is deliberately not here

- No personal emails of named individuals.
- No ranking of people by how likely they are to agree.
- No bulk-send plan.

Three reasons in the figures' own words:

1. **Agreement is not an anchor.** Fig 2 :35: *"Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you."* Selecting recipients for receptiveness manufactures exactly the correlation that makes their agreement worthless.
2. **A shared pool is one witness.** Fig 2 :40: *"disqualifying for a verdict: agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool."* A second seat simulated from the same figures is internal consistency, not corroboration.
3. **Do not spend the outside frame's independence.** Fig 10 :42: *"A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it."* Stage 1 carries the residual and its refuter, never the source.

**Suggested next step.** Take `08_expert_verification_packet.md` to the public venues in `03_public_contact_points.csv`. Ask what a failure would look like. Record the answer before showing any theory. A refutation is more useful than an endorsement.
