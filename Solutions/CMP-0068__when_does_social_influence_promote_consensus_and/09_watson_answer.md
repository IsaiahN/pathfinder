## QUESTION

Decide, in effect terms, what the Tether says about a set of frames whose opinions update under mutual influence. Option A: treat consensus as the intrinsic product of more influence and more contact, and fragmentation as a failure to overcome. Option B: treat agreement as an ambiguous reading with three distinct sources, add a fourth case where disagreement should not be closed, and return the conditions that separate them. What each leads to: A cannot distinguish triangulation from drift from a shared pool, so it mistakes the observable for the verdict; B returns a checkable discrimination with operational tests and refuters, but no numeric threshold the figures do not supply.

## DOMAIN CENSUS

R by arity, symmetry and scale, then one line per domain with actors / currency / anchor, partition or union, and order by dependency.

- **R (the residual).** The unobservable difference behind an agreement reading. Arity: at least two conditions, so a union compound, not one hard question (Fig 9 :55). Symmetry: influence is a directed, weighted relation (Fig 2 :15 names "whoever had the most influence"), not a symmetric join. Scale: first order inside one group, second order between rooms (Fig 7).
- **Domain 1, frame updating against a ground.** Actors: frames, opinions, weights on private history versus inherited prior. Currency: updates by averaging and by contact with a signal. Anchor: the ground, "An anchor is whatever settles the question and does not change its answer because of what any frame thinks." Question type: partition (does a non-updating ground exist for this residual?).
- **Domain 2, evidence pool and independence.** Actors: sources, media diets, homophilous ties, correlated samples. Currency: evidence overlap. Anchor: "Independence, between two frames, is how little they draw on the same evidence." Question type: partition (one pool or several?).
- **Domain 3, between-room composition and disagreement type.** Actors: rooms, closures, the residual each surfaces. Currency: agreement within, residual between. Anchor: the bracket's fidelity and the ground's pressure across a handoff (Fig 7 :38-39). Question type: union (is a persistent disagreement one question or several?).
- **Order by dependency.** Anchor test and independence test first (Fig 3 :20-21), then the update, then the disagreement-type decision, then any composition of rooms. A reading of Domain 3 taken before Domain 2 is a reading of nothing.

Strain test applied: Fig 2 :40 disqualifies one-pool agreement as a verdict, while Fig 2 :41 says agreement about shared meaning is the product. Re-census shows different objects (world-directed verdict versus meaning-directed convention), so no strain remains. The second potential strain, classical averaging reaching consensus under high confidence versus Fig 7 :14 blending swamping novelty, is resolved by re-census: within-closure averaging versus across-closure handoff.

## VERDICT (from the figures)

The figures decide a structural verdict, not an empirical one. Social influence promotes genuine consensus exactly when frames range separately against a non-updating ground and draw on independent evidence. It intensifies polarization or fragmentation through two distinct failures: mutual update with no anchor, where the settled position tracks influence weight; and a shared evidence pool, where agreement is the pool talking. More homogeneous contact and more of the same rooms compound agreement without adding evidence. A persistent disagreement that does not shrink with effort is a union of conditions to split, not a defect to push through. The same influence mechanism changes sign with the residual, so no context-free list of good and bad mechanisms is licensed. No numeric threshold is returned. Mode: `specified` on the discrimination, `general` on the shape, `candidate` and ungrounded throughout.

- **cost if wrong:** bounded and locatable. The packet states its mode, refuters and figure lines before the conclusion, so an error is findable. The real cost is spending the independence of outside readers by asking for agreement before they have checked the discriminator. Fig 2 :42 "Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong." That cost is avoidable by leading with the three outcomes and the refuter.
- **undoable:** yes. The discrimination is a set of tests, not an installed institution. A bad application can be superseded without withdrawing the figure reading. Nothing in the derivation requires a new primitive, and the gap at the level of thresholds is declared rather than filled with a guess.
- **reversed by:** a ground reading, not agreement. A controlled case showing accurate consensus with no ground contact and one updating pool; a demonstration that adding a non-updating ground and independent evidence increases polarization with other conditions held; or a pre-declared sample in which the discriminator fails to beat an agreement score. Until one of these fires, the verdict stands as `candidate`.
