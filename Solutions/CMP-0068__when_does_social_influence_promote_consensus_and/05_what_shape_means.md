## 1. A shape is a constraint you can state before you can name the outcome

The canonical figures README says the framework returns *"what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)"*. Fig 9 :32 "you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself." Fig 13 :134 "Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence," and Fig 13 :135 "It cannot say which primitive is missing, because naming it would mean already holding it."

From *The Library of Babel*: *"You can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."* And: *"With each new term gained, you narrow the space and deepen your vocabulary."*

So for this question a **shape** is: three distinguishable outcome classes with the conditions that separate them, plus the instruction about which disagreements should not be closed. It is not a critical confidence bound, not an effect size, and not a claim about any platform. "More contact" is not a shape; "contact with a non-updating ground under independent evidence" is.

## 2. How the shape is obtained, and why resistiveness is built in

- *Library of Babel*: use the vocabulary closest to the thing, look for recurring new terms that overlap it, recurse. The question is the tether: *"you need something to tether and anchor you to reality... This anchor is provided by your own question."*
- Fig 6 :30 "The description travels. Fails by being sharpened into terms only we can read. Cast it in effect terms." Fig 9 :30-31 "your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect rather than the cause."
- *Decoding Tacit Knowledge*: *"When contact isn't possible, the best you can send is a plain description of what you'd expect to see, because anyone can check that against the world."*
- *Library of Babel* on stopping: *"you will know that you are done because the finish line will also be the starting place"*, and the paradox: by the time you have the terms you have *"composed the answer through the process of locating it."*

The shape obtained here is therefore a set of checkable expectations: if the anchor is captured, expect drift toward influence weight; if the pool is shared, expect agreement that does not discriminate; if the disagreement does not shrink, expect a union to split.

## 3. What licenses a name (a number, a platform, a threshold)

Only two things, both outside the shape:

1. **The ground settles it.** Formula step 5: the ground settles it, verified only by what pays. The README: the framework *"stops at the limit of what the available instruments can distinguish, and records the lineage so the stop is honest."*
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs** (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8 :50-52), with source gate and shadow test.

*They're Scanning Books* sharpens the limit: *"The real import is not a new term in the existing space. It is a new dimension of the space."* And: *"A minimum viable set of six terms is a human's careful guess at reality's compression axes; another sensorium's minimum set need not be those six."* Also: *"Declaring the primitives does not make them neutral. It makes the choice visible."*

So the ten world sources in this folder are imports with provenance, not derivations from the figures. Their agreement with the figure shape is corroboration from different closures, not a proof.

## 4. The essays name this exact failure mode in plain language

*How Intelligence Actually Works* is almost a gloss on Fig 2 for our question: *"if you are just going off of what is present in your heads, then eventually, the room will be swayed by negotiation which is persuasion, or by coherent drift (both of you found a middle ground to agree on). The problem is that the room session only produces an outcome that you agree on, having come from diverse frames with different vantage points of the problem. It does not mean that the output was objective truth."* And immediately: *"That's where RLVR steps in... The ground must be immovable by persuasion and not bi-directional like the frames are."*

*How a Society of Agents Would Work* gives the network version: *"A group is only worth having while its members stay different."* and *"Ten copies watching the same thing are one opinion said ten times."* and *"If every agent's w drifts to the average, the spread is gone and the gradients are spent. The room then slides into what Figure 2 calls coherent drift."*

*Decoding Tacit Knowledge* adds the time dimension: *"Long, close contact pulls the frames together, so the process that makes communication easy slowly uses up that extra reach."* And the convention/verdict split: *"Shared pretext is good for one thing and ruins another. Agreement is the whole point when you're settling what words mean. It's worthless for settling what's true when both sides learned from the same sources."*

These are not new claims. They are the figure lines restated at human scale, and they are why the answer leads with the anchor and independence conditions rather than with a recommendation to deliberate more.

## 5. Why a shared reading cannot name the source

*The Hard Problem is About Source* gives the discriminator problem: *"If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information"*, and *"An instrument that can't distinguish these cases demonstrates its own inadequacy."* Applied here: a measured agreement reading cannot tell you whether it came from triangulation, drift, or a common pool. The spectrometer, not an argument, resolved the colour case. The corresponding instruments here are operational tests of independence and anchor status, not further arguments about whether a group seems healthy.

This is also why the pre-registered refuter in `01` asks for a case that separates the three mechanisms rather than another description of agreement.

## 6. Two consequences for outreach and for this document's own status

**Prestige is not action.** From *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*: *"Prestige must never be allowed to validate an action directly... Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it. The Ground Maintainer must be blind to prestige."* A list of famous people who agree with the discriminator would be the named collapse, not corroboration. The legitimate ask is a case that could refute it.

**My own audit is a simulation, not a ground reading.** Same essay: *"LLMs can quickly simulate an approximate answer/output with the formula found in the architecture post... However, the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself."* The article's demo caveat adds that such outputs *"could never be verified if the solutions presented were not contrived to fit the formula."* So `01` and this file are simulated derivations checked for internal consistency against the figures. They are **not** tests of the discriminator and cannot settle it. That is stated on every substantive file.

## 7. Correction recorded, not erased

An earlier framing of this question could have been "identify the influence mechanism that produces consensus." That framing is refused here. Per Fig 11 :49-51, valence is relative to the residual: the same mechanism has different signs in different residuals. The correct object is the *condition* (anchor status, evidence independence, disagreement type), not a list of good and bad mechanisms. This correction is recorded in `01` §6 and is not silently removed.
