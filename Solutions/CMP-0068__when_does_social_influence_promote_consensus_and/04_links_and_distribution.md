## Two findings from the world sources that change how the pitch is read

1. **Non-monotonicity.** Lorenz 2006 and the 2023 HK amendment both report that lowering or raising the confidence bound does not move the system smoothly from fragmentation to consensus. So the outreach claim is deliberately *not* "more diversity, more contact, or more confidence produces consensus." The figure-grounded claim is about ground contact and independence, which are categorical conditions, not amounts of one variable.
2. **Disagreement can be a filter.** Winklmayr et al. find that stalemates improve collective accuracy when information is correlated. This is the world-side echo of Fig 9 :4-5: a disagreement that does not shrink with effort may be several well-formed questions or evidence of a correlated cue, so closing it is not automatically progress.

## Distribution: offer, do not push

- **Fig 4 :22** "Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down." **Fig 4 :25** "A recording carried upward looks like knowledge and is a description of one occasion." Sending the whole framework before a reader has described a residual sends a recording. Lead with the discriminator and the refuter.
- **Fig 10 :42** "A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it." A recipient who has absorbed the framework before being asked for a verdict has joined the frame being checked.
- **Fig 9 :27-28** "describe it before you go looking, or any frame you pick will seem to fit." Ask what they would expect to see if the discriminator were true before giving them the links.
- **Fig 2 :40** "agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool." Do not gather a list of people likely to agree, and never pool later agreement as corroboration.
- **Seat Map :163** "High correlation is disqualifying for a verdict and efficient for a convention." A mass send selected for receptiveness produces a convention, not a check.

**Recommended order in a reply:** (1) the three-outcome discriminator and its refuter; (2) ask what they would count as a failure and what ground would settle it; (3) only then the links, with the note that the figures, not the summary, are the authority.
