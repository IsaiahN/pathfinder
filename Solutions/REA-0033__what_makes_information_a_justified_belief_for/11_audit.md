Symmetry: ordered, not a magnitude; the ground settles and the frame ranges (Fig 2 :45). Confidence is scalar, warrant is directional, so no threshold on one yields the other. - **X1, confidence as self-certificate.** Anchor moves with the frame, so none nameable. Not a domain yet (Fig 2 :30).
- **Union, not partition.** Justification lives at the intersection of a held term with a live residual, an anchor settlement, a refusable vocabulary and a constitutive lineage. A confidence lever clears only the magnitude in Domain 1 and leaves the intersection standing (Fig 9 :54-57).
- **Order by dependency.** Perception, vocabulary and objective, route to ground, settlement, lineage. Refusal before mint; describe the residual before choosing a frame (Fig 3 :20; Fig 9 :27).

Conversely, one thing survives cleanly: the exclusion of a self-produced score as a verdict, and the requirement of a non-updating reference. That is the residue worth sending. R_T was **not** measured numerically, because no ground reading exists; it is recorded qualitatively as the enumerated set of dropped constraints.
