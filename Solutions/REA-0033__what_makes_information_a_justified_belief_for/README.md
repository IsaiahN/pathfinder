## The residual restated as an effect

A machine emits a claim with a high confidence score. We must decide what further property turns "the model scores this highly" into "this output is warranted by something the model does not itself produce." The candidate options lead to different places. If the answer is a magnitude on confidence, then any sufficiently high score certifies itself, and a frame scores itself with a quantity it also produces (Fig 1 :27). If the answer is a relation to something outside the frame, then the output must be settled by an anchor that does not move when the frame changes its mind (Fig 2 :29), carried in a form that can be refused (Operators table :58), and accompanied by a lineage that is part of the belief rather than decoration on it (Fig 13 :130). What an answer would look like, bound as an ordinary term before the run: a four-part shape, namely anchor, settlement, refusability, lineage, with confidence demoted from verdict to budget.

## What is deliberately not here

- No personal emails of named individuals.
- No ranking of people by how likely they are to agree with the claim.
- No bulk-send plan.

Reason, in the figures' own words: "High correlation is disqualifying for a verdict and efficient for a convention" (Seat Map :163) and "A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it." (Fig 10 :42). Selecting recipients for receptiveness manufactures the correlation that would make their agreement worthless, and handing them the framework before asking for a verdict spends the only thing they contribute.
