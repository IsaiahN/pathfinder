**Residual in effect terms.** A child in a particular community comes to share, reciprocate, distribute fairly and feel obligations in ways that match that community and often differ from other communities. The residual R is the gap between what a generic learning capacity predicts the child will do and what the child actually does in that local setting. An answer would have to name four things: the unit of learning (child alone or child-in-habitat), the currency that crosses from community to child (method or recording), the structure of the four norms (one thing or a union of differently bonded things), and the ground that settles whether learning occurred. Binding that description as an ordinary term is the Formula "Direction" step; it makes the sought-for shape refusable rather than a preference. See `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` section 0.
