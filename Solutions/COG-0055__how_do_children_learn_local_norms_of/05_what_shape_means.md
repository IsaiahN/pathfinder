## 1. A shape is a constraint, not a filler

Local figures README (reopened): the framework returns "what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)". Fig 9 :32: "you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself". Fig 13's lineage section: a system with a closure "can answer that before it searches, and what it returns is a shape rather than a name."

From *The Library of Babel* (inherited): "You can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."

For this question a **shape** is the form of the learning route we can state before we can name the mechanism. "The capacity is child-in-habitat; the transfer is a head start, not a recording; the four norms are a union of differently bonded relations" is a shape. "The mechanism is natural pedagogy" or "the mechanism is reinforcement learning" is a name.

## 2. How the shape is obtained here

- Use the vocabulary closest to the residual and recurse (*Library of Babel*, inherited): "With each new term gained, you narrow the space and deepen your vocabulary." Here the close terms are habitat, membrane, import and bond, taken from the figures.
- Fig 6 :30: "The description travels. Fails by being sharpened into terms only we can read. Cast it in effect terms." Fig 9 :30-31: "your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect rather than the cause." So describe the residual as "local content from a general capacity," not as "a module that computes fairness."
- *Decoding Tacit Knowledge* (inherited): "When contact isn't possible, the best you can send is a plain description of what you'd expect to see, because anyone can check that against the world." This is the outreach rule in `02` and `04`: send a shape a researcher can check, not a theory they must first inhabit.
- *Library of Babel* on stopping (inherited): the answer is reached when "the finish line will also be the starting place," because you have "composed the answer through the process of locating it." In practice here: we stop at three converged constraints and a written refuter, not at a mechanism name.

## 3. What would license a name

Only two things, both outside the shape itself:

1. **The ground settles it.** Formula step 5: "the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays. A gate is not the ground." The README says the framework "stops at the limit of what the available instruments can distinguish, and records the lineage so the stop is honest." For a question about people, the instrument is developmental data (Fig 2 :36-37).
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs** (Fig 6 :23), with a source gate and a shadow test. If developmental psychology has a settled mechanism term, importing it is legitimate only with its provenance and a demonstration that it explains a gap the shape left open.

*They're Scanning Books* (inherited): "The real import is not a new term in the existing space. It is a new dimension of the space." And: "Declaring the primitives does not make them neutral. It makes the choice visible." The six-term substrate is a visible guess, so any claim that a psychological mechanism is "in the framework" must show where it sits, not assert it.

## 4. Why the specific mechanism is the specific forbidden error here

*The Hard Problem is About Source* (inherited) gives the mechanism. Metamerism: an instrument reads green and "cannot comment on the source"; one green light, or yellow plus blue, or RGB, or CMYK, or a scheme not yet discovered can all give the same reading. "If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information." And: "An instrument that can't distinguish these cases demonstrates its own inadequacy." It was the spectrometer, not an argument, that resolved it.

Here the analogue: observing that a child shares fairly does not tell us which mechanism produced it. Teaching, group identification, reinforcement, third-party enforcement and innate other-regarding preferences can yield the same behaviour. Naming one from the reading is the metamerism error. What is owed is a discriminating instrument (a design that separates the mechanisms), not a name.

*The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap* (inherited): "the output is only as good as the validator. The agent, or the tool, or the frame, or the theory, cannot validate itself."

## 5. Q: does "shape, not a name" apply directly here? ANSWERED

Yes, and more directly than in the QMGR exemplar. There the structure being named (Gradient) was already one of the six primitives, so the exemplar corrected itself: "shape, not a name" governs a *missing* primitive, and naming a held atom is a binding, not the invention of a new one (`06` Q1 in that folder). Here the psychological mechanism is **not** in the figure set at all: the figures contain no term for natural pedagogy, shared intentionality or reinforcement. So the mechanism is a genuinely missing primitive from the figure's point of view, and the limit applies without the QMGR-style correction.

Consequence: `07`, `08` and `09` must leave the mechanism unnamed, record it as `gap`, and point to the ground. No retraction is owed on this point. The related correction that *is* recorded in this folder is in `01` section 7: "human anchor" was narrowed so that no single informant becomes authoritative.

## 6. Two consequences for this question

**Prestige is not action.** From *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap* (inherited): "Prestige must never be allowed to validate an action directly... Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it. The Ground Maintainer must be blind to prestige." A campaign that gathers well-known developmentalists to agree with the shape is the named collapse, not corroboration. The legitimate ask is a budget to try the shape against data.

**My own audit is a simulation, not a ground reading.** Same essay (inherited): "LLMs can quickly simulate an approximate answer/output... However, the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself." LLM output is "simulacra." So `01`, `06`, `07`, `08`, `09` and `11` are simulated derivations checked for internal consistency against the figures. They are not tests of the claim and cannot settle anything they report on.
