# The Tether's answer: how children learn local norms

**Mode: `specified` on the shape, `general` on the structural reading, explicit `gap` on the mechanism. Not `grounded`.** Simulated derivation checked against the figures (`05_what_shape_means.md` section 6), not a ground reading. No measurement was taken.

## 1. The residual it answers

The question is not "what is a norm" but how a general capacity comes to carry locally specific content. R is per partner and per resource, not a global score: Fig 1 :19 "The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated." Fig 1 :22 "Aggregating across slots is how a live signal disappears into an average." The four named norms are a union of overlapping conditions with at least one compound (conventional obligation x ingroup), so they need separate loops, not an average (Fig 9 :54).

## 2. The shape

1. **The unit of learning is the child-in-habitat.** Fig 11 :39 "capability is a property of agent-and-habitat, never of the agent alone." Fig 11 :40 "An improvement that does not change contact changes nothing, however much it improves." This is what "local" means structurally: change the contact field and the content changes, even with identical machinery.
2. **What crosses the boundary is a head start, not a recording.** Fig 4 :22 "Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down." Fig 4 :11 "a head start, never a recording" and Fig 4 :34-35 "A seed carries no small tree: it carries what will grow one, and the conditions it lands in decide what it becomes." The child re-derives the norm under local conditions. That is why the result is local rather than a universal rule pasted in.
3. **The four norms are differently bonded molecules over one enumerated atom set.** Fig 12 :117 "Same atoms, different bond, different substance." Sharing and fairness are threshold comparisons with different operand scope; reciprocity is a cycle realised by a returning sequence (Fig 12 :121 "cycle. The output re-enters as input."); obligation is a status designation in a context composed with sequence and subtraction. See `06_isomers_and_order.md`. This is `specified`, with one operand-type strain named there.
4. **The objective link is the one to instrument.** Fig 3 :24 "Why the middle link is where chains usually break." Before asking how much a child shares, ask what the child counts as doing it right. The ground record says three year olds can already say it, with normative protest (W2).
5. **The mechanism is not named.** Fig 9 :32 "you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself." Natural pedagogy, shared intentionality, reinforcement and theory of mind are candidate names from the developmental frame, not outputs of the figures. Each would need a provenanced import and a shadow test, or a ground reading. Until then: `gap`.

## 3. What it explicitly does not say

- Not that the four norms are one module, one score or one bond. A pooled "norm acquisition" factor is the averaging error of Fig 1 :22.
- Not that children copy rules verbatim, or that adults simply install norms. What crosses is a method and the child re-derives it.
- Not that any named psychological mechanism is the answer. That is a `gap`.
- Not that moral norms and conventional norms have the same scope. The ground record separates them (W3, W4), and the figures require unbundling.
- Not `grounded`. No measurement here settles `left(R,phi)`.

## 4. What would settle it, and the pre-registered refuter

Written before the conclusion, per the design-instrument rule. The shape claim is refuted if:

1. norm acquisition is shown to be child-only, independent of contact (breaks Fig 11 :39-40);
2. a norm is shown to cross as a recording that fully fixes behaviour with no re-derivation and no local sensitivity (breaks Fig 4 :11, :22, :34-35);
   2b. the round trip from local norm to compressed rule and back has zero gap `R_T`, that is, no loss of local sensitivity (Fig 4 :30 "So take the round trip and measure the gap.");
3. the four norms collapse to one condition with one bond (breaks Fig 9 :54, Fig 12 :117);
4. the question is shown not to be constitutively about people, so behaviour is not the anchor (breaks Fig 2 :36-37);
5. a genuine figure derivation names the mechanism, which would close the `gap` in the framework's own terms.

The ground that could settle it is developmental data with discriminating designs: cross-community or cross-contact comparisons with machinery held constant; transfer tests that separate a verbatim rule from a re-derived method; ingroup versus outgroup and moral versus conventional conditions within the same child; and instruments that separate teaching, identification and reinforcement as sources of the same observed sharing.

## 5. The honest stop

**Reached a shape, did not reach a mechanism.** The figures narrow what any answer must look like: child-in-habitat, method not recording, unbonded union rather than one thing, per-slot residual, human behaviour as anchor. They do not name the mechanism, and naming one would exceed what the figures return. Fig 2 :40 "agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool" applies to this folder itself: the second seat in `11_audit.md` draws on the same evidence pool, so its agreement is internal consistency and not corroboration. The stop is recorded with its lineage so it stays checkable.

## 6. One-paragraph version for outreach

> The Tether does not supply a psychology of norm learning and does not claim the final answer. It says that the capacity to learn a norm lives in the child and the child's contact field together, so the content is local by construction; that what passes from community to child is a head start the child re-derives under local conditions, not a copied rule; that sharing, reciprocity, fairness and obligation are a union of differently bonded relations over one small atom set rather than one module, with the ingroup convention as its own compound; and that the thing to instrument first is what the child counts as doing it right. It refuses to name the underlying mechanism, because the framework returns a shape, not a name. What it offers is that shape, the four pre-registered refuters, and the kind of experiment that would close it. The mechanism stays open until the ground settles it.
