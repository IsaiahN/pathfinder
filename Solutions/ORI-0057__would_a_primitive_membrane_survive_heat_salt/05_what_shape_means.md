## 1. A shape is a constraint, not a filler

The canonical rule: the framework reports "what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)" (figures README, local copy). Fig 9 :32 says "you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself".

From *The Library of Babel*: "This means that you can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."

So a shape is the narrowed form of an answer you can state before you can name it. For ORI-0057 the shape is: survival is a relation between a boundary and an enumerated habitat; conditions are unbundled; the union has its own cases; an assay settles each slot; second-order contact can decide the first-order outcome. The shape does **not** name a particular amphiphile set or return a yes or no.

That last point matters. A global "yes, primitive membranes survive" or "no, they do not" would be a filler masquerading as a shape. The world literature itself declines to give one: one mixture retains contents from 0 °C to 100 °C (W1) while pure fatty-acid vesicles dissolve below their critical aggregate concentration and flocculate at moderate NaCl (W1, W3).

## 2. How the shape is obtained

- *Library of Babel*: "With each new term gained, you narrow the space and deepen your vocabulary." The terms here are retention, leakage, critical vesicle concentration, ionic strength, phase behavior and wet-dry cycling, all supplied by the world frame, not minted from the six-term substrate.
- Fig 11 :43 "The habitat is enumerated, never composed." The condition list is read off geology (W4), not composed from the substrate.
- Fig 9 :27 "describe it before you go looking, or any frame you pick will seem to fit." The residual was described in effect terms in `01` §0 before the membrane papers were opened.
- *Decoding Tacit Knowledge*: "When contact isn't possible, the best you can send is a plain description of what you'd expect to see, because anyone can check that against the world." That is why `02_outreach_email.md` asks what the reader would expect to see before showing any source.
- *Library of Babel* on stopping: "you will know that you are done because the finish line will also be the starting place". Here the stop is the assay result, where the description of what would count as survival meets the measurement that settles it.

## 3. What licenses a name, and why this question resists one

Two things license a name, both outside the shape itself:

1. **The ground settles it.** `THE_FORMULA.md` step 5: "the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays." Here a named mixture can be settled for a named condition by a retention assay. The essays add that the instrument, not the argument, resolves a source: *The Hard Problem is About Source* says "If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information" and reports that the spectrometer, not an argument, resolved the color case.
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs**, with a source gate and shadow test (Fig 6, Fig 8 :50). The six substrate terms do not contain "NaCl" or "pH 8.5", so the habitat is an import from geochemistry, recorded as such.

They're Scanning Books sharpens the status of the substrate itself: "A minimum viable set of six terms is a human's careful guess at reality's compression axes; another sensorium's minimum set need not be those six." Declaring the six makes the choice visible, not neutral. That is why this solution never claims the six terms derive lipid chemistry.

## 4. Why the union reading is a shape, not an answer

*Library of Babel*: "Perhaps your problem was a bundle or conflation of several problems masquerading as one? You will need to search all of those problems individually to find their answers and then combine them in a satisfactory way." Fig 9 :54 says the same: "And when the split lands on a union rather than a partition, unbundle before you search again."

Applied here, the union reading narrows without filling. It says: do not report one survival number; report per-composition, per-condition numbers including the intersections. It does **not** say what those numbers are. The numbers are the ground's, and only some have been measured.

## 5. Correction to my own §1 and §3

I first wrote this section as if "shape, not a name" forbade naming any specific amphiphile. That overstates the rule. The canonical phrase governs a **missing** primitive, one not held in the library. A fatty acid or glycerol ester is not a missing primitive of the framework; it is a term imported from the chemistry frame and bound to a held atom or composition. What is forbidden is inventing a seventh substrate term, or claiming that the binding moves the framework's wall (Fig 6: "IMPORT adds an atom" adds none here).

Both readings are kept. The remaining error in naming a source from a reading is the metamerism point in §3: a leakage measurement does not by itself identify which molecular arrangement caused it, and an instrument that cannot discriminate among sources demonstrates its own inadequacy. The fix is a better instrument or a provenance record, not a confident name.

## 6. Prestige is not action

*The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*: "Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it." A well-known review of protocell membranes can fund a lab and a grant; it cannot make a survival claim true. The legitimate ask in `02_outreach_email.md` is for a failure condition, not an endorsement.

## 7. This reading is a simulation, not a ground reading

Same essay: "the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself", and LLM output is simulacra. *How Intelligence Actually Works* adds that fitness is "the test that environment applies" and that provenance is lost in projection. So `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` and this file are simulated derivations checked for internal consistency against the figures. They are not measurements and cannot settle ORI-0057.
