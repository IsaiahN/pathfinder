## 0. The claim under audit, stated as a residual

The question asks for a yes or no: would a primitive membrane survive heat, salt, acidity, or other plausible early-Earth conditions?

Restated in effect terms, with the shape committed as an ordinary term before the search (Formula step "Direction"):

> Given an amphiphilic boundary of **unspecified** composition, placed in contact with an environment whose **temperature, ionic strength and pH are unspecified**, does the boundary persist as a compartment that still retains its contents?

Two things are missing from the question as asked, and both are load-bearing:

1. **The membrane.** No amphiphile set, chain length, headgroup or mixture is named. The context of a term is part of what it is: Fig 13 :76 "A gradient over space and one over time are different terms, because which state it is bonded to is part of what it is." A fatty-acid-only boundary and a fatty-acid/glycerol-ester boundary are not the same object for this question.
2. **The conditions.** "Heat, salt, acidity, or other plausible early-Earth conditions" is an open list, not a variable. It is a habitat to be enumerated, not a parameter to be averaged.

So the residual is not one quantity with an unknown value. It is a **union compound** with at least four overlapped conditions (Fig 9 :55 "A bundled problem is usually a union of overlapping conditions, not a list of separate ones"). What an answer would look like, bound before the run: a per-condition, per-composition verdict, each settled by an assay, with the intersections treated as their own cases. That is the shape. A single global yes/no would be an aggregation across slots, which Fig 1 :19 disallows: "The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated."

**Status of this shape claim:** `specified`, ungrounded. It says what any answer must contain. It does not say yes or no.

## 1. Domain census (before any figure line was chosen)

R by arity, symmetry and scale, then one line per domain with actors, currency and anchor. No nameable anchor means it is not a domain yet.

- **R, the residual.** Arity: four named conditions plus an open "other", so a union, not a scalar. Symmetry: broken. Heat, divalent cations and pH act by different mechanisms and do not commute; a border that survives 100 °C can leak under Mg2+, and one stabilized against salt by a glycerol ester can have different permeability. Scale: first order is integrity of one boundary under one condition; second order is persistence across cycles and across contact cascades. Fig 9 names this a union compound: "a case in two conditions is not a harder case of either" (Fig 9 :57).
- **Domain 1, membrane composition.** Actors: fatty acids, fatty alcohols, glycerol monoesters, chain length, headgroup, bound bases and sugars. Currency: packing, hydrogen bonding, critical vesicle concentration, amphiphile residence time, leakage fraction. Anchor: an assay that returns retention versus release. This domain has no historical maintainer (Fig 11 :56 "what survives is what the ground kept"); the lab worker today maintains an experiment, not the Hadean event.
- **Domain 2, the habitat.** Actors: temperature, ionic strength (NaCl, Mg2+, Ca2+), pH, UV, wet-dry cycling, mineral surfaces. Currency: contact. Anchor: geological and physical measurement, not agreement among researchers. The list is read off the world, not derived from the six terms.
- **Domain 3, verification.** Actors: assays and instruments, retention/leakage measurements, phase behavior, dynamic light scattering. Currency: passes and failures against a declared threshold. Anchor: the measurement itself. Fig 2 :29: "An anchor is whatever settles the question and does not change its answer because of what any frame thinks."
- **Domain 4, the compound intersections.** Actors: heat plus salt, salt plus pH, heat plus pH. Currency: compound failure modes that do not reduce to either single condition. Anchor: a separate assay for each intersection. This domain exists only because the split landed on a union.
- **Partition or union.** Union, not partition. The four conditions overlap; a lever that clears the heat-only region leaves the heat-and-salt intersection standing.
- **Order by dependency (Fig 3).** Enumerate the habitat first (Fig 11 :43), describe the residual per slot in effect terms, unbundle the union (Fig 9 :54), then settle each slot at its ground. A reading taken before the assay is a reading below the break: "A step whose input never arrives cannot be diagnosed, only its predecessor can" (Fig 3 :22).

Strain test applied during the census: the word "membrane" in Figure 4 invited a direct grab. That line governs a scale boundary for methods and head starts, not a lipid bilayer. The strain goes back to census, not into a softer reading. See F7.

## 3. Findings

### F1: Survival is a property of membrane-and-habitat, not of the membrane alone (`general`, triangulated)

Fig 11 :39: "And capability is a property of agent-and-habitat, never of the agent alone."
Fig 11 :40: "An improvement that does not change contact changes nothing, however much it improves."
Fig 13 :76: "A gradient over space and one over time are different terms, because which state it is bonded to is part of what it is."

The figure's "agent" is bound here to the physical boundary, and "habitat" to the chemical conditions. That binding is a `specified` import from the origin-of-life frame onto a framework term, declared as such (Fig 8 :50: "Convergent derivation and adopted import look identical in the contents. Only the record of where each came from separates them"). It is not a claim that the figures know lipid chemistry.

Triangulation: Fig 11 (capability) and Fig 13 (context part of the term) are two figure lines from different figures with low overlap. World corroboration is strong but shares an evidence pool among its three main papers (W1, W2, W3 in `10_source_ledger.csv`), so it counts as one witness at the ground, not three.

**Refuter:** show one amphiphilic composition whose retention is identical across all tested temperature, salt and pH conditions with no measurable interaction. That would break the agent-and-habitat verdict.

### F2: The verdict is settled by a measurement, not by agreement (`general`, triangulated)

Fig 2 :29: "An anchor is whatever settles the question and does not change its answer because of what any frame thinks."
Fig 2 :45: "Frames range against the ground. They do not negotiate with it."
Fig 10 :24: "the ground does not decay, the channel does."

For this question the anchor is a retention or leakage assay with acceptance criteria declared before data are generated. The engineering source W5 states the operating form directly: "Define alert limits for particle size, PDI, zeta potential, encapsulation efficiency, leakage, assay, and impurity trends before data are generated." That is the channel to the ground, and it is the part a verification arrangement must keep reachable. Agreement among specialists about how a membrane "ought" to behave is panel 1 of Fig 2, not the anchor.

**Refuter:** settle a survival claim by expert vote with no assay, and show the vote does not drift when the experts are rotated. The figures predict the vote will drift; if it does not, the ground rule is wrong here.

### F3: The habitat must be enumerated, never composed (`general`, framework single-source, world low-overlap corroboration)

Fig 11 :43: "The habitat is enumerated, never composed."
Fig 11 :44: "List everything in contact with the residual, then what is in contact with those, and outward until the cascade stops mattering."
Fig 11 :48: "You do not invent the list. You read it off the world, and what you cannot perceive or measure yet is the residual."

"Or other plausible early-Earth conditions" is the figure's instruction in the question's own words. Temperature, ionic strength, pH, UV, cycles and mineral surfaces are not composable from the six substrate terms; they are read off geology. The geochemistry source W4 supplies that independent read: "Critical to the origin of life are the ingredients of life, of course, but also the physical and chemical conditions in which prebiotic chemical reactions can take place. These factors place constraints on the types of Hadean environment in which life could have emerged." W4 is from a different field and a different evidence pool from the membrane papers, so this is the one genuinely low-overlap corroboration in the file.

**Refuter:** derive the full set of environmental conditions from the six-term substrate without consulting the world. If that succeeds, "enumerate, never compose" fails.

### F4: The valence of each condition is relative to the residual (`specified`, triangulated)

Fig 11 :49: "An actor's valence is relative to the residual, not a property it carries."
Fig 11 :50: "The same actor is a benefactor to one residual and a malfactor to another"
Fig 11 :51: "No standing list of good actors survives a change of residual."

Heat, salt and acidity carry no fixed sign. In the world literature: pure fatty-acid vesicles are "only stable within a fairly narrow pH range" (W1), divalent cations are "very destabilizing to fatty-acid vesicles" (W1), and vesicles "flocculate in the presence of low concentrations of divalent ions or moderate (~0.3 M) concentrations of NaCl" (W3). Yet the same ionic and thermal axes are tolerable, even useful, in mixtures: one composition "retained internal RNA and DNA oligonucleotides at temperatures ranging from 0 °C to 100 °C" (W1), and a 2:1 acid/glycerol-ester mixture withstands Mg2+ up to 4 mM (W2). The high-temperature excursion that destroys one boundary is the strand-separation and nutrient-uptake phase for another (W1). This is Fig 11 :50 applied at the ground.

This is why the question cannot be answered for "a primitive membrane" in the abstract. There is no standing list of hostile conditions; there is a list of conditions relative to a stated composition and a stated residual.

**Refuter:** exhibit a condition that is destabilizing for every amphiphilic composition at every concentration and time scale. That would restore a fixed valence and break this finding.

### F5: The question is a union and must be unbundled (`general`, single-source for the figure, essay corroboration with declared overlap)

Fig 9 :54: "And when the split lands on a union rather than a partition, unbundle before you search again."
Fig 9 :57: "a case in two conditions is not a harder case of either"
Fig 1 :19: "The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated."

The Library of Babel essay states the same move in different vocabulary: "Perhaps your problem was a bundle or conflation of several problems masquerading as one? You will need to search all of those problems individually to find their answers and then combine them in a satisfactory way." That essay shares an author with the figures, so it is corroboration with high overlap, count one witness.

Practical consequence: four single-condition experiments and their intersections, each with its own acceptance criterion. One combined "harshness score" is Fig 1 :22's error: "Aggregating across slots is how a live signal disappears into an average."

**Refuter:** show that heat, salt and pH act through one mechanism with one threshold for every composition, so a single scalar predicts all outcomes. If that holds, the union reading is wrong and the bundle can be searched as one question.

### F6: Contact cascades make first-order integrity insufficient (`specified`, triangulated)

Fig 11 :52: "And contact cascades." The figure continues: scoping to direct contact is how DDT reached the eagles, first-order enumeration with the residual that mattered three steps out.

World instance: "These bound building blocks stabilized fatty acid membranes against salt-induced flocculation and increased the rate of vesicle formation" (W3). The salt is directly in contact with the membrane, but the thing that decides whether the membrane survives the salt is a second-order actor (bound nucleobases and sugars) that the first-order enumeration would miss. A second instance: high temperature is not only a stressor but enables strand separation and nucleotide uptake inside a surviving vesicle (W1), so the survival question and the replication question are coupled at two steps out.

**Refuter:** show that for every composition tested, the survival outcome under a condition is fully predicted by that condition and the amphiphile set, with no significant contribution from bound cargo, surfaces or prior cycling. Then the cascade finding fails.

### F7: Figure 4 does not govern physical membranes (retraction, `general`)

Fig 4 :21: "The membrane is the boundary between the sizes, and the rule about what may cross it. Not a wall: a filter with a direction."

Figure 4 is titled "The Membrane Between Scales". Its "membrane" is the boundary between one agent and a population: methods cross up, head starts cross down. It is not a lipid bilayer and says nothing about leakage, flocculation or phase transitions. The first census pulled it in because the question uses the word "membrane". Fig 9 :27: "describe it before you go looking, or any frame you pick will seem to fit" names that failure. Adopting Fig 4 here would be an import with no source gate and no shadow test; Fig 8 :50 says the adopted import and the convergent derivation look identical in the contents.

**Resolution:** Fig 4 is cited in this solution only for the round-trip rule in `11_audit.md`, never for membrane stability. The earlier reading is kept visible here as evidence of the correction.

**Refuter of the retraction:** show a figure line in which Figure 4's membrane governs material permeability of a physical boundary under heat, salt or pH. None exists in the verbatim text.

### F8: This is not a gap, but it is not a yes/no either (`general`, triangulated)

Fig 8 :39: "So a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence."
Fig 9 :44: "Only a sealed room can be searched to the end, and no frame is a sealed room. So searching and finding nothing proves nothing."
Fig 13 :134: "Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence"

The ground exists. Mansy and Szostak measured retention from 0 °C to 100 °C (W1), Black and Blosser measured salt-induced flocculation and its inhibition (W3), and Westall constrained the physical setting (W4). So the honest stop is not `unreachable`. It is: the figures return a shape (enumerate, unbundle, per-composition, assay-settled), and the ground has already begun to fill some slots. No single global answer follows, because the question did not specify a composition or a site.

**Refuter:** produce a figure line that returns a yes/no for a boundary under unspecified conditions, or show that the world literature gives one composition-independent survival threshold. Either would break this finding.

## 4. Pre-registered refuter

Written before the conclusion, per Seat Map :287: "a falsifier written before the run is a design instrument, not only an honesty device." These four were committed before the verdict in `07_the_tether_answer.md` was drafted.

1. **Composition-independence fails the shape.** One amphiphilic composition shows identical retention across all tested temperature, ionic and pH conditions, with no interaction term. This breaks F1 and F4.
2. **Composability fails the enumeration rule.** The full habitat of conditions is derivable from the six-term substrate without reading the world. This breaks F3.
3. **Agreement replaces the assay.** A survival claim is settled by a panel vote whose result is stable across independent rotations, with no measurement. This breaks F2.
4. **The union collapses.** Heat, salt and pH share one mechanism and one threshold for every composition, so one scalar predicts all outcomes. This breaks F5 and F6.

If any fires, the shape claim is withdrawn. Until one is run, no part of this derivation is labelled `grounded`.

## 5. Recommendation

1. Bind the sought-for shape as an ordinary term before searching: per-composition, per-condition, assay-settled, intersections separate. That is Formula step "Direction" applied here.
2. Enumerate the habitat from geology and chemistry (W4), then sort into partition versus union. Do not average heat, salt and pH into one severity.
3. Pre-register acceptance criteria for each assay before running it (W5). Otherwise the channel to the ground is a convention that decides an outcome.
4. State the mode on every claim. The figures give a shape; the experiments give per-slot readings; neither gives a global yes/no.
5. Send the refuter and the shape, not the theory (Fig 10 :52 "Bring a characterised residual, never the source."). Offer links only after the stage-1 verdict is recorded.

### Round trip in brief

The shorthand carried into `07`, `08` and `09` is: survival is membrane-and-habitat, conditions enumerated never composed, union unbundled, assay-settled, cascades counted. Bringing that shorthand back down to the concrete question drops three things: which composition is meant, which conditions were actually plausible at a given site, and what counts as retention rather than rupture. `11_audit.md` records the gap as `R_T` without pretending it is zero.

### Updated verdict

The shape survives. A primitive membrane's survival is not a scalar property of a membrane; it is a per-composition, per-condition verdict against an enumerated habitat, with intersections treated as compounds and a declared assay as the anchor. The figure verdict is `general` structural and `specified` in its binding to lipid chemistry. No world source conflicts with it. Strains after re-census: none hidden; the binding of "agent" to a lipid boundary is declared as an import, and Figure 4 is retracted.
