## A correction found while verifying venues

The first pass assumed that *Origins of Life and Evolution of Biospheres* (OLEB) was the current field journal. ISSOL's own journal page (https://issol.org/oleb/) says OLEB published from 1968 to 2023 and was terminated by the publisher, and that a new official journal is under consideration. Springer's journal 11084 (https://link.springer.com/journal/11084) is now titled *Discover Life* and has a broader scope than OLEB. The correction is recorded here and in `10_source_ledger.csv`; the old reading is not silently erased.

## How the literature bears on the shape claim

1. **Composition matters, and mixtures beat pure lipids.** W1 and W2 are two papers from one research school and therefore one witness at the ground, not two. They report the same shape the figures demand: the boundary and its habitat decide together.
2. **Second-order actors decide first-order survival.** W3 is where bound building blocks stabilize a membrane against salt. This is Fig 11 :52 "And contact cascades." at the bench.
3. **The habitat is constrained but not fixed.** W4 gives temperature, pH and ionic strength bounds and says the early-ocean pH is debated. So enumerating the habitat is a reading task, not a lookup of one accepted value.
4. **A stability claim needs a declared readout.** W5 shows that a stability verdict is only meaningful against pre-set acceptance limits and orthogonal readouts. That is the ground channel, not a framework preference.

No world source conflicts with the figure verdict. The conflict rule was not triggered.

## Two-stage offer, restated

Stage 1 sends the residual and the pre-registered refuter and asks for a failure condition. Stage 2 is opt-in and sends the sources. Stage 3, only on request, offers the formula as a method with the instruction that each user state their own ground, mode and refuter. A failure at stage 1 is more useful than an endorsement, and a stage-2 agreement is never pooled as a verdict.

**Hard precondition:** stages 1 to 3 only work if the stage-1 shape survives the four refuters. This file records the links; it does not assert that the question has been settled.
