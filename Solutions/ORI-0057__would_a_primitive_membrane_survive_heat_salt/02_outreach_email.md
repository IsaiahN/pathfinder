# Outreach draft: check the shape before you decide whether the theory is worth reading

**Use:** one-to-one, or as a cover letter attached to a submission through a public venue in `03_public_contact_points.csv`. **Not** for bulk send.

**Hard precondition:** do not send until the four refuters in `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` §4 are written down in the packet you actually send. Stage 1 must fail clearly if it is going to fail.

---

**Subject:** One shape claim about protocell membrane survival, with a failure condition

> Hi [name],
>
> I have one specific claim to put against your own knowledge of protocell membranes. I am not asking you to read the framework first, and I am not asking you to agree with it. If the claim does not survive, there is nothing else to look at.
>
> **The claim (`general` structural, `specified` in its binding, not `grounded`):**
> "Would a primitive membrane survive heat, salt, acidity?" is not one question with one yes/no answer. It is a union of conditions applied to an unstated composition. Survival is a property of the boundary and its habitat together, not of the boundary alone. The habitat has to be enumerated from the world, not derived from a list; the conditions must be unbundled before they are searched; each per-composition, per-condition verdict has to be settled by a declared assay; and second-order contact (bound cargo, mineral surfaces, prior cycling) can decide the first-order outcome.
>
> **What I am not claiming:** that the framework knows lipid chemistry, that it returns a yes or no, that Figure 4's "membrane" is a lipid bilayer, or that any particular fatty-acid mixture is *the* primitive membrane. Figure 4 governs what crosses between scales, not permeability under salt. I retracted that reading after the re-census.
>
> **What would refute the shape, written before this message was sent:**
> 1. One amphiphilic composition with identical retention across all tested temperature, ionic and pH conditions, with no interaction term.
> 2. The full habitat of conditions derived from the six-term substrate without reading the world.
> 3. A survival verdict settled by a stable expert vote with no measurement.
> 4. Heat, salt and pH sharing one mechanism and one threshold for every composition, so a single scalar predicts all outcomes.
>
> If any one holds, I withdraw the claim. A one-line "this fails because..." is more useful to me than agreement. If you would rather not engage, no reply is needed.
>
> **If it survives, and only then:** here are the sources. The figures are the authority, not my summary of them.
> - Mansy and Szostak, "Thermostability of model protocell membranes", PNAS 2008: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC2528865/
> - Black and Blosser, "A Self-Assembled Aggregate Composed of a Fatty Acid Membrane and the Building Blocks of Biological Polymers", Life 2016: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC5041009/
> - Westall et al., "A Hydrothermal-Sedimentary Context for the Origin of Life", Astrobiology 2018: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC5867533/
>
> **If you want the method:** the figures and formula are public at https://github.com/IsaiahN/tether/tree/general-agent/figures . Run it on a problem you know well and can check against a ground. Write down what would refute your result *before* you run it, and state the mode of what you get. The framework's own rule is that a loop cannot check the direction of its own question.
>
> - [name]

## Distribution rules

- Send to one person at a time through a public or opt-in channel. No purchased lists, no bulk send, no automation.
- Record the stage-1 verdict before any theory is shown. Later agreement is never pooled as corroboration (Fig 2 :40 "agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool").
- A venue's public contact form is not confidential. Do not send unreleased claims through it if you need protection.
- An unsolicited email to a published institutional address carries GDPR/CAN-SPAM style duties in many jurisdictions. Prefer the society, seminar, conference and journal routes in `03_public_contact_points.csv`.
