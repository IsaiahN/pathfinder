## The residual in one line

Given an amphiphilic boundary of unknown composition placed in contact with an environment whose temperature, ionic strength and pH are not specified, does the boundary persist as a compartment that retains its contents? The figures do not answer yes or no. They say what any answer must look like: survival is a property of membrane-and-habitat, the conditions must be enumerated rather than composed, the union must be unbundled, and an assay must settle each case. `Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence` (Fig 13 :134) is not needed here: a ground exists and has already begun to speak.

## What is deliberately not here

- No personal emails of named individuals, and no reuse of the personal addresses published on the ISSOL contact page.
- No ranking of people by how likely they are to agree with the shape claim.
- No bulk-send plan.
- No claim that this simulated derivation settles the question. It is checked for internal consistency against the figures only.

Three reasons, in the figures' own words:

1. Fig 2 :35: "Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you." Selecting recipients for receptiveness manufactures the correlation that would make their agreement worthless.
2. Fig 10 :42: "A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it." Sending the theory before the stage-1 verdict spends the reader's independence.
3. Seat Map :163: "High correlation is disqualifying for a verdict and efficient for a convention." Agreement among workers sharing one literature is one evidence pool talking.
