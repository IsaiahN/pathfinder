## VERDICT (from the figures)

The shape survives; a global yes/no does not exist in it. Survival is a property of boundary-and-habitat, so the question must name a composition and a site before it can be answered. Conditions are enumerated from the world, never composed; heat, salt and acidity form a union with its own intersections, not a severity scale and not a sequence. Valence is relative to the residual: the same salt or heat that destroys one fatty-acid boundary is tolerated or exploited by a mixed one, and bound cargo or mineral surfaces can decide the outcome three steps out. The word "survive" is settled by a declared assay with pre-set acceptance limits, never by specialist agreement. Figures 4's membrane is retracted as a physical-membrane claim. This is a `general` structural and `specified` bound result, ungrounded by this derivation. The ground has already begun to answer for specific mixtures, so the honest stop is reached-as-shape, not `unreachable`.

- **cost if wrong:** bounded and locatable. The packet states mode, four refuters and provenance before the claim, so an error is findable rather than invisible. The real cost is spending the independence of outside readers by sending a recording instead of a generator. Fig 2 :42 "Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong." That cost is avoidable by leading with the shape claim and its refuters.
- **undoable:** yes. The binding of "membrane" to the habitat figure and the union reading are removable without touching the figure text. A specific refutation can be copied into `11_audit.md` with provenance and re-evaluated by the auditor seat. No substrate primitive has to be withdrawn.
- **reversed by:** a ground reading, not agreement. One composition with identical retention across all tested conditions, a derivation of the habitat from the six terms, a stable vote replacing an assay, or one mechanism and threshold shared by heat, salt and pH would each break the shape. A named primitive composition and a named Hadean site with a factorial retention dataset would fill the open slots and convert the per-domain verdicts toward `grounded`. Until one of these fires, the verdict stands as `candidate`.
