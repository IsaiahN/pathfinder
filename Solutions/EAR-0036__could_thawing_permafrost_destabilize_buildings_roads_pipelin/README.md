## What this package concludes

The figures cannot settle the empirical question. They contain no rate, no date and no dollar cost for permafrost thaw. What they give is the shape of a checkable answer: a per-object-slot residual comparison between an observed destabilization rate and an expected rate, anchored on the ground, with the "expected" term exposed as a movable frame rather than an anchor. The specific claim "yes, faster than expected" is not in the figures' closure. It is a provenanced import from permafrost science (WATSON, secondary). The strongest current witness is Manos, Witharana and Liljedahl 2025, who document that earlier loss estimates were too low because the infrastructure inventory was incomplete. See `09_watson_answer.md`.

## What is deliberately not here

- No personal emails of named individuals. The IPA contact page publishes role-based institutional mailboxes; we list the public page, not a person.
- No ranking of people by how likely they are to agree with the claim. That manufactures the correlation that makes agreement worthless (Fig 2 :40).
- No bulk-send plan. One-to-one, opt-in venues only.
- No assertion that this simulated derivation settles the question it reports on. LLM output is a simulacrum; the ground settles it.
