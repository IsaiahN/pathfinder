## Independent domain census

I redid the census from the residual line alone, before reading the primary's findings, using the WATSON order: R by arity, symmetry and scale; one line per domain with actors, currency and anchor; partition or union; order by dependency (Fig 3).

- **R (arity, symmetry, scale).** The residual is the gap between an observed destabilization rate and an expected rate. Arity: two quantities and several object slots (asset classes and sites), so a **union compound** rather than one hard question. Symmetry: ordered, because "faster than" is directional and the causal chain is directional. Scale: per site and per asset, years to decades. The minimum test for a live signal is `R⁺_s > 0` at a named slot with a working channel.
- **Domain 1, frozen ground.** Actors: ground ice, active layer, ground temperature, soil, water. Currency: heat transfer, bearing capacity, settlement. Anchor: the ground, accessed by instruments. Question type: the physical substrate and the legitimate anchor, because the question is constitutively about ground state.
- **Domain 2, prediction frame.** Actors: design codes, hazard maps, inventories, scenarios. Currency: expected rates and cost estimates. Anchor: none of its own; it must range against Domain 1. Question type: the movable term.
- **Domain 3, instruments and observation.** Actors: boreholes, InSAR, surveys, local observers. Currency: observations. Anchor: the ground through a channel that can decay. Question type: the gate that decides whether a rate comparison is possible at all.
- **Domain 4, verification.** Actors: expert readers. Currency: residuals and agreement. Anchor: the ground, never agreement. In this simulation its independence is zero.

Order by dependency: describe the residual in effect terms, name the anchor, expose the expected term, check for a live instrument, refuse the wrong assumption, then offer theory (Fig 8 :53, Fig 3 :20-21). My census matches the primary's four domains and its union reading. I derived the union from the arity and symmetry of R and from Fig 9 :55; the primary derived it from the bundled-asset observation and the world per-asset divergence. Different starting points, same boundary. Still one evidence pool.

## Round trip gap R_T

The Symbols table defines R_T as `gap(x, (T_E ∘ T_A)(x))`, the gap between what was sent up and what came back down, measuring what the coarser description cannot hold. Fig 4 :30 requires it to be measured rather than assumed: "So take the round trip and measure the gap. Without it you are navigating by dead reckoning: confident about a position nobody checked." I ran the round trip on the condensation the folder actually uses.

1. **Sent up (x):** the verbatim figure set, chiefly Fig 1 :19 and :22 (per-slot residual), Fig 2 :29 and :36 (ground anchor), Fig 5 :59 (persistent residual), Fig 9 :55 (union compound) and Fig 11 :44 (cascade enumeration).
2. **Abstraction (T_A):** the shorthand in `07_the_tether_answer.md`: ask per slot, anchor on the ground, expose the expected frame, refuse on persistence, enumerate the cascade, stop at the shape.
3. **Concretisation (T_E):** the concrete world claim that damage losses were roughly doubled by a corrected inventory (Manos et al. 2025) and that up to 80 percent of buildings in some Russian cities report damage (Hjort et al. 2022).

**R_T is nonzero.** Three things the shorthand holds do not survive the trip back down:

- the **slot boundary**. "Per slot" is a rule, not a taxonomy. Which slots are live (building foundations, paved road segments, buried pipelines) and at what spatial resolution is decided by the inventory, not by the figure.
- the **instrument threshold**. A frame is movable, but moving it requires a specific instrument with a stated resolution and error. The shorthand does not carry the 0.5 m imagery versus OpenStreetMap distinction, which is the entire mechanism of the 2025 correction.
- the **share-pool penalty**. The shorthand says "world evidence is secondary"; the concrete case needs the stronger claim that four papers share inventories, hazard functions and authors, and that only one instrument difference constitutes a second witness.

Conversely, one thing survives cleanly: a positive, persistent, per-slot residual against a movable expected frame is the shape of the answer, and the rate is not in the figures. That is the residue worth sending. R_T was **not** measured numerically, because no ground reading exists in this derivation; it is recorded qualitatively as the enumerated set of dropped constraints. Treating "the formula is the condensed version of the metalaws" as identity would set R_T to zero and would be the dead-reckoning error Fig 4 :30 names.
