## QUESTION

Decide, in effect terms, what the Tether says about the claim that thawing permafrost could destabilize buildings, roads, pipelines and other northern infrastructure faster than expected. Option A: treat the question as a single empirical yes-or-no and answer it from published percentages. Option B: treat it as a per-slot residual comparison whose shape is figure-derived and whose rate is a provenanced world import, then state what would settle it. What each leads to: A presents a shared-pool aggregate as a verdict and hides which slot is live; B yields a checkable shape, written refuters, an explicit anchor and an honest gap until a ground reading arrives.

## DOMAIN CENSUS

R by arity, symmetry and scale, then one line per domain with actors, currency and anchor; partition or union; order by dependency (Fig 3).

- **R (the residual).** The gap between an observed destabilization rate at an asset slot and the expected rate at that slot. Arity: two quantities (observed, expected) and multiple slots (buildings, roads, pipelines, railways, airports). Symmetry: not symmetric, the comparison is ordered and the causal chain is ordered. Scale: per site and per asset over years to decades. Fig 9 :55 makes it a union compound, not one hard question: a case in two conditions is not a harder case of either.
- **Domain 1, frozen ground.** Actors: ground ice, active layer, mean annual ground temperature, soil particles, water. Currency: heat and mass transfer, bearing capacity, settlement. Anchor: the ground itself, read through boreholes, InSAR and settlement surveys. Question type: union member, the physical substrate.
- **Domain 2, prediction frame (the expected rate).** Actors: design codes, hazard maps, infrastructure inventories, climate scenarios. Currency: predictions, hazard ratings, cost estimates. Anchor: must range against Domain 1; not an anchor itself, because it moves when the inventory is corrected. Question type: the movable term.
- **Domain 3, observation and instruments.** Actors: GTN-P boreholes, satellite interferometry, field surveys, local observers. Currency: observations. Anchor: the ground, reached through an instrument already returning something. Question type: the channel, which can decay even when the ground does not (Fig 10 :24).
- **Domain 4, verification and outreach.** Actors: expert readers and reviewers. Currency: residuals and agreement. Anchor: the ground, never agreement. In this simulation all four domains draw on one evidence pool, so their independence is zero.
- **Order by dependency.** Enumerate slots, name the anchor, identify the expected term, check for a live instrument, then refuse the wrong assumption (Fig 8 :53, Fig 3 :20-21). Theory is offered only after the stage-1 verdict.

Strain test applied: the six-term substrate of Fig 13 was tried and retracted as a strain, since it governs primitive composition and carries no empirical rate. The ⋛ operator is retained with an operand-type caveat (two rates rather than the usual structures). Both are recorded in `01` F9, not smoothed over.

## VERDICT (from the figures)

The shape survives; the rate does not come from the figures. Per asset slot and site, "faster than expected" is an ordered comparison whose observed term must be read at the ground and whose expected term is a frame that moves when the inventory is corrected. A persistent residual points at the expected model. The question is a union compound, the harm sits several steps down a contact cascade, and the instrument gate is asymmetric. The figures supply the shape and three refuters, but no rate, date or cost. Those are world imports, provenanced in `10_source_ledger.csv`, and the strongest current one (Manos et al. 2025) reports that earlier estimates were too low. This is a `general` and `specified` result, not a `grounded` one. No figure line was found governing the specific rate, so that part is a `gap` reported plainly.

- **cost if wrong:** bounded and locatable. The packet states its mode, refuters and provenance before its conclusion, so an error is findable rather than invisible. The real avoidable cost is presenting a shared-pool aggregate as a verdict, which Fig 2 :42 prices: "Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong." That cost falls on a reader who accepts the headline instead of checking a slot.
- **undoable:** yes. The world estimates are replaceable without touching the shape. A corrected inventory, a new borehole time series or a different hazard product can be substituted; no primitive has to be withdrawn. The outreach packet can be superseded by a corrected one.
- **reversed by:** a ground reading, not agreement. A settlement or tilt time series at a named site exceeding the design-expected rate over a declared interval would move the empirical claim toward `grounded` at that slot; a measurement showing no excess across all slots with a live instrument would fire refuter 2; an expected term that does not move under corrected inventory would fire refuter 3; a figure line asserting a direct rate would convert the gap into figure-derived content. Until one of these fires, the verdict stands as `candidate`.
