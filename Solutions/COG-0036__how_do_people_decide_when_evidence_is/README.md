## Residual, stated as an effect

A decision maker tracks an object slot, acts, observes, and carries a per-slot residual `R_s = |Gamma(b,a) - o'|`. The decision is a stop/go commit. Acting now pays whatever residual remains if the act is wrong. Waiting pays an action cost for another observation in the hope that the residual shrinks. The failure the question is trying to avoid is spending the whole budget searching inside a closed reach, or averaging a live slot away. An answer would be a rule that says when the residual is enough, and what changes when delay is costly.

## What is deliberately not here

- No personal emails of named individuals.
- No ranking of people by how likely they are to agree.
- No bulk-send plan.

Three figure-grounded reasons:

1. Fig 2 :35 "Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you."
2. Fig 2 :40 "agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool"
