## Two things in the wider literature that bear directly on the claim

1. **Threshold rules are the normal form, not a defect.** Wald's SPRT stops when a likelihood ratio crosses one of two boundaries; the secretary problem's optimal policy is a cutoff; Simon's satisficing stops at an aspiration level. All three are independent of the framework and all three contradict the reading of "sufficient" as a fixed certainty bar. They do **not** corroborate the specific two-currency claim; they corroborate the shape that sufficiency is a comparison.
2. **The cost of delay enters the threshold, not just the budget.** Drugowitsch et al. show human observers tune the speed-accuracy setting toward reward rate; Constantino and Daw show foraging thresholds move with the opportunity cost of time; Payne, Bettman and Johnson found that under time constraints some heuristics were more accurate than a truncated normative procedure. These are low-overlap witnesses. They agree with the direction of the shape claim, but Fig 2 :40 applies: if they draw on a shared evidence pool their agreement is one pool talking. Here the fields differ (mathematics, decision neuroscience, behavioural ecology, JDM), so the overlap is declared as low, not zero.

## Distribution: offer, not push

Figure-grounded reasons:

- Fig 10 :52 "Bring a characterised residual, never the source." Send the shape claim and the five refuters first. Sending the full framework before anyone has described what they would count as a failure sends a recording, not a checkable claim.
- Fig 10 :42 "A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it." A recipient who has absorbed the framework before giving a stage-1 verdict has joined the frame being checked.
- Fig 9 :27-28 "describe it before you go looking, or any frame you pick will seem to fit." Ask what they would expect to see if the shape were true before giving them the links.
- Fig 2 :40 "agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool"
- Fig 4 :22 "Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down." Stage 3 sends a method, which is permitted, not an answer.

**Recommended order in a reply:** (1) shape claim and refuter; (2) ask what they would count as a failure and record it; (3) only then the three links, with the note that the figures, not the summary, are the authority; (4) if asked, the formula as a method with a user-written refuter and a stated mode.

## The two-stage offer

1. **Stage 1 before stage 2** is Fig 10 :52 and Fig 2 :45 "Frames range against the ground. They do not negotiate with it."
2. **Stage 2 is opt-in by construction.** The stage-1 verdict is recorded before any theory is shown. Later agreement is never pooled as corroboration.
3. **Stage 3 sends a method**, and each user must state a ground and write a refuter before running it, because the framework's "Direction" section says a frame cannot check the direction of its own question.
4. **Hard precondition:** stages 1 to 3 only work if the stage-1 output survives. If a refuter fires, stop and retract rather than send the links.

## Practical limits

Nothing in this folder is sent by this agent. Hosting the packet and contacting anyone is the user's action. Prefer an opt-in download page to a pushed attachment, so a recipient can decline contact. No personal emails, no bulk send, no list of people ranked by likely agreement.
