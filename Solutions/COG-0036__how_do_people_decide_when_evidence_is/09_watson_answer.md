## QUESTION

Decide, in effect terms, what the Tether says about sufficiency to act when waiting for certainty is costly. Option A: treat "enough evidence" as a fixed certainty bar that observation fills up. Option B: treat it as an ordered comparison between the residual that would remain if one acted now and the cost of one more observation, with the residual tracked per live case and the two costs held apart. What each leads to: A spends budget searching a swept region and hides the live case in an average; B gives a checkable shape with written refuters and an open slot until the ground settles it.

## DOMAIN CENSUS

R by arity, symmetry and scale, then one line per domain with actors / currency / anchor, partition or union, and order by dependency.

- **R (the residual).** A decision maker tracks a live case, acts, observes, and carries `R_s = |Gamma(b,a) - o'|`. Arity: two conditions, evidence sufficiency and cost of delay, so a union compound: "a case in two conditions is not a harder case of either." Symmetry: the stop relation is ordered, not a symmetric join. The operators table :49 calls comparison "a threshold relation between two quantities". Scale: first order inside one episode, second order when the stop rule itself is learned from paid outcomes.
- **Domain 1, the single decision episode.** Actors: decision maker, object slot `s`, observation stream `o'`, candidate action. Currency: residual mass `R⁺_s` and description bits. Anchor: the outcome of the act. Type: partition, which slot is live. Admitted, anchor nameable.
- **Domain 2, the stopping rule.** Actors: the two compared quantities and the `⋛` operator. Currency: description bits and settling actions, two currencies that do not add. Anchor: the ground payoff, for example reward rate or a correct outcome. Type: union compound with Domain 1, ordered comparison inside it. Admitted, anchor nameable.
- **Domain 3, the verification and research frame.** Actors: participants, experimenters, journals, conferences. Currency: agreement and residuals. Anchor: behavioural data, not agreement. Type: checking frame over Domains 1 and 2. Admitted, anchor nameable. In this simulated run all three domains draw on one evidence pool, so Domain 3 independence is zero.
- **Order by dependency.** Perceive and track the residual per slot, describe it in effect terms, decide stop or wait, let the ground settle the act, repeat with a changed reach. SEARCHED before INWARD before OUTWARD (Formula step 7). Describe the residual before naming a frame (Fig 9 :27-28).
- **Strain test applied.** Fig 12 two-currency rule versus world reward-rate models: re-censused as a level difference (composition price inside a frame versus ecological comparison within a frame). Fig 5 bargain versus Fig 12 comparison: re-censused as mint versus act. No verdict flip from either.

## VERDICT (from the figures)

The shape survives; the descriptive mechanism and the number do not. Sufficiency to act is an ordered comparison between the residual that would remain if one acted now and the cost of one more observation. The residual is per live case, never a global average. Description cost and settling cost are in different units and must be held apart. Waiting pays only while independent search can still find something or a different closure can be imported; once the residual persists and stops trending down, the rule is act, refuse, or change frame. The act is a comparison, the mint is a bargain, and the rightness of the act is settled by its paid outcome, not by agreement. This is a `specified`, ungrounded candidate. The strongest point for outreach is that the framework offers a shape with written refuters and an honest stop, not a final decision theory.

- **cost if wrong:** bounded and locatable. The packet states mode, five refuters and the lineage before the conclusion, so an error is findable rather than invisible. The real cost is spending the independence of outside readers by sending a recording instead of a checkable claim, which Fig 2 :42 prices directly: "Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong." That cost is avoidable by leading with the shape claim and its refuter.
- **undoable:** yes. The gap domains are empty slots, not commitments. No number, mechanism or primitive has to be withdrawn for the shape to stand. A sent claim can be superseded by a corrected packet if a refuter fires.
- **reversed by:** a ground reading, not agreement. A discriminating experiment that moves opportunity cost of time and measures the stop threshold would test F3; one that separates description cost from settling cost would test F2; one that contrasts global-confidence with per-case instructions would test F4; a demonstration that the stop decision and the mint decision are one scalar rule across tasks would break F1. Until one of these fires, the verdict stands as `candidate`.
