## 1. A shape is a constraint, not a filler

The canonical README states the limit: the framework returns "what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)". Fig 9 :32 "you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself". Fig 13 :134 "Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence". Fig 13 :135 "It cannot say which primitive is missing, because naming it would mean already holding it."

From *The Library of Babel*: "you can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."

A **shape** is the narrowed form of an answer you can state before you can name its parameters. It constrains; it does not fill. For this question:

- "sufficiency is a comparison of two quantities, not a certainty bar" is a shape;
- "stop when the likelihood ratio crosses 19" is a name;
- "the residual is per case, never aggregated" is a shape;
- "use a 0.05 boundary at 40 trials" is a name.

The shape survives a change of domain. The name belongs to a ground reading in that domain.

## 2. How a shape is obtained

- *Library of Babel*: "If you don't know the right terms to search for, you will have to use the existing vocabulary you do know that is closest to the words that you wish to describe and grasp the shape of." and "With each new term gained, you are able to narrow the space by definition and deepen your vocabulary."
- Fig 6 :30 "The description travels. Fails by being sharpened into terms only we can read. Cast it in effect terms." Fig 9 :30-31 "your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect rather than the cause."
- *Decoding Tacit Knowledge*: "When contact isn't possible, the best you can send is a plain description of what you'd expect to see, because anyone can check that against the world."
- *Library of Babel* on stopping: "You will know that you are done because the finish line will also be the starting place, as there is nowhere else to search and nothing else that can describe the answer better." The paradox is that by then "you have already composed the answer through the process of locating it."
- *How Intelligence Actually Works*: "The test that environment applies is what we call fitness." The shape is steered by contact, not by preference.

For this question the effect description is deliberately behavioural: a live case, a remaining residual, a cost of one more observation, a paid outcome. Those are words a JDM researcher can check without accepting any framework vocabulary.

## 3. What licenses a name

Only two things, both outside the shape itself:

1. **The ground settles it.** Formula step 5: "the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays. A gate is not the ground." The canonical README: the framework "stops at the limit of what the available instruments can distinguish, and records the lineage so the stop is honest."
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs**, with a source gate and a shadow test (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8 :50-52).

*They're Scanning Books When They Should* sharpens this: "A minimum viable set of six terms is a human's careful guess at reality's compression axes; another sensorium's minimum set need not be those six." And: "Declaring the primitives does not make them neutral. It makes the choice visible."

So a numeric stop rule is not licensed by the figures. It is licensed by an experiment, or by an import with its provenance and shadow test recorded. Naming one from the shape alone turns a visible choice into an invisible one.

## 4. Why the "just another decision theory" charge is the named error

*The Hard Problem is About Source* gives the mechanism. Metamerism: an instrument reads green and "it cannot comment on the source". The reading does not determine the source. "If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information." What resolved the colour case was "the arrival of a better instrument", not an argument.

Applied here: a satisficing model, a drift-diffusion model, a sequential likelihood-ratio test and a marginal-value rule can all produce the same observed pattern of stopping. Observing that pattern does not identify which internal rule ran. The framework would call this a projection with lossy information and would owe a discriminating instrument, not a name. The same essay adds "The answer is unreached until a better instrument exists."

*Arrival of the Fittest* records the refused near-misses in "Three mappings offered and declined". Bayesian updating was declined because "there is no prior, no likelihood and no posterior anywhere in the mechanism." This is the precedent for not claiming that the Tether is a Bayesian stop model just because evidence accumulates in both. The resemblance is real and the definitions do not align.

## 5. Q: does the framework name the stop rule? ANSWERED, no further question needed

**What licenses a particular threshold or boundary? Nothing in the framework.** The framework returns a shape. A particular numeric rule is licensed only by a ground reading (an experiment that pays for it), or by a provenanced import with a shadow test. Until one of those, the number is the searcher's own contribution and must be recorded as such.

**Does this folder commit the forbidden error?** It does not. `01`, `06`, `07`, `08` and `09` all stop at the comparison, the two currencies, the per-slot constraint and the flat-residual stop signal. No drift rate, boundary height, aspiration level or likelihood cutoff is asserted as the framework's answer. The world sources are cited as corroboration of the shape, with overlap declared, never as a derivation of a number.

## 5b. Correction to my own section 3 (see `06_isomers_and_order.md` Q1)

I first wrote the "shape, not a name" rule as if it forbade any numeric or procedural claim at all. That overstates it. The canonical phrase is "what a **missing** thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)". The subject is a primitive not held in the library. The comparison operator `⋛`, the residual `R`, and the slot `s` are **already held** in the framework's symbols and operators tables. Saying "a stop decision is a comparison" is a binding onto held vocabulary, not the invention of a new primitive.

What remains forbidden is claiming that the framework returns a particular threshold, a drift rate, or a cognitive mechanism. That is a name where the framework gives a shape, and where several different mechanisms produce the same behaviour. Both readings are now recorded: the narrow, correct one and the broader one I initially over-applied, so neither is quietly lost.

## 6. Two consequences for outreach

**Prestige is not action.** From *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*: "Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it. The Ground Maintainer must be blind to prestige." A campaign gathering respected names to agree with the shape claim is the named collapse, not corroboration. The legitimate ask gives the claim a budget to be tried and lets the ground, an experiment or a paid outcome, settle it.

**From *How a Society of Agents Would Work*:** "the group's agreement is never the verdict." The essay adds that a group is only worth having while its members stay different, and that independence is "how little they draw on the same evidence". A list of recipients chosen for receptiveness manufactures the correlation that makes agreement worthless.

## 7. My own audit is a simulation, not a ground reading

Same essay as section 6: "LLMs can quickly simulate an approximate answer/output with the formula found in the architecture post" and "the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself." *Arrival of the Fittest* adds that outputs "could never be verified if the solutions presented were not contrived to fit the formula." So `01` is a simulated derivation, checked for internal consistency against the figures. It is **not** a test of the claim and must be labelled as such. It cannot settle anything it reports on.

## 8. Honest stop

*The Library of Babel* says the researcher who succeeds "will leave as the author". Here the shape was composed from the figures and the essays, and the number was not composed at all, because no ground paid for it. The stop is at the limit of what the instruments distinguish. *How Intelligence Actually Works*: "indiscernibility is not identity." The record of the route is what keeps that stop checkable.
