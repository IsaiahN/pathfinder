## What the wider literature bears on the separator

1. **Reasons are not sources.** Haidt 2001 gives the mechanism: the verbal justification is constructed after the judgment. This is why the separator must read the behaviour of the disagreement (does R shrink against an anchor, does each rule work on a subset) instead of asking people why they disagree. It corroborates Fig 9 :30-31 (*"describe the effect rather than the cause"*), not the figure verdict itself.
2. **The domains are real but overlapping.** The SEP entry documents a criterion-judgment distinction between moral and conventional judgments (authority independence and generalizability), while Shweder and Haidt show the boundary varies with culture and SES. That is exactly the two-anchored-domains-plus-overlap result: moral judgments are treated as non-updating and generalizable (D2), conventional judgments as local and authority-dependent (a social fact, D1), and the boundary itself moves with the frame's closure.
3. **Is and ought are different quantities.** Greene 2003 blocks the move from a neural or survey reading to an "ought." It corroborates Fig 2 :37-38: an instrument can be wrong on axes it does not measure. No world source asserts that the framework's figures are true.
4. **Values can shape the facts.** Kahan 2012 and 2013 show that cultural commitments predict factual beliefs about consequential risks, and that more reflective reasoners can be better at defending group-congenial beliefs. This is world evidence for the union verdict (F1) and for the collapse condition (F4), not a conflict with the figures. No conflict rule was triggered.
5. **The value terms are shared, not arbitrary.** de Waal 2011 finds reciprocity and empathy in other mammals. This corroborates the re-census in F7: naming a value is a binding onto held atoms, not the invention of a seventh primitive. It is the genuinely low-overlap witness in the set.

**Overlap declaration.** Haidt 2001, Haidt 1993 and the SEP entry draw on one moral-psychology pool; they are one witness for the "reasons are post hoc / domains vary" shape. Kahan 2012 and Kahan 2013 are one author and one research programme, counted as one witness. de Waal (animal behaviour) and Greene (neuroethics / philosophy of science) are lower-overlap fields. The four framework essays share the figures' author and pool completely. Agreement from a shared pool is one witness, not two (Fig 2 :40).

## Distribution: offer, do not push

Figure-grounded reasons:

- **Fig 10 :52** - *"Bring a characterised residual, never the source."* Sending the full framework before anyone has described a disagreement sends the source, not the residual. Lead with the separator and its refuter.
- **Fig 9 :27-28** - *"describe it before you go looking, or any frame you pick will seem to fit."* Ask what the recipient would expect to see if each attribution were true before offering the figure text.
- **Fig 2 :35** - *"Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you."* Do not ask for agreement.
- **Seat Map :163** - *"High correlation is disqualifying for a verdict and efficient for a convention."* Selecting recipients for receptiveness manufactures the correlation that makes their agreement worthless.
- **Fig 4 :22** - *"Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down."* Stage 3 sends a method, which is permitted; it does not send an answer.

**Recommended order in a reply:** (1) the separator and the refuter; (2) ask what they would count as a failure and record it; (3) only then the four links, with the note that the figures, not the summary, are the authority. Later agreement is never pooled as corroboration.

## One practical constraint

The SPSP submission page requires data to be collected before podium submission and states that poster and podium presentations are for presenting accepted scientific research. A purely simulated derivation must not be represented as an empirical finding. The JPSP guidelines require transparency, data/code availability statements and preregistration at Level 2 for empirical work. Journal of Moral Education desk-rejects work with no educational or developmental component. These are practical constraints on where this packet can go, not evidence about the question. The packet should be offered as a method and a separator to be tested, never as a result.
