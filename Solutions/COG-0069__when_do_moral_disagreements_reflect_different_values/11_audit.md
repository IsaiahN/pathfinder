## Independent domain census (from the residual alone)

I used the WATSON order: R by arity, symmetry and scale; one line per domain with actors / currency / anchor; partition or union; order by dependency.

- **R (arity / symmetry / scale).** Three frames of reference are implicated: two judges and one act, with a three-way source attribution. Arity three or more, so a three-way answer can only be a partition if the conditions are disjoint. The disagreement relation is symmetric, while the source relation is ordered; facts constrain goals and not conversely. Scale spans the single judge, the dyad and the culture, with a lossy transform between (Fig 4).
- **Domain 1, record-ranged condition.** Actors: the judges' terms about what happened and its consequences. Currency: residual reduction; a mis-attached term is re-fit. Anchor: a record or measurement that does not move when either argues (Fig 2 :29). Admitted.
- **Domain 2, goal-ranged condition.** Actors: comparison terms, thresholds, rights, harms. Currency: whether a different comparison is owed. Anchor: human judgment, because the question is constitutively about people (Fig 2 :37). Admitted.
- **Domain 3, "interests."** Actors: weighting w, reference group, evidence pool, stake. Currency: agreement and independence. Anchor: none independent of the frames; a "fact" that tracks a group's stance is another frame. **Not admitted.** It splits into a record question (who benefits) absorbed into D1 and a goal question (whose end prevails) absorbed into D2, with the residue a collapse of independence.
- **Union, not partition.** The conditions overlap in the world: cultural commitments shape factual beliefs, and affective reactions predict judgments better than harm appraisals. One lever leaves the intersections standing (Fig 9 :55-57).
- **Dependency order.** Describe R per slot; find an anchor; test shrinkage; test subsets and split; test independence; plan last (Fig 3 :20, Fig 9 :54).

My census matches the primary's two admitted domains and its refusal to admit the third. Where the primary derived that refusal from the anchor definition (Fig 2 :29) and the valence rule (Fig 11 :49), I derived it from the arity of R plus the "no nameable anchor means not a domain" census rule. Different starting points, same domain boundary. Low overlap between routes within the framework, but still one evidence pool. The agreement is internal consistency, not corroboration.

## Round trip gap R_T

The Symbols table defines R_T as gap(x, (T_E ∘ T_A)(x)), what the coarser description cannot hold, and Fig 4 :30 requires it to be measured rather than assumed.

2. **Abstraction (T_A):** the folder's shorthand: the three answers are a union of two anchored conditions plus a collapse; facts shrink against a record, values split on the objective, interests are a verification failure; the fraction is a gap.
3. **Concretisation (T_E):** a concrete case, for example a dispute about whether a policy is harmful where one side cites outcome data and the other cites a duty of loyalty.

**R_T is nonzero.** Four things the shorthand holds do not survive the trip back down:

- the **per-slot indexing** of R (Fig 1 :19), which a shorthand "this is a values dispute" drops;
- the **requirement that the anchor not update** (Fig 2 :30, :33), which a casual "evidence settles it" silently relaxes when the evidence is itself group-mediated;
- the **import independence debit** (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8 :50-51), which a shorthand "the frame supplies value terms" drops when those terms come from one shared cultural pool;
- the **gap itself** (Fig 9 :32, Fig 13 :134), which a concrete attribution erases by naming a source.

Conversely, one thing survives cleanly: where a non-updating anchor exists and R shrinks against it, the dispute is a factual-belief dispute; where it does not and each rule works on a subset, split and name the comparison. That is the residue worth sending. R_T was **not** measured numerically, because no ground reading exists; it is recorded qualitatively as the enumerated set of dropped constraints. Treating "the figures and the summary are the same thing" would set R_T to zero and would be the dead-reckoning error Fig 4 :30 names.
