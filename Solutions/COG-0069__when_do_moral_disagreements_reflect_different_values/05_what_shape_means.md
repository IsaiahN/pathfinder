## 1. A shape is a constraint, not a filler

The figures README states the limit: the framework returns *"what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)."* Fig 9 :32: *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself."* Fig 13 :134-135: *"Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence,"* and *"It cannot say which primitive is missing, because naming it would mean already holding it."*

From *The Library of Babel*: *"You can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."*

So a **shape** here is the narrowed form of an attribution you can state before you can name its source. For COG-0069 the shape is: a separator that tests for a non-updating anchor, measures whether R shrinks under effort, checks independence between frames, and carries a written refuter. It is not a list of which values people hold, and not a percentage split.

## 2. How the shape is obtained

- *Library of Babel*: use the vocabulary you already have that is closest to the thing, then look for recurring new terms that overlap or correlate with it. *"With each new term gained, you are able to narrow the space by definition and deepen your vocabulary."*
- The same essay names the exact failure this question invites: *"Perhaps your problem was a bundle or conflation of several problems masquerading as one? You will need to search all of those problems individually."* That is Fig 9 :55 in narrative form and is the governing move for F1 of the audit.
- Fig 6 :30: *"The description travels. Fails by being sharpened into terms only we can read. Cast it in effect terms."* Fig 9 :30-31: *"your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect rather than the cause."* We describe the behaviour of the disagreement (does it shrink? does each rule work on a subset? are the frames independent?), not the alleged cause.
- *Decoding Tacit Knowledge*: *"When contact isn't possible, the best you can send is a plain description of what you'd expect to see, because anyone can check that against the world."* This is why the outreach packet asks "what would you expect to see if this attribution were true?" before showing the figures.
- *Library of Babel* on stopping: *"you will know that you are done because the finish line will also be the starting place"* and *"you have already composed the answer through the process of locating it."*

## 3. What licenses a name

Only two things, both outside the shape itself:

1. **The ground settles it.** Formula step 5: *"the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays."* The README: the framework *"stops at the limit of what the available instruments can distinguish, and records the lineage so the stop is honest."* For this question, an instrument or a record that discriminates values from facts in a described case is still owed.
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs** (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8 :50-51), with a source gate and a shadow test. The moral-psychology frame supplies names like harm, fairness, purity and loyalty. Those are bindings onto held atoms, with provenance, not derivations from the six-term substrate.

*The Hard Problem is About Source* makes the governing point for this question. An instrument reads green but *"it cannot comment on the source"*: one green light, or yellow plus blue, or RGB, or CMYK, or a scheme not yet discovered. *"If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information."* A moral verdict is exactly such a reading: several sources (a value comparison, a factual belief, a social pressure) can produce the same "this is wrong" output. What is owed is a discriminator, not an argument. *"The answer is unreached until a better instrument exists."*

## 4. Why the three offered answers are not three values of one variable

*Library of Babel* again: *"Perhaps your problem was a bundle or conflation of several problems masquerading as one?"* Fig 9 :55: *"A bundled problem is usually a union of overlapping conditions, not a list of separate ones."* The three answers have different anchors. Factual-belief disagreements have a record that does not update. Value disagreements have human judgment as the legitimate anchor because the question is constitutively about people (Fig 2 :37). Social-interest disagreements have no independent anchor and are a failure of the checking arrangement (Fig 2 :18-21). Different anchors, different verdicts, none averaged.

## 5. Q: Does "shape, not a name" forbid naming a value? CORRECTION TO MY OWN FIRST PASS

**No.** The README's phrase is *"what a **missing** thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)."* The subject is a primitive not held in Γ. Harm, fairness, purity, loyalty and authority are not missing primitives under this reading; they are comparison terms (goals, Operators :51) bound from the moral-psychology frame onto atoms the framework already holds. Naming one is a provenanced binding, not the invention of a seventh term.

**I overstated this in the first pass** of `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` (F7). The correct claims are: (i) the framework returns a shape for missing primitives; (ii) naming a held atom is provenance; (iii) claiming that the binding moves the wall is false, because *"MINT composes inside the closure and can never add an atom"* (Fig 6 :21). Retracted as applied to naming values; the instrument requirement from §3 is **not** retracted. Both claims are recorded so neither is quietly lost. Triangulated (README + Fig 13 :134-135 + Fig 6 :21 + Operators :51).

## 6. Prestige, independence and my own status

**Prestige is not action.** *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*: *"Prestige must never be allowed to validate an action directly... Someone let prestige stand in for proof... Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it. The Ground Maintainer must be blind to prestige."* A list of famous moral psychologists who agree with the separator is the named collapse, not corroboration. The legitimate ask is a budget to try the separator against a ground.

**Independence is spent by use.** *Decoding Tacit Knowledge*: *"Long, close contact pulls the frames together, so the process that makes communication easy slowly uses up that extra reach."* Two reviewers who share this folder share an evidence pool; by Fig 2 :40 their agreement is *"the pool talking."* The second seat in `11_audit.md` is labelled accordingly.

**My own audit is a simulation, not a ground reading.** Same essay as §6's first paragraph: *"LLMs can quickly simulate an approximate answer/output with the formula found in the architecture post... However, the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself."* And: *"the output is only as good as the validator. The agent, or the tool, or the frame, or the theory, cannot validate itself."* So `01`, `06`, `07` and `09` are simulated derivations checked for internal consistency against the figures. They cannot settle any attribution they report on.
