# Outreach draft - validate the output first, then decide about the theory

**Use:** one to one, or as a cover letter attached to a submission through the public venues in
`03_public_contact_points.csv`. **Not** for bulk send.

**Hard precondition:** do not send until the stage-1 shape and refuter have been checked internally.
The output must survive validation or the two-stage offer collapses on contact.

---

**Subject:** Check one output about sources of durable advantage before you decide whether the framing is worth reading

> Hi [name],
>
> I am working on a figure-grounded derivation in strategic management and organisation studies. I
> have one specific output I would like you to check against what you already know. I am not asking
> you to read the framework first, and I am not asking you to agree with it. If the output does not
> survive, there is nothing else to look at.
>
> **The output (`specified`, not `grounded`):** the question "are durable advantages built mainly from
> valuable resources, distinctive capabilities, market position, or institutional relationships?"
> has the wrong shape. The four candidates are a union of overlapping conditions, not a list of
> separate levers. Each keeps working on a different subset, so the disagreement does not shrink with
> effort. In the framework's terms, capability is a property of agent-and-habitat, never of the agent
> alone, and no standing list of good actors survives a change of the residual. So the durable part
> is the contact, per firm-slot, and the four candidates are low-overlap frames whose union predicts
> the shape. That is a prediction of a **shape**, not a measurement or a new empirical result.
>
> I also note that "institutional relationships" bundles two different things: rent-generating
> interfirm ties, and legitimacy-producing field isomorphism. The second predicts firms becoming more
> alike, not more durably different. They cannot be scored as one source.
>
> **What I am not claiming:** that this settles the strategy debate; that the framework is the only
> true account of firm performance; that any one school is wrong in its own domain. The empirical
> variance literature is corroboration for the union shape, not a settlement of the duration
> question. I do not claim a name for the ground-level mechanism. The framework returns a shape, not
> a name.
>
> **What would refute the shape claim:**
> 1. one of the four frames, ranged against an independent anchor, predicts the full persistent
>    variance across sectors and time while the others add no surplus;
> 2. a decomposition in which one effect dominates *persistent* variance stably across sectors and
>    decades;
> 3. a firm's capability is shown to raise its persistent return with no change in contact with its
>    habitat;
> 4. a decomposition of "institutional relationships" in which rent and legitimacy mechanisms
>    predict the same output for the same residual.
>
> I wrote these down before sending this. If any one holds, I will retract the shape. A one-line
> "this fails because..." is more useful to me than agreement. If you would rather not engage, no
> reply is needed.
>
> **If it survives, and only then:** the sources are below. The figures are the authority, not my
> summary of them.
> - Figures and formula for direct download (branch `general-agent`): https://github.com/IsaiahN/tether/tree/general-agent/figures
> - Architecture and the cited figure set: https://adventuresinml.substack.com/p/arrival-of-the-fittest-the-tether
> - Empirical anchor for the union reading: https://www.hbs.edu/faculty/Pages/item.aspx?num=2398
>
> **If you want to use the machinery:** the formula is in the figures link. Run it on a problem you
> know well and can check against a ground. Write down what would refute your result *before* you
> run it, and state the mode of what you get. A reported failure is more useful to me than a reported
> success.
>
> Isaiah

## Why this version is ordered this way

| the move | figure-grounded reason |
|---|---|
| Output first, theory second | Fig 10 :52 - *"Bring a characterised residual, never the source."* Fig 2 :45 - frames range against the ground separately. |
| Do not ask for agreement | Fig 2 :35 - *"Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you."* |
| Pre-registered refuter | a falsifier written before the run is a design instrument, not only an honesty device. |
| Mode stated on every claim | `THE_FORMULA.md` - mode rule: general, specified, grounded; state which. |
| Anchor named and unmovable | Fig 2 :29-30 - an anchor does not change its answer because of what any frame thinks. |
| Union stated, not a winner | Fig 9 :54 - unbundle a union before searching again. |
| No "mainly X" assertion | Fig 11 :39 - capability is a property of agent-and-habitat; Fig 11 :51 - no standing list of good actors. |
| Prestige is not action | *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*: *"Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action."* |
| Framework returns a shape, not a name | figures README: *"what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)"*. |
| Empirical literature held as corroboration | Fig 4 :30 - take the round trip and measure the gap; McGahan and Porter measure variance of profitability, not duration of residual. |
| Offer the formula as a method to reuse | Fig 4 :22 - *"Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down."* |
| Ask users to state their own ground and refuter | Formula "Direction": a frame cannot check the direction of its own question; the architecture essay warns outputs may be *"contrived to fit the formula."* |
| No personal emails, no receptiveness rank | Fig 2 :40 - correlation disqualifying for a verdict; Fig 7 :35 - a room's agreement is variation, not a verdict. |
