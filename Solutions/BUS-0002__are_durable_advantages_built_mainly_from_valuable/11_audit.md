## 2. Independent domain census (written first)

### Arity, symmetry, scale

- Arity: one residual per firm-slot. The four candidates are not operands of R; they are frames
  proposed to predict it.
- Symmetry: no relabeling maps "resource" to "position" while preserving meaning. The four are
  asymmetric frames. A symmetric-bond reading (Fig 12 :92) would be a category error.
- Scale: the candidates live at four different strata. A verdict at one stratum need not hold at
  another (Fig 12 :104).

### Domains from the anchor test alone

| domain | actors | currency | anchor | admitted |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| internal library | firm, bundles, routines | rents, reconfiguration | none in isolation | no, until paired with habitat |
| industry structure | firm, rivals, entrants | profitability, barriers | observed industry differential | yes |
| interfirm relation | dyad, network | relational rent | observed net-of-solo performance | yes |
| institutional field | state, professions, peers | legitimacy | observed homogenisation | yes, predicts sameness |

The second seat reaches the same four-line census and the same exclusion of the internal domain in
isolation. Partition or union: union. Reason independent of the primary: the word "mainly" implies a
single lever, but each candidate is anchored at a different stratum, so an effect at one stratum is
not substitutable for an effect at another. That is a union by construction.

Dependency order: field and structure conditions, then an affordance vocabulary, then a stated
objective, then reconfiguration, then the return. Independent agreement with the primary order.

## 5. Round-trip gap R_T

R_T = gap(x, (T_E ∘ T_A)(x)) (Formula step 6; Fig 4 :30).

- **x (sent up):** the primary census, a concrete derivation over four named business schools and one
  empirical variance decomposition.
- **T_A (abstraction):** "durable advantage is a union-level property of agent-and-habitat contact;
  contributor valence is relative to the residual."
- **T_E (coming back):** re-instantiate that shape into four candidate mechanisms and an instrument.

**R_T (observed):** the abstract shape does not recover which of the four schools dominates in a given
sector. It also does not recover the difference between the duration of a small residual and the
variance of profitability. Both losses were present in the primary; the second seat confirms them
independently.

**R_T verdict:** nonzero and honestly reported. The gap is not an error to eliminate (Fig 4 :31-32);
it is what the coarser description cannot hold. It is the reason the answer stays `specified`.
