## 1. A shape is a constraint, not a filler

The figures README states the limit: the framework returns *"what a missing thing would have to look
like (a shape, not a name)."* Fig 9 :32: *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself."* Fig 13
:135 states: *"It cannot say which primitive is missing, because naming it would mean already holding
it."*

From *The Library of Babel*: *"You can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small
and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."*

So a **shape** is the narrowed form of an answer you can state before you can name it. It constrains;
it does not fill. For BUS-0002, "persistent advantage is a property of firm-and-habitat contact and the
four candidates are a union" is a shape. "Resources win" or "capabilities win" are names.

## 2. How a shape is obtained

- *Library of Babel*: use the vocabulary you already have that is *closest* to the thing, look for
  recurring new terms that overlap or correlate with it, recurse. *"With each new term gained, you
  narrow the space and deepen your vocabulary."*
- Fig 6 :30: *"The description travels. Fails by being sharpened into terms only we can read. Cast it
  in effect terms."* Fig 9 :30-31: *"your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect rather than
  the cause."*
- *Decoding Tacit Knowledge*, fetched this session: *"When contact isn't possible, the best you can
  send is a plain description of what you'd expect to see, because anyone can check that against the
  world."*
- *Library of Babel* on stopping: the answer is reached when *"the finish line will also be the
  starting place"*, and the paradox is that by then you have *"composed the answer through the
  process of locating it."*
- Fig 11 :43: *"The habitat is enumerated, never composed."* For this question the relevant habitat
  was enumerated before any figure line was chosen: actors, conditions, relations across four scales.

## 3. What licenses a name

Only two things, both outside the shape itself:

1. **The ground settles it.** Formula step 5: *"the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays. A
   gate is not the ground."* The README: the framework *"stops at the limit of what the available
   instruments can distinguish, and records the lineage so the stop is honest."*
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs** (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8 :50-52), with a
   source gate and a shadow test.

*They're Scanning Books* sharpens this: *"The real import is not a new term in the existing space. It
is a new dimension of the space."* And: *"A minimum viable set of six terms is a human's careful
guess at reality's compression axes; another sensorium's minimum set need not be those six."* Also:
*"Declaring the primitives does not make them neutral. It makes the choice visible."*

For BUS-0002 this matters because the four schools are themselves visible choices of vocabulary. Naming
one as *the* main source turns a visible choice into an invisible one.

## 4. The specific forbidden error here: a source named from a projection

*The Hard Problem is About Source* gives the mechanism. Metamerism: an instrument reads green and
*"the instrument is speaking only about what it measured, it cannot comment on the source."* One green
light, or yellow plus blue, or RGB, or CMYK, or a scheme not yet discovered, all give the same reading.
*"If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information."*
It was the **spectrometer**, not an argument, that resolved it.

The strategic-management analogue is precise. A variance decomposition (McGahan and Porter: 19 percent
industry, 32 percent business-specific, 4 percent corporate-parent, 2 percent year) is a reading of a
projection. It cannot by itself name the generative source, and it does not even measure the same
quantity as BUS-0002, which is *duration of a small residual*, not variance of profitability. What is
owed is a better instrument that discriminates duration from level and rent from legitimacy, not a
name attached to a percentage.

## 5. Correction to my own §3 and §4

I overstated the "shape, not a name" rule on a first pass. The README's phrase is *"what a **missing**
thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)."* It governs a primitive not held in Γ. The four
schools are not missing primitives; they are held frames in a neighbouring discipline. So naming them
is not forbidden. What is forbidden is declaring a partition where Fig 9 :54 says the split is a
union: *"And when the split lands on a union rather than a partition, unbundle before you search
again."*

My §4 metamerism point is **not** retracted. The variance reading still cannot name the source, and a
cross-scale import still needs its round-trip gap measured. But the reason is the missing
discriminating instrument, not a blanket ban on naming held frames. Both readings are recorded so
neither is quietly lost.

## 6. Why "mainly" is the wrong word

Fig 11 :49: *"An actor's valence is relative to the residual, not a property it carries."* Fig 11 :51:
*"No standing list of good actors survives a change of residual."* A word like "mainly" presupposes a
standing list with fixed valence. The framework refuses that. A shape that survives is: the condition
is a union of contact-dependent contributions, indexed per slot, with each contributor's weight
relative to that slot's residual.

Fig 12 :94 adds *"The arrangement is the substance."* Two firms with the same resource list and
different relational arrangements are different substances, which is why a stock-only answer fails.

## 7. Two consequences for outreach

**Prestige is not action.** From *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*, fetched this session:
*"Prestige must never be allowed to validate an action directly... Prestige can fund an action. It can
give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it."*
A campaign that gathers prestigious names to agree that "no single source wins" is the named collapse,
not corroboration. The legitimate ask is a budget to be tried and a ground that can refuse it.

**My own audit is a simulation, not a ground reading.** Same essay: *"LLMs can quickly simulate an
approximate answer/output with the formula found in the architecture post. It will often be better
than the generic outputs that you will usually get otherwise too... However, the truly testable
output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself."* The
architecture essay is blunter: such outputs *"must be seen as simulacra."* So `01` is a simulated
derivation, checked for internal consistency against the figures. It is **not** a test of the strategy
question and must be labelled as such. It cannot settle anything it reports on.

## 8. What would move this from shape to name

- A source-discriminating instrument that separates *duration of a small residual* from *level of
  profitability*, and that separates rent-generating ties from legitimacy-producing isomorphism.
- A persistent-variance decomposition, repeated across sectors and decades, using that instrument.
- A provenanced import from a low-overlap frame whose closure predicts the union shape, with source
  gate, shadow test, and a recorded round-trip gap. Chollet 2019 is such a witness for the
  agent-plus-history half, but it is a parallel, not a settlement.

Until one of those lands, the honest output is the shape plus the four refuters, and the mode stays
`specified`.
