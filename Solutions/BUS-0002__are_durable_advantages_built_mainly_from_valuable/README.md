## The question restated as an effect

What is being decided is not the *source* of a firm's advantage as a thing it owns, but the *duration of the residual staying small*. In effect terms: a firm's library Γ predicts a market return Γ(b,a); the market returns o′; R = |Γ(b,a) - o′| is measured per firm-slot, not averaged across firms (Fig 1 :19, Fig 1 :22). Durable advantage is the case where one slot's R stays near zero for a long duration.

- Option A, "advantage is built mainly from one of the four listed things": leads to the prediction that persistence is a property the firm, its position, or its relationships carry alone, and that one lever governs it.
- Option B, "advantage is a property of firm-and-habitat contact, and the four are low-overlap frames whose union predicts the residual": leads to the prediction that persistence fails when contact or reach fails, that each candidate governs a different subset, and that no single lever clears the compound (Fig 11 :39, Fig 11 :40, Fig 9 :54).
- What an answer would look like, written down and bound as an ordinary term before the run (Formula, "Direction"): a *shape* naming (a) the condition or conditions that must hold for a slot's R to stay small over a long duration, (b) whether those conditions form a partition or a union, and (c) an instrument that discriminates a failure of one condition from a failure of another. That shape is the sought-for term; until it is bound it predicts nothing and no check in the loop can see it.

The verdict recorded in `09_watson_answer.md` is Option B in `specified` mode. It is recorded per domain and never averaged into a single "main" source.

## What is deliberately not here

- No personal emails of named individuals.
- No ranking of people by how likely they are to agree.
- No bulk-send plan.
- No claim that this derivation is `grounded`, no claim that it settles the strategy question, and no claim that the framework is the only true account of firm performance.

Three figure-grounded reasons, plus one practical one:

1. A room is not an anchor. *"A room's agreement is variation, not a verdict. Selection between rooms is where the ground votes."* (Fig 7 :35) and *"Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you."* (Fig 2 :35). Gathering agreeable readers manufactures the correlation that disqualifies a verdict.
2. Correlation has valence. *"disqualifying for a verdict: agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool"* (Fig 2 :40). A list selected for receptiveness is one witness wearing many names.
3. A verifier must be outside the interior. *"A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it."* (Fig 10 :42). The first contact therefore carries the residual and its refuter, not the framework.
4. Legally, unsolicited bulk email to scraped addresses breaches GDPR and CAN-SPAM style rules in many jurisdictions and gets the sending domain blacklisted. Public opt-in routes avoid that failure as well as the structural one.
