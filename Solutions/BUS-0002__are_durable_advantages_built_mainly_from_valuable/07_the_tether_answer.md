# The Tether's answer on sources of durable advantage

**Mode: `specified` - not `grounded`.** This is a shape and a route, not a result. Simulated derivation
checked against the figures (`05_what_shape_means.md` section 7), not a ground reading.

## 1. The residual it answers

The question asks which of four things "mainly" builds durable advantage. Restated: R = |Γ(b,a) - o′|
is measured per firm-slot (Fig 1 :19), and durable advantage is the case where a slot's R stays small
for a long duration. The four candidates are four frames that each claim to predict that persistence.
The question's own shape assumes a partition. The derivation finds a union.

## 2. The shape

1. **No single main source.** Fig 9 :54: *"And when the split lands on a union rather than a partition,
   unbundle before you search again."* Fig 9 :55: *"A bundled problem is usually a union of overlapping
   conditions, not a list of separate ones."* Each school works on a different subset, so the
   disagreement does not shrink with effort (Fig 9 :4-5).
2. **Advantage is a property of firm-and-habitat, not of the firm alone.** Fig 11 :39: *"capability is a
   property of agent-and-habitat, never of the agent alone"* and Fig 11 :40: *"An improvement that does
   not change contact changes nothing, however much it improves."* This refuses internal-only
   ("mainly resources", "mainly capabilities") and external-only ("mainly position") as general
   answers.
3. **No standing list of good sources.** Fig 11 :49: *"An actor's valence is relative to the residual,
   not a property it carries."* Fig 11 :51: *"No standing list of good actors survives a change of
   residual."* Whether a resource or a relationship helps depends on the slot and the duration.
4. **The four frames are low overlap, so their union has surplus.** Fig 8 :45: *"Two frames together
   reach what neither reaches alone."* Fig 8 :48-49 says the surplus lives where the closures differ.
   Triangulation, not negotiation: Fig 2 :35, *"Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror
   cannot correct you."*
5. **"Institutional relationships" must be unbundled.** Rent-generating interfirm ties (Dyer and Singh,
   1998) and legitimacy-producing field isomorphism (DiMaggio and Powell, 1983) predict different
   outputs. The second makes firms more alike, so it cannot be the main source of durable
   *differential* advantage.
6. **The anchor is unmoving and external to the schools.** Fig 2 :29-30: *"An anchor is whatever settles
   the question and does not change its answer because of what any frame thinks."* Here it is a sound
   persistent-performance measure; each frame ranges against it separately (Fig 2 :45).
7. **The framework names no winner.** It returns a shape: a union of contact-dependent contributions,
   indexed per slot. Which contribution dominates in a given slot is a matter for the ground, not for
   the metatheory.

## 3. What it explicitly does not say

- Not that resources, capabilities, position and relationships are unimportant. Each governs a subset.
- Not that one school is false in its own domain. Barney's VRIN governs which internal resources
  qualify; Teece's processes govern reconfiguration; Porter's structure governs industry effects;
  Dyer and Singh govern relational rents. The error is declaring one "mainly".
- Not that McGahan and Porter's percentages answer BUS-0002. They decompose the variance of
  profitability, not the duration of a small residual. That is a lossy cross-scale import (Fig 4 :30).
- Not that the four schools are isomers over one atom set. They are a functional group (see `06`).
- Not `grounded`. No instrument here separates duration from level or rent from legitimacy.
- Not a claim that the framework is the only true account of firm performance.

## 4. What would settle it - the ground

A source-discriminating instrument, not a further argument. From *The Hard Problem is About Source*: an
instrument that reads green *"cannot comment on the source"*; the spectrometer resolved it.

- **Duration instrument:** separate "R stays small for a long time" from "profitability variance is
  high in this sector". The current literature measures the second, not the first.
- **Relation discriminator:** separate relational-rent mechanisms from legitimacy-isomorphism
  mechanisms in the same firms, and test which moves the persistent residual.
- **Union-surplus test:** fit each frame alone and all four together against the persistent-residual
  anchor. If the union adds no out-of-sample surplus, F5 fails.
- **Contact test:** show a capability change that moves the ground with no change in contact, or show
  that none does (Fig 11 :40).
- **Standing refuters from the audit:** one frame explaining all persistent variance; one effect
  dominating persistently across sectors and decades; an institutional decomposition in which rent
  and legitimacy predict the same output.

## 5. The honest stop

**Unreached, not unreachable.** The shape stops at the limit of the available instruments: no current
measure in this field separates duration from level or rent from legitimacy. Fig 13 :134: *"Unreachable
is a verdict here, not a silence"* - but here a positive union shape is available, so `gap` is not
claimed. Fig 8 :39: *"So a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence."* The lineage is
recorded so the stop is checkable. *"Indiscernibility is not identity."*

Two strains are kept visible: the capability claim is an uncited axiom inside the framework, and the
question's phrase "institutional relationships" bundles two mechanisms. Neither is smoothed over.

## 6. One-paragraph version for outreach

> The Tether does not name a winner among valuable resources, distinctive capabilities, market
> position and institutional relationships. It says the question has the wrong shape: those four are a
> union of overlapping conditions, not a list of separate levers, and each keeps working on a different
> subset. Durable advantage is a property of firm-and-habitat contact, indexed per firm-slot, and no
> standing list of good sources survives a change of the residual. The four frames are low overlap, so
> their union, ranged against one unmoving performance anchor, reaches what none reaches alone; and
> "institutional relationships" must be split into rent-generating ties and legitimacy-producing
> isomorphism, of which the second predicts sameness rather than durable difference. What the Tether
> offers is that shape, four refuters written in advance, and the instruments that would close it. It
> does not claim to settle the strategy debate, and it is a `specified` simulated derivation, not a
> ground reading.
