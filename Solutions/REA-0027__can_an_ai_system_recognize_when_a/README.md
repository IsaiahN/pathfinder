## Question restated as an effect

What is being decided: whether a system's report "this problem is not fixed by the information I hold" can be earned from inside the system, or only issued relative to a declared library and a live instrument. Option A (a world-level verdict from inside) leads to a frame certifying its own limit, which the figures forbid. Option B (a relative reachability report plus a characterised shape) leads to a checkable claim with a pre-registered refuter and an honest stop. An answer would look like a shape: the border between what a declared closure can decide and what requires a ground or an import. Per the formula's "Direction" step, that sought-for shape is written down as an ordinary term so it can accrue a residual and be refused.

## What is deliberately not here

- No personal emails of named individuals.
- No ranking of people by how likely they are to agree.
- No bulk-send plan.

Reasons, in the figures' own words:

1. *"Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you."* (Fig 2 :35). A list selected for receptiveness is a list selected to agree.
2. *"agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool"* (Fig 2 :40). Agreement is not a witness.
3. *"A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it."* (Fig 10 :42). The first contact carries the residual and its refuter, never the framework's interior.

Legally: unsolicited bulk email to scraped personal addresses breaches GDPR and CAN-SPAM style rules in many jurisdictions. Public opt-in channels avoid that failure and the structural one.
