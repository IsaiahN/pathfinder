## Wider literature that bears directly on the question

Three findings, one from each of three low-overlap fields, shape the WATSON verdict:

1. **Contrastive underdetermination is structural, not a failure of effort.** The SEP entry notes that for any finite set of data points there are infinitely many functions passing through them, and *"No finite amount of data will ever be able to narrow the possibilities down to just a single function or indeed, any finite number of candidate functions."* This is a world-side analogue of Fig 6 :27: from inside the data, depth and absence look the same.
2. **Statistics has a name and a method for the same border.** Non-identifiability means observationally equivalent parametrizations; identifiability analysis checks it with a declared model. This corroborates the figure-derived YES for reachability relative to a declared Γ, because the judgement is always relative to a stated model.
3. **Computability theory separates "not found" from "not there."** The halting problem is undecidable, and the "Recognizing partial solutions" section shows that even recognising a partial solver is at least as hard. This corroborates the figure-derived NO for world-level certification from inside a frame. The ML self-evaluation paper (*Language Models (Mostly) Know What They Know*, Kadavath et al., 2022) is the empirical counterpart: models predict P(True) and P(IK) with encouraging calibration in-domain, but *"they struggle with calibration of P(IK) on new tasks"*, which is exactly the channel decay condition of Fig 10 :24.

## Distribution rules: offer, do not push

Figure-grounded reasons:

- **Fig 10 :52** *"Bring a characterised residual, never the source."* Send the shape claim and its refuter first. A recipient who has absorbed the framework before being asked for a verdict has joined the frame being checked.
- **Fig 2 :45** *"Frames range against the ground. They do not negotiate with it."* Ask what they would expect to see if the claim were true, before giving them the article.
- **Fig 9 :27-28** *"describe it before you go looking, or any frame you pick will seem to fit."* The residual drives the choice of checker, not the other way round.
- **Fig 2 :40** *"agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool"*. Never pool later agreement as corroboration.
- **Fig 7 :13** *"agreement compounds, evidence does not"*. A chain of agreeing readers is motion, not progress.

**Recommended order in a reply:** (1) the two-condition shape claim and the four refuters, (2) the question of what they would count as a failure, (3) only then the links, with the note that the figures, not the summary, are the authority.

## The two-stage offer

**Stage 1:** the recipient checks the output with no access to the framework interior. Their verdict is recorded before any theory is shown.

**Stage 2, opt-in only:** if curious, they read the figure text and the essays. Their later agreement is a conversation, never a witness.

**Stage 3:** offer the formula as a method to reuse. Fig 4 :22 *"Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down."* A generator can be given away without giving away an answer. Each user must write their own refuter and state their own mode, because `THE_FORMULA.md` "Direction" says a frame *"cannot check the direction of its own question, because the shape a frame is searching for is not a term it holds."*

**Hard precondition:** stages 1 to 3 only work if the stage-1 output survives. The four refuters in `02_outreach_email.md` are the standing test. Nothing is sent by this agent; hosting and sending are yours to do. Prefer an opt-in download page over a pushed attachment.
