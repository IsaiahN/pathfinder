# Expert verification packet: underdetermination detection

**What this is:** a claim in a form a checker in statistics, uncertainty in AI, inverse problems or philosophy of science can test. Mode, derivation and refuters are stated before the conclusion, so a reader knows what to look for and what would settle it.

**Mode: `specified` on the substantive claims, `general` on the structural shape, not `grounded`.** No measurement of a real system is claimed. The figures are primary; world sources are corroboration only.

**Status:** a simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures, not a ground reading. It cannot settle the question it reports on.

## 1. The claim, in one paragraph

"Can an AI system recognize when a problem is underdetermined by the available information?" is two conditions. Relative to a declared library Γ, the answer is yes: a system that can enumerate closure(Γ) can decide reachability before searching, and it returns the effect-term shape of what is missing rather than a name. About the world, the answer is no from inside: genuine absence, depth beyond budget, and a closed channel are indiscernible within one frame. The report therefore comes in three parts, a relative reachability result, a characterised shape, and a refuter, and it must be checked by an outside frame. The 20-minute check below tests each part separately.

## 2. Three checkable sub-claims

### C1: framework-internal, checkable by reading Figures 13 and 6

- **Statement:** a system with an enumerable closure can decide, before searching, whether a candidate is reachable from what it holds. This is the figure-licensed YES.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 13 :132-133 *"The second is decidability: given the library as it stands, could this have been reached at all. A system with a closure can answer that before it searches, and what it returns is a shape rather than a name."* Also Fig 6 :21 *"MINT composes inside the closure and can never add an atom."*
- **Refuter:** show a system that reports non-reachability for a candidate that is in its closure, or a system that can certify non-reachability without exposing any closure at all. Either breaks the condition or the claim.

### C2: framework-internal barrier, checkable by reading Figures 6, 8, 9 and 10

- **Statement:** depth, genuine absence and a closed channel are indiscernible from inside a non-sealed frame; therefore no world-level underdetermination verdict can be certified from inside.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 6 :27 *"Which is why some unreachability is depth and some is genuine absence, and from inside the frame the two look the same."* Fig 8 :39 *"So a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence."* Fig 9 :44 *"Only a sealed room can be searched to the end, and no frame is a sealed room. So searching and finding nothing proves nothing."* Fig 8 :37 *"A frame cannot certify its own limit."*
- **Refuter:** produce a derivation inside a single frame that discriminates genuine absence from depth with no imported instrument and no outside ground. This must defeat Fig 6 :27 and Fig 8 :39 together.

### C3: structural prerequisite, checkable in the Operators table and Figure 1

- **Statement:** "not fixed by I" requires negation as well as a threshold comparison, and detection must be per slot, not per global average.
- **Source, verbatim:** Operators table :73-74 *"A vocabulary with − and without ¬ can say what is missing from an arrangement and cannot say that an arrangement is wrong, so it can only ever accumulate."* Fig 12 :112 *"A plan is a sequence. A goal is a comparison. Progress is a subtraction. A refutation is a negation, and that one is not a bond."* Fig 1 :22 *"Aggregating across slots is how a live signal disappears into an average."*
- **Refuter:** exhibit a system that carries "not fixed" as a refusable term using only subtraction, or a case where a global score suffices to locate every open slot.

## 3. What is explicitly not claimed

- Not that any current LLM has an enumerable closure(Γ), or that calibration scores are the anchor.
- Not that a failure to find an answer proves absence.
- Not that "underdetermined" is one operator. It is ¬ plus ⋛, and the two are not interchangeable.
- Not a theory of everything, no final room, no new primitive, and no import that moves the wall.
- Not `grounded`. Nothing here is asserted as settled by an experiment.
- Not that the simulated second seat in `11_audit.md` is a witness. It shares the primary's evidence pool.

## 5. What would settle it

A discriminating instrument, not a further argument. From *The Hard Problem is About Source*: an instrument that reads green *"cannot comment on the source"*; the spectrometer resolved the case. Here:

1. **Channel test:** distinguish a closed channel from a converged one from inside the frame.
2. **Metaframe test:** certify one's own limit with a derivation the outside ground pays.
3. **Slot test:** show a global score sufficient to locate every open slot.
4. **Decidability test:** give a real system a declared Γ and score its in/out classification against an independent derivation.

## 6. What a checker can do in twenty minutes

1. Read Fig 13 :132-133, Fig 6 :27, Fig 8 :39, Fig 9 :44 and Operators table :73-74. Five short passages.
2. Check C1: is decidability-before-search a real consequence of a declared closure, or is it being assumed?
3. Check C2: name one instrument that discriminates depth from genuine absence from inside a frame, or say why none is possible.
4. Check C3 against your own field: in identifiability analysis, is the non-uniqueness judgement model-relative, and is the per-slot warning real?
5. Send the refutation. A failure is more useful than an endorsement.
