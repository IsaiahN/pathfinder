## 1. A shape is a narrowed constraint, not a filled answer

The canonical figures README states the limit: the framework returns *"what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)"*, then stops *"at the limit of what the available instruments can distinguish, and records the lineage so the stop is honest."* Fig 9 :32: *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself"*. Fig 13 :133: *"A system with a closure can answer that before it searches, and what it returns is a shape rather than a name."*

From *The Library of Babel* (2026-10-02): *"You can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."*

So a **shape** is the form of an answer you can state before you can name it. It constrains; it does not fill. For this question the shape is: *a border between what a declared closure can decide and what requires an imported atom or a live ground reading.* That is a shape. "This model is definitely underdetermined by the world" is a name, and a world-level verdict a frame cannot issue.

## 2. How a shape is obtained

- *Library of Babel*: use the vocabulary already held that is closest to the thing, look for recurring terms that overlap or correlate with it, recurse. *"With each new term gained, you narrow the space and deepen your vocabulary."*
- Fig 6 :30: *"The description travels. Fails by being sharpened into terms only we can read. Cast it in effect terms."* Fig 9 :30-31: *"your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect rather than the cause"*.
- *Library of Babel* on the two stopping cases: *"If the final book in your exhaustive search doesn't answer the question, then there are only two possibilities: the nature of your question was unanswerable, or malformed."* It adds the union case: *"Perhaps your problem was a bundle or conflation of several problems masquerading as one?"* This is the essay-level source for F1 in `01_figure_grounded_audit.md`.
- Fig 9 :27-28: *"describe it before you go looking, or any frame you pick will seem to fit"*. The residual selects the checker.

## 3. What licenses a name

Two things, both outside the shape itself:

1. **The ground settles it.** `THE_FORMULA.md` step 5: *"the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays. A gate is not the ground."* The canonical README: the framework *"stops at the limit of what the available instruments can distinguish"*.
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs**, with a source gate and a shadow test (Fig 6 :23; Fig 8 :50-52).

For this question, saying "the answer is not in closure(Γ)" is a shape relative to a declared Γ. Saying "the world leaves this open" is a name that only a ground could license, and the figures forbid a frame from issuing it about itself (Fig 8 :37).

## 4. The instrument question is the source question

*The Hard Problem is About Source* (2026-08-28) gives the mechanism that matters here: *"If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information (it cannot define consciousness in full)."* The metamerism example shows an instrument reading green that *"cannot comment on the source"*: one green light, or yellow plus blue, or RGB, or CMYK. The essay's verdict: *"An instrument that can't distinguish these cases demonstrates its own inadequacy, it cannot claim anything substantial about what is being measured or what causes it."* And the resolution was not an argument: *"It was proven wrong not by argument, but by the arrival of a better instrument."*

Applied here: a system's report "nothing more is resolving" is a reading. From that reading alone, several sources are compatible: genuine absence, depth, or a closed channel. Naming one of them as the source is the metamerism error. What is owed is a discriminating instrument, which is the essay's point and Fig 6 :32's point: *"An instrument is not built from a description. It is improved from a worse instrument already returning something."*

## 5. Why this derivation cannot settle its own question

*The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap* (2026-11-19) states the validator rule: *"the output is only as good as the validator. The agent, or the tool, or the frame, or the theory, cannot validate itself. An external validator, or ground, is always needed to tether the veracity of the output to reality. Anything else is drift."*

It also names the exact failure this question is about: *"A broken channel looks exactly like a working one, until the drift is so large that the system collapses."*

And the standing warning about this artifact: *"LLMs can quickly simulate an approximate answer/output with the formula found in the architecture post. It will often be better than the generic outputs that you will usually get otherwise too (intelligence is essentially arrangement). However, the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself."*

So `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` is a simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures. It is not a test, and it cannot settle the question it reports on.

## 6. Standing correction to my own reading

An earlier pass of this file treated "shape, not a name" as if it forbade naming any specific capability state of an AI. That overstates it. The canonical phrase governs *"a missing thing"*: a primitive not held in Γ. Naming a property of a system whose library is declared is a binding from an outside frame onto held terms, provided its provenance is recorded. What remains forbidden is (a) inventing a seventh kind where the six suffice, (b) claiming that such a binding moves the wall (Fig 6 :21 *"MINT composes inside the closure and can never add an atom"*), and (c) promoting a relative report to a world verdict (Fig 8 :37). The correction is kept visible rather than silently applied.

## 7. Consequence for outreach

*The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap* on prestige: *"Prestige must never be allowed to validate an action directly... Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it. The Ground Maintainer must be blind to prestige."*

The legitimate ask is therefore the opposite of gathering endorsements: give the shape claim a budget to be tried by people who can refute it, and let an instrument settle it. This is why `02_outreach_email.md` leads with the refuter, never with trust or agreement.
