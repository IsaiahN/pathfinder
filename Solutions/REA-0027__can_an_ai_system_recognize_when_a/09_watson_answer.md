## QUESTION

Decide, in effect terms, what the Tether says about the claim that an AI system can recognize when a problem is underdetermined by the available information. Option A: answer yes as a single world-level capability verdict. Option B: split the question into reachability relative to a declared library and certification about the world, answer yes to the first and no to the second, and return a shape with a refuter. What each leads to: A issues a verdict a frame cannot certify, conflates depth with absence and spends the independence of the people checking it; B returns a checkable border, separate domain verdicts and an honest stop. The sought shape is bound in section 0 of `01` as an ordinary term so it can accrue a residual and be refused.

## VERDICT (from the figures)

The question is a union and must not be answered with one averaged verdict. For a system that exposes its library, relative underdetermination is decidable before search and returns a shape. World-level underdetermination is not certifiable from inside a non-sealed frame: depth, genuine absence and a closed channel look the same. The report needs negation and comparison, must be per slot, and must be brought to an outside frame as a characterised residual with a refuter written first. This is a `specified`, ungrounded result. Strongest single point: reachability is decidable relative to what is held, but the frame cannot certify its own limit, so "unreachable" is a verdict about a library, not a verdict about the world.

- **cost if wrong:** bounded and locatable. The packet is mode-labelled, states refuters before the conclusion and records the lineage, so an error is findable rather than invisible. The real cost is spending the independence of outside readers by sending a verdict instead of a residual. Fig 2 :42 "Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong." That cost is avoidable by leading with the shape claim and its refuter.
- **undoable:** yes. The world-level verdict is simply not issued; no primitive has to be withdrawn. A sent claim can be superseded by the corrected packet, and the relative reachability claim stands on its own.
- **reversed by:** a ground reading, not agreement. A channel discriminator that distinguishes a closed channel from a converged one, a derivation that certifies a frame's own limit with outside ground payment, a case where a global score locates every open slot, or an accuracy measurement of in/out closure classification against an independent derivation, would each move a domain. The five pre-registered refuters are in `01` section 4. Until one fires, the verdict stands as `candidate`.
