Order by dependency: negation vocabulary, reachability check, ground contact, outside verification. Likely break between vocabulary and objective, because a system can have uncertainty scores without a bound notion of its own closure.

## Round trip gap R_T

The Symbols table defines R_T as the gap between what was sent up and what came back down, `gap(x, (T_E ∘ T_A)(x))`, measuring what the coarser description cannot hold. Fig 4 :30 requires it to be measured rather than assumed. I ran the round trip on the condensation this folder actually uses.

1. **Sent up (x):** the verbatim figure set, chiefly Fig 13 :132-133 (decidability before search), Fig 6 :27 (depth versus absence), Fig 8 :39 and :37 (no proof of absence, no self-certification), Fig 1 :22 (per slot), Operators :73-74 (negation).
2. **Abstraction (T_A):** the shorthand in `07_the_tether_answer.md`, namely "relative yes, world no, split the union, per slot, with a refuter".
3. **Concretisation (T_E):** a concrete product claim about an AI system, for example "this model can tell when its answers are underdetermined".

**R_T is nonzero.** Three things the shorthand holds do not survive the trip back down:

- the **Γ-exposure condition**. The shorthand says "a system that can enumerate its closure". A concrete system usually cannot, and calling a model's confidence score a closure silently drops the condition.
- the **channel condition**. The shorthand says "a closed channel looks like a converged one". A product claim about confidence cannot tell which is the case without an instrument that is not in the figures.
- the **operator prerequisite**. The shorthand says "needs ¬ and ⋛". A scoring model may have neither as bound, refusable terms, so its "not fixed" output is a reading, not a refusal.

One thing survives cleanly: reachability relative to a declared set is decidable before search, and world-level absence is not certifiable from inside. That is the residue worth sending. R_T was **not** measured numerically, because no ground reading exists; it is recorded qualitatively as the enumerated set of dropped constraints. Treating "the formula is the condensed version of the figures" as identity would set R_T to zero and would be the dead-reckoning error Fig 4 :30 names.
