## 1. A shape is a constraint, not a filler

The figures README states the limit: the framework returns "what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)". Fig 9 :32: "you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself". Fig 13: "It can say the answer is out of reach with the library as it stands... It cannot say which primitive is missing, because naming it would mean already holding it."

From *The Library of Babel* (reopened this session): "you can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."

For INS-0022 a **shape** is therefore: a union of overlapping conditions, indexed per institution and group slot, ranged against a behavioural anchor, composed through typed bonds whose arrangement carries the information, with the winning configuration left open until a source-discriminating instrument settles it. That is a set of constraints on any answer. It is not an answer such as "participation is the lever" or "trust is 0.62".

## 2. How the shape is obtained

- *Library of Babel*: use the vocabulary you already have that is closest to the thing, then "With each new term gained, you narrow the space and deepen your vocabulary." The essay also notes that if the final exhaustive book fails, "the nature of your question was unanswerable, or malformed" and that "Perhaps your problem was a bundle or conflation of several problems masquerading as one? You will need to search all of those problems individually". That is precisely Fig 9's union rule applied to the four conditions.
- Fig 6 :30: "The description travels. Fails by being sharpened into terms only we can read. Cast it in effect terms." Fig 9 :30-31: "your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect rather than the cause." So describe the residual as behavioural reliance failing in named slots, not as an abstract "trust deficit".
- *Decoding Tacit Knowledge*: "When contact isn't possible, the best you can send is a plain description of what you'd expect to see, because anyone can check that against the world."
- *Library of Babel* on stopping: "You will know that you are done because the finish line will also be the starting place" and "you have already composed the answer through the process of locating it." The shape is reached when the search terms and the answer converge, not when a survey number stabilises.

## 3. What licenses a name

Only two things, both outside the shape itself:

1. **The ground settles it.** Formula step 5: "the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays." The README: the framework "stops at the limit of what the available instruments can distinguish, and records the lineage so the stop is honest."
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs** (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8 :50-51), with a source gate and a shadow test.

*They're Scanning Books* sharpens this: "The real import is not a new term in the existing space. It is a new dimension of the space." And: "A minimum viable set of six terms is a human's careful guess at reality's compression axes; another sensorium's minimum set need not be those six." So the binding of fairness, effectiveness, identity and participation onto framework operators is a visible choice from the institutional-trust frame, not a discovery of nature's own list.

For INS-0022, naming "participation wins" would be a name where the framework returns a shape. The honest form stays `which configuration closes R in slot s`.

## 4. The specific forbidden error here: reading the source off a projection

*The Hard Problem is About Source* (reopened this session) gives the mechanism. Metamerism: an instrument reads green and "cannot comment on the source". It lists yellow plus blue, RGB, CMYK, and schemes not yet discovered, then: "If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information". And: "The answer is unreached until a better instrument exists." The spectrometer, not an argument, resolved the case.

Applied here: a rise in self-reported trust is a reading that several sources could produce, improved fairness, improved effectiveness, identity alignment, participation, or a change in question framing. Naming one of those as the source from the reading alone is the metamerism error. What is owed is a source-discriminating instrument, not a larger survey. This is the deepest reason F3 and F8 in `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` refuse the survey score as anchor.

*The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*: "the output is only as good as the validator. The agent, or the tool, or the frame, or the theory, cannot validate itself."

## 5. Two corrections to my own reading

### 5a. "Shape, not a name" does not forbid using the four condition names

The README's phrase is "what a **missing** thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)". It governs a primitive not held in the library. Fairness, effectiveness, identity and participation are the question's own operands; they are not claimed as new primitives of the framework. What the framework forbids is promoting one of them to *the answer* without a ground or a provenanced import. Recorded so neither the permission nor the limit is lost.

### 5b. "Capability in the agent alone" does not apply to an institution alone

Fig 11 :39-40 says capability is a property of agent-and-habitat. Reading a trust score as a property of the institution alone, independent of the citizen habitat, is the named error. The earlier framing in §4 that treated a trust reading as a property of the instrument was too narrow; narrowed here to: the reading is a projection whose source is institution-and-habitat, and the discriminator is behavioural.

## 6. Two consequences for outreach

**Prestige is not action.** From *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*: "Prestige must never be allowed to validate an action directly... Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it. The Ground Maintainer must be blind to prestige." Gathering well known names to agree that the union claim is plausible is the named collapse. The legitimate ask is to give the claim a budget to be tried against behaviour.

**This audit is a simulation, not a ground reading.** Same essay: "LLMs can quickly simulate an approximate answer/output... However, the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself." So `01` is a simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures. It is not a test of the claim and must be labelled as such. It cannot settle anything it reports on.
