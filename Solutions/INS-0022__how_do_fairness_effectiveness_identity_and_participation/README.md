## The question restated as an effect

What is being decided is not "is trust high or low" but what configuration of four conditions closes the gap, in which institution-and-group slot, and under what measurement. Option A treats fairness, effectiveness, identity and participation as four additive inputs to one national trust score and optimises the score. Option B treats them as a union of overlapping conditions indexed per slot, ranges each against a behavioural ground, and refuses to name the winning configuration until a source-discriminating instrument settles it. A leads to a single lever applied to a compound, an anchor that updates on framing, and a list of checkers selected for agreement. B leads to an unbundled shape, per-slot verdicts, and written refuters.

An answer would look like: a statement of the four conditions and their intersections, an anchor that does not move when frames move, a bond-level account of how the four compose, and a declared `gap` where the framework can only return a kind of thing. That sought-for shape is bound as an ordinary term in `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` so it can accrue a residual and be refused, per the formula step "Direction".

## What is deliberately not here

- No personal emails of named individuals.
- No ranking of people by how likely they are to agree that trust is shaped this way.
- No bulk-send plan.

Three reasons in the figures' own words. First, a room does not upgrade the label: agreement among frames is a weighted average of influence, and adding people raises the vocabulary and does not add an anchor (seat map, failure genera). Second, correlation disqualifies a verdict: "High correlation is disqualifying for a verdict and efficient for a convention" (seat map :163), so selecting recipients for receptiveness manufactures exactly the correlation that makes their agreement worthless. Third, asking people to trust the framework before they check the residual spends their independence: "A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it" (Fig 10 :42). A failure is more useful to this package than an endorsement.

Legally, scraping personal emails for unsolicited bulk outreach breaches GDPR and CAN-SPAM style rules in many jurisdictions and gets the sending domain blacklisted. That is a practical failure on top of the structural one. All venues in `03_public_contact_points.csv` are public institutional submission or role-based contact routes.
