**Use:** one to one, or as a cover note attached to a submission through the public venues in `03_public_contact_points.csv`. **Not** for bulk send.

**Hard precondition:** do not send until the shape claim in `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` is checked against the verbatim figure text. The two-stage offer collapses on contact if the output does not survive.

**Subject:** Check one output about institutional trust before you decide whether the framework is worth reading

> Hi [name],
>
> I have one specific output I would like you to check against what you already know about institutional trust. I am not asking you to read the framework first, and I am not asking you to agree with it. If the output does not survive, there is nothing else to look at.
>
> **The output (`specified`, not `grounded`):** fairness, effectiveness, identity and participation do not combine as four additive inputs to one trust score. They behave as a union of overlapping conditions, indexed per institution and per group. A lever that clears a slot where only one condition is missing leaves standing the slots where two conditions bind together. The three things I would ask you to check are:
>
> 1. The four are not interchangeable. Identity behaves like an identity relation (two names, one referent), participation produces an input, effectiveness is a threshold on an outcome, and fairness is a comparison. Same elements under a different arrangement behave differently.
> 2. No standing list of trust builders survives a change of group. The same reform can raise trust in one group-slot and lower it in another. What counts as progress has to be described in the institution's own terms before a lever is chosen.
> 3. The measurement anchor is behaviour, not the survey score. A self-reported trust number is produced by the same frames it is used to check. The question is whether behavioural cooperation, ranged against independently measured institutional performance, settles the claim.
>
> **What I am not claiming:** that I can name which of the four closes the gap in your institution. The framework returns a shape, not a name. I am also not claiming that a national trust average is a decision variable; it is a filter for where to look, not a witness for what is true.
>
> **What would refute this, written before I sent it:**
> 1. one lever closes the gap in every intersection at once, with no categorically different residue in any slot;
> 2. one scalar trust score predicts behavioural cooperation equally well in every institution and group, with no live residual anywhere;
> 3. the survey score is invariant to framing and to respondent identity and fully predicts cooperation, with no behavioural measure adding anything;
> 4. institutions with the same four elements under different arrangements show identical trust trajectories.
>
> If any of those holds, the shape is wrong and I will retract it. A one-line "this fails because..." is more useful to me than agreement. If you would rather not engage, no reply is needed.
>
> **If it survives, and only then:** the sources are below. The figures, not my summary of them, are the authority.
> - Verbatim figure text with line numbers (local framework file): `FRAMEWORK/reference/03-FIGURES_TEXT.md`
> - Canonical figures README, branch `general-agent`: https://github.com/IsaiahN/tether/blob/general-agent/figures/README.md
> - Figures and formula for direct download: https://github.com/IsaiahN/tether/tree/general-agent/figures
>
> **If you want to use the machinery:** the formula is in the figures link. Run it on a problem you know well and can check against a ground. Write down what would refute your result *before* you run it, because a loop cannot check the direction of its own question, and state the mode of what you get. A reported failure is more useful to me than a reported success.

## Two-stage (plus one) sequence

1. **Stage 1:** send the residual, the shape claim and the refuter. Ask what the recipient would count as a failure. Record their verdict **before** showing any framework material.
2. **Stage 2, opt in only:** if they are curious, offer the three links, with the note that the figures are the authority. Their later agreement is never pooled as corroboration, because a shared evidence pool makes agreement the pool talking (Fig 2 :40).
3. **Stage 3:** offer the formula as a generator others can reuse, with each user writing their own refuter and mode.

Nothing is sent by this agent. You host and send. Prefer a route where a recipient can decline contact, an opt-in submission or public channel rather than a pushed attachment.
