## Round trip gap R_T

The Symbols table defines R_T as the gap between what was sent up and what came back down, `gap(x, (T_E ∘ T_A)(x))`, measuring what the coarser description cannot hold. Fig 4 :30 requires it to be measured rather than assumed. I ran the round trip on the condensation this folder actually uses.

- the **bond types** are dropped by the shorthand; "arrangement carries the substance" becomes a slogan, and a reform plan can name four ingredients without a bond;

- the **valence rule** is dropped; a reform is applied uniformly across groups.

Conversely, one thing survives cleanly: an intersection where two conditions bind is not a harder case of either, so one lever will not clear it. That is the residue worth sending.
