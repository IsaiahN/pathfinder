# Expert verification packet: the Tether on cellular information routing

**What this is:** a claim in a form a cellular decision making researcher can check. Mode, derivation and refuters are stated before the conclusion, so a reader knows what to look for and what would settle it.

**Mode: `specified` on the routing claims, `general` on the structural shape, `gap` on the ligand list. Not `grounded`.** This is a shape and a route, not a biological result. The whole packet is a simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures, not a ground reading, and cannot settle the question it reports on.

## 1. The claim, in one paragraph

The residual that drives a cellular decision is indexed per signal channel, not pooled. Fig 1 :19 "The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated." The words ignore, retain and act are not three bins but a union of five overlapping routes: held with nothing owed, novel and parked, mis-attached and needing a re-fit, broken and needing new machinery, and refuted because a held regularity is false. A cell cannot acquire a sense for a molecule it has no instrument for, and a new instrument comes from a worse one already returning a reading. Retention crosses a scale boundary as a head start rather than a recording of one occasion. The anchor that settles the routing is fitness, which does not update on the cell's internal state. The framework returns this separator, not a list of ligands.

## 2. Three checkable sub-claims

### C1. Framework-internal, checkable by reading Figures 1, 5 and 9

- **Statement:** the three verbs are a union of five routes with per-slot residuals, not a partition, and two different routes can both look like "ignore".
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 1 :19 "The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated."; Fig 1 :22 "Aggregating across slots is how a live signal disappears into an average."; Fig 5 :11 "held: the model was right, nothing owed"; Fig 5 :53 "refuted: a term we hold is wrong; a term is owed that says so"; Fig 9 :55 "A bundled problem is usually a union of overlapping conditions, not a list of separate ones."
- **What to check:** is the five-way sort faithful to the figure text, and does the mapping from TRANSFERRED and REFUTED to "ignore" preserve the sign difference? If REFUTED is a negation and TRANSFERRED is merely a held term, they cannot be one bin.
- **Refuter:** exhibit a single criterion that sorts every signal of a given cell at one time into exactly one of the three bins with no overlap, or show that R is genuinely pooled across channels in the receptor cluster. Either breaks this sub-claim.

### C2. Biology-facing, checkable by a chemotaxis or receptor-evolution researcher

- **Statement:** the instrument law and the head-start rule describe how signal specificity and memory are actually acquired. New ligand specificity arises by duplication or modification of a receptor that already returns a reading; within one cell the methylation level is a working memory, and across generations the inherited receptor design is a head start, not a replay of one ligand encounter.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 6 :32 "An instrument is not built from a description. It is improved from a worse instrument already returning something."; Fig 4 :22 "Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down."; Fig 12 :119 "catalyst. A part that survives its own use. Spent and regenerating parts behave differently across uses, and nothing records which a term is."
- **What to check:** Gavira et al. 2020 report that the broad-range PctA receptor is ancestral and the narrow-range PctB and PctC arose from it by duplication, with the signaling domain more than 96 percent identical and the sensory domain less than 56 percent identical. Lan and Tu 2016 report ligand-specific methylation as a working memory and MWC cross-talk as its failure mode. Do those findings support the shape, or are they being read too loosely?
- **Refuter:** demonstrate a new ligand specificity with no precursor sensor that already returned a reading, or show that the exact recorded state of one ligand encounter, rather than a reusable receptor design, is what crosses a generation. Either breaks this sub-claim.

### C3. Framework-internal but empirically anchored, checkable by anyone who measures growth

- **Statement:** the anchor that separates the domains is fitness, which does not change when the cell's internal state changes. The habitat can be changed by the cell (chemotaxis, secretion), but the ground is not the habitat. "Decide" is a retrospective label at the lineage scale and a description of controller work at the cellular moment.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 2 :29 "An anchor is whatever settles the question and does not change its answer because of what any frame thinks."; Fig 11 :73 "The habitat is not the ground. The habitat is where actors come from and it updates; the ground is what settles, and it does not."; Fig 11 :36 "Where nothing maintains anything, intelligence is a label applied afterwards to a lineage that met its ground. Nothing was intelligent; selection ran. The word does no work there."; Fig 11 :39 "So the answerable question is not what intelligence is, but what makes a system capable. And capability is a property of agent-and-habitat, never of the agent alone."
- **What to check:** Pedraza et al. 2018 distinguish the amount of information from its fitness value, and Fulda 2017 concludes that bacteria are adaptive agents but not cognitive agents. Do those two claims, from biophysics and philosophy, support the two-scale reading without collapsing it into "cells do not decide" or "cells are rational agents"?
- **Refuter:** show two different routings that remain equally fit under every available measurement, or show that one mechanism does moment-to-moment routing and lineage selection at the same scale with the same anchor. Either breaks this sub-claim.

## 3. What is explicitly not claimed

- Not a ligand list, receptor name, concentration threshold or numerical allocation. The figures return a shape, not a name (Fig 9 :32).
- Not that a cell is a conscious or norm-responsive agent. Fulda 2017 is cited to refuse that reading, not to support it.
- Not that mutual information is the objective. It is a budget filter; fitness is the witness (Fig 9 :60).
- Not that a synthetic circuit demonstrates capability (Fig 11 :70).
- Not grounded. No measurement was taken for this packet.
- Not that the second seat is independent. It shares the primary evidence pool.

## 5. What would settle it

A source-discriminating instrument, not a further argument. From *The Hard Problem is About Source*: an instrument that reads green "cannot comment on the source"; the spectrometer resolved it. Here:

1. Perturb one ligand and test whether the response to a second ligand is independent, which discriminates per-slot from pooled residuals.
2. Find a single sorting criterion with no overlapping intersections, which would refuse the union reading.
3. Trace a new ligand specificity to a precursor sensor already returning a reading, which tests the instrument law.
4. Measure two different routings against growth rate, which tests the anchor.
5. Distinguish environmental states that produce the same methylation reading, which is the cell's analogue of the spectrometer.

## 6. What an expert can check in twenty minutes

1. Read Fig 1 :19, Fig 5 :11 and :53, Fig 9 :55, and Fig 6 :32. Five short passages.
2. Check C1: is REFUTED genuinely a different sign from TRANSFERRED in the figure text, so the two cannot share a bin?
3. Check C2 against your own field: did PctB and PctC arise from a broad-range precursor, and is receptor methylation a working memory? One line of literature is enough.
4. Check C3: is fitness an anchor in the figure's sense, and does the two-scale reading avoid both "cells do not decide" and "cells are rational agents"?
5. Name one measurement that would distinguish two routings, or say why none is possible.
6. Send the refutation. A failure is more useful than an endorsement.
