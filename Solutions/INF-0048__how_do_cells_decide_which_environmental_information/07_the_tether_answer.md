# The Tether's answer on cellular information routing

**Mode: `specified` on the per-domain routing claims, `general` on the structural shape, `gap` on the literal ligand list. Not `grounded`.** Simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures, not a ground reading (`05_what_shape_means.md` section 7). No cellular measurement was taken.

## 1. The residual it answers

A cell is in contact with many molecular signals. Each signal is a slot. The residual `R_s = |Γ(b,a) - o'|` is indexed per slot, never pooled into a single "how much information the cell keeps" scalar. Fig 1 :13 "a global R near zero with one live slot residual is a legal state." The question asks how each slot is routed: ignore, retain, or act.

The question is a union compound, not one dial. Fig 9 :55 "A bundled problem is usually a union of overlapping conditions, not a list of separate ones." The compound has its own loop and its own ground.

## 2. The answer, per domain, never averaged

**Domain 1, the cell-internal signaling frame.** The three verbs are five routes with overlapping conditions. Fig 5 :11 "held: the model was right, nothing owed"; Fig 5 :12 "new: extend perception, aim the probe here"; Fig 5 :13 "mis-attached: refit what you have, do not invent"; Fig 5 :14 "mechanism broken: a new term is owed"; Fig 5 :53 "refuted: a term we hold is wrong; a term is owed that says so". The first and last both look like "ignore" from outside and have opposite signs. Retention is the methylation working memory plus the re-fit; action is the mint plus the rebind. Because the conditions overlap, a single lever clears only the cases where one condition holds (Fig 9 :55). **Verdict: `specified`, triangulated** across Fig 1, Fig 5, Fig 9 and chemotaxis modelling (Lan and Tu 2016; Tu 2013).

**Domain 2, the habitat and contact frame.** R comes from contact with ligands, temperature, pH, other cells and phage. The habitat is enumerated, never composed (Fig 11 :43 "The habitat is enumerated, never composed."), and contact cascades beyond first order. The cell can introduce an action that changes its own contact, but it cannot subtract the habitat (Fig 11 :61 "The seat's power is asymmetric. Introduction is available; exclusion is not."). Ignoring a molecule means gating a channel, not removing the molecule from the world. Capability is a property of cell-and-habitat, never of the cell alone (Fig 11 :39). **Verdict: `specified`, triangulated** across Fig 11 and Perkins and Swain 2009.

**Domain 3, the lineage and selection frame.** Fig 11 :36 "Where nothing maintains anything, intelligence is a label applied afterwards to a lineage that met its ground. Nothing was intelligent; selection ran. The word does no work there." At this scale no seat maintains the ground; the receptor repertoire is what survived. At the cellular moment a controller exists and does work (Fig 11 :37). These are two scales, not one contradictory claim. The compiled prior crosses the scale membrane as a head start, not a recording of one occasion (Fig 4 :22 "Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down."). Fulda 2017 independently concludes that bacteria are adaptive agents but not cognitive agents. **Verdict: `specified`, triangulated** across Fig 4, Fig 10, Fig 11 and two low-overlap sources.

**Domain 4, the verification frame.** Agreement between readers is not a witness. Fig 2 :40 "disqualifying for a verdict: agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool." This simulated derivation and its reader would draw on overlapping pools, so the second seat in `11_audit.md` is internal consistency only. **Verdict: `general`, single-source on the framework rule.**

No verdict from one domain is transferred to another or averaged with it.

## 3. What follows for the three verbs

1. **Ignore** is not one operation. It is either TRANSFERRED (nothing owed), PARKED (novel but no reachable import), or REFUTED (a held regularity is false). Fig 5 :41 "Any one of them at zero stops the mint entirely." The third is a negation, not a bond (Fig 12 :112). Anticipatory responses in E. coli that disappear when the environmental association is broken are the REFUTED case (Perkins and Swain 2009).
2. **Retain** is either a working memory inside one cell, the receptor methylation level, or a head start delivered across a generation (LacY, Gal1p, chromatin marks, receptor gene duplication). Fig 4 :24 "downward: something that makes the next search cheaper. Never a replay of what worked once."
3. **Act** is a mint that pays its bargain: Fig 5 :43 "A new term is worth having when stating it, plus what remains unexplained after it, costs less than what was unexplained before." At the level of the cell the action is paid in growth rate and opportunity cost, not in bits (Fig 12 :125 "A composition costs in two currencies, and they do not add."). Under irreducible uncertainty the reachable response may be a phenotype distribution rather than a term: Pedraza et al. 2018 report that cells can enter persistence before the stress, and that a bet-hedging switching rate can beat sensing.

## 4. What it explicitly does not say

- Not a ligand list. Fig 9 :32 "you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself." The list is an empirical `gap`, to be filled by experiment for a named cell in a named habitat.
- Not a claim that a cell is a conscious decision maker. Fig 11 :36 governs the unmaintained lineage scale; Fig 11 :37 governs the maintained cellular moment. The word "decide" is retained only as a description of controller work.
- Not a claim that mutual information is what the cell maximises. Information amount is a budget filter; fitness value is the witness (Fig 9 :60).
- Not a claim that a synthetic circuit proves cellular capability. Fig 11 :70 "A test harness is a substituted habitat. Which is why a synthetic solve proves wiring and never capability."
- Not a derivation of one domain from another. The ground, not agreement, settles each.
- Not `grounded`. No measurement at any scale was taken here.

## 5. What would settle it, and what would reverse it

The ground settles it, not a further argument. The discriminating instruments are:

- **Per-slot discrimination:** perturb one ligand in a mixed receptor cluster and measure whether the response to a second ligand is independent. If it is not, F2 and the per-slot route fail. Lan and Tu 2016 provide the model and the experimental comparison.
- **Union discrimination:** find a single criterion that sorts a cell's signals into exactly one of three bins at one time, with no overlap. If it exists, the union reading fails.
- **Instrument discrimination:** trace a new ligand specificity to the duplication or modification of a sensor that already returned a reading. Gavira et al. 2020 did this for PctA to PctB and PctC. A case with no such precursor would refuse Fig 6 :32-34.
- **Anchor discrimination:** measure two different routings against growth rate. If no measurement distinguishes them, the anchor is absent.
- **Source discrimination:** distinguish environmental states that give the same receptor methylation reading. The local adaptation model is a step in this direction; a true spectrometer analogue for the cell is still owed.

Standing refuters from `01` section 4: pooled residual, partition criterion, sensor from description, no fitness discriminator, collapse of the scale separation, or a figure line naming a ligand. Any one fires the retraction of the corresponding finding.

## 6. The honest stop

**Unreached, not unreachable.** The figures return the separator and the discrimination criteria. They do not return a ligand list, and naming a ligand would mean already holding the measurement that would justify it (Fig 13 :135 "It cannot say which primitive is missing, because naming it would mean already holding it."). Fig 8 :39 "So a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence." The stop is recorded here so a reader can see what was given up: the specific biology of a named cell, the numerical thresholds, and the fitness weights are left to the ground. Indiscernibility is not identity.

## 7. One-paragraph version for outreach

> The Tether does not tell a cell which molecules to ignore, and it does not claim a conscious decision maker. It says that the residual has to stay indexed per signal channel, because averaging across channels throws away the only signal that says where to look; that the three words in the question are a union of five overlapping routes, where ignoring a novel signal, ignoring a held signal, and ignoring a signal that has just been proved false are different operations with opposite signs; that a new sense can only come from an instrument already returning a reading, duplicated or modified, never from a description; that retention is a head start handed down a generation, not a replay of one occasion; and that what settles the routing is fitness, which does not change when the cell changes its mind. The ligand list is a declared gap for experiment. What the framework offers is the shape any answer must have, four refuters written in advance, and the instruments that would close it.
