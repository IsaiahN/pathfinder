## QUESTION

Decide, in effect terms, what the Tether says about a cell's allocation of finite signaling machinery among many environmental signals. Option A: treat ignore, retain and act as three bins and answer with a rule that assigns each signal to one bin. Option B: treat the three verbs as a union of overlapping routes, keep verdicts per domain and per slot, and return a separator with a refuter while declaring the ligand list a `gap`. What each leads to: A produces a single threshold that leaves the intersections standing and cannot distinguish a held signal from a refuted one; B produces a checkable shape, five named routes, four pre-registered refuters, and an honest stop where experiment must take over.

## DOMAIN CENSUS

R by arity, symmetry and scale, then one line per domain with actors, currency and anchor, partition or union, and order by dependency.

- **R (the residual).** The per-channel gap between prediction and observation, `R_s = |Γ(b,a) - o'|`. Arity: many slots, one per signal channel, so there is no single "the environment". Symmetry: the three verbs are ordered, not symmetric; a sequence and a production cannot be read backwards, and valence is relative to the residual rather than carried by the molecule. Scale: molecular, cellular and lineage, with a lossy transform between them. Fig 9 names the question a union compound: a case in two conditions is not a harder case of either.
- **Domain 1, the cell-internal signaling frame.** Actors: chemoreceptors, CheA, CheW, CheY, CheZ, CheR, CheB, transcription factors, methylation level, receptor occupancy. Currency: concentration, binding free energy, bits. Anchor: the actual local ligand concentration and the growth-rate ground. Question type: an attempted partition of R by kind that resolves to a union, because retention and action overlap in one response.
- **Domain 2, the habitat and contact frame.** Actors: ligands, nutrients, toxins, temperature, pH, quorum signals, phage, neighbouring cells. Currency: molecular flux and time. Anchor: the physical environment, which does not update on the cell's belief. Question type: enumeration, not composition; contact cascades beyond first order.
- **Domain 3, the lineage and selection frame.** Actors: lineages, mutation, duplication, horizontal transfer, selection. Currency: fitness. Anchor: reproductive success, which is not a frame. Question type: a union of unmaintained, maintained and isolated modes.
- **Domain 4, the verification frame.** Actors: expert readers and reviewers. Currency: agreement and residuals. Anchor: the ground, never agreement. Question type: checking arrangement. In this simulated run all four domains draw on overlapping evidence pools, so independence is low.
- **Order by dependency.** Habitat contact supplies R; perception and vocabulary route it and state an objective; selection pays for the receptor repertoire across generations; verification checks last. Refuse before mint, per Formula step 2. Describe the residual before naming a frame (Fig 9 :27-28).

Strain test applied: Fig 11 :36 was first read as a claim that cells do not decide. Re-census assigns it to the unmaintained lineage scale and assigns Fig 11 :37 to the maintained cellular moment. Different actors, currency and anchor, so no strain after re-census. Fig 13's six-term substrate was refused as a mapping onto receptor families; it governs physical states and structures, not signal-transduction proteins.

## WORLD (secondary sources read)

World sources corroborate only; they never overrule. Overlap is declared.

- **Biophysics / statistical physics.** Ganhui Lan and Yuhai Tu, "Information Processing in Bacteria: Memory, Computation, and Statistical Physics: a Key Issues Review", Reports on Progress in Physics 79(5):052601, 2016, opened in full: "when one type of ligand is saturated, all receptors in the MWC cluster will be methylated to the highest methylation level" and the cluster loses the ability to respond to a second ligand; the local adaptation model restores ligand-specific memory. Predicts pooled-reading failure and its repair. Corroborates Domain 1 and the instrument law. Overlap: low with the framework, but Tu co-authors the 2013 review below, so the two physics rows are partly one pool.
- **Biophysics / modelling.** Yuhai Tu, "Quantitative modeling of bacterial chemotaxis: Signal amplification and accurate adaptation", Annual Review of Biophysics 42:337-359, 2013, opened in full: the adapted activity is independent of background concentration, and at low frequency the cell computes the time derivative of the signal. Predicts that a single activity reading cannot name the source environmental state; corroborates the memory-before-gradient order and the metamerism point. Overlap: partially shared with the Lan and Tu row.
- **Systems biology.** Theodore J Perkins and Peter S Swain, "Strategies for cellular decision-making", Molecular Systems Biology 5, MSB200983, 2009, opened in full: "the cellular decision-making can only be probabilistic and occurs at three levels", including inference of the probable environment, weighing costs and benefits, and deciding among competing decision-makers. Reports temperature predicting oxygen and the loss of that anticipation when the association is broken, plus generational LacY and Gal1p memory and Dekel and Alon's growth-rate measurements. Predicts REFUTED, head-start retention and a measurable objective. Corroborates Domains 1, 2 and 3. Overlap: low with the framework; medium with the biophysics rows through shared chemotaxis literature.
- **Biophysics / information and fitness.** Juan M Pedraza, David A Garcia and Muriel F Perez-Ortiz, "Noise, Information and Fitness in Changing Environments", Frontiers in Physics 6:83, 2018, opened in full: "every bit of information is in principle equally valuable in abstract communication channels, but for an organism some bits are more valuable than others"; also "it might be better for a cell not to try to determine precisely what environment it is facing but randomly switch between possible responses". Predicts a fitness-weighted anchor and bet hedging as a route when no term can be composed. Corroborates Domains 1 and 3. Overlap: low with the framework; it cites the same chemotaxis literature as the systems-biology row.
- **Philosophy of cognition.** Fermin C Fulda, "Natural Agency: The Case of Bacterial Cognition", Journal of the American Philosophical Association 3(1):69-90, 2017, abstract and reference list opened, full text paywalled: "Bacteria, I conclude, are adaptive agents, hence not mere automata, but not cognitive agents." Predicts that the word "decide" must be narrowed to controller work and retrospective selection. Corroborates Domain 3. Overlap: genuinely low, different field and method.
- **Molecular evolution.** Jose Antonio Gavira, Vadim M Gumerov, Miriam Rico-Jimenez and colleagues, "How Bacterial Chemoreceptors Evolve Novel Ligand Specificities", mBio 11(1):e03066-19, 2020, opened in full: "two chemoreceptors with a narrow ligand range have evolved from an ancestral protein with a broad chemoeffector spectrum"; the signaling domain is more than 96 percent identical while the sensory domain is less than 56 percent. Predicts the instrument law and the functional-group reading. Corroborates Domain 3 and the supporting derivation. Overlap: low, different field.

## VERDICT (from the figures)

The shape survives; the three-bin reading does not. The residual is indexed per signal channel, not pooled. Ignore, retain and act form a union of five overlapping routes, with TRANSFERRED and REFUTED both looking like "ignore" from outside but having opposite signs. New sensing comes only from an instrument already returning a reading, duplicated or modified; recognition of a new ligand is an import with provenance, not a sensor specified from a description. Retention is a head start across a scale membrane, not a replay of one occasion. The anchor is fitness, which does not update on the cell's internal state, and capability belongs to cell-and-habitat rather than to the cell alone. At the lineage scale "decide" is a retrospective label; at the cellular moment it names controller work. The literal ligand list is a declared `gap`, unreachable at this budget rather than underivable in principle. Mode `specified` on the routing claims, `general` on the structural shape, not `grounded`.

- **cost if wrong:** bounded and locatable. The packet states mode, three checkable sub-claims and four refuters before the conclusion, so an error is findable rather than invisible. The avoidable cost is spending an outside reader's independence by sending the framework interior first, which Fig 2 :42 prices: "Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong." Lead with the separator and the refuter. A second cost is presenting a simulated derivation as a biological result, which would mislead a reader about what has been measured.
- **undoable:** yes. The three-bin reading, the pooled residual and the conscious-agent reading are removable without touching the separator. `07_the_tether_answer.md` and `08_expert_verification_packet.md` already remove them. The ligand gap can be filled by experiment without withdrawing any figure claim, because no figure line names a ligand.
- **reversed by:** a ground reading, not agreement. A per-channel perturbation showing genuinely pooled residuals would break the indexing claim; a single sorting criterion with no intersections would break the union claim; a new ligand specificity with no precursor sensor would break the instrument law; two routings indistinguishable by growth rate would break the anchor; a figure line naming a ligand would break the shape limit. Until one of these fires, the verdict stands as `candidate`.
