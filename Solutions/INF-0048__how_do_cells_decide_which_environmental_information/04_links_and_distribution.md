## Distribution rules: offer, do not push

Figure-grounded reasons:

- Fig 10 :52 "Bring a characterised residual, never the source." Sending the full architecture before a reader has described a residual sends a recording, not a generator. Lead with the separator and the refuter; offer the links after the reader's verdict on stage 1 is recorded.
- Fig 10 :42 "A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it." A recipient who has absorbed the framework before being asked for a verdict has joined the frame being checked.
- Fig 9 :27-28 "describe it before you go looking, or any frame you pick will seem to fit." Ask what the reader would expect to see if the separator were true before giving them the figures.
- Fig 2 :35 "Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you." Do not ask for agreement.
- Fig 2 :40 "disqualifying for a verdict: agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool." Do not select recipients for receptiveness, and do not pool later agreement as corroboration.
- Fig 2 :42 "Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong." The cost here is moderate: a wrong routing claim can waste a reader's time but does not move a patient. One careful reader is worth more than a list.

**Recommended order in a reply:** (1) the separator and its four refuters; (2) ask which route the reader would assign to a named signal and what they would count as a failure; (3) record that verdict; (4) only then the three article links and the local figure extract, with the note that the figures, not the summary, are the authority.

## The two-stage offer, and the optional third stage

Stage 1: validate the output against the ground. The output is the per-slot union separator, not the framework. The reader checks it without holding the framework's interior.

Stage 2, opt in only: if curious, read the sources that produced it. Their agreement with the framework after seeing the interior is not corroboration and is never pooled as such (Fig 2 :40).

Stage 3: offer the formula as a method to reuse. This sends a generator, which is permitted by Fig 4 :22 "Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down." Each user must still name a ground and write a refuter before running it, because a frame cannot check the direction of its own question.

**Hard precondition:** stages 1 to 3 only work if the stage-1 output survives. It is currently a simulated derivation, so it is offered as a checkable shape, never as a settled biological result.
