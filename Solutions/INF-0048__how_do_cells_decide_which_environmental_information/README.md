## What an answer would look like

Restated as an effect: a cell is in contact with many molecular signals at once. Each signal is a slot. The cell holds an internal state (receptor occupancy, methylation level, transcription factor concentration) and takes actions (tumble, express an operon, divide, enter a persister state). The residual is `R_s = |Γ(b,a) - o'|` per slot, not one global number. An answer is a separator which, for each slot, states which route the residual takes and what would have to be true for that route to change. The sought-for shape is bound as an ordinary term before the run, per Formula step "Direction", so it can accrue a residual and be refused.

The question's three verbs are not a partition. They are a union of overlapping conditions with different anchors, and one lever leaves the intersections standing (Fig 9 :54-57). The verdict is therefore per domain and per slot, never averaged.

## What is deliberately not here

- No personal emails of named individuals.
- No ranking of people by how likely they are to agree.
- No bulk-send plan.
- No claim that this derivation has settled which signals a real cell ignores.

Reasons, from the figures: Fig 2 :35 "Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you."; Fig 2 :40 "disqualifying for a verdict: agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool"; Fig 10 :42 "A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it." Selecting readers for receptiveness manufactures the correlation that makes their agreement worthless, and sending the framework interior before asking for a verdict spends the only thing an outside reader contributes.
