## Independent domain census, written from the residual line alone

I redid the census using the WATSON order before reading the primary's conclusions. The residual line given to me was only: *a cell must allocate finite signaling machinery among many environmental signals, routing each signal as ignore, retain, or act, against a fitness ground.*

- **R (arity / symmetry / scale).** Arity is many: one gap per signal channel, so this cannot be a single scalar decision. Symmetry: the verbs are ordered (a production is not reversible, a negation is not a bond), and the valence of a signal is relative to the residual, not carried by the molecule. Scale: molecular, cellular, lineage, with a lossy transform between them. Question type: union compound, because at least two of the three verbs can be live in the same response (methylation memory and tumbling occur together in chemotaxis).
- **Domain 1, signaling frame.** Actors: receptors, methylation and demethylation enzymes, response regulator, motor, transcription factors. Currency: concentration and binding energy. Anchor: the true local ligand concentration and the growth-rate ground. Question type: attempted partition of R by kind that resolves to a union because the bins overlap.
- **Domain 2, habitat.** Actors: ligands, repellents, temperature, pH, quorum molecules, phage, neighbours. Currency: molecular flux. Anchor: the physical environment, which does not update on the cell's state. Question type: enumeration, not composition, with cascading contact.
- **Domain 3, lineage.** Actors: lineages, duplication, horizontal transfer, selection. Currency: fitness. Anchor: reproductive success. Question type: union of unmaintained, maintained and isolated modes.
- **Domain 4, verification.** Actors: readers and reviewers. Currency: agreement and residuals. Anchor: the ground, never agreement. Independence is near zero in this simulated run.

Order by dependency: habitat supplies R; perception and vocabulary route it and state an objective; selection pays for the repertoire across generations; verification checks last. Refuse before mint; describe the residual before choosing a frame.

My census matches the primary's four domains and its union reading. ## Round trip gap R_T

The Symbols table defines R_T as the gap between what was sent up and what came back down, `gap(x, (T_E ∘ T_A)(x))`, measuring what the coarser description cannot hold. Fig 4 :30 requires it to be measured rather than assumed. I ran the round trip on the condensation this folder actually uses.

2. **Abstraction (T_A):** the shorthand carried in `07` and `08`: five routes per channel, fitness anchor, head start across membrane, receptor from prior receptor, ligand list withheld.
3. **Concretisation (T_E):** the empirical reading: E. coli chemotaxis with ligand-specific methylation, cross-talk as pooled reading, PctA to PctB/C duplication, bet hedging, anticipatory cue loss.

**R_T is nonzero.** Four things the shorthand holds do not survive the trip back down:

- the **identity of the five routes in a named species**, which the shorthand labels but does not measure; the biology supplies only some of them, and the mapping from "parked" to persistence is an interpretation, not a derived term;
- the **fitness value of a bit**, which the shorthand treats as a single anchor but which Pedraza et al. show is environment-specific and measured through long-term average growth, not instantaneous fitness;
- the **source-discriminating instrument**, which the shorthand names as an obligation but which does not exist for the general case of reading environmental history from methylation state;
- the **gap itself**, which a shorthand that says "five routes" tends to erase, because a count looks like an answer.

One thing survives cleanly: per-slot indexing and the union of overlapping conditions, with a fitness ground that does not update. That is the residue worth sending. R_T was **not** measured numerically, because no ground reading was taken; it is recorded qualitatively as the enumerated set of dropped constraints. Treating the formula as identical to the figure set would set R_T to zero and would be the dead-reckoning error Fig 4 :30 names.
