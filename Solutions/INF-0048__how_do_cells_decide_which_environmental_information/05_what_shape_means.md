## 1. A shape is a constraint, not a filler

The canonical usage rule states the limit: the framework returns "what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)". Fig 9 :32 "you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself." Fig 13 :135 "It cannot say which primitive is missing, because naming it would mean already holding it."

From *The Library of Babel*, opened this session: "You can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."

So a **shape** is the narrowed form of an answer you can state before you can name it. For this question the shape is: a per-channel residual routed through five overlapping conditions, anchored to a fitness ground that does not update, with new sensors possible only by improving an instrument that already returns something. The name, which the framework does not supply, would be: the exact ligands this cell ignores, keeps and acts on, and the exact molecular implementation in this species.

A compact way to say it: "five routes per channel, not three bins" is a shape. "E. coli ignores indole and acts on aspartate at 1 mM" is a name, and it is a name the framework cannot print.

## 2. How a shape is obtained

- *The Library of Babel*, opened this session: use the vocabulary you already have that is closest to the thing, look for recurring new terms that overlap or correlate with it, and recurse. "With each new term gained, you narrow the space by definition and deepen your vocabulary." The same essay warns that a bundle masquerading as one question must be split: "Perhaps your problem was a bundle or conflation of several problems masquerading as one?"
- Fig 6 :30 "outward: does another frame already hold it? The description travels. Fails by being sharpened into terms only we can read. Cast it in effect terms." Fig 9 :30-31 "your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect rather than the cause."
- *Decoding Tacit Knowledge*, opened this session: "When contact isn't possible, the best you can send is a plain description of what you'd expect to see, because anyone can check that against the world." This is why the outreach draft in `02` describes what a pooled residual would look like in an experiment rather than sending the framework first.
- *The Library of Babel* on stopping: "You will know that you are done because the finish line will also be the starting place, as there is nowhere else to search and nothing else that can describe the answer better." For this question that stop is not reached: the ligand list is empirical and unmeasured here.

For this question, bootstrapping by proximity worked as follows. The ordinary words in the question, ignore, retain, act, were treated as a candidate vocabulary. The figure text supplied closer terms: held, novel, mis-attached, mechanism broken, refuted (Fig 5 :11-14, :53). Those terms narrowed the space from three bins to five routes, and the biological literature supplied the closer terms "working memory", "cross-talk", "local adaptation", "bet hedging" and "functional group". Each new term narrowed the space without naming a ligand.

## 3. What licenses a name

Only two things, both outside the shape itself.

1. **The ground settles it.** `THE_FORMULA.md` step 5: "SETTLE the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays. A gate is not the ground." For a cell, the paying quantity is growth rate or reproductive success. Fig 5 :70 "A term is settled when the ground pays for it."
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs**, with a source gate and a shadow test (Fig 6 :12 "source gate: provenance"; Fig 6 :13 "shadow test: explains a gap"). A horizontal transfer of a receptor gene is such an import; Fig 6 :25 "An import adds no atom to the world." Fig 8 :51 "Convergent derivation and adopted import look identical in the contents. Only the record of where each came from separates them, and the surplus lives only where the closures"

From the carried mapping of *They're Scanning Books When They Should* (not reopened this session, so quoted only through the exemplar's record): "The real import is not a new term in the existing space. It is a new dimension of the space." And: "Declaring the primitives does not make them neutral. It makes the choice visible." The six-term substrate is a visible guess, not nature's own list; turning a guess into a named answer for a cell would hide the choice.

## 4. The source problem: a reading cannot name its source

*The Hard Problem is About Source*, opened this session, gives the mechanism that matters most here. An instrument reads green and "cannot comment on the source". The source might be one green light, or yellow plus blue, or RGB, or CMYK, or a scheme not yet discovered. The essay's statement: "If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information." And: "An instrument that can't distinguish these cases demonstrates its own inadequacy." It was the spectrometer, not an argument, that resolved the case.

For this question the metamerism problem is literal. A receptor methylation level is a reading of past ligand concentration, but several environmental histories can produce the same methylation state. A dose-response curve is a reading of receptor occupancy, but several combinations of ligand concentration and receptor number can produce the same curve. Lan and Tu 2016 make the same point in biological terms: in the strongly coupled MWC model, different ligands collapse onto the same methylation trajectory, which is "memory contamination", and the local adaptation model is the better instrument that discriminates sources. Tu 2013 adds that the adapted activity is independent of background concentration, which is exactly why the activity reading alone cannot name the environment. Naming the environmental state from the reading without a discriminator is the error the essay names.

What is owed is a better instrument, not a name. In figure terms: Fig 6 :32 "An instrument is not built from a description. It is improved from a worse instrument already returning something." The local adaptation model and the ligand-specific receptor are better instruments built from the worse one that already returned a reading.

## 5. Answered question: what licenses a particular ligand list?

Nothing in the framework. The framework returns a separator. A ligand list is licensed only by the ground settling it for a named cell in a named habitat, or by a provenanced import with a shadow test. Until one of those, the list is the experimenter's contribution and must be recorded as such.

Pedraza et al. 2018 sharpen this in fitness terms: "every bit of information is in principle equally valuable in abstract communication channels, but for an organism some bits are more valuable than others." So even a complete catalogue of what a cell senses would not tell you which of those channels it should act on. The value of a bit is fitness-weighted, and that weight is external to the reading. This is Fig 9 :60 "Use filters for the budget and witnesses for the verdict. Never let a filter hand you a verdict." Mutual information is a budget filter; the fitness ground is the witness.

## 6. Correction to my own section 3 and 4

I initially wrote "the shape, not a name" as if it forbade naming any receptor family or molecular mechanism for a cell. That overstates it. The canonical rule reads "what a **missing** thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)". Its subject is a primitive not held in Γ. Naming CheR, CheB, methylation, LacY or the dCache receptor family is a binding from the biology frame onto an already available mechanism, not the invention of a new primitive. What remains forbidden is inventing a seventh substrate member, or claiming that such a binding moves the wall (Fig 6 :25 "An import adds no atom to the world.").

My section 4 metamerism point is **not** retracted. It still holds that a reading cannot name its source without a discriminator. The reason is the missing instrument, not a ban on naming held mechanisms. Both claims are recorded so neither is quietly lost.

A second correction, kept visible: I first read Fig 11 :36 ("Nothing was intelligent; selection ran") as a claim that cells do not decide at all. Re-census assigns :36 to the unmaintained lineage scale and :37-39 to the maintained cellular moment. The word "decide" is a retrospective label at one scale and a description of controller work at the other. Both stand.

## 7. Prestige is not action, and this derivation is a simulation

*The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*, opened this session: "Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it." A request to a well known researcher to endorse the claim would be the named collapse, not corroboration. The legitimate ask is for a check against the ground.

Same essay: LLMs "can quickly simulate an approximate answer/output with the formula found in the architecture post", but "the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself." Therefore every substantive file in this folder, including this one, is a simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures. It is not a test of the claim. It cannot settle anything it reports on, and it must be labelled as such wherever it is sent.

## 8. What the shape is, in one list

For auditability, the shape claimed by this solution is exactly:

1. R is indexed per signal channel; aggregation across channels destroys the only signal that says where to look (Fig 1 :19, :22).
2. The three verbs are a union of five routes with overlapping conditions (Fig 9 :54-57; Fig 5 :11-14, :53).
3. The fitness anchor does not update on the cell's belief (Fig 2 :29; Fig 11 :73).
4. New sensing comes only from an instrument already returning something (Fig 6 :32-34).
5. Retention is a head start across a boundary, not a replay of one occasion (Fig 4 :22-25).
6. The ligand list is a `gap` that experiment must fill (Fig 9 :32; Fig 13 :134-135).
