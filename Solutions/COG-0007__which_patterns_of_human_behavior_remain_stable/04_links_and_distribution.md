## Wider literature that bears on this question

1. **The field's own scope statement.** Cultural Evolution Society: culture is information transmitted by social learning, and evolution is variation, inheritance and selection applied to it. Its chid/chided example is a concrete case of a stable mechanism with a changing form over 200 years, which is exactly the kernel/cast distinction at a single linguistic slot.
2. **Historical psychology.** Elias's *The Civilizing Process* is the canonical case for behavior changing with social conditions: shifting thresholds of shame and repugnance over roughly 800 to 1900 AD. It supplies the changing cast, not the kernel.
3. **Universals and their interpretation.** Brown's list of cultural universals supplies candidate stable patterns, while the non-nativist caution supplies the reason a universal reading is not a source reading. Same behavior, different possible mechanism.
4. **Cross-cultural sampling.** The Henrich volume and its reviews supply the independence lesson: a broad claim built on a narrow evidence pool is one witness, not many. The Grosjean critique shows a specific historical binding being disputed by dated evidence, which is the correct way to move an item between bins.
5. **Animal culture.** Whiten's chimpanzee comparisons and the broader animal-culture literature supply a low-overlap field: stability and change of behavioral traditions without human language. Convergence here is corroboration at higher independence, though it still cannot name a human universal.

None of these sources overrides a figure line. They corroborate or conflict at the world level only.

## Distribution rules: offer, do not push

Figure-grounded reasons, all checked against the verbatim text:

- **Fig 4 :22-25**: "Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down." A recording carried upward looks like knowledge and is a description of one occasion. Sending a finished list before anyone has described a residual sends a recording.
- **Fig 10 :42**: "A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it." Seat 4 must not hold the interiors. A recipient who has absorbed the whole framework before being asked for a verdict has joined the frame being checked.
- **Fig 9 :27-28**: "describe it before you go looking, or any frame you pick will seem to fit." Ask what they would expect to see if the shape claim were true before giving them the literature.
- **Seat Map :163**: "High correlation is disqualifying for a verdict and efficient for a convention." A send selected for people likely to agree produces a convention, not corroboration.
- **Fig 2 :40**: "agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool." A reply from someone who reads the same three essays is not a second witness.

**Recommended order in a reply:** (1) the shape claim and its four refuters; (2) ask what they would count as a failure and record the answer; (3) only then the three links, with the note that the figures, not the summary, are the authority; (4) offer the method for reuse, with the instruction that each user writes their own refuter and states their own mode.

## The two-stage offer

**Stage 1.** Validate the output against the ground: the invariant kernel, the contact-varying cast, and the union reading. No theory is shown. The verdict is recorded before stage 2.

**Stage 2, opt-in only.** If they are curious, offer the figure text, the field's own scope statement and the Library of Babel essay. Their later agreement is never pooled as corroboration, because once they hold the interiors their agreement is the pool talking.

**Stage 3.** Offer the loop as a method to reuse. This is permitted, since a generator can be given away without giving away an answer. Each user still needs a ground and a pre-registered refuter for their own problem.

**Hard precondition:** stages 1 to 3 work only if the stage-1 claim survives. The falsifiers in `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` §4 are the test. If the first or third fires, stop and withdraw the corresponding claim before any distribution.
