## 1. A shape is a constraint, not a filler

The canonical usage rule states the limit: the framework returns "what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)." Fig 9 :32: "you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself." Fig 13 supplies the same limit from the closed-set side: it can say the answer is out of reach with the library as it stands, and it cannot say which primitive is missing, because naming it would mean already holding it.

From the Library of Babel essay, opened this session: "This means that you can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."

So a **shape** is the narrowed form of an answer you can state before you can name the items. For this question the shape is: an invariant kernel, a contact-varying cast, a union rather than a partition, and a per-slot instrument. A table of "these twenty behaviors are universal" is a name, not a shape.

## 2. How the shape is obtained

- **Proximity and recursion.** Library of Babel: "With each new term gained, you are able to narrow the space by definition and deepen your vocabulary." Applied here, begin with the closest terms the field already has (universals, cultural variation, social learning, transmission bias) and let the figure lines narrow them.
- **Describe the effect, not the cause.** Fig 6 :30: "The description travels. Fails by being sharpened into terms only we can read. Cast it in effect terms." Fig 9 :30-31: "your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect rather than the cause." So state "stability is a contact relation" rather than "gene X causes universal Y."
- **A bundle must be split first.** Library of Babel: "Perhaps your problem was a bundle or conflation of several problems masquerading as one? You will need to search all of those problems individually." Fig 9 :54 gives the same instruction as a figure line. This is the single most useful essay line for COG-0007, because the question is exactly such a bundle.
- **Stopping.** Library of Babel: "you will know that you are done because the finish line will also be the starting place." For this question, that means the shape stops when further study keeps returning the same kernel and the same union structure without naming new slots.

## 3. What licenses a name

Two things, both outside the shape itself:

1. **The ground settles it.** `THE_FORMULA.md` step 5: "the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays. A gate is not the ground." For this question, a source-discriminating instrument must pay for a specific binding.
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs**, with a source gate and a shadow test (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8 :50-52). A finding from animal culture or historical psychology can be imported, but only with the date, the frame and the gap it explains recorded.

The Hard Problem is About Source gives the mechanism for why a reading is not a name. An instrument that reads green "cannot comment on the source." The same behavioral reading can come from shared biology, from a common environment, or from independent invention. Cultural universal (Wikipedia), opened this session, states the field-side version: "The observation of the same or similar behavior in different cultures does not prove that they are the results of a common underlying psychological mechanism."

So the list the question asks for is a metamerism error. It names a source from a reading without an instrument that discriminates among sources. What is owed is the instrument.

## 4. Two consequences for this question

**Prestige is not action.** The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap, opened this session: "Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it. The Ground Maintainer must be blind to prestige." A list of famous scholars who agree that a pattern is universal is the named collapse, not corroboration. The legitimate ask is to give the shape a budget to be tried and let an instrument settle it.

**This audit is a simulation, not a ground reading.** Same essay: "LLMs can quickly simulate an approximate answer/output with the formula found in the architecture post... However, the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself." So `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` is a simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures. It is not a test of any hypothesis about human behavior and must be labelled as such.

## 5. A correction to the obvious reading

The canonical phrase is "what a **missing** thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)." It governs a primitive not held in Γ. It does not forbid using terms the framework already holds, such as the loop, the kernel, the comparison bond or the functional group. So saying "stability is a contact relation" is legitimate figure-internal work, not a forbidden naming.

What remains forbidden here is naming **which** specific behavior occupies a stable slot on the strength of a cross-cultural reading alone. That is a binding from the world frame onto a held distinction, and it must be provenanced as such. Both claims are now recorded so neither is quietly lost.

## 6. What would settle the shape into a list

The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap, opened this session, contains a line unusually close to this exact question: "Humans in the 1800s had different values, customs, and cultures than humans of today. What does it mean to align an AI to human values when human values are a moving target? You cannot align to something that changes." The essay's answer is to tie to what does not change with culture: priors and primitives, grown under ground contact. That is the same shape this solution derives from the figures, arrived at from a different starting point. It is corroboration from a different route within the same author's work, so it raises confidence in the shape but is not an independent witness.

To settle the empirical list itself would need: (i) an instrument that discriminates among the sources of a behavioral reading, (ii) at least two frames with non-overlapping evidence pools, and (iii) a per-slot residual that persists and does not trend down. None of the three was available at this budget. The answer stops at the shape and records the lineage.
