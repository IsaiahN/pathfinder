# The Tether's answer: stable and changing patterns of human behavior

**Mode: `general` on the structural shape, `specified` on the domain claims, not `grounded`.** This is a shape and a route, not a list. Simulated derivation checked against the figures (`05_what_shape_means.md` §4), not a ground reading.

## 1. The residual it answers

We are asked to sort behavior patterns into those stable across historical periods and those that change when social conditions change. The hidden residual is the missing rule for what makes a pattern persist when its contact changes. The question's grammar hides a second operand and invites an intrinsic-kind answer. Fig 11 :49 refuses that: "An actor's valence is relative to the residual, not a property it carries."

## 2. The shape

1. **Stability is a contact relation, not a property of a pattern.** Fig 11 :51: "No standing list of good actors survives a change of residual." A behavior pattern carries no stability into every habitat. This is `general` and triangulated (Fig 11 :49-51 with Fig 4 :19-20).
2. **The real partition is kernel versus cast.** Fig 4 :19-20: "The kernel is the part that does not change with size: the same loop and the same rule for naming what worked, whether one agent is running it or a thousand. Only the actors, the currency and the anchor change." What stays stable is the loop and the naming rule. What changes is the cast. `general` on the distinction, `specified` on its application to human societies.
3. **The set is closed, the arrangements are not.** Fig 13 :115: "The set is closed. The arrangements are not. That gap is why import exists." Recurring patterns are functional groups; changing patterns are isomers over the same atoms (Fig 12 :124, :117). `specified`, triangulated across Fig 12 and Fig 13.
4. **The question is a union, not a partition.** Fig 9 :4-5: "First: is there a fact here? Disagreements that do not shrink with effort, where each rule keeps working well on a different subset, are not one hard question but several well-formed ones. Split it rather than search." Fig 9 :54: "And when the split lands on a union rather than a partition, unbundle before you search again." A pattern can be stable on one axis and changing on another. `specified`, triangulated with Seat Map :211.
5. **Change arrives by import, not by inner search.** Fig 6 :23: "No frame escapes its own closure. The bound moves only on contact with a frame whose closure differs." A social condition changes a repertoire by changing contact and importing from a differing closure. `specified`, triangulated across Fig 6 and Fig 8.
6. **The answer is a shape plus an instrument, not a list.** Fig 9 :32: "you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself." A null result is not proof of absence (Fig 8 :39), so the empirical list is `gap`. `general`, triangulated with the canonical usage rule.

## 3. What it explicitly does not say

- Not a table of universals and a table of variables. That is the union error the figures forbid.
- Not that no behavior is stable. The kernel is stable; the claim is about where stability lives, not whether it exists.
- Not that culture is free-floating. Capability is a property of agent and habitat (Fig 11 :39), so a behavior changes when its contact changes.
- Not a final list. There is no last room (Fig 7 :6) and no last route (Fig 13 :90).
- Not grounded. No instrument discriminating among sources of a behavioral reading was run.
- Not an endorsement of any particular scholar's universal list. Such a list is a binding with provenance, not a derivation.

## 4. What would settle it: the ground

A source-discriminating instrument, not a further argument. The Hard Problem is About Source: a color instrument "cannot comment on the source," and the spectrometer, not an argument, resolved the ambiguity. Here the corresponding needs are:

- **Source discrimination:** for a behavior that recurs across periods, does the instrument distinguish shared biology from a shared environment from independent invention? If not, it has a reading and not a source.
- **Two independent frames:** Fig 2 :31, "Independence, between two frames, is how little they draw on the same evidence." Two studies drawing on the same narrow set of societies are one witness. The field's own WEIRD critique is the standing warning.
- **Per-slot residuals:** Fig 1 :19, "The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated." A single universality score destroys the signal that says which pattern moved.
- **The standing refuters:** an intrinsic-stability case; a society with no loop or naming rule; a genuine partition under a single condition; a framework that names an unheld primitive. Any one moves the corresponding claim.

## 5. The honest stop

**Unreached, not unreachable.** Fig 8 :39: "So a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence." Underivable in principle and unreachable at this budget are different facts. This run had no source-discriminating instrument and no second independent evidence pool. It therefore reports the structure and marks the empirical list `gap`. The lineage is recorded so a later reader can locate the error rather than only feel it. Prestige or agreement is not an instrument: "Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action."

## 6. One-paragraph version for outreach

> The Tether does not deliver a list of universal behaviors and a list of variable ones. It says that stability is not carried by a behavior pattern; it is a relation between the pattern and the habitat that contacts it. What stays stable is the kernel, the loop and the rule for naming what worked; what changes is the cast, the actors, the currency and the anchor. Recurring patterns are functional groups with changing qualifiers, and change enters a repertoire through import from a different closure, not through more search inside it. The stable/changing question is a union of overlapping conditions, so it must be unbundled before any list is attempted. What the framework returns is that shape, a set of refuters written in advance, and the instrument owed for each slot: a source-discriminating measurement with genuine independence between frames. The empirical list stays open until that instrument pays.
