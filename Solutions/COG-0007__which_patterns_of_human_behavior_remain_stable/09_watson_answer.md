## QUESTION

Decide, in effect terms, what the Tether says about the claim that some patterns of human behavior remain stable across historical periods while others change when social conditions change. Option A: treat stability as intrinsic to a pattern and deliver two lists. Option B: keep the figure-derived shape, treat stability as a contact relation, unbundle the union, and refuse the list. What each leads to: A delivers a verdict in `general` mode with a hidden second operand and a false partition, and it spends the independence of the people checking it; B delivers a checkable shape, refuters written in advance, and an open empirical slot until a source-discriminating instrument settles it.

## DOMAIN CENSUS

R by arity, symmetry and scale, then one line per domain giving actors, currency and anchor, partition or union, and order by dependency.

- **R (the residual).** The missing rule for what makes a behavior pattern persist when its contact changes. Arity: two operands, a pattern and a condition, so stability is a binary relation rather than a monadic property. Symmetry: ordered and comparative, a persistence threshold over time, which is the comparison bond's shape, not a symmetric conjunction. Scale: three, individual, population and historical, with a lossy transform between each. Fig 9 names the question a union compound: a case in two conditions is not a harder case of either.
- **Domain 1, the individual learning loop.** Actors: one agent, its library Γ, its bets and actions. Currency: per-slot residual R, priced in bits and actions. Anchor: the perceptual or experimental ground. Nameable: yes.
- **Domain 2, population and transmission.** Actors: many agents, a shared library, and something that ranks what they produce. Currency: methods crossing up, head starts crossing down. Anchor: ground-visible reproduction, that is whether a method survives renewed contact. Nameable: yes.
- **Domain 3, the historical habitat.** Actors: conditions such as institutions, subsistence, technology and climate, and relations such as kinship, trade and competition. Currency: contact; introduction is available and exclusion is not. Anchor: the ground, since what survives is what the ground kept. Nameable: yes.
- **Domain 4, the measurement frame.** Actors: researchers and instruments, including cross-cultural datasets and longitudinal records. Currency: independence between frames. Anchor: a measurement that does not update on what any frame thinks. Nameable in principle, but the channel is partly stale because much evidence is drawn from a narrow set of societies.
- **Partition or union.** Union. A pattern's stability is a valence relative to a residual, so the same pattern can be stable on one axis and changing on another. Unbundle before searching.
- **Order by dependency.** D1 before D2 before D3 before D4. Settle the individual loop before the population comparison; settle both before a measurement frame can compare them. Unbundle the union before naming a frame (Fig 9 :27-28).

Strain test applied: Fig 11 valence and Fig 4 kernel are not in tension after re-census. Fig 11 governs an actor's valence relative to a residual; Fig 4 governs what crosses a scale membrane. Different quantities, so no strain. The one live strain is recorded under VERDICT.

## VERDICT (from the figures)

The shape survives, the list does not. Stability is a contact relation, not a property carried by a behavior pattern. The invariant is a kernel: the loop and the rule for naming what worked, over a closed set of kinds. The variable is the cast: actors, currency, anchor and the arrangements over those kinds. Recurring patterns are functional groups with changing qualifiers; change enters through import from a differing closure. The stable/changing question is a union of overlapping conditions and must be unbundled before any list is attempted. The deliverable is that shape, four pre-registered refuters, and the source-discriminating instrument owed at each slot. The empirical list is `gap`, and the verdict is `candidate`. This is `general` on the structure and `specified` on the field reading; nothing here is `grounded`.

- **cost if wrong:** bounded and locatable. The packet states its mode, lists refuters before the conclusion and records provenance, so an error is findable. The real cost is spending the independence of outside readers by sending a list instead of a shape. Fig 2 :42 says "Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong." That cost is avoidable by leading with the refuter.
- **undoable:** yes. The empirical bindings are removable without touching the structural derivation. `07_the_tether_answer.md` and `08_expert_verification_packet.md` already remove them. Nothing has been sent, so no correction has to reach an audience. No primitive has to be withdrawn.
- **reversed by:** a ground reading, not agreement. A source-discriminating instrument showing that a recurring behavior has a single source across periods would name one slot. A pattern whose stability is intrinsic under every condition would break Fig 11 :49. A society with no loop or naming rule would break Fig 4 :19-20. A single condition producing a genuine partition would break Fig 9 :54. Two low-overlap instruments returning a conflicting per-slot partition would change the empirical verdict. Until one fires, the verdict stands as `candidate`.
- **live strain, named:** the identification of "stable" with a threshold comparison (⋛) is structurally correct, but the Operators table defines ⋛ over two quantities, while the comparison here is between a pattern at t and a pattern at t+1 under a changed habitat. Whether those count as two quantities at that level needs an instrument, not an argument. It is recorded, not smoothed.
