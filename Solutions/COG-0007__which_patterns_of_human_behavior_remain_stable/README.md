## The question as an effect

Stated in effect terms: given a behavior pattern B observed under social conditions C in period t, decide what determines whether B recurs when C changes. Option A is that stability is intrinsic to B, so B is a universal or a variable by its own nature, and the deliverable is two lists. Option B is that stability is a relation between B and the habitat that contacts it, so the deliverable is an invariant kernel plus a changing cast, with the boundary located by contact. A third, hidden option is that the question is not a partition at all but a union of overlapping conditions, in which case the deliverable is an unbundling before any list is attempted.

**What an answer would look like (Direction, bound as an ordinary term before the run):** a checkable statement of (i) which quantities are invariant across every social condition examined, (ii) which quantities vary with contact, (iii) whether the two sets are a partition or a union, and (iv) the instrument that would move any item between them. This shape is written down and refuted in `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` §4 and §4b, before the findings are concluded.

## What is deliberately not here

- No personal emails of named individuals.
- No ranking of people by how likely they are to believe the claim.
- No bulk-send plan.

Three reasons, in the figures' own words.

1. A room does not upgrade the label. Seat Map :283: "A room does not upgrade the label. A room reaching a conclusion without the ground is agreement, and agreement among frames is a weighted average of influence. Adding people raises the vocabulary and does not add an anchor." A list selected for agreement manufactures the correlation that would make its agreement worthless.
2. Correlation disqualifies a verdict. Seat Map :163: "High correlation is disqualifying for a verdict and efficient for a convention." Fig 2 :35: "Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you."
3. Asking people to trust before they check spends their independence. Fig 10 :42: "A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it." Seat 4 must not hold the interiors.

Legally: harvesting personal emails for unsolicited bulk outreach breaches GDPR and CAN-SPAM style rules in many jurisdictions. Public opt-in channels avoid that failure as well as the structural one.
