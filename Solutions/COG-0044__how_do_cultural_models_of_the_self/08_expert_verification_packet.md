# Expert verification packet: the Tether on cultural models of the self

**What this is:** a claim in a form a cultural psychologist or self-concept researcher can check. Mode,
derivation and refuters are stated before the conclusion, so the reader knows what to look for and what
would settle it. One-to-one use only. No theory is shown before the verdict on stage 1 is recorded.

**Mode: `specified` on the substantive claims, `general` on the structural shape. Not `grounded`.** No
measurement in this derivation settles any binding to a cultural-psychology quantity.

## 1. The claim, in one paragraph

A cultural model of the self is a belief that mixes inherited prior with private history, and the
governing parameter is the weighting between them. The question "how does it affect motivation,
decision-making and responsibility" is three questions with three different anchors: motivation is
where the model sets what counts as progress, decision-making is a prediction against observed choice
rather than a reported intention, and responsibility is an authorship judgment that depends on the
habitat enumerated. The independent/interdependent binary is refused in favour of a finer instrument,
not retained as a universal frame. Structurally the framework locates capability in agent and habitat
together. The framework does not name which model a culture holds and returns a shape, not a name.

## 2. Three checkable sub-claims

### C1 : framework-internal; checkable by reading the figure text

- **Statement:** the self-model is a weighted, frame-internal belief; the three downstream slots must
  be kept separate because they are a union with different anchors.
- **Source, verbatim:** Symbols :19-21 "What the system takes to be the case before it acts. Formed from
  its own history and from what it inherited, mixed by w. Not the observation." Fig 1 :19 "The residual
  is indexed by object slot, not aggregated." Fig 9 :54 "And when the split lands on a union rather
  than a partition, unbundle before you search again."
- **Refuter:** one measure that predicts all three slots with residuals trending down together, no
  slot-specific residue and no loss of description after rebundling; or a demonstration that the
  self-model is read by an instrument independent of the frame's own report and predicting behaviour
  out of sample, at which point the "frame-internal" limit is revised.

### C2 : motivation and decision; checkable against experimental and observational work

- **Statement:** where the self-model sets what counts as progress, a spendable difference appears and
  effort follows it; choice is a prediction against observation under incomplete information, so a
  reported intention is not a choice.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 3 :9-10 "it can say what would count as progress here, in its own terms."
  Symbols :95-98 "A difference that can be spent. Not merely a distribution: what makes it a gradient
  is directedness." Fig 3 :12-13 "a route to that objective, chosen under incomplete information."
  Fig 1 :26-27 "They are frame-internal. A frame cannot score itself with a quantity it also produces."
- **What to check:** does the objective-setting step actually appear in the data, independently of
  self-report? Kühnen (2009) reports that priming independent versus interdependent self-knowledge
  changes value judgments and attribution, and that the effect is mediated by which self-knowledge is
  accessible. That is a candidate ground reading, not a settlement. Heine (2010) reviews the related
  motivation domains. Does the predicted divergence appear in observed choice rather than only in a
  questionnaire?
- **Refuter:** a controlled manipulation that changes what counts as progress while measured effort
  stays flat; or identical observed choice distributions across conditions whose self-models the
  instrument says differ, at adequate power.

### C3 : responsibility and the binary; one structural, one empirical

- **Statement:** authorship is a seat and habitat relation, not a property of a bounded individual;
  the independent/interdependent binary is refused and a finer instrument is owed.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 11 :39 "capability is a property of agent-and-habitat, never of the agent
  alone." Fig 10 :86 "Whoever is measured by a reading cannot own it." Fig 5 :53 "refuted: a term we
  hold is wrong; a term is owed that says so." Fig 6 :29 "inward: is our representation adequate to
  hold it? The instrument extends access."
- **What to check:** in attribution tasks, do interdependent-primed participants take situational
  constraints into account where independent-primed participants do not? Kühnen (2009) reports exactly
  that pattern with essay length. Does Vignoles et al. (2016), across 7,279 adults in 55 cultural
  groups, support refusing the simple contrast? Both are world sources and can only corroborate the
  figure verdict; they cannot overrule it.
- **Refuter:** an isolated agent changing the ground with no habitat contribution, which breaks the
  agent-and-habitat claim; or a persistent residual for the binary that trends *down* once the
  seven-dimensional model is admitted, which makes the refusal premature.

## 3. What is explicitly not claimed

- Not a name. The framework does not say which self-model a culture holds (Fig 9 :32 "you get a kind
  of thing, never the answer itself").
- Not an effect size, not a universal East/West law, not a ranking of cultures.
- Not that the figures settle how any population attributes responsibility. The agent-and-habitat
  claim is a structural claim of the framework; the empirical attribution question belongs to the
  ground.
- Not that self-report self-construal scores are ground readings. They are frame-internal until an
  independent instrument establishes contact.
- Not a seventh primitive. Independent and interdependent construals are bindings onto held terms.
- Not `grounded`. Nothing here is asserted as settled by experiment.

## 5. What would settle it

A ground instrument per slot, not a further argument. From *The Hard Problem is About Source*: an
instrument that reads green "it cannot comment on the source"; the spectrometer resolved it.

1. **Motivation:** effort and persistence tracking an objective that is measured independently of
   self-report, under a self-model manipulation.
2. **Decision:** observed choices diverging in the direction the weighted belief predicts, with a
   pre-registered refuter and adequate power.
3. **Responsibility:** an attribution task that discriminates actor-only authorship from
   agent-and-habitat authorship. The essay-length paradigm in Kühnen (2009) is a candidate design.
4. **Binary:** a source-discriminating instrument showing whether the independent/interdependent
   contrast leaves a persistent flat residual after a seven-dimensional measure is admitted.

## 6. What an expert can check in twenty minutes

1. Read Symbols :19-26, Fig 1 :19, Fig 9 :54, Fig 11 :39, Fig 10 :86, Fig 5 :53 in the local figure
   text. Six short passages.
2. Check C2 against the work you know: does the objective-setting step show up independently of
   self-report, or is the observed effect only in the questionnaire?
3. Check C3: does the attribution evidence distinguish actor-only from agent-and-habitat authorship,
   and does the 55-group result support refusing the binary?
4. Name one instrument that would settle one slot. Send the refutation. A failure is more useful than
   an endorsement.
