# Outreach draft: check one output before you decide about the frame

**Use:** one-to-one, or as a cover letter attached to a submission through the public venues in
`03_public_contact_points.csv`. **Not** for bulk send. No personal emails are used anywhere in this
folder.

**Hard precondition:** send the output and the refuter first. Do not attach the figures or the source
links to the first message. A recipient who has absorbed the framework before giving a verdict has
joined the frame being checked (Fig 10 :42: "A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify
it.").

---

**Subject:** One checkable output on culture, self and responsibility, before any theory

> Hi [name],
>
> I have one specific output I would like you to check against what you already know about cultural
> psychology and self-concept. I am not asking you to read a framework first, and I am not asking you
> to agree with anything. If the output does not survive, there is nothing else to look at.
>
> **The output (`specified`, not `grounded`):**
>
> 1. A cultural model of the self is a belief about the self that mixes inherited prior with private
>    history. It is a frame-internal term, not an observation. The interesting parameter is the
>    weighting, how far the belief leans on what was inherited rather than on the person's own record.
> 2. The question "how does it affect motivation, decision-making and responsibility" is three
>    questions, not one. Motivation, decision and responsibility have different anchors: effort,
>    observed choice, and an authorship judgment. A single averaged "effect of self-construal" hides
>    which slot is live. A near-zero average with one live slot is a legal state.
> 3. Where the self-model sets what counts as progress, a spendable difference appears and effort
>    flows down that path. An "independent" model makes unique internal attributes the objective; an
>    "interdependent" one makes fitting in and obligation the objective. This is a prediction about
>    the shape of the dependence, not a claim about which model is correct.
> 4. Choice is a prediction against observation under incomplete information, so a reported intention
>    is not itself a choice. The ground for this slot is observed behaviour.
> 5. Authorship is not a property of a bounded individual. Capability is a property of the agent and
>    the habitat together, and whoever is measured by a reading cannot own it. A model that treats the
>    person as a context-free author has dropped the habitat from the composition. I state this as a
>    structural claim of the framework, not as a finding about any population.
>
> **What I am not claiming:** that the framework says which self-model a culture holds. It returns a
> shape, not a name. I am not claiming the independent/interdependent binary is a discovered truth;
> I treat it as refused in favour of a finer instrument, and I would rather you test that than accept
> it. I am not claiming any effect size, and nothing here is settled by measurement.
>
> **What would refute the output, written before this message:**
> 1. One measure explains all three slots with residuals that trend down together and no
>    slot-specific residue.
> 2. A self-model manipulation changes no behaviour on an independent instrument, at adequate power.
> 3. A controlled case shows an isolated agent changing the ground with no habitat contribution.
> 4. The independent/interdependent binary survives with no persistent flat residual after a
>    seven-dimensional alternative is admitted.
>
> If any holds, I withdraw the affected claim and send the figure line back to re-census. A one-line
> "this fails because..." is more useful to me than agreement. If you would rather not engage, no
> reply is needed.
>
> **What I would like from you:** what would *you* count as a failure for each of the three slots?
> Please answer that before I show you any theory. I will record your verdict at that point.
>
> **If it survives, and only then:** I will send three links. The figures, not my summary of them,
> are the authority. Later, if you want to use the method yourself, the formula is public. Run it on
> a problem you know well and can check against a ground, write down what would refute your result
> before you run it, and label the mode of what you get. A reported failure is more useful to me than
> a reported success.
>
> Thanks,
> [name]

## Why this version is ordered this way

| the move | figure-grounded reason |
|---|---|
| Output first, theory second | Fig 10 :52, "Bring a characterised residual, never the source." Each frame ranges against the ground separately (Fig 2). |
| Do not ask for agreement | Fig 2 :35, "Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you." |
| Refuter before conclusion | Seat Map :287, "a falsifier written before the run is a design instrument, not only an honesty device." |
| Three slots kept separate | Fig 1 :19, "The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated." Fig 9 :54 requires unbundling a union. |
| Mode on every substantive claim | `THE_FORMULA.md`: "Mode. general · specified · grounded. State which." |
| No claim about which model a culture holds | Fig 9 :32, "you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself" |
| Binary treated as refused, not as fact | Fig 5 :53, "refuted: a term we hold is wrong; a term is owed that says so" |
| Authorship stated as structural, not empirical | Fig 11 :39, "capability is a property of agent-and-habitat, never of the agent alone" |
| Verdict recorded before showing theory | Fig 10 :42: a verifier who can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it. Later agreement is never pooled as corroboration (Fig 2 :40). |
| Offer the formula as a method, not an answer | Fig 4 :22, "Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down." |
| No request for trust or prestige | *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*: prestige can fund an action but cannot settle it; only the ground settles it. |
| No bulk send or receptiveness rank | Seat Map :163, "High correlation is disqualifying for a verdict and efficient for a convention." Seat Map :283, "A room does not upgrade the label." |

**Note on the field.** This draft is addressed to people who already know the self-construal
literature. That is a competence filter, not a receptiveness filter. Selecting recipients because
they are likely to agree would manufacture exactly the correlation that disqualifies their agreement
as a verdict (Seat Map :163).
