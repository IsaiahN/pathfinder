## How the wider literature bears on the claim

1. **Foundational claim, high overlap with everything else.** Markus and Kitayama (1991) give the
   independent/interdependent distinction and explicitly tie it to cognition, emotion and motivation.
   It is the theory being checked, not an independent witness for it.
2. **Agency and responsibility, lower overlap.** Kühnen (2009) reports priming experiments in which
   independent-primed participants show correspondence bias regardless of essay length, while
   interdependent-primed participants take the situational constraint into account when the essay is
   short. That is the closest empirical material to the responsibility slot, and it comes from a
   different method than the cross-national surveys.
3. **Ground not settled, low overlap.** Vignoles et al. (2016) use 55 cultural groups and a new
   seven-dimensional instrument to refuse the simple East/West contrast. Matsumoto (1999) reaches a
   similar adverse judgment earlier. These are the world's own refuters of the binary and are treated
   in `01` F7 as a REFUTED residual, not as a reason to abandon the figure reading.
4. **Motivation domain.** Heine (2010) reviews cultural differences in self-enhancement,
   approach/avoidance, primary and secondary control, and distinctiveness versus conformity. Useful
   for the motivation slot, but a review of the same primary pool, so corroboration is weak.

No world source overrules a figure line. When the world conflicted with the binary, the derivation
went back to the census and changed which operator is owed (a refusal plus an INWARD refinement),
which is what the conflict rule requires.

## Distribution: offer, do not push

Figure-grounded reasons:

- **Fig 4 :22-23:** "Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down." Sending the full framework
  before anyone has described the residual sends a recording, not a generator. Lead with the shape
  claim and its four refuters; offer the links afterwards.
- **Fig 10 :42:** "A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it." A recipient who has
  absorbed the interior before giving a verdict has joined the frame being checked.
- **Fig 9 :27-28:** "describe it before you go looking, or any frame you pick will seem to fit." Ask
  what they would expect to see in each of the three slots if the shape claim were true, before
  giving them the article.
- **Fig 2 :35:** "Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you." Do not
  ask for agreement, and do not select recipients for likely agreement (Seat Map :163).
- **Fig 7 :12-13:** "same closure, handed along; agreement compounds, evidence does not." A chain of
  agreeing readers is one long room, not corroboration.

**Recommended order in a reply:** (1) the three-slot shape claim and the four refuters; (2) the
question "what would you count as a failure in each slot?", with their verdict recorded; (3) only if
they are curious, the primary links above, with the note that the figures and the original papers, not
this summary, are the authority; (4) the formula as a method to reuse, with the instruction that each
user writes their own refuter first and states their own mode.

## The two-stage offer, with the method stage kept separate

1. **Stage 1, output only.** Send the shape claim and the refuters. The recipient checks without
   holding the framework interior. Fig 10 :52: "Bring a characterised residual, never the source."
2. **Stage 2, opt-in only.** If they ask, send the links. Their stage-1 verdict is already recorded
   and is never pooled as later corroboration, because shared theory and shared literature make the
   agreement one evidence pool (Fig 2 :40).
3. **Stage 3, method.** Fig 4 :22 permits sending a generator. The formula is public; each user must
   state a ground and a refuter before running it, because a frame cannot check the direction of its
   own question (THE_FORMULA.md, "Direction").
