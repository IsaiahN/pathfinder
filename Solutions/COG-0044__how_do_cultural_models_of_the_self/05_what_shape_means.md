## 1. A shape is a constraint, not a filler

The figures README states the limit: the framework returns "what a missing thing would have to look
like (a shape, not a name)." Fig 9 :32: "you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself." Fig 13
says the system "It can say the answer is out of reach with the library as it stands, so something has
to be imported before it is asked again" and that it "Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence."

*The Library of Babel* (fetched) gives the searcher's version: "you can cut your search short if the
set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer
that satisfies your question." And: "This brings your search of unlimited books to a finite sum."

For COG-0044 a **shape** is therefore the narrowed form of the dependence before we can name its size
or direction. "A weighted belief sets an objective, an action and an authorship judgment, and the
three slots stay separate with a refusal written in advance" is a shape. "Culture X produces more
interdependent motivation than culture Y, with effect size d" is a name, and the framework does not
produce it.

## 2. How a shape is obtained

- *Library of Babel* (fetched): use the vocabulary already held that is *closest* to the thing, then
  recurse. "With each new term gained, you are able to narrow the space by definition and deepen your
  vocabulary." Also: "If your problem was a bundle or conflation of several problems masquerading as
  one? You will need to search all of those problems individually." That is the union reading of F2.
- Fig 6 :30: "The description travels. Fails by being sharpened into terms only we can read. Cast it
  in effect terms." Fig 9 :30-31: "your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect rather than the
  cause." So describe the effect (effort, choice, authorship attribution) before choosing the
  "self-construal" instrument.
- *Decoding Tacit Knowledge* (fetched): "When contact isn't possible, the best you can send is a plain
  description of what you'd expect to see, because anyone can check that against the world." It also
  says directly of culture: "what they share isn't a set of beliefs but the backdrop they read each
  other against." That is unusually close to the residual here: a cultural model of the self is part
  of the pretext a person brings, not a single shared proposition.
- *Library of Babel* on stopping: "You will know that you are done because the finish line will also
  be the starting place" and "you have already composed the answer through the process of locating it."

## 3. What licenses a name

Only two things, both outside the shape itself:

1. **The ground settles it.** Formula step 5: "the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays. A
   gate is not the ground." The README: the framework "stops at the limit of what the available
   instruments can distinguish, and records the lineage so the stop is honest."
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs** (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8 :50-52), with a
   source gate and a shadow test.

*They're Scanning Books When They Should* sharpens the second: "The real import is not a new term in
the existing space. It is a new dimension of the space." And: "Declaring the primitives does not make
them neutral. It makes the choice visible."

For this question the seven-dimensional model of selfhood (Vignoles et al. 2016) is closer to an
INWARD instrument refinement than to an import that adds a new kind: it resolves a held term with
more dimensions. That is F7, and it is a `specified` reading, not a measurement.

## 4. Why "which self-model does this culture hold?" is the source error

*The Hard Problem is About Source* (fetched) gives the mechanism. Metamerism: an instrument reads
green and "it cannot comment on the source." A green light, or yellow plus blue, or RGB, or CMYK can
give the same reading. "If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with
lossy information." The spectrometer, not an argument, resolved it. And: "An instrument that can't
distinguish these cases demonstrates its own inadequacy."

Apply that to a self-report scale. A person endorses "I prefer to work with others" (a reading). The
source could be an interdependent self-model, a situational prime, an acquiescent response style, or a
temporary accessibility shift. Kühnen (2009) shows that priming changes the same judgment within one
population, so the reading does not determine the source. Vignoles et al. (2016) show that removing
acquiescence variance changes the structure of the self-construal measure. A source named from a
reading is the metamerism error. What is owed is a discriminating instrument, not a name.

*The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap* (fetched): "the output is only as good as the validator. The
agent, or the tool, or the frame, or the theory, cannot validate itself." A self-report instrument
validated only by another self-report instrument is a frame checking itself.

## 5. Q answered

**What licenses a particular self-model as the answer to a slot?** Nothing in the framework. The
framework returns a shape. A particular binding is licensed only by the ground settling a minted term,
or by a provenanced import with a shadow test. Until then, the name is the searcher's own contribution
and must be recorded as such.

**Does the derivation commit the forbidden error?** Not as written: `01` never names which self-model
a culture holds and never turns the independent/interdependent binary into a ground fact. The one
place a name enters is the structural claim that capability is agent-and-habitat (Fig 11 :39), which
is a figure line, not a world import, and is stated as a structural claim of the framework.

## 5b. Correction to my own sections 3 and 4

I initially read "shape, not a name" as forbidding any mention of independent or interdependent
self-construal. That overstates it. The README's phrase is "what a **missing** thing would have to
look like (a shape, not a name)." Its subject is a primitive not held in Γ. Independent and
interdependent construals are **bindings from the psychology frame onto held terms** (the belief `b`
and the weighting `w` in the Symbols table). Naming them is not the invention of a seventh primitive.

What remains wrong is different and is **not retracted**:

- Treating the binding as a derivation from the six-term substrate. It is a binding, provenanced as
  such, and adds no atom (Fig 6 :21: "MINT composes inside the closure and can never add an atom.").
- Treating the binary as universal after the world refused it. That is a REFUTED residual owing a
  negation (Fig 5 :53), not a term to keep composing over.
- Naming the source of a self-report reading without an instrument that discriminates sources. The
  metamerism point stands; the reason is the missing discriminator, not a ban on naming held atoms.

Both the overstatement and its narrowing are recorded so neither is quietly lost.

## 6. Two consequences for outreach

**Prestige is not action.** From *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap* (fetched): "Prestige must never
be allowed to validate an action directly." And: "Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a
budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it. The Ground
Maintainer must be blind to prestige." A packet that gathers famous cultural psychologists to agree
with the shape claim is the named collapse, not corroboration. The legitimate ask is a budget to try
the refuters, and a ground, an experiment or an observation, to settle them (Seat Map :163, :283).

**My own audit is a simulation, not a ground reading.** Same essay (fetched): "LLMs can quickly
simulate an approximate answer/output with the formula found in the architecture post." And: "the
truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula
yourself." So `01` is a simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures.
It is not a test of the claim and cannot settle anything it reports on.
