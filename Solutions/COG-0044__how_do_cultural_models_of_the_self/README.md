## The question restated as an effect

A "cultural model of the self" is a transmitted answer to "what is the self?" In effect terms it is a belief about the self that leans on an inherited prior (culture) to a degree `w`, rather than on private history. The residual is the dependence of three slots on that belief: (1) motivation, what counts as progress and where a spendable difference sits; (2) decision-making, which action gets chosen under incomplete information; (3) personal responsibility, who is treated as the author and owner of an action. An answer would look like a shape for each slot plus a stated failure condition. That shape is bound here as an ordinary term so the preference can be refused (THE_FORMULA.md, "Direction").

## What is deliberately not here

- No personal emails of named researchers.
- No ranking of people by how likely they are to agree with the shape claim.
- No bulk-send plan.

Three reasons in the figures' own words:

1. **Agreement is not a ground.** Fig 2 :35: "Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you." Adding agreeable readers raises the vocabulary and does not add an anchor (Seat Map :283).
2. **Selecting for receptiveness manufactures the disqualifying correlation.** Seat Map :163: "High correlation is disqualifying for a verdict and efficient for a convention."
3. **Showing the interior first spends the checker's independence.** Fig 10 :42: "A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it."

Legally: public opt-in channels avoid the GDPR and CAN-SPAM style failures that accompany unsolicited bulk mail, as well as the structural failure above.
