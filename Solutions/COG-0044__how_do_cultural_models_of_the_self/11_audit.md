## Independent domain census

I redid the census from the residual line alone before reading the primary's conclusions, using the
WATSON order: R by arity, symmetry and scale; one line per domain with actors / currency / anchor;
partition or union; order by dependency.

- **R (arity / symmetry / scale).** One cause (a transmitted self-belief) and three effect slots, plus
  the transmission slot. Arity four. Fig 1 :19 "The residual is indexed by object slot, not
  aggregated." Symmetry: asymmetric within a run, with a feedback cycle across generations. Scale:
  one frame, one run, one handoff across scales.
- **Domain 1, transmission and self-model.** Actors: carriers, membrane, library, belief, weighting.
  Currency: inherited prior and private history mixed by w. Anchor: behaviour under manipulation.
  Question type: upstream.
- **Domain 2, motivation.** Actors: person, objective, gradient. Currency: a spendable difference.
  Anchor: measured effort. Separate loop.
- **Domain 3, decision.** Actors: person, action, plan. Currency: prediction against observation.
  Anchor: observed choice. Separate loop.
- **Domain 4, responsibility.** Actors: author, observer, seat, habitat. Currency: authorship and
  provenance. Anchor: Fig 11 :39 structurally, attribution experiments empirically. Separate loop.

Order by dependency: transmit, hold the belief, set the objective, choose the action, judge authorship
(Fig 3). Refuse before minting (Formula step 2). Describe the effect before choosing the instrument
(Fig 9 :27-28).

My census matches the primary's four domains and its union reading. Where the primary derived the
union from Fig 1 :19 and Fig 9 :54, I derived it from Fig 9 :55-56 ("A bundled problem is usually a
union of overlapping conditions, not a list of separate ones") and from the difference in anchors
between the three slots. Different starting lines, same boundaries: low overlap between routes, so the
agreement is weak but not circular within the framework. It is still one evidence pool.

## Round trip gap R_T

The Symbols table defines R_T as the gap between what was sent up and what came back down,
`gap(x, (T_E ∘ T_A)(x))`, measuring what the coarser description cannot hold. Fig 4 :30 requires it to
be measured rather than assumed. I ran the round trip on the condensation this folder actually uses.

1. **Sent up (x):** the verbatim figure set, chiefly Symbols :19-26 (belief and weighting), Fig 1 :19
   (per-slot residual), Fig 9 :54-57 (union), Fig 11 :39 (agent-and-habitat), Fig 5 :53 (refuted kind).
2. **Abstraction (T_A):** the shorthand carried in `07` and `09`, "culture shapes self, self shapes
   motivation, decision and responsibility, keep the slots separate, refuse the binary."
3. **Concretisation (T_E):** a concrete empirical claim, "culture X produces interdependent self
   construal, which raises obligation-based motivation and group-serving choice and locates
   responsibility in the relationship."

**R_T is nonzero.** Four things the shorthand holds do not survive the trip back down:

- the **frame-internal status of the self-report score**, which the concrete claim treats as a reading
  of the self rather than a reading the frame produces about itself;
- the **union structure**, which the concrete claim collapses into a single "self construal raises"
  sentence spanning three different anchors;
- the **agent-and-habitat authorship claim**, which the concrete claim turns into an empirical finding
  about a population rather than a structural claim of the framework;
- the **refusal of the binary**, which the concrete claim reinstates by naming "interdependent self
   construal" as if the refused term were still composable.

Conversely, one thing survives cleanly: the belief is weighted, the objective governs motivation, and
the three slots have different anchors. That is the residue worth sending. R_T was **not** measured
numerically, because no ground reading exists; it is recorded qualitatively as the enumerated set of
dropped constraints. Treating "the formula is the condensed version of the metalaws" as identity would
set R_T to zero and would be the dead-reckoning error Fig 4 :30 names. The honest form is a lossy
condensation with a measured gap, which is what `09_watson_answer.md` states.
