# The Tether's answer on cultural models of the self (COG-0044)

**Mode: `specified` on the substantive claims, `general` on the structural shape. Not `grounded`.**
This is a shape and a route, not a result. Simulated derivation checked against the figures
(`05_what_shape_means.md` section 6), not a ground reading.

## 1. The residual it answers

A cultural model of the self is a belief about what the self is, mixing inherited prior with private
history by a weighting `w`. The question asks how that belief affects motivation, decision-making and
personal responsibility. The effects are a **union**, not one hard problem: Fig 9 :55-56 "A bundled
problem is usually a union of overlapping conditions, not a list of separate ones." Each slot has its
own anchor and its own loop, so each gets its own verdict.

## 2. The shape, one loop per domain

**Domain 1, self-model.** It is a belief and a weighting, not an observation. Symbols :19-21 "What the
system takes to be the case before it acts. Formed from its own history and from what it inherited,
mixed by w. Not the observation." Symbols :25-26 "Two frames with identical architecture diverge
because their histories and their w diverge." So the unit of analysis is the weighting, not the
culture label. Verdict: `specified`, single-source (Symbols table), corroborated in the world only
weakly because the sources share a pool.

**Domain 2, motivation.** The self-model sets what counts as progress. Fig 3 :9-10 "it can say what
would count as progress here, in its own terms." A spendable difference then appears: Symbols :95-98
"A difference that can be spent. Not merely a distribution: what makes it a gradient is directedness."
Where the model places the objective, effort and persistence flow. The framework says this is exactly
the middle link that gets the least instrumentation (Fig 3 :25-27), so a null result there is not
evidence that the link works. Verdict: `specified`, unmeasured at the ground.

**Domain 3, decision-making.** Choice is a prediction against observation under incomplete
information: Fig 3 :12-13 "a route to that objective, chosen under incomplete information." Because
`b` and `w` differ, predictions and choices differ. A reported intention is frame-internal and cannot
score the frame (Fig 1 :26-27 "They are frame-internal. A frame cannot score itself with a quantity it
also produces."). Verdict: `specified`, triangulated across two figure lines.

**Domain 4, personal responsibility.** Authorship is a seat and habitat relation. Fig 11 :39
"capability is a property of agent-and-habitat, never of the agent alone." Fig 10 :86 "Whoever is
measured by a reading cannot own it." A self-model that treats the person as a bounded, context-free
author has dropped the habitat from the composition. This is a structural claim of the framework. It
does not say how any population actually attributes responsibility, and that empirical question is
left to experiments such as the priming work in Kühnen (2009). Verdict: `specified`, single-source
figure claim, empirically corroborated but not decided.

**Across domains, the operator order matters.** The independent/interdependent binary is held and
predicting, and its residual persists and does not trend down, so it owes a refusal before any
replacement: Fig 5 :53 "refuted: a term we hold is wrong; a term is owed that says so." The
seven-dimensional model is an INWARD instrument refinement (Fig 6 :29 "inward: is our representation
adequate to hold it? The instrument extends access."), not an import that adds a new kind. Verdict:
`specified`, triangulated across Fig 5 and Fig 6, with the world conflict left to the ground.

**Across scales.** Within one life the path is a dependency chain; across generations the output
re-enters as input: Fig 12 :121 "cycle. The output re-enters as input. The sign of the loop decides
whether it closes, and a chain cannot close by construction." What decides the sign is whether the
handoff is corrected by the ground or carried by agreement (Fig 7 :12-13 "same closure, handed along;
agreement compounds, evidence does not").

## 3. What it explicitly does not say

- Not a name. The framework returns "you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself" (Fig 9 :32).
  It does not say which self-model a culture holds, and it does not rank cultures.
- Not an effect size, not a universal East/West law. The 55-group evidence refuses that contrast.
- Not that the figures settle the empirical attribution question. The agent-and-habitat claim is
  structural; the measurement of attribution belongs to the ground.
- Not that self-report is a ground reading. A questionnaire answer is frame-internal.
- Not a seventh primitive. Independent and interdependent are bindings onto held terms, not members
  of the six-term substrate.
- Not `grounded`. No measurement taken here settles `left(R,φ)` for any slot.

## 4. What would settle it, the ground

A ground instrument per slot, not a further argument. *The Hard Problem is About Source* (fetched) says
an instrument that reads green "it cannot comment on the source"; the spectrometer resolved it.
Here the corresponding tests are:

- **Motivation:** an instrument that measures what counts as progress independently of self-report and
  shows effort tracking the objective when the self-model is manipulated.
- **Decision:** observed choice distributions that diverge in the direction the weighted belief
  predicts, with adequate power and a pre-registered refuter.
- **Responsibility:** an attribution task that discriminates actor-only authorship from
  agent-and-habitat authorship, where the two make different observable predictions. The Kühnen
  (2009) essay-length paradigm is a candidate, not a settlement.
- **Binary:** a persistent flat residual for the independent/interdependent contrast after a
  seven-dimensional instrument is admitted; that would confirm the refusal. If the residual trends
  down, the refusal was premature.
- **Standing falsifiers from `01` section 4:** one aggregated measure for all three slots; no
  behavioural change under manipulation; an isolated agent changing the ground with no habitat; a
  culture's self-model named as the framework's answer.

## 5. The honest stop

**Unreached, not unreachable.** The ground has not fully settled the empirical picture: Markus and
Kitayama (1991) and Vignoles et al. (2016) disagree about the basic structure, and the measurement
literature itself is contested. The framework stops at the limit of what its instruments can
distinguish and records the lineage so the stop is checkable. Fig 8 :39 "So a search that finds nothing
is never a proof of absence." Fig 13 :134 "Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence." The shape is
what survives: keep the slots separate, weight the belief, refuse the false binary, refine the
instrument inward, and locate authorship in agent and habitat.

## 6. One-paragraph version for outreach

> The Tether does not tell you which self-model a culture holds, and it offers no effect size. It says
> that a cultural model of the self is a belief that mixes inherited prior with private history, and
> that the interesting quantity is the weighting. It says the question is three questions, not one:
> motivation is where the model sets what counts as progress, decision is a prediction against
> observed choice rather than a reported intention, and responsibility is an authorship judgment that
> depends on the habitat you enumerate. It treats the independent/interdependent binary as refused,
> because its residual persists, and the seven-dimensional model as a sharper instrument rather than a
> new kind of self. Structurally it locates capability in agent and habitat together, which is a claim
> of the framework and not a finding about any population. What it offers is the shape any answer must
> have, four refuters written in advance, and the instruments that would close each slot. The center
> stays open until the ground settles it.
