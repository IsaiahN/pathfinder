## 0. The residual in effect terms

The question asks how a *cultural model of the self* affects three downstream slots: motivation,
decision-making and personal responsibility.

In effect terms a cultural model of the self is a belief `b` about what the self is, formed partly
from inherited prior (the culture) and partly from private history, mixed by the weighting `w`. The
Symbols table defines the belief as "What the system takes to be the case before it acts. Formed from
its own history and from what it inherited, mixed by w. Not the observation." (Symbols :19-21) and
the weighting as "How far the belief leans on private history versus inherited prior." (Symbols
:23-24). A cultural self-model is therefore a **frame-internal term**, not an observation and not a
ground.

The residual `R` is the dependence of three slots on that belief:

| slot | what is being decided | what each option leads to |
|---|---|---|
| motivation | what counts as progress, where a spendable difference sits | an objective is selected, so effort and persistence flow down one path rather than another |
| decision-making | which action is taken under incomplete information | the prediction `Γ(b,a)` changes with `b` and `w`, so observed choices diverge |
| personal responsibility | who is the author and owner of an action | authorship is located in the bounded individual, in the agent-and-habitat, or in a relation |

An answer would look like a **shape per slot** plus a failure condition per slot. The sought-for
shape is bound here as an ordinary term (Formula, "Direction") so that it can accrue a residual and
be refused, rather than remaining an invisible preference.

## 1. Domain census (before choosing any figure line)

### R by arity, symmetry, scale

- **Arity:** one cause (the self-model) and three effect slots (motivation, decision, responsibility),
  plus one upstream slot (the transmission of the model itself). Four slots total. Fig 1 :19: "The
  residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated." So the three effects are never averaged into
  one "effect of self-construal" score.
- **Symmetry:** asymmetric. Culture conditions the self-model; the self-model conditions the effects
  in the same run. The effects do not symmetrically produce the model at that scale. Responsibility
  is a verdict and seat relation, not the same kind of quantity as effort or choice.
- **Scale:** the self-model is a belief inside one person's frame (within-run). Its transmission runs
  across scales (Fig 4 membrane, Fig 7 room chain). Responsibility is a judgment that can cross the
  seam between actor and observer (Fig 10).

### One line per domain: actors / currency / anchor

| domain | actors | currency | anchor (nameable?) |
|---|---|---|---|
| D0 transmission (enculturation) | carriers of the model, the person's Γ, the membrane | head starts and methods passed down, not recordings | yes: the observed behaviour of the enculturated person; self-report alone is not an anchor |
| D1 self-model as weighted belief | person (Γ, b, w), culture as inherited prior | prediction residual, the value of w | yes: behaviour under manipulation, an experiment (Fig 2 :29) |
| D2 motivation | person, objective, gradient | a difference that can be spent; what counts as progress | yes: effort, persistence, goal choice measured by an instrument |
| D3 decision-making | person, action a, plan | prediction Γ(b,a) against observation; route under incomplete information | yes: observed choice, not reported intention |
| D4 personal responsibility | author of the action, observer, seat, habitat | authorship, provenance, standing to be composed over | yes: Fig 11 :39 gives a structural anchor, capability is agent-and-habitat; empirical attribution is settled only by experiment |

Every row has a nameable anchor, so all five are domains. If a row had no anchor it would not be a
domain yet and would remain a residual.

### Partition or union

**Union, not partition.** D2, D3 and D4 are different conditions with different instruments and
different grounds; they are not three versions of one quantity. Fig 9 :54: "And when the split lands
on a union rather than a partition, unbundle before you search again." Fig 9 :57: "A case in two
conditions is not a harder case of either." D0 and D1 are upstream of all three. The correct move is
one loop for transmission and self-model, then three separate loops with separate verdicts.

### Dependency order (Fig 3)

D0 transmission -> D1 self-model held -> D2 objective set -> D3 action chosen -> D4 authorship
judged. Fig 3 :20: "The chain is ordered by dependency, not by importance. Each link consumes what
the one above produces." Fig 3 :22: "A step whose input never arrives cannot be diagnosed, only its
predecessor can." If D1 is measured only by self-report, its ground contact is broken and every
downstream regression on that score is a reading of nothing (Fig 3 :23: "It is a reading of nothing").

**Strain test before conclusion.** The natural temptation is to map "self" onto a single atom in the
six-term substrate (for example, energy or network) and then derive all three effects from it. That
mapping strains: Fig 13 :4-5 puts the six in two kinds in lockstep with no member senior, and none is
a "self." A line that only fits by naming the self as a seventh primitive governs a different
quantity, so the mapping is declined at census. The self-model is treated as a belief term in Γ, which
is where the Symbols table actually places it.

## 3. Findings

Mode on each finding is stated. All are `specified` unless marked `general`; none is `grounded`.

### F1 : A cultural self-model is a frame-internal weighted belief, not an observation (`specified`)

Symbols :19-21 and :23-26 place the self-squarely in the belief and weighting slots. The inherited
prior is the culture; the private history is the person's own record; `w` sets the mixture and is
learnable from outcomes. Two frames with the same architecture diverge because histories and `w`
diverge. So the question is not "which culture does the person belong to" but "how is the self-belief
weighted, and what does that weighting predict." The world's distinction between independent and
interdependent construal is a **binding onto a held frame term**, not a new primitive and not an
import that moves the wall (Fig 6 :21: "MINT composes inside the closure and can never add an atom.").

**Refuter:** show that the self-model is read by an instrument that does not depend on the frame's own
report and that predicts behaviour at non-zero residual; or derive `w = 1` (pure inheritance, no
private-history term) for a normal enculturated adult. Either breaks the claim that `w` is the
governing parameter.

### F2 : The three effects are a union, so no single averaged "effect of self-construal" is admissible (`general` structural)

Fig 1 :19 forbids aggregating the residual across slots, and Fig 9 :54 requires unbundling a union.
Motivation, decision and responsibility have different anchors (effort instrument, observed choice,
authorship judgment). A single number that "self-construal affects outcome X" hides which slot is
live. The live-slot rule matters here: a global near-zero effect with one live slot is a legal state
(Fig 1 :13), so a null average does not mean no effect.

**Refuter:** one measure that predicts all three slots with residuals that trend down together across
all three and survive an unbundling test. If such a measure exists, the union reading is wrong and the
three effects may be one quantity.

### F3 : Motivation is the under-instrumented middle link (`specified`)

Fig 3 :25-27 says the link between having terms for a situation and saying what counts as progress
"attracts the least attention and the fewest instruments," and Fig 3 :27 adds that a link with no
instrument is not a link that works. Here that link is self-model -> objective. The self-model sets
what counts as progress (Fig 3 :10); the objective decides where a spendable difference sits
(Symbols :95-98). An "independent" self-model makes unique internal attributes the objective; an
"interdependent" one makes fitting in and obligation the objective. That is a prediction about where
effort flows, not a claim about which model is correct.

**Refuter:** a controlled manipulation that changes what counts as progress while measured effort and
persistence stay flat, with adequate power; or a case of motivated effort with no objective comparison
at all. Either kills the objective-to-gradient link.

### F4 : Decision-making is prediction against observation, so reported intention is not choice (`specified`)

Action follows the prediction `Γ(b,a)` (Symbols :33-35) and the route is chosen under incomplete
information (Fig 3 :13). Because `b` and `w` differ, the same situation yields different predictions
and different choices. Fig 1 :26-27 disqualifies quantities the frame produces itself: a self-report
of "what I would choose" is frame-internal and cannot score the frame against the ground. The anchor
for this slot is observed choice.

**Refuter:** identical observed choice distributions across conditions whose self-models the
instrument says differ, at a power sufficient to detect the predicted divergence. That would sever
the `b -> a` link at the ground.

### F5 : Responsibility is a seat and habitat question, and the figures make a structural claim here (`specified`)

Fig 11 :39: "capability is a property of agent-and-habitat, never of the agent alone." Fig 11 :40
says an improvement that does not change contact changes nothing. Fig 10 :86 says whoever is measured
by a reading cannot own it. So authorship of an action is not a property of a bounded individual; it
is a seat relation that depends on which habitat was enumerated (Fig 11 :43-44) and which actor's
valence is relative to the residual (Fig 11 :49). A self-model that treats the person as a bounded,
context-free author drops the habitat from the composition. A figure-grounded verdict follows:
**the structural claim of the framework sides with a distributed account of agency, and it does not
depend on which culture happens to be studied.** This verdict is about the framework's own
composition rule. It is not a claim that any empirical population attributes responsibility one way
or the other, and it is not `grounded`.

**Refuter:** a controlled case in which an isolated agent changes the ground with no habitat
contribution whatsoever, or a derivation of capability from the agent alone that survives Fig 11 :40.
Either breaks the agent-and-habitat claim.

### F6 : Self-report self-construal scores are not ground readings (`specified`)

Fig 1 :23 and :26-27 make the ground the only metric and disqualify frame-internal bookkeeping. A
questionnaire answer about the self is a reading the frame produces about itself. This does not make
the scores useless; it changes their status to a candidate instrument whose contact with behaviour
must be established, not assumed. World corroboration, not a figure line: Vignoles et al. (2016)
report that "commonly used self-report measures of independence and interdependence frequently fail
to show predicted cultural differences." Heine (2010) reviews the same measurement problem across
motivation and attribution domains. Two different author groups and methods, but they cite a shared
primary literature, so this is weak corroboration, not a second witness (Fig 2 :40).

**Refuter:** an instrument independent of self-report that reads the self-model and predicts behaviour
out of sample. That would promote the score to a ground-visible instrument.

### F7 : The independent/interdependent binary is a REFUTED residual, not a new mint (`specified`)

This is the sharpest figure-grounded reading of the world conflict. Vignoles et al. (2016), across
7,279 adults in 55 cultural groups, conclude that "a simple contrast between independence and
interdependence does not adequately capture the diverse models of selfhood that prevail in different
world regions." Matsumoto (1999) reaches a similar adverse judgment on the evidence for the binary.
The figures sort a gap by what is *wrong* as well as what is missing: Fig 5 :53 "refuted: a term we
hold is wrong; a term is owed that says so," and the refusal removes the term's standing to be
composed over (Fig 5 :54-55). Negation is a unary operator, not a seventh bond (Operators table :70-74).

So the correct treatment is a **refusal** of the binary as a universal frame, plus an **INWARD**
instrument refinement (Fig 6 :29) that produces the seven-dimensional model. It is not an OUTWARD
import of a new atom (Fig 6 :23). The 7D result re-describes dimensions already implicit in the
original theory; it adds resolution, not a new kind of thing.

**Refuter:** retain the binary with no persistent, flat residual after the seven-dimensional model is
admitted, or show the 7D dimensions are a genuinely new primitive that cannot be composed from the
held vocabulary. If the binary survives, F7 fails and the refusal was premature.

### F8 : Independence between witnesses is a budget question, and most of this literature is one pool (`general`)

Kühnen (2009) uses priming experiments and attribution tasks; Vignoles et al. (2016) use a
55-group survey with a new instrument; Markus and Kitayama (1991) supply the theory both cite. The
priming work and the survey are lower-overlap than two review articles quoting the same review, but
they still share the self-construal construct and much of the primary literature (Fig 2 :31-32).
Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong (Fig 2 :42: "Buy independence in proportion
to the cost of being wrong."). Since the cost here is a wrong account of motivation and responsibility
in applied settings, more independent instruments are worth their price. Filters such as journal tier
or sample size decide the budget only; they never hand over the verdict (Fig 9 :60).

**Refuter:** show that the priming studies and the 55-group survey draw on the same instrument and the
same sample pool, in which case their agreement is one witness wearing two names.

## 4. Pre-registered refuter

Written before the WATSON run and the conclusion, per Seat Map :287: "a falsifier written before the
run is a design instrument, not only an honesty device." The shape claim is refused if any of the
following holds:

1. A single aggregated "effect of self-construal" explains all three slots with residuals trending
   down together and no slot-specific residue. This kills F2.
2. A self-model manipulation changes no behaviour on an independent ground instrument, with adequate
   power. This kills F1, F3 and F4 at the point of contact.
3. A controlled case shows an isolated agent changing the ground with no habitat contribution, or
   capability derived from the agent alone. This kills F5.
4. The independent/interdependent binary survives with no persistent flat residual after a
   seven-dimensional alternative is admitted. This kills F7.
5. The framework names a specific culture's self-model as *the* answer to a slot. This kills the
   shape-not-a-name limit (Fig 9 :32).

If any fires, the affected finding is withdrawn and the line in question is sent back to census rather
than read more softly. Until one is run, nothing is `grounded`.

### Round trip note

The shorthand "culture shapes self, self shapes motivation, decision and responsibility" is a lossy
condensation, not an identity. Going up (Symbols :69-70 abstraction) drops the union structure, the
frame-internal status of self-report, the agent-and-habitat claim and the refutation of the binary.
Coming back down without measuring that gap is dead reckoning (Fig 4 :30). `11_audit.md` records the
gap `R_T` qualitatively as the list of dropped constraints. No numerical ground reading exists.

### Honest stop

The figures return a shape: a weighted belief sets an objective, an action and an authorship judgment,
and the three slots must be kept separate with a refusal written in advance. They do not return which
self-model any person holds, the size of any effect, or whether any culture's attribution practice is
correct. That is ground work and the ground has not fully settled it, as the disagreement between
Markus and Kitayama (1991) and Vignoles et al. (2016) shows. Fig 8 :39: "So a search that finds nothing
is never a proof of absence." Unreachable is a verdict, not a silence (Fig 13 :134).
