## 1. The claim, in one paragraph

In an unmaintained habitat there is no maintainer seat and no encoded apparatus to occupy one, so a compartment need not possess complex cellular machinery in order to grow and divide. The shape such a compartment must have is already in the figure set: a boundary that persists through its own use (a catalyst) and a loop whose output re-enters as input (a cycle), so growth feeds division feeds growth. The boundary is a functional group whose chemistry is a qualifier; the framework does not name a lipid or a fission protein. Modern division machinery belongs to a later, maintained condition. The Fig 4 "membrane" is a boundary between levels of description, not a chemical bilayer, and is not used as evidence here. What is offered is a constrained shape, four refuters written in advance, and the instruments that would close it.

## 2. Three checkable sub-claims

### C1: framework-internal; checkable by reading Figure 11

- **Statement:** the no-machinery claim rests on the unmaintained habitat, not on Fig 4's membrane.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 11 :56: "Unmaintained. No maintainer and no live frame in the seat. The habitat is pure contact, nothing selects, and what survives is what the ground kept." Fig 11 :36: "Where nothing maintains anything, intelligence is a label applied afterwards to a lineage that met its ground. Nothing was intelligent; selection ran. The word does no work there." Contrast Fig 11 :58: "Maintained. A seat exists and can act: choose the instruments, distil the impactful actors, introduce what was not there. That is the garden, and it is how arrival is worked on rather than waited for."
- **Refuter:** show that growth or division requires a term outside the closure of simple amphiphiles plus environment in every setting examined, an encoded apparatus with no substitute. That puts a maintainer seat in the origin frame and breaks the claim. Also refuted if the Fig 4 membrane line is shown to govern a chemical bilayer, which would collapse the homonym distinction.

### C2: framework-internal derivation with a chemistry-facing check

- **Statement:** the required shape is a catalyst plus a cycle, not a list of parts; a boundary is a functional group and a lipid is a qualifier.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 12 :119: "catalyst. A part that survives its own use. Spent and regenerating parts behave differently across uses, and nothing records which a term is." Fig 12 :121: "cycle. The output re-enters as input. The sign of the loop decides whether it closes, and a chain cannot close by construction." Fig 12 :123: "functional group. A substructure whose presence fixes the outcome. The group says what kind; the qualifier says which member."
- **What to check:** in a real preparation, does the boundary material persist into daughters (catalyst), does the product feed a further growth-division round (cycle), and is the compartment property fixed by the bilayer class rather than by a single lipid species (functional group)? The chemistry-facing witness is Chen and Walde 2010 (opened this session), which reports autocatalytic vesicle formation ("the presence of vesicles accelerates the formation of more vesicles"), growth by amphiphile addition with fast flip-flop, and division reminiscent of Rayleigh instability without a contractile ring.
- **Refuter:** every division consumes the boundary so no parent material persists; or the loop cannot close and the lineage stops after one iteration; or the outcome depends on a specific lipid, which breaks the group/qualifier split.

### C3: method and scope

- **Statement:** growth and division are two slots, and the verdict is `specified`, not a historical reconstruction or an existence proof.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 1 :19: "The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated." Fig 8 :39: "So a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence." Fig 9 :32: "you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself."
- **What to check:** are growth and division separable in the data, or perfectly correlated? Can one occur without the other (overgrown filaments, fragmentation without net growth)? If they are one slot, the per-slot method is unnecessary. If the packet is read as claiming a particular early-Earth pathway existed, that is a misreading: the framework returns a shape, not a name or a history.
- **Refuter:** a demonstration that the two operations never separate in any preparation (breaks the slot split), or a ground result showing no machinery-free pathway under plausible conditions (breaks the shape claim at the ground).

## 3. What is explicitly not claimed

- Not that the framework names a lipid, a fission protein, or an early-Earth setting. No name is derived.
- Not that life began by this route. The shape constrains a candidate answer; it is not a history.
- Not that modern division machinery is unnecessary. It is required in modern cells; the claim is about the origin.
- Not an existence proof. A shape is not evidence that any pathway existed (Fig 8 :39).
- Not a derivation from Fig 4's "membrane." That line governs scale crossing and is quarantined (F1 in `01`, Q1 in `06`).

## 5. What would settle it

A discriminating instrument, not agreement. From the essay *The Hard Problem is About Source*: an instrument reading green "cannot comment on the source," and the case resolved by "the arrival of a better instrument," not by argument.

1. **Growth slot:** does a boundary persist through material addition under plausible pH, ionic strength, and temperature?
2. **Division slot:** does fission occur without an encoded apparatus, and by which physical route?
3. **Closure and source discrimination:** does the daughter re-enter the loop, and can an instrument distinguish among the mechanisms producing the same grow-and-split reading?
