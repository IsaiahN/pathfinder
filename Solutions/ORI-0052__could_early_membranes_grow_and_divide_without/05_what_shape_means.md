## 1. A shape is a constraint, not a filler

The figures README states the limit: the framework returns "what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)." Fig 9 :32 "you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself." Fig 13 states that it "can say the answer is out of reach with the library as it stands... It cannot say which primitive is missing, because naming it would mean already holding it."

From the essay *The Library of Babel* (source 5): "you can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."

So a **shape** is the narrowed form of an answer you can state before you can name it. It constrains; it does not fill. For ORI-0052 the shape is: a boundary that persists through use, a growth-division loop that closes, and no maintainer seat. "Oleic acid vesicle" or "FtsZ" is a name. The shape does not include either.

## 2. How a shape is obtained

- *The Library of Babel*: use the vocabulary you already have closest to the thing, look for recurring new terms that overlap or correlate with it, recurse. "With each new term gained, you are able to narrow the space by definition and deepen your vocabulary." The searcher must "Become a Better Searcher," because "the problem is you," not the library.
- Fig 6 :30 "The description travels. Fails by being sharpened into terms only we can read. Cast it in effect terms." Fig 9 :30 to :31 "your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect rather than the cause."
- *Decoding Tacit Knowledge* (source 3): "When contact isn't possible, the best you can send is a plain description of what you'd expect to see, because anyone can check that against the world." This is why `02_outreach_email.md` sends a description of what would count as failure, not the framework's interior.
- *The Library of Babel* on stopping: "the finish line will also be the starting place" and "you have already composed the answer through the process of locating it."
- *How Intelligence Actually Works* (source 1) on the operational stop: "The recursion converges toward indiscernibility, and indiscernibility is not identity." The framework's own warning at the end of the canonical Medium article is that outputs "could never be verified if the solutions presented were not contrived to fit the formula." A shape is not a proof.

## 3. What licenses a name

Two things, both outside the shape itself:

1. **The ground settles it.** Formula step 5 (local): "the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays. A gate is not the ground." The canonical README says the framework "stops at the limit of what the available instruments can distinguish, and records the lineage so the stop is honest." For ORI-0052 this is a bench vesicle experiment, not an argument.
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs** (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8 :50 to :52), with a source gate and a shadow test. Assigning a particular lipid to the boundary is an import from the chemistry frame, and must be recorded as such.

*They're Scanning Books When They Should Be Studying Nature* (source 6): "The real import is not a new term in the existing space. It is a new dimension of the space." And: "A minimum viable set of six terms is a human's careful guess at reality's compression axes; another sensorium's minimum set need not be those six." Also: "Declaring the primitives does not make them neutral. It makes the choice visible."

The six are a visible guess, not a discovery of nature's own list. Naming one as *the* answer to a slot turns a visible choice into an invisible one.

## 4. Why reading the source off a projection is the forbidden error

*The Hard Problem is About Source* (source 7) gives the mechanism. Metamerism: an instrument reads green and "it cannot comment on the source." The source might be one green light, yellow plus blue, RGB, CMYK, or a scheme not yet discovered. "If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information." "An instrument that can't distinguish these cases demonstrates its own inadequacy." It was the **spectrometer**, not an argument, that resolved it: "It was proven wrong not by argument, but by the arrival of a better instrument."

For ORI-0052, observing that a membrane grew and split does not by itself name the mechanism that did it. Several physical routes (amphiphile addition plus flip-flop, osmotic strain, shear, freeze-thaw fusion/fission, Rayleigh instability) can produce the same observation. Naming one from the reading alone is the metamerism error. What is owed is a discriminating instrument, not a name.

*How Intelligence Actually Works* (source 1): the video-frame-versus-photograph case, "the frame and the photograph are indistinguishable as artifacts, and they carry different warrants. One was taken; one was extracted. And nothing in the image records which." The same warrant problem applies to a claim that a pathway was *derived* when it was actually *bound* from chemistry.

## 5. The shape answer for ORI-0052

**What licenses "no machinery required"?** Only the figure verdict, stated as `specified`: in the unmaintained habitat there is no maintainer seat (Fig 11 :56), and the compositional structures available are a catalyst and a cycle (Fig 12 :119, :121). No machinery term is needed to compose that shape. A particular lipid, fission mechanism, or early-Earth setting is licensed only by the ground or a provenanced import.

**Does this folder commit the forbidden error?** Not if it keeps the slot marked with a shape. It does commit the error if it asserts that the framework names oleic acid, or that the historical pathway is settled. `07_the_tether_answer.md` and `08_expert_verification_packet.md` keep the slot open and state the mode.

## 5b. Correction to my own §3 and §4

I initially read "shape, not a name" as forbidding any binding at all. That is too broad. The canonical wording governs "a **missing** thing": a primitive not held in Γ. "Boundary" and the structural pair "catalyst / cycle" are compositions already available in the figure set (Fig 12 ; Fig 13 :74). Naming them is not inventing a seventh term; it is selecting from what the framework holds. What remains forbidden is (a) inventing a new primitive, (b) claiming a binding moves the wall (Fig 6: "IMPORT adds an atom" adds none here), and (c) naming the source of a reading without a discriminator.

My §4 metamerism point is **not** retracted: a growth-and-split observation still does not identify the mechanism. The reason is the missing discriminator, not a ban on naming held structures. Both readings are recorded so neither is quietly lost.

## 6. Two consequences for this solution

**Prestige is not action.** From *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap* (source 2): "Prestige must never be allowed to validate an action directly." And: "Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it. The Ground Maintainer must be blind to prestige." A packet sent to famous names to gather endorsements is the named collapse. The legitimate ask is a budget to try the claim and a refutation if it fails.

**This audit is a simulation, not a ground reading.** Same essay: "LLMs can quickly simulate an approximate answer/output" and "the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself." The output is a "simulacrum." So `01_figure_grounded_audit.md`, `06_isomers_and_order.md`, `07_the_tether_answer.md`, `08_expert_verification_packet.md`, `09_watson_answer.md`, and `11_audit.md` are simulated derivations checked for internal consistency against the figures. They are **not** tests of the protocell claim and must be labelled as such. They cannot settle anything they report on.

## 7. Why the essays do not make the verdict independent

All eight essays are by the framework's author, and several cite the figures directly. The canonical Medium article explicitly warns that outputs "could never be verified if the solutions presented were not contrived to fit the formula." So the essays' agreement with the figures is expected and carries little independent weight. They ground the *meaning* of shape, provenance, and prestige; they do not corroborate the verdict as a second witness. That is why `10_source_ledger.csv` records high overlap for every essay row, and why the WATSON world section uses the independent chemistry, physics, and complexity sources for corroboration.
