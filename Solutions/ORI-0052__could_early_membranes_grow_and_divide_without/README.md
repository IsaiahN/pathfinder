## The residual in effect terms

Decide whether a bounded, persistent compartment can grow and divide when no encoded apparatus is present. Option A, yes: the physical chemistry of amphiphiles plus the environment is enough, so "machinery" is a later arrival. Option B, no: growth or division requires a term that simple chemistry plus environment cannot supply, so machinery is a prerequisite. A leads to a shape made of a persisting boundary and a closing growth-division loop in an unmaintained habitat. B leads to a maintainer seat being required at the origin. Both are stated before either is chosen.
