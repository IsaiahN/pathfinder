## QUESTION

Decide, in effect terms, whether a bounded compartment can grow and divide when no encoded apparatus is present. Option A, yes: the chemistry of amphiphiles plus the environment is enough, so machinery is a later arrival. Option B, no: growth or division requires a term that simple chemistry plus environment cannot supply, so machinery is a prerequisite. What each leads to: A gives a shape made of a persisting boundary plus a closing loop in an unmaintained habitat; B puts a maintainer seat at the origin. The two operations, growth and division, are kept as separate slots (Fig 1 :19).

## VERDICT (from the figures)

Yes at the level of shape, `specified` and ungrounded; open at the level of history. The figures require no maintainer seat for growth and division and already hold the structures needed: a boundary that persists through use and a loop that closes. Complex cellular machinery is a later arrival in a maintained habitat, not an origin prerequisite. The framework does not name the chemistry or the pathway; that is a ground question. Domains are kept separate: the chemical anchor was reached by the world sources, the composition shape comes from the figures, the lineage unit comes from selection, and agreement is disqualified as a verdict. The tempting Fig 4 membrane line is a homonym and is refused.

- **cost if wrong:** bounded and locatable. The packet states its mode, four refuters, and the quarantined homonym, so an error is findable rather than invisible. The real cost would be naming a mechanism from a single reading, which is the metamerism error the source essay warns about, or selecting contact venues by likely agreement, which manufactures the disqualifying correlation (Fig 2 :40; Fig 2 :42 "Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong.").
- **undoable:** yes. The shape stands even if a specific chemical binding is refuted; no primitive has to be withdrawn. A sent claim can be superseded by a corrected packet, and the corrections stay visible in `01_figure_grounded_audit.md`.
- **reversed by:** a ground reading, not agreement. A controlled experiment under plausible early-Earth conditions showing that compartments repeatedly fail to grow or divide without an encoded apparatus; a closure demonstration that machinery is required in every setting; a cycle that cannot close; or a figure re-census that puts a maintainer seat in the unmaintained habitat, which would break Fig 11 :56. Until one of these fires, the verdict stays `candidate`.
