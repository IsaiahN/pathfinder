## What the wider literature contributes

1. **Growth does not need encoded machinery.** Chen and Walde describe autocatalytic vesicle formation and growth by amphiphile addition plus flip-flop. This is world corroboration of the figure shape (persisting boundary, closing loop), not a replacement for the figure verdict.
2. **Division does not need the modern contractile apparatus.** Chen and Walde report that Bacillus subtilis can divide without a contractile ring and read the result as Rayleigh-type instability; the independent fluid-dynamics source explains why a thread breaks into droplets by surface tension alone. Two low-overlap fields (cell biology and fluid dynamics) converge on the same physical point.
3. **Coupling growth and division has been demonstrated in the lab.** Tsuji et al. observed fusion, fission, RNA replication, and distribution to daughters over 10 cycles. This is the ground-facing witness for the `specified` claim.
4. **A theoretical predecessor exists.** Ganti's chemoton (1971) models a compartment that grows and divides by an autonomous chemical cycle without enzymes. This is an independent complexity-systems analogue of Fig 12's catalyst-and-cycle shape.

Overlap warning: sources 1 and 2 come from the same review; they are one witness, not two. The Wikipedia overview overlaps heavily with both. Low-overlap corroboration comes from fluid dynamics and from the theoretical chemoton model. No world source conflicts with the figure verdict, so the conflict rule was not triggered.
