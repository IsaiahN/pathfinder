# The Tether's answer: could early membranes grow and divide without complex cellular machinery?

**Mode: `specified` on the substantive claim, `general` on the structural definitions, not `grounded`.** This is a shape and a route, not a result. Simulated derivation checked against the figures (`05_what_shape_means.md` section 6), not a ground reading.

## 1. The residual it answers

Two operations need to be possible without an encoded apparatus: growth (adding material, increasing boundary area) and division (splitting one compartment into two while retaining contents). They are two object slots (Fig 1 :19), not one score. What each option leads to:

- **Yes (figure answer at the shape level):** the capability belongs to the compartment-and-habitat (Fig 11 :39). A boundary that persists through use plus a growth-division loop that closes is enough; machinery is a later arrival.
- **No:** a maintainer term is needed at the origin, which would put a seat in the unmaintained habitat and contradict Fig 11 :56.

## 2. The shape

1. **The unmaintained habitat needs no maintainer seat.** Fig 11 :56 "Unmaintained. No maintainer and no live frame in the seat. The habitat is pure contact, nothing selects, and what survives is what the ground kept." Fig 11 :36 "Where nothing maintains anything, intelligence is a label applied afterwards to a lineage that met its ground. Nothing was intelligent; selection ran. The word does no work there." An encoded division apparatus is a maintainer's tool; in unmaintained mode there is no seat to hold it. **Triangulated** with step 2.
2. **The required structures are already in the figure set.** Fig 12 :119 "catalyst. A part that survives its own use." A boundary that persists through repeated growth and division is a catalyst. Fig 12 :121 "cycle. The output re-enters as input. The sign of the loop decides whether it closes, and a chain cannot close by construction." Growth feeding division feeding growth is a cycle. No new primitive is imported.
3. **Capability is agent-and-habitat, not a machine inside the compartment.** Fig 11 :39 "capability is a property of agent-and-habitat, never of the agent alone." Fig 11 :40 "An improvement that does not change contact changes nothing, however much it improves." Adding a motor to an isolated compartment would not change its contact with the habitat, so it would not change what it can do at this level.
4. **The boundary is a functional group, and its chemistry is a qualifier.** Fig 12 :123 "A substructure whose presence fixes the outcome. The group says what kind; the qualifier says which member." The framework returns the group, not a lipid name. A particular amphiphile is a binding from the chemistry frame, provenanced as such.
5. **Machinery is arrival, not origin.** Fig 11 :58 "Maintained. A seat exists and can act: choose the instruments, distil the impactful actors, introduce what was not there. That is the garden, and it is how arrival is worked on rather than waited for." The modern contractile ring belongs here, after a maintainer exists, not at the start.
6. **The Fig 4 membrane line is refused for this question.** Fig 4 :21 "The membrane is the boundary between the sizes, and the rule about what may cross it. Not a wall: a filter with a direction." ## 3. What it explicitly does not say

- Not "life began this way." The shape is a constraint on a candidate answer, not a historical reconstruction.
- Not a named molecule. No lipid, peptide, or fission protein is derived. The slot stays open.
- Not a proof that any early-Earth pathway existed. "a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence" (Fig 8 :39), and the converse also holds: a shape is not an existence proof.
- Not that modern division machinery is unnecessary. It is necessary for modern cells; the claim is only that it is not a prerequisite for the *origin* of growth and division.
- Not `grounded`. No measurement was taken by this agent; the lab results in `10_source_ledger.csv` are second-hand witnesses, not this run's ground reading.
- Not a derivation from Fig 4's membrane. That line is quarantined as a homonym.

## 4. What would settle it: the ground

A discriminating instrument, not more agreement. From the essay *The Hard Problem is About Source* (source 7 in `05`): an instrument reading green "cannot comment on the source," and it was "the arrival of a better instrument" that resolved the case. Here the corresponding questions are:

- **Growth slot:** does a boundary persist through material addition under plausible pH, ionic strength, and temperature? A bench measurement.
- **Division slot:** does fission occur without an encoded apparatus, and by which physical route (shear, osmotic strain, freeze-thaw, Rayleigh-type instability)? A second measurement, separate from growth (Fig 1 :19).
- **Closure test:** does the daughter's output re-enter as input so a lineage continues beyond one generation (Fig 12 :121)?
- **Source-discrimination test:** can an instrument distinguish among the candidate mechanisms that produce the same grow-and-split reading? Without it, naming one is metamerism.
- **Standing falsifiers** from `01` section 5: a closure demonstration requiring machinery, a cycle that cannot close, a controlled ground failure, or perfect correlation of the two slots.
