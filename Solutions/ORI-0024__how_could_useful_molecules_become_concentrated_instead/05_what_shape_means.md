## 1. A shape is a constraint, not a filler

The figures README states the limit: the framework returns *"what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)."* Fig 9 :32: *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself."* Fig 13 :134-135: *"Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence,"* and *"It cannot say which primitive is missing, because naming it would mean already holding it."*

From *The Library of Babel*: *"You can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."*

So a **shape** is the narrowed form of an answer you can state before you can name it. It constrains; it does not fill. For this question, the shape is:

> an imported spendable difference, composed over space, held in a substituted habitat, restored by a cycle, and handed to the next composition.

That is a shape. "Evaporation in a tidal pool," "eutectic concentration in ice," "thermophoresis in a vent pore" and "encapsulation in a coacervate" are names. The framework returns the former and leaves the latter to the ground.

## 2. How a shape is obtained

- *Library of Babel*: use the vocabulary already closest to the thing, look for recurring new terms that overlap with it, recurse. *"With each new term gained, you narrow the space and deepen your vocabulary."*
- Fig 6 :30: *"Cast it in effect terms."* Fig 9 :30-31: *"your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect rather than the cause."* The effect here is "local concentration above threshold," not "drying" or "freezing," which are causes and therefore borrowed from a frame chosen too early.
- *Decoding Tacit Knowledge*: *"When contact isn't possible, the best you can send is a plain description of what you'd expect to see, because anyone can check that against the world."* For this residual, what you would expect to see is a local excess co-located with a boundary and a cycling driver, not a uniform soup.
- *Library of Babel* on stopping: the answer is reached when *"the finish line will also be the starting place"* and you have *"composed the answer through the process of locating it."*

The procedure matters because of Fig 9 :27: *"describe it before you go looking"*, or any frame you pick will seem to fit. If "drying ponds" is named first, every observation will be read as supporting drying ponds.

## 3. What licenses a name

Only two things, both outside the shape:

1. **The ground settles it.** Formula step 5: *"the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays."* The README: the framework *"stops at the limit of what the available instruments can distinguish, and records the lineage so the stop is honest."* Measured concentration factors, isotopic traces and mineral fabrics are such instruments.
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs** (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8 :51), with a source gate and a shadow test. Assigning "the mechanism was eutectic ice" is an import from the geology frame. It needs a record of where it came from and an explanation of a gap the shape alone leaves open.

*They're Scanning Books* sharpens this: *"The real import is not a new term in the existing space. It is a new dimension of the space."* And: *"A minimum viable set of six terms is a human's careful guess at reality's compression axes; another sensorium's minimum set need not be those six."* Also: *"Declaring the primitives does not make them neutral. It makes the choice visible."*

The six are a visible guess, not nature's own list. Naming one mechanism as *the* answer turns a visible choice into an invisible one.

## 4. Why "more starting material" is the specific forbidden error

*The Hard Problem is About Source* gives the mechanism. Metamerism: an instrument reads green and *"cannot comment on the source"* - one green light, or yellow plus blue, or RGB, or CMYK, or a scheme not yet discovered. The reading does not determine the source. *"If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information."* And: *"An instrument that can't distinguish these cases demonstrates its own inadequacy."* It was the spectrometer, not an argument, that resolved it.

The "more HCN, more meteorites" answer reads the bulk concentration (the artifact) and names its source as "supply." That is the metamerism error: several distinct causes (more supply, a longer residence time, a smaller volume, a phase change, a boundary) give the same bulk reading. What is owed is a discriminator, not a larger number.

*The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*: *"the output is only as good as the validator. The agent, or the tool, or the frame, or the theory, cannot validate itself."*

## 5. Q - Does the framework select a mechanism? ANSWERED: no

**What licenses a particular mechanism?** Nothing in the framework. The framework returns a shape. A particular mechanism is licensed only by the ground settling a term, or by a provenanced import with a shadow test. Until one of those, the name is the searcher's own contribution and must be recorded as such.

**Does naming evaporation commit the error the README forbids?** Yes, in the same way as naming Gradient in the QMGR case. In a `specified` record it names a concrete route where the framework returns a shape, and it names it from a bulk reading that several routes could produce. The correct form remains the four-part shape with the mechanism slot open.

## 5b. Correction to my own §3 and §4

I overstated the "shape, not a name" rule in a first pass. The README's phrase is *"what a **missing** thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)."* It governs a primitive not held in Γ. The gradient **is** already one of the six (Fig 13 :21 names it as a structure; Fig 13 :22 defines it: *"a difference that can be spent"*). So saying "the residual needs a gradient" is **not** naming a missing primitive; it is selecting an atom already in Γ and binding the residual to it. What is forbidden is inventing a seventh primitive or claiming the binding adds an atom (Fig 6 :25: *"An import adds no atom to the world."*).

My §4 metamerism point is **not** retracted. Reading bulk concentration and naming "supply" as its source is still a metamerism error. The reason is the missing discriminator, not a ban on using held atoms. Both claims are now recorded so neither is quietly lost.

## 6. Two consequences for this question

**Prestige is not action.** From *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*: *"Prestige must never be allowed to validate an action directly... Someone let prestige stand in for proof... Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it. The Ground Maintainer must be blind to prestige."* A campaign that gathers famous names to agree with the shape is the named collapse. The legitimate ask is the opposite: give the claim a budget to be tried, and let an experiment or a rock settle it.

**My own audit is a simulation, not a ground reading.** Same essay: *"LLMs can quickly simulate an approximate answer/output... However, the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself."* LLM output is *"simulacra."* So `01` is a simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures. It is **not** a test of any prebiotic mechanism and must be labelled as such. It cannot settle anything it reports on.

## 7. What shape means here, in one line

A shape is the part of the answer that survives before you know which experiment will decide it. For ORI-0024 it is the four-part constraint above; the name of the mechanism is the part that must wait for the ground.
