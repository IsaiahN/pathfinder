## Independent domain census

I redid the census before reading the primary's conclusions, using the WATSON order: R by arity, symmetry and scale; one line per domain with actors / currency / anchor; union or partition; order by dependency.

- **R (arity / symmetry / scale).** The residual is the gap between bulk dilution and the local concentration threshold for the next composition. Arity: two conditions, so a **union compound**, not one hard problem (Fig 9 :57: "a case in two conditions is not a harder case of either"). Symmetry: asymmetric, introduction available and exclusion not (Fig 11 :61), with direction in the gradient (Fig 13 :47: "space has directedness"). Scale: first order inside a locality, second order at the handoff to a next composition (Fig 12 :104).
- **Domain 1, dilute bulk / equilibrium.** Actors: water, monomers, diffusion, hydrolysis. Currency: concentration and chemical potential. Anchor: measured bulk concentration and measured reaction threshold. Nameable. A closed equilibrium frame.
- **Domain 2, external difference.** Actors: light, heat, gradients, freeze-thaw, wet-dry, tides, surfaces. Currency: a spendable difference. Anchor: a measured gradient or phase. Nameable. The only frame that can move the wall (Fig 6 :23).
- **Domain 3, boundary / habitat.** Actors: pores, veins, films, vesicles, droplets, gels. Currency: retention and contact. Anchor: partition coefficient, encapsulation yield, diffusion restriction. Nameable. Enumerated, never composed (Fig 11 :43).
- **Domain 4, verification.** Actors: experimenters, instruments, rock record. Currency: concentration factor and yield. Anchor: the experiment or the rock (Fig 2 :29). Nameable.

Order by dependency: import a difference before a local excess can exist; hold the excess before it can compose; settle the composition before it becomes an atom; verify last (Fig 3 :20). My census matches the primary's four domains and its union reading. Where the primary derived from Fig 13 :29/:46/:76 (substrate and gradient) and Fig 6 :23 (import), I derived from Fig 9 :57 (union split) and Fig 3 :20 (dependency order). Conversely, one thing survives cleanly: a spendable difference imported from a different closure, composed over space and held by a boundary and cycle, is the shape any answer must have.
