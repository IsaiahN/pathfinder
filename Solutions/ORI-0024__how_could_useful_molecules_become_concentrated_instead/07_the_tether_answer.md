# The Tether's answer: how useful molecules become concentrated

**Mode: `specified` on the substantive claims, `general` on the structural shape. Not `grounded`.** This is a shape and a route, not a result. Simulated derivation checked against the figures (`05_what_shape_means.md` §6), not a ground reading.

## 1. The residual it answers

Useful molecules are supplied at low molarity in a large aqueous reservoir, while the next compositions (polymerization, template copying, membrane assembly) require a local excess. The gap is not a shortage of material. It is a **state with no magnitude**: Fig 13 :29: *"A state with no structure has no magnitude: three of what, ordered how, varying across what?"*

It is a **union compound**: producing a difference, holding it, and handing it to the next composition are three conditions with different grounds (Fig 9 :57: *"a case in two conditions is not a harder case of either"*). It is measured per slot (a pore, a brine vein, a drying film), not per ocean: Fig 1 :19: *"The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated."*

## 2. The shape

1. **Concentration is a cross-kind composition, not more of the same.** Fig 13 :74: *"Everything else is a composition across the two kinds, never within one."* Fig 13 :75-76: *"Energy and a gradient compose a flow. A gradient over space and one over time are different terms, because which state it is bonded to is part of what it is."* A concentrated locality is a structure bonded to space; a uniform dilute reservoir is space with no such structure.

2. **The difference must be imported from a frame whose closure differs.** Fig 13 :46: *"What makes a gradient a gradient is that it can be spent"* and Fig 13 :47: *"space has directedness"*. Fig 6 :23: *"No frame escapes its own closure. The bound moves only on contact with a frame whose closure differs."* A closed dilute frame at equilibrium cannot manufacture its own spendable difference. Sunlight, geothermal heat, freeze-thaw, wet-dry cycling, tides and mineral surfaces supply it. Crucially, Fig 6 :25: *"An import adds no atom to the world."* They add no new molecule; they give the local frame access to a difference it can spend.

3. **A substituted habitat holds the excess.** Fig 11 :66: *"Isolation is not removal of the habitat. It is substitution of one habitat for another."* Fig 11 :61: *"Introduction is available; exclusion is not."* You can add a boundary; you cannot subtract water, diffusion or the second law. The habitat is enumerated, not invented: Fig 11 :43: *"The habitat is enumerated, never composed."*

4. **A cycle restores what diffusion and hydrolysis erase.** Fig 12 :121: *"cycle. The output re-enters as input. The sign of the loop decides whether it closes, and a chain cannot close by construction."* Fig 10 :24: *"the ground does not decay, the channel does"*. A one-off evaporation or freezing event is a link, not a route.

5. **The concentrated mixture becomes an atom for the next composition.** Fig 12 :14: *"the ingredients are recorded and the arrangement is not"*; Fig 12 :104: *"A settled molecule becomes an atom for whatever composes over it."* Concentration matters because the next reaction reads the arrangement, not the ingredient list.

6. **The framework returns the shape, not the name of a mechanism.** Fig 9 :32: *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself."* Fig 13 :134: *"Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence,"* and Fig 13 :135: *"It cannot say which primitive is missing, because naming it would mean already holding it."* It cannot say whether the historical carrier was evaporation, eutectic ice, thermophoresis, adsorption or encapsulation. That is owed by the ground.

## 3. What it explicitly does not say

- Not that any one mechanism is *the* answer. The mechanism slot stays open.
- Not that the second law is suspended. The gradient is imported and spent, not created from nothing.
- Not that adding more starting material closes the residual. That changes a state, not the structure over it.
- Not that concentration alone solves the origin of life. It is one upstream link: Fig 3 :20: *"The chain is ordered by dependency, not by importance."*
- Not `grounded`. No measurement here settles the overall shape; individual mechanisms are measured by others.

## 4. What would settle it, the ground

An experiment or a rock, not a further argument. Fig 2 :29: *"An anchor is whatever settles the question and does not change its answer because of what any frame thinks. A measurement, an experiment, a counter, a market, a patient outcome."*

- **Concentration-factor test:** measure the local excess and the downstream yield in the same coupled setting. Does crossing the threshold change what the next composition produces (F1, F4)?
- **Closure test:** run a closed, well-mixed dilute reservoir with no imported difference, boundary or cycle. Does a local excess persist? The shape predicts no (F2, refuter 1).
- **Substitution test:** remove the boundary while holding the difference constant. Does the excess vanish? The shape predicts yes (F3, refuter 3).
- **Cycle test:** compare one drying or freezing event with repeated cycling. Does only the latter accumulate products? The shape predicts yes (F5).
- **Standing refuters:** the four in `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` §4, including a seventh primitive breaking the six-term set.

## 5. The honest stop

**Unreached, not unreachable.** The six-term set is *"a human's careful guess at reality's compression axes"* (They're Scanning Books); declaring it makes the choice visible, not neutral. The answer stops where the instruments can no longer distinguish mechanisms and records the lineage so the stop is checkable. Fig 2 :35: *"Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you."* Agreement among readers is not an instrument.

## 6. One-paragraph version for outreach

> The Tether does not name a single prebiotic concentration mechanism, and it does not suspend thermodynamics. It says that a dilute reservoir is a state with no magnitude, so concentration is a cross-kind composition: a spendable difference over space. That difference cannot be manufactured inside the closed dilute frame; it must be imported from a frame whose closure differs (light, heat, freeze-thaw, wet-dry, tides, a mineral surface), and the import adds no new molecule, only access to a difference that can be spent. The local excess must then be held in a substituted habitat (a brine vein, pore, drying film, vesicle or droplet), enumerated from what is actually in contact with the molecules, and restored by a cycle, because diffusion and hydrolysis erase a one-off event. Once settled, the concentrated mixture becomes an atom the next composition can read. What the framework returns is that four-part shape, with the mechanism slot left open and four falsifiers written in advance. Which route was historical is a question for experiment and geology, not for the derivation.
