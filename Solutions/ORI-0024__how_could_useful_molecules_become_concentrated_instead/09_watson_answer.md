## QUESTION

Decide, in effect terms, what must be true for useful molecules to move from a dilute, approximately uniform distribution in water to a local concentration above the threshold at which the next composition (polymerization, template copying, membrane assembly) becomes favourable. Option A: treat dilution as a shortage of starting material and look for a larger source. Option B: treat dilution as a state with no magnitude and derive the shape any source must have. What each leads to: A changes the amount without changing the structure and leaves the residual open; B gives a checkable four-part shape, four falsifiers and an honest stop, and leaves the mechanism name to the ground. This run takes B.

## VERDICT (from the figures)

The shape survives; a mechanism name does not. Dilution is a state with no magnitude, so concentration is a cross-kind composition: a spendable difference over space. That difference cannot be manufactured inside the closed dilute frame; it must be imported from a frame whose closure differs, and the import adds no new molecule, only access to a difference that can be spent. The local excess must be held in a substituted habitat enumerated from contact, restored by a cycle, and handed to the next composition once settled. The candidate mechanisms are isomers, or members of one functional group, over the same held terms. Four refuters are pre-registered in `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` §4. Which mechanism was historical is a `gap` for the figures and a task for the ground. Mode: `specified`, ungrounded, candidate.

- **cost if wrong:** bounded and locatable. The packet states mode, arrangement and refuters before the conclusion, so an error is findable rather than invisible. The real cost is spending the independence of outside readers by sending a mechanism name instead of a shape, which Fig 2 :42 prices ("Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong."). That cost is avoidable by leading with the shape and its refuter.
- **undoable:** yes. The mechanism name was never asserted, so nothing has to be withdrawn to send a corrected packet. A sent claim can be superseded, and no primitive is removed. Only the arrangement of the offer changes.
- **reversed by:** a ground reading, not agreement. A controlled experiment showing a persistent local excess in a closed dilute reservoir with no imported difference would break the shape; a demonstration that local concentration makes no difference to downstream yield would break the arrangement claim; a seventh primitive would break the six-term set; and a source-discriminating measurement that identifies one mechanism would close the mechanism gap. Until one of these fires, the verdict stands as `candidate`.
