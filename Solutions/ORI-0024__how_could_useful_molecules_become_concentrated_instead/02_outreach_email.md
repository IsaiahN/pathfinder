# Outreach draft - check the shape before you decide about the mechanism

**Use:** one-to-one, or as a cover letter attached to a submission through the public venues in `03_public_contact_points.csv`. **Not** for bulk send.

**Hard precondition:** the shape claim in `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` §3 and its four refuters must be intact before this is sent. The stage-1 output must survive, or the two-stage offer collapses on contact.

---

**Subject:** One checkable claim about prebiotic concentration, before any theory

> Hi [name],
>
> I have one specific output I would like you to check against what you already know about prebiotic chemistry. I am not asking you to read the framework first, and I am not asking you to agree with it. If the output does not survive, there is nothing else to look at.
>
> **The output (`specified`, not `grounded`):** "the concentration problem" is not a shortage of starting material. In effect terms, a dilute reservoir is a state with no structure that says "more here, less there," while the next compositions (polymerization, template copying, membrane assembly) require a local excess. So any answer must supply four things, and it must supply all four:
>
> 1. a **spendable difference** over space, not just more molecules;
> 2. an **import** of that difference from a frame whose closure differs from the dilute reservoir (light, a thermal gradient, freeze-thaw, wet-dry, tides, a mineral surface), because a closed equilibrium frame cannot manufacture its own difference;
> 3. a **substituted habitat** that holds the local excess against the reservoir (a brine vein, mineral pore, drying film, vesicle or coacervate), enumerated from what is actually in contact with the molecules;
> 4. a **cycle** that restores the difference, because diffusion and hydrolysis erase a one-off event.
>
> Once those hold, the concentrated mixture becomes an arrangement the next composition can read; settled products then become atoms for whatever composes over them. What the framework returns is this **shape**. It does not name the historical mechanism. Whether it was evaporation, eutectic ice, thermophoresis in vent pores, adsorption on clay or encapsulation is a question for experiment and geology, not for the derivation.
>
> **What I am not claiming:** that any one of those mechanisms is *the* answer; that the framework suspends the second law; that concentration alone solves the origin of life (it is one upstream link); or that this is measured. It is a specification of what to check, not a verdict.
>
> **What would refute the shape (written before this message):**
> 1. a closed, well-mixed dilute reservoir at equilibrium sustaining a local excess with no imported difference, surface, boundary or cycle;
> 2. a concentration increase with no spendable difference and no cross-kind composition, that is, pure scalar scaling of a state with no gradient directedness;
> 3. a local excess persisting in contact with the bulk reservoir with no substituted habitat, adsorption site or restoring cycle;
> 4. a seventh primitive needed to explain concentration, breaking the six-term set.
>
> If any one holds, I retract the claim. A one-line "this fails because..." is more useful to me than agreement. If you would rather not engage, no reply is needed.
>
> **If it survives, and only then:** I will send the source links. The figures, not my summary, are the authority.
>
> **If you want to use the method:** run it on a problem you know well and can check against a ground, write down what would refute your result *before* you run it, and label the mode of what you get. A reported failure is more useful to me than a reported success.
>
> - [name]

## Stage discipline

1. **Stage 1:** residual, shape claim, four refuters. Ask what the recipient would count as a failure. Record their verdict **before** any theory is shown.
2. **Stage 2, opt-in only:** if they are curious, send the three source links in `04_links_and_distribution.md`. Their later agreement is never pooled as corroboration (Fig 2 :40).
3. **Stage 3:** offer the formula as a method to reuse, each user writing their own refuter and stating their own mode.

One-to-one only. No bulk send. No ranking by likelihood of belief.
