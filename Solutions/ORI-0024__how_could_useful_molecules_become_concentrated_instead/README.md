## The question restated as an effect

What is being decided is not "which prebiotic mechanism is true" but: what must any mechanism supply so that locally useful molecules move from a dilute, approximately uniform distribution to a local concentration above the threshold at which the next composition (polymerization, catalysis, encapsulation, copying) becomes favourable? Option A: treat dilution as a shortage of material and look for a bigger source. Option B: treat dilution as the absence of a spendable difference and a retention boundary, and derive the shape any source must have. This solution takes B and records why A is a filter on the budget, not a witness for the verdict (Fig 9 :60).

## What is deliberately not here

- No personal emails of named individuals.
- No ranking of people by how likely they are to agree.
- No bulk-send plan.

Three reasons, in the figures' own words:

1. **Agreement is not an anchor.** *"Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you."* (Fig 2 :35). A list selected for receptiveness is a list selected to agree.
2. **Correlation disqualifies a verdict.** *"agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool"* (Fig 2 :40). This simulated second seat shares the primary's pool, so its agreement is internal consistency, not corroboration.
3. **Do not spend the checker's independence.** *"A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it."* (Fig 10 :42) and *"Bring a characterised residual, never the source."* (Fig 10 :52). The first contact carries the residual and its refuter, not the framework's interior.

Legally: scraping personal emails for unsolicited bulk outreach breaches GDPR and CAN-SPAM style rules in many jurisdictions. Public opt-in venues avoid that failure as well as the structural one.
