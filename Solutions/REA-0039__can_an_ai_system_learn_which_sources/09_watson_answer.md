## QUESTION

Decide, in effect terms, whether a system can form trust verdicts about sources that track the world rather than the sources' bias. Option A: yes, by weighting and aggregating sources by track record or consensus. Option B: partly, by ranging each source against a non-updating ground, buying independence, recording provenance, and making bias refusable, with an honest limit on self-certification. What each leads to: A sends trust collapsing into the common-cause pool, so the bias is inherited through the weight; B sends a checkable shape and a stated limit, so the bias is auditable and refusable but never self-certified as absent.

## DOMAIN CENSUS

R by arity, symmetry and scale, then one line per domain with actors / currency / anchor, partition or union, and order by dependency.

- **R (the residual).** The gap between a source's claim and what survives contact with a world. Arity: three conditions (weight learning, bias non-inheritance, self-certification), so a union compound, not one hard question, per Fig 9 :55 "A bundled problem is usually a union of overlapping conditions, not a list of separate ones." Symmetry: not a symmetric join, because "Correlation has valence" (Fig 2 :39); two agreeing sources are not two independent readings. Scale: per source (per slot, Fig 1 :19), and across scales from a single frame to a population of frames.
- **Domain A, the trust-verdict domain.** Actors: sources, the system, the ground (measurement, outcome, counter, board). Currency: residuals against ground and trust weights. Anchor: the ground, which does not update. Nameable anchor: yes. Question type: how to range frames against an anchor.
- **Domain B, the bias-inheritance domain.** Actors: frames, evidence pools, the weighting w. Currency: independence (shared-evidence overlap) and w. Anchor: the measured overlap and the provenance record. Nameable anchor: yes. Question type: how to keep the weight from being the bias.
- **Domain C, the self-certification domain.** Actors: the system checking its own calibration. Currency: its own output. Anchor: none available from inside (Fig 8 :34, :37). Nameable anchor: no. This is not yet a domain that can be settled internally; it is underivable-in-principle and needs an outside frame or instrument.
- **Order by dependency (Fig 3).** Define A before B (you cannot measure independence until each source's residual is defined), and C last, answered by import rather than internal search.
- **Partition or union.** Union. Unbundle before searching (Fig 9 :54), keep verdicts separate, never average (Fig 9 :60).

Strain test: an initial reading assigned Fig 2's anchor rule to self-certification. It strains. Re-census assigns self-certification to the metaframe limit (Fig 8 :34, :37), a different object. Different domains, no strain after re-census (recorded in `01` section 7).

## VERDICT (from the figures)

Partly yes, and only as a discipline. Weights are learnable from outcomes, so "learn which sources deserve trust" has a real finite sense. The weight is not the bias removed: shared-pool agreement is disqualifying as a verdict, so trust cannot come from consensus. To avoid inheriting bias, range each source separately against a ground that does not update, buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong, record provenance so convergence is told from adopted import, and carry negation so a biased frame can be refused. There is no permanent trusted-source list, because trust is relative to the residual. Self-certification of zero inherited bias is unreachable from inside; that is a `gap`, stated as a limit. The result is `general`/`specified`/`gap`, ungrounded. The strongest independent corroboration is Shumailov et al. (2024) for bias inheritance, and the epistemic-vigilance literature for the anti-reputation finding; neither supplies the discriminating instrument that would close F3.

- **cost if wrong:** bounded and locatable. The packet states mode and four refuters before the conclusion, so an error is findable rather than invisible. The real cost is spending the independence of outside readers by sending a prestige-framed claim instead of a shape with refuters. Fig 2 :42 prices it: "Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong." That cost is avoidable by leading with the union split and the refuter, and excluding maker evidence (Fig 9 :51).
- **undoable:** yes. The forbidden moves (consensus as verdict, reputation as evidence, a global trust score, a permanent whitelist) are removable without touching the learnable-weight mechanism. `07_the_tether_answer.md` and `08_expert_verification_packet.md` already remove them. A sent claim can be superseded by a corrected packet; no primitive has to be withdrawn.
- **reversed by:** a ground reading, not agreement. A source-discriminating instrument showing whether a source's output tracks ground or a shared pool would name the slot; a demonstration that shared-pool agreement adds ground evidence would break the bias-inheritance verdict; a demonstration that a frame certifies its own metaframe would break the self-certification gap; a demonstration that two systems with identical architecture, history and w diverge in trust with no bonded difference would break the isomer reading. Until one of these fires, the verdict stands as candidate.
