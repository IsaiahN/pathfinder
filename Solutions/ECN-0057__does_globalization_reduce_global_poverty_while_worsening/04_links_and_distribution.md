## What in the wider literature bears on this question

1. **Ravallion 2019 is the load-bearing external witness.** His abstract says the absolute and the country-specific relative standard move in opposite directions over the same thirty years. This is a different method and a different question from the figures, so it is genuine corroboration of the *shape*, but it is not the figure verdict and it does not attribute the movement to globalization.
2. **The World Bank PIP handbook shows the measurement anchor is constructed, not given.** It lists the steps from acquiring surveys to calculating global and regional estimates, and says all decisions and protocols are governed by a working group. That is consistent with Fig 2's warning that an anchor must not move when frames push on it: the survey line is legitimate because the question is constitutively about it, while a causal attribution built on the same surveys is not.
3. **The inequality literature refuses a single global actor.** The OWID inequality page notes large variation across countries *"even among countries with similar levels of economic development, technology adoption, or exposure to global markets"*, and Atkinson and Milanovic are cited there for the role of national institutions and policy choice. That matches Fig 11's valence-is-relative law and counts against any claim that globalization has a fixed sign for the spread residual.
4. **Source discrimination, not more argument, is what would settle the causal part.** *The Hard Problem is About Source* puts it as metamerism: several underlying causes can create identical readings. Here, a falling headcount and a rising Gini can be produced by several combinations of growth, redistribution, demographic change and trade. The remedy is a discriminating instrument, not another review essay.
5. **Overlap declaration.** All OWID pages lean on the World Bank PIP surveys. They are therefore strongly overlapping with one another and with the PIP handbook. They are one evidence pool for the measurement facts, not five independent witnesses. The essays all share an author with the figures and are also one pool.
