## 1. A shape is a constraint, not a filler

The canonical README states the limit directly: the framework returns *"what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)"*, then *"stops at the limit of what the available instruments can distinguish, and records the lineage so the stop is honest."* Fig 9 :32: **"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself"**

From *The Library of Babel*: *"This means that you can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."*

For ECN-0057 the shape is therefore **not** "globalization reduces poverty and raises inequality" and **not** the reverse. It is: any admissible answer must be a two-slot answer, with the scale of each slot named, the actor's valence stated per residual, a constitutive anchor declared for each measurement, and an explicit `gap` where no instrument settles the causal magnitude. That constrains without filling. It does not tell us the sign of any coefficient.

## 2. How the shape is obtained

*The Library of Babel* describes bootstrapping by proximity: *"With each new term gained, you narrow the space and deepen your vocabulary."* It also warns that the question itself is the tether: the books returned *"need to be tested or ranged against the problem you want answered in order to steer the direction of the next search."* And crucially for this question: *"Perhaps your problem was a bundle or conflation of several problems masquerading as one? You will need to search all of those problems individually to find their answers and then combine them in a satisfactory way."* That is the essay form of Fig 9's union rule.

Fig 9 :27-28: **"describe it before you go looking, or any frame you pick will seem to fit"** Fig 6 :30: *"outward: does another frame already hold it? The description travels. Fails by being sharpened into terms only we can read. Cast it in effect terms."* The effect terms here are "headcount against a line" and "spread within a slot", not "globalization" and "inequality" as slogans.

## 3. What licenses a name

Only two things, both outside the shape itself:

1. **The ground settles it.** `THE_FORMULA.md` step 5: *"the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays. A gate is not the ground."* A discriminating measurement would name which component of the observed movement is attributable to trade.
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs** (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8 :45), with a source gate and a shadow test.

*The Hard Problem is About Source* gives the discriminator argument: *"You can have several underlying causes that create identical readings but the definition of the output would be read as the same."* And: *"If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information"*. In this question, a headcount decline and a Gini rise can be generated by growth, redistribution, demographic change and trade in several combinations. Naming globalization as *the* source from the two readings alone is the metamerism error. What is owed is a source-discriminating instrument.

So the two readings are named freely, because they are readings we hold and can define. The **cause** is not named by the framework.

## 4. Independence and why a crowd of studies is not a verdict

*How a Society of Agents Would Work*: *"A group is only worth having while its members stay different."* It restates Fig 2's two collapses: agents updating on each other converge on *"whoever had the most influence"*, and agents that never talk but share one source produce *"the agreement is the pool talking"*. The avalanches of commentary on globalization and poverty mostly draw on the same World Bank PIP surveys, so they are one frame wearing many names.

Fig 2 :31: **"Independence, between two frames, is how little they draw on the same evidence."** Fig 7 :13: **"agreement compounds, evidence does not"** For this question, a low-overlap second frame would be a source-discriminating design (a natural experiment, a border discontinuity, a tax-record series), not another review of the same surveys.

## 5. Answer to the shape question

**What does "shape" mean here?** A constraint on any admissible answer, stated before the empirical work: two slots, each with a declared anchor and scale; the actor's valence given per residual; the cross-scale gap measured rather than assumed zero; the causal magnitude marked `gap` until a source-discriminating instrument speaks. Fig 9 :32, the canonical README, and *The Library of Babel* all say the same thing at different levels.

**Does the framework name the cause?** No. It returns the shape of the answer any cause must have. A specific causal claim is licensed only by the ground or by a provenanced import with a shadow test.

## 6. Consequences for this solution, including its own status

**Prestige is not action.** *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*: *"Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it. The Ground Maintainer must be blind to prestige."* A list of famous economists who agree about globalization is a budget to be tried, not a verdict.

**This audit is a simulation, not a ground reading.** Same essay: *"The catch is that the output is only as good as the validator. The agent, or the tool, or the frame, or the theory, cannot validate itself."* And: *"LLMs can quickly simulate an approximate answer/output with the formula found in the architecture post... However, the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself."* So `01_figure_grounded_audit.md`, `06_isomers_and_order.md`, `07_the_tether_answer.md`, `09_watson_answer.md` and `11_audit.md` are simulated derivations checked for internal consistency against the figures. They are **not** tests of the empirical claim and must be labelled as such. They cannot settle anything they report on.
