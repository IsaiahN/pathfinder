## What is deliberately not here

- No personal emails of named individuals.
- No ranking of people by how likely they are to agree with the claim.
- No bulk-send plan.

Reason, in the figures' own words: Fig 2 :35, *"Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you."* Selecting recipients for receptiveness manufactures the disqualifying correlation (Fig 2 :40). Fig 10 :42: *"A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it."*

## The verdict in one line

The figures decide that this is **two slots and a union compound, not one scalar verdict**. A global aggregate can fall while a per-slot residual stays live, and one actor can carry opposite valence for two different residuals. Whether globalization actually caused those two trends, net of other causes, is reported as `gap` from the figures and answered only by world measurement. That measurement is secondary, not a verdict handed to the figures.
