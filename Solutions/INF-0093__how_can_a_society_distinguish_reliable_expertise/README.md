## What is deliberately not here

- No personal emails of named individuals.
- No ranking of people by how likely they are to agree with this answer.
- No bulk-send plan.
- No claim that this package settles the question. It is a simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures. Fig 2 :35: *"Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you."* A second seat that shares this evidence pool is a mirror with a different name, so the agreement in `11_audit.md` is internal consistency, not corroboration.

Three figure reasons for the omissions:

1. **Agreement is not a verdict.** Fig 7 :35: *"A room's agreement is variation, not a verdict. Selection between rooms is where the ground votes."* A list selected for receptiveness is a room chosen to agree.
2. **Shared pools disqualify a verdict.** Fig 2 :40: *"disqualifying for a verdict: agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool."*
3. **The verifier must not hold the interiors.** Fig 10 :42: *"A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it."* Sending the framework before asking for a verdict spends the only thing an outside reader contributes.

Legally, unsolicited bulk email breaches GDPR and CAN-SPAM style rules in many jurisdictions. Public opt-in channels avoid that failure as well as the structural one.
