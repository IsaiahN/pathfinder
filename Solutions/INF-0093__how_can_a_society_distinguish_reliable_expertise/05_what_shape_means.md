## 1. A shape is a constraint, not a filler

The canonical rule: the framework returns *"what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)"*. Fig 9 :32: *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself."*

From *The Library of Babel* (opened): *"This means that you can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."*

For this question the shape is:

> Reliability is per-claim contact with an anchor that does not move when belief moves, measured per slot, with checkers who are independent frames ranging against that anchor separately. Social marks of credibility are frame-internal and carry zero verdict weight on their own.

That is a constraint on any answer. It does not name a trust score, a certification body, a platform, or a policy. It says what such a thing must do if it is to be an answer at all.

## 2. How the shape is obtained here

*The Library of Babel* (opened) describes the search method: use the vocabulary you already hold that is closest to the thing, note recurring terms that correlate with it, and narrow recursively. *"With each new term gained, you narrow the space by definition and deepen your vocabulary."* The searcher stops when *"the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough"* to give the shape.

The same essay gives the union warning that governs the census in `01`: *"Perhaps your problem was a bundle or conflation of several problems masquerading as one? You will need to search all of those problems individually to find their answers and then combine them in a satisfactory way."* That is Fig 9 :54 in the essay's own words, and it is why "reliable expertise" was split into ground contact and independence before any remedy was chosen.

*How a Society of Agents Would Work* (opened) gives the operational form of the shape as four rules for keeping a group wise:

1. **Votes come from outcomes, not popularity.** A result in a checker's own world is the vote. The essay adds the per-try refinement: *"Use the success rate per try, not the total."* That is Fig 1 :19, *"The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated,"* in operational clothing.
2. **Independent discoveries count most.** *"Convergent derivation and adopted import look identical in the contents. Only the record of where each came from separates them."* Provenance is the whole issue.
3. **Decay so failures can be retried.** The record is never deleted; reach can fall while the record only grows.
4. **Bad news travels in the same lane as good news.** A refusal pays the same bargain as a mint. Without negation the vocabulary can only accumulate endorsements.

The same essay states the structural form: one room per problem, a commons of methods only, and the ground under all three. *"The ground sits under all three: no store's agreement replaces it."*

## 3. Why the reading cannot supply the name: metamerism

*The Hard Problem is About Source* (opened) is the essay that bears most directly on this question. An instrument reads green and *"the instrument is speaking only about what it measured, it cannot comment on the source."* The source might be one green light, or yellow plus blue, or RGB, or CMYK, or a scheme not yet discovered. *"If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information."*

That is exactly the situation with a persuasive claim. Agreement, fluency, confidence and credentials are one reading with several possible sources: contact with a ground, coherent drift, or a shared evidence pool. The reading does not determine the source. The essay's resolution is the important part: *"It was proven wrong not by argument, but by the arrival of a better instrument."* So the answer to "how can a society distinguish" is not a better argument about trust. It is a better instrument that discriminates among sources. The framework can specify the shape of that instrument (per slot, against an immovable ground, independent checkers). It cannot specify which one, because that depends on the domain and on what can be measured there.

## 4. What licenses a name, when a name is needed

Two things, both outside the shape:

1. **The ground settles it.** `THE_FORMULA.md` step 5: *"the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays. A gate is not the ground."*
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs.** Fig 6 :23: *"No frame escapes its own closure. The bound moves only on contact with a frame whose closure differs."* Fig 8 requires a source gate and a shadow test.

**Correction to my own first reading.** I initially wrote that "shape, not a name" forbids naming any instrument for this question. That overstates the rule. The canonical phrase governs a *missing* primitive: *"what a missing thing would have to look like."* A Brier score, a replication protocol and a zero-knowledge proof are not missing primitives of the six-term set. They are bindings from other frames onto capacities the framework already describes (a per-slot residual, a second frame that ranges separately, a verifier without reconstruction). So naming them is permitted as provenance, provided the import is recorded. What remains forbidden is claiming that the framework itself derives the name or that adding one moves the wall. Both the first reading and this correction are kept visible here rather than silently replaced.

## 5. Prestige is not action

*The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap* (opened) states the rule that governs the outreach artifacts:

> *"Prestige must never be allowed to validate an action directly."*
> *"Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it. The Ground Maintainer must be blind to prestige."*

A credential, a famous journal, a large follower count and an institutional title are prestige. They can give a claim a budget to be tried. They cannot settle it. Fig 10 :61 adds the sharper point: *"And standing is a stake that survives the outcome it was earned on."* That is why `02_outreach_email.md` carries no CV and no institution prestige, and why the verifier is denied evidence bearing only on the maker (Fig 9 :51).

The same essay states the limit of the whole exercise, including this file: *"the output is only as good as the validator. The agent, or the tool, or the frame, or the theory, cannot validate itself."*

## 6. This audit is a simulation, not a ground reading

From the same essay (opened): *"LLMs can quickly simulate an approximate answer/output with the formula found in the architecture post. It will often be better than the generic outputs that you will usually get otherwise... However, the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself."*

So `01_figure_grounded_audit.md`, this file, and `09_watson_answer.md` are simulated derivations checked for internal consistency against the figures. They are not a test of any society and cannot settle the question they report on. They state a shape and a falsifier, then stop where the instruments stop. Fig 13 :134: *"Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence"*.

## Falsifier for this file's reading

If the framework is shown to return a name for a missing primitive, rather than a shape, then section 1 is wrong and the claim that this question yields a shape rather than an instrument name collapses. Equivalently: if a social mark of credibility is shown to be a reading of the ground rather than a projection with several possible sources, section 3 fails and the metamerism argument is withdrawn.
