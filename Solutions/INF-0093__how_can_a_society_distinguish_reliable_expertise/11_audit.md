## Independent domain census (written from the residual line alone)

I started from the residual as an effect statement before reading the primary's census: what a society can observe is a credibility reading, and what it cannot observe is whether the claim pays against something that does not move when belief moves. I then did the census in the WATSON order.

- **R (arity / symmetry / scale).** Arity: two conditions bind, a ground contact and a checking arrangement whose independence can be measured; that is a **union**, not one property (Fig 9 :54). Symmetry: the credibility reading is symmetric under exchange of frames, so agreement between A and B is the same datum as agreement between B and A and carries no direction; ground contact is asymmetric. Scale: step, cycle (track record), level (institution), seat (who authors conventions), lineage (standing that outlives its outcome).
- **Domain 1, the claim and its ground.** Actors: claim, predictor, outcome. Currency: payment against a residual. Anchor: the world. Nameable: yes.
- **Domain 2, the social reading.** Actors: people, claims, prestige, repetition. Currency: influence and credibility accrued. Anchor: none internal. By the census rule this is not yet a domain; it is a failure mode of the arrangement.
- **Domain 3, the checking seat.** Actors: checkers and the institution hosting them. Currency: independence. Anchor: the ground, kept reachable by a seat that may author conventions but not verdicts.
- **Compound.** A certifying institution has a reputation to protect, so it becomes a frame with a stake. The union condition binds here: a correct claim can be lost and a wrong claim can be certified in the same room.
- **Order by dependency.** Instrument before separation, because independence needs a common anchor to range against; settlement before carry, because nothing composes over an unsettled term.

My route into this census was **Fig 8's union/surplus law and Fig 10's seat offices**, not the primary's starting points of Fig 2 and Fig 1. Different starting figures, same domain boundaries. That is weak independence of route inside one evidence pool, not corroboration.

## Round trip gap R_T

The Symbols table defines R_T as gap(x, (T_E compose T_A)(x)), the amount the coarser description cannot hold. Fig 4 :30 requires it to be measured rather than assumed. I ran the round trip on the condensation this folder actually uses.

1. **Sent up (x):** the verbatim figure set, chiefly the anchor definition, the frame-internal disqualification, the verifier non-access rule, the per-slot law, the union law and the standing-as-stake rule.
2. **Abstraction (T_A):** the slogan used in `07_the_tether_answer.md`, "score claims against a ground and keep checkers independent".
3. **Concretisation (T_E):** a concrete policy, for example a public panel that publishes a reliability score for claims.

**R_T is nonzero.** The following constraints in x do not survive the trip down into the slogan:

- the anchor must not move when belief moves (Fig 2 :25, :29);
- independence is quantitative and must be bought in proportion to the cost of being wrong (Fig 2 :31, :42; Fig 8 :47);
- the residual is per slot, so one trust number throws away the live signal (Fig 1 :19, :22);
- the verifier must be denied the maker's standing and, where possible, the ability to reconstruct the claim (Fig 9 :51; Fig 10 :42);
- the problem is a union and must be split before a remedy is chosen (Fig 9 :54);
- the certifying institution is itself a stake and can be disqualified (Fig 10 :61, :93);
- a refusal must be available in the vocabulary, otherwise the system can only accumulate endorsements (Operators table, negation).

One thing survives cleanly: agreement is not a verdict, and contact with an immovable ground is. R_T was **not** measured numerically, because no ground reading of a society was taken. It is recorded qualitatively as the enumerated set of dropped constraints. Treating the formula as identical to the law set would set R_T to zero and would commit the dead-reckoning error Fig 4 :30 names. The honest form is a lossy condensation with a measured gap.

## Strains and re-census

- **Human judgment as an anchor.** The primary initially tested Fig 2 :36-38 as a competing rule. This matches the canonical rule that a reading which makes figures contradict one another has misidentified its domain.
- **Isomer versus functional group.** Fig 12 :117 and :123 appear to give two answers for the two collapses. Re-census resolves it by level: isomer at the fine grain of the bond, functional group at the coarse grain of the shared outcome. Fig 12 :103 permits both. Not strained.
- **Honest signalling caveat.** The animal-behaviour source says both that costly signals are reliable and that honest signals need not be costly and the principle remains empirically unconfirmed. This is a world-side caveat, not a figure strain. The figures never claim that cost settles a claim; Fig 1 :27 disqualifies frame-internal cost-like quantities. Reported, not hidden.
- **After re-census: no figure strains remain.** The one world-side caveat is recorded in `09_watson_answer.md`.
