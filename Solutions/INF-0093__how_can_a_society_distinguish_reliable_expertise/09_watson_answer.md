# WATSON answer: how can a society distinguish reliable expertise from persuasive but poorly grounded claims?

**Mode: `general` on the shape, `specified` on the instruments, not `grounded`.**

**Status: SIMULATED DERIVATION, not a ground reading.** This run synthesizes `01_figure_grounded_audit.md`, `05_what_shape_means.md`, `06_isomers_and_order.md`, `07_the_tether_answer.md` and `08_expert_verification_packet.md`. It is checked for internal consistency against the figures. *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap* (opened 2026-11-23) says LLM output of this kind is a simulation and *"the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself."* It cannot settle the question it reports on. No measurement of any society was taken.

## QUESTION

Decide, in effect terms, what a society would have to measure to separate a claim that pays against the world from a claim whose standing comes from persuasion. Option A: treat the visible marks of credibility (confidence, fluency, repetition, credentials, consensus, endorsement) as evidence of reliability and build institutions that amplify them. Option B: treat those marks as frame-internal quantities with zero verdict weight, require per-contact with an immovable ground and measurable independence of the checkers, and leave the specific instrument open until a ground settles it. A leads to a system that can only accrete endorsements; B leads to a shape, a dependency order, a pre-registered refuter and an honest stop.

## DOMAIN CENSUS

R by arity, symmetry and scale, then one line per domain with actors / currency / anchor, partition or union, and order by dependency.

- **R (the residual).** The gap between the appearance of reliability and contact with a ground. Arity: two conditions bind, ground contact and independence of the checkers, so a **union** compound (Fig 9 :54), not one property. Symmetry: agreement is symmetric between frames and carries no direction; ground contact is asymmetric, the ground ranges the frame and the frame cannot range the ground. Scale: step (one claim, one checker), cycle (a scored track record), level (institution, market, court), seat (who may author conventions), lineage (standing that survives an outcome).
- **Domain 1, claim-ground contact.** Actors: a claim, a predictor, an outcome. Currency: residual R = |Γ(b,a) - o′| and payment. Anchor: the world, a measurement, experiment, counter, market or patient outcome. This is where reliability is constituted.
- **Domain 2, social transmission.** Actors: people, claims, prestige, repetition, social identity. Currency: agreement, influence, credibility accrued. Anchor: **none internal to the frame**, so on its own this is not a domain; it becomes one only when ranged against Domain 1's ground. Fig 1 :26 lists credibility accrued as frame-internal.
- **Domain 3, the checking arrangement.** Actors: checkers, journals, markets, courts, institutions. Currency: independence between frames. Anchor: the ground, kept reachable by a seat that may not author what it checks.
- **Domain 1 intersect Domain 3, the compound.** Actors: a certifying institution and its reputation. Currency: standing and provenance. Anchor: the ground, if the institution does not move it. A ground with a reputation to protect is disqualified.
- **Order by dependency (Fig 3).** Perceive the claim as predicting; find an instrument already returning a signal; range independent frames separately against it; settle at the ground; carry the record with refusals intact. The instrument feeds back from independent searchers, so the loop is a cycle that must be kept open.

Strain test applied: Fig 2 :36-38 makes human judgment the right anchor for "is this a good answer for a person." Re-census shows this governs a different question from the one about claims about the world, so it is not a strain. No strains remain after re-census.

## VERDICT (from the figures)

A society distinguishes reliable expertise from persuasive claims by refusing to let the visible marks of credibility decide anything. A claim earns standing only by paying, per claim and per domain, against an anchor that does not move when belief moves; the checkers must be independent frames ranging against that anchor separately, denied the claimant's standing and as far as possible unable to reconstruct the claim. Agreement produced by mutual influence or a shared pool is a reading of the social frame, not of the claim, and carries zero verdict weight. Two collapses that look the same as agreement are isomers with opposite remedies. Credentials and institutional endorsement are provenance about a lineage, not readings of the current claim, and a ground with a reputation to protect is disqualified. The shape and the dependency order are `general` and `specified` respectively; nothing here is `grounded`. The framework gives the shape of the instrument, not its name, and the name is owed to a ground reading of a particular domain.

- **cost if wrong:** if the shape is wrong, institutions built on it will discard usable testimony whenever it lacks a scored track record, and will overvalue formally independent panels whose evidence pools are in fact shared. The second error is the costly one because it is invisible: agreement feels like truth, so nobody notices the drift (Fig 2 :34). The cost is bounded by the pre-registered refuter, which makes the error findable rather than invisible. Fig 2 :42: buy independence in proportion to this cost.
- **undoable:** yes. The shape is a filter on what may count as evidence, not an irreversible deletion. Instruments can be added, independence can be increased, and a certifying institution can be made ineligible to score its own output. Fig 6 says a refusal adds to the record and removes standing to compose; nothing is deleted, so a later change can restore reach. The outreach artifacts are one-to-one and opt-in, so no mass commitment has been made.
- **reversed by:** a ground reading, not agreement. A controlled test showing consensus or credentials predicting accuracy as well as scored outcomes with no access to outcomes; an anchor that moves when belief moves; shared-pool frames failing no more often than separate-instrument frames; a checker able to reconstruct the claim producing sound verdicts at the same rate; or a credential shown to register current contact rather than earned lineage. Any one fires the pre-registered refuter and withdraws the shape. Until one does, the verdict stands as `candidate`.
