## Distribution: offer, do not push

Figure-grounded rules:

- **Fig 4 :22** - *"Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down."* Send the checking method and the refuter, not a list of certified conclusions. A recording presented as knowledge is a description of one occasion.
- **Fig 10 :42** - *"A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it."* If a reader has absorbed the whole framework before giving a verdict, their agreement is worthless as evidence.
- **Fig 9 :27-28** - *"describe it before you go looking, or any frame you pick will seem to fit."* Ask what they would expect to see if the claim were true before showing them any source.
- **Fig 2 :40** - *"disqualifying for a verdict: agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool."* No mass send to a receptive list.
- **Fig 2 :42** - *"Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong."* Choose venues whose reviewers are unlikely to share this package's evidence pool. The judgment and decision community and the replication community are low overlap with the framework author.
- **Fig 12 :125-126** - *"A composition costs in two currencies, and they do not add."* The cost of stating the claim and the cost of settling it are different. Do not present a short email as if it had paid the settling cost.

**Recommended order in a reply:** (1) the shape claim and its refuter; (2) ask what they would count as a failure and record the answer; (3) only then the three public source links, with the note that the figures, not this summary, are the authority.

## The two-stage offer

1. **Stage 1:** residual, three consequences, refuter. The recipient checks the output without holding the framework's interior. Fig 10 :52: *"Bring a characterised residual, never the source."*
2. **Stage 2, opt-in:** if curious, send the sources. Later agreement is never pooled as corroboration, because the reader now shares the evidence pool (Fig 2 :40).
3. **Stage 3:** offer the loop as a method to reuse. Each user writes their own refuter before running it and states a mode. The method crosses up; the answer does not.

**Hard precondition:** stages 1 to 3 only work if stage 1 survives. The refuter in `08_expert_verification_packet.md` is the instrument for checking that, and it was written before this derivation was concluded.
