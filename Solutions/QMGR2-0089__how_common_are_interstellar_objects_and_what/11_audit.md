## Round trip gap R_T

The Symbols table defines R_T as the gap between what was sent up and what came back down, `gap(x, (T_E ∘ T_A)(x))`; Fig 4 :30 requires it to be measured rather than assumed. I ran the round trip on the condensation this folder actually uses.

1. **Sent up (x):** the verbatim figure set, chiefly Fig 1 :19-22 (per-slot residual), Fig 4 :30 (measured gap), Fig 9 :54 and :32 (union and shape), Fig 11 :43 (habitat enumeration).
2. **Abstraction (T_A):** the shorthand carried in `01` and `07`: count is per characterized survey slot and budget-limited; scaling carries a gap; reveal returns a class plus a discriminator; ejecta mechanisms are enumerated; 1I and 2I are a functional group.
3. **Concretisation (T_E):** the concrete literature claims: near 0.1 au^-3; 10^4 bodies inside Neptune and 10^26 in the Galaxy; 2 to 50 percent of stellar mass in ejected objects; a metallicity range for 2I and 3I; one asteroid-like and one comet-like interloper.

**R_T is nonzero.** Three things in the shorthand do not survive the trip back down:

- the **selection function** behind each number density estimate (the shorthand says "per slot" without carrying the survey depth, area and cadence that make a slot an anchor);
- the **size-frequency distribution** on which both the local-to-galactic scaling and the mass budget depend (a wrong SFD changes the mass estimate by orders of magnitude);
- the **discriminator** that would move a parent-system class toward a name (the shorthand says "class plus instrument" without carrying the isotope or ammonia measurement).

Conversely, one thing survives cleanly: unbundle count from reveal, keep the residual per survey, measure the scaling gap, and refuse a unique name. That is the residue worth sending. R_T was **not** measured numerically, because no ground reading exists; it is recorded qualitatively as the enumerated set of dropped constraints. Treating "the formula is the condensed version of the metalaws" as identity would set R_T to zero and would be the dead-reckoning error Fig 4 :30 names.
