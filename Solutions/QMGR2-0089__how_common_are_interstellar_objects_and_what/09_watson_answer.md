## QUESTION

Decide, in effect terms, what the Tether says about the two-part question: how common interstellar objects are, and what they can reveal about other planetary systems. Option A: treat abundance as one settled scalar and composition as naming one parent system. Option B: unbundle count from reveal; hold abundance per characterized survey slot as a budget-limited residual, and return a class of parent systems plus a discriminator for the reveal. What each leads to: A publishes a density with hidden selection-function assumptions and a named source, and spends the independence of the people checking it; B publishes a shape, four refuters written in advance, and the instruments that would close each half.

Count: a survey predicts a detection count Γ(b,a) from an assumed density and selection function; the sky returns o′; R = |Γ(b,a) − o′| is indexed by survey-object slot. Symmetry: count is a scalar per slot, source inference is many-to-one. Scale: local au^-3 to galactic 10^25 to 10^26 objects. Fig 9 names a union compound, so the two halves are unbundled before search.
- **Domain 1, detection and abundance.** Actors: characterized surveys (Pan-STARRS), informal surveys (Borisov discovery), future surveys (Rubin/LSST, NEO Surveyor), interlopers 1I/2I/3I. Currency: detection counts, number density, Poisson interval. Anchor: the characterized sky survey, which does not update on a model's preference. Type: partition, detection versus non-detection per slot.
- **Domain 2, formation and ejection.** Actors: protoplanetary disks, giant planets, planetesimals, birth cluster, stellar flybys. Currency: ejected mass per star, size-frequency distribution, Safronov number. Anchor: debris disks and simulations. Type: habitat enumerated from the world, never composed.
- **Domain 3, composition and inference.** Actors: CO, H2O, NH3, N2, isotope ratios, spectrographs, parent stars. Currency: production rates and metallicity ranges. Anchor: spectroscopy. Type: lossy projection, many parents to one reading.
- **Domain 4, verification and outreach.** Actors: expert readers, proposal reviewers. Currency: agreement and residual. Anchor: the ground, never agreement. All sources in this run draw on the same three objects, so this domain's independence is near zero.
- **Order by dependency.** Perceive (a survey detects an interloper), acquire vocabulary (n, SFD, Safronov number), fix the objective (what counts as "common", a comparison), plan (design a deeper survey), learn and carry. The chain stops at the objective: the threshold is ungrounded, so survey effort alone cannot rescue it. More search is still the right move for the Poisson half of the residual.

## VERDICT (from the figures)

The shape survives and the names do not. Unbundle count from reveal. Keep the count residual per characterized survey slot; the near-0.1 au^-3 density is a budget-limited Poisson estimate whose threshold for "common" is ungrounded while the size distribution is unconstrained, so the instruction is more independent survey search rather than a new object class. Scaling that count to a galactic population carries a round-trip gap that must be measured, so the mass-budget claim stays `specified`. The reveal returns a class of parent systems plus a discriminating instrument, never a unique star, because different routes can arrive at the same substance. Ejection mechanisms are enumerated from the world; 1I and 2I share a functional group, not an atom inventory. This is a `specified`, ungrounded result, reported per domain and never averaged. The strongest single outreach point is the honest one: unreachable at this budget is a verdict, not a silence.

- **cost if wrong:** bounded and locatable. The packet states mode, four refuters and the lineage before the conclusion, so an error is findable rather than invisible. The real cost is spending the independence of outside readers by sending a recording (a named star, a settled density) instead of a generator. Fig 4 :30 prices dead reckoning, and Fig 2 :42 says "Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong." That cost is avoidable by leading with the shape and its refuter.
- **undoable:** yes. The defective readings are removable without touching the derivation: the single-scalar density, the invertible count-to-mass assumption, the named parent star and the isomer claim are all withdrawn or narrowed in `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` section 6 and `06_isomers_and_order.md`. A sent claim can be superseded by the corrected packet, and no primitive has to be withdrawn.
- **reversed by:** a ground reading, not agreement. A characterized survey that shrinks the Poisson interval and constrains the size distribution would settle the count half; an invertible count-to-mass map would set R_T to zero; an isotope or volatile discriminator that separates candidate parents would narrow the reveal toward a name; a shared anchor for count and source would break the union reading. Until one of these fires, the verdict stands as `candidate`.
