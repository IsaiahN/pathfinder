## What is deliberately not here

- No personal emails of named individuals.
- No ranking of people by how likely they are to agree with the claim.
- No bulk-send plan.

Three reasons, in the figures' own words:

1. **A room does not upgrade the label.** Adding people raises the vocabulary and does not add an anchor (Seat Map, as quoted in the exemplar). Fig 2 :35: "Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you."
2. **Correlation disqualifies a verdict.** Selecting recipients for receptiveness manufactures exactly the correlation that makes their agreement worthless. Fig 2 :40: "agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool." Fig 8 :49: "Two frames that overlap almost completely are one frame wearing two names."
3. **Asking for trust before checking spends independence.** Fig 10 :42: "A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it." Fig 10 :52: "Bring a characterised residual, never the source."

Legally: scraping personal emails for unsolicited bulk outreach breaches GDPR and CAN-SPAM style rules in many jurisdictions. That is a practical failure on top of the structural one. The venues in `03_public_contact_points.csv` are public institutional submission routes or opt-in channels.

## Suggested next step

Take `08_expert_verification_packet.md` and `02_outreach_email.md` to the public venues in `03`. The audience that can help is the one that has opted in and is positioned to refute the shape claim, not a purchased list of believers. A reported failure is more useful than an endorsement.
