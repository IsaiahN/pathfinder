**Mode: `specified` on the shape, `general` on the barrier rules, `gap` on protocol ranking. Not `grounded`.** No AI system was tested. This is a simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures; it cannot settle the question it reports on.

## 1. The claim, in one paragraph

The interaction that lets an AI system learn the perspectives and expectations of different people is constrained by six necessary conditions, and by no named protocol. It must create contact with a frame whose closure differs from the system's; it must score each person's prediction as that person's residual; it must check against a response the system did not author; it must draw on people with genuinely separate evidence; it must carry the person's history as a provenanced import and permit refusal of the system's generic guess; and it must occur in the real habitat rather than a substituted harness. These are a conjunction over a union, not a menu. Which named interaction protocol best realises them is an open `gap`, not a hidden answer.

## 2. Three checkable sub-claims

### C1 : Contact with a differing closure (framework-internal; checkable by reading Figures 6 and 8)
- **Statement:** a person's expectation is underivable from the system's closed library; only contact with a differing frame adds the needed atom.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 6 :23 "No frame escapes its own closure. The bound moves only on contact with a frame whose closure differs." Fig 8 :45 "Two frames together reach what neither reaches alone." Fig 8 :32 "Closure is idempotent. Composing inside a closed set never leaves it, so no search, however long, adds an atom."
- **Status:** triangulated (Fig 6 closure/reachability and Fig 8 union surplus, distinct arguments).
- **What to check:** is the person's expectation a term in a frame whose closure differs from the system's library, or is it already derivable from that library? If it is already derivable, the contact condition does not bite.
- **Refuter:** per-person residual falls and stays down with no new contact with a differing frame, for several people and situations, with provenance showing no import.

### C2 : Per-person attribution and an anchor that does not update (partly framework-internal, partly empirical; checkable by reading Figures 1 and 2)
- **Statement:** the residual is indexed per person; a pooled score destroys the signal; and the correct anchor for a question about people is human judgment, provided that anchor does not update on the system's own output.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 1 :19 "The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated." Fig 1 :22 "Aggregating across slots is how a live signal disappears into an average." Fig 2 :37 "Human judgment is the right anchor for is this a good answer for a person, because the question is about people." Fig 2 :33 "The anchor must not update." Fig 2 :35 "Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you."
- **Status:** triangulated for attribution (Fig 1 and the Symbols table); single-source for the anchor rule (Fig 2).
- **What to check:** in the recipient's own work, is the measurement per person or pooled? If the anchor is shown only system-generated summaries, is there any reading left that the system did not author?
- **Refuter:** one pooled signal preserves held-out per-person residuals as well as attributed scoring, or people exposed only to system summaries match an unupdating-anchor control, with no coherent drift over a declared horizon.

### C3 : History, refusal, and habitat (checkable by reading Figures 5, 9, 11 and 12)
- **Statement:** what crosses is the person's history in effect terms; the system must be able to refuse a held generic prediction; and the interaction must be situated in the real habitat.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 6 :30 "The description travels. Fails by being sharpened into terms only we can read. Cast it in effect terms." Fig 5 :53 "refuted: a term we hold is wrong; a term is owed that says so." Fig 12 :140 "A vocabulary that cannot refuse can only accumulate." Fig 11 :40 "An improvement that does not change contact changes nothing, however much it improves." Fig 11 :70 "A test harness is a substituted habitat. Which is why a synthetic solve proves wiring and never capability."
- **Status:** triangulated for refusal (Fig 5 and Fig 12); single-source for the habitat claim (Fig 11).
- **What to check:** can the recipient's system name a case where a held prediction about a person was revoked rather than merely qualified? Does a synthetic persona benchmark predict performance with the same people in situ?
- **Refuter:** removing history and situated corrections changes nothing in novel situations; no term ever loses standing to be composed over after repeated attributed contradictions; or substituted and real habitats transfer with no loss.

## 5. What would settle it

The green-light/spectrometer case in *The Hard Problem is About Source* is the model: "If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information."
2. The attribution ablation (pooled versus per-person scoring) in §2 C2.
3. The independence manipulation (disjoint versus shared evidence) in §2 C2.
