## Round trip gap R_T

The Symbols table defines R_T as `gap(x, (T_E ∘ T_A)(x))`, the gap between what was sent up and what came back down. Fig 4 :30 requires it to be measured rather than assumed zero. I ran the round trip on the condensation the folder actually uses.

1. **Sent up (x):** the verbatim figure set, chiefly Fig 1 :19 and :22, Fig 2 :29 and :33, Fig 6 :23, Fig 8 :45, Fig 11 :39 and :70.
2. **Abstraction (T_A):** the shorthand in `07`: contact with a differing closure, per-person residual, unupdating anchor, independence, history and refusal, real habitat. Six conditions, union of two compounds, no named protocol.
3. **Concretisation (T_E):** a proposed experiment on an AI system: per-person held-out accuracy, attribution ablation, independence manipulation, habitat transfer, and a source-discriminating test.

**R_T is nonzero.** Three things the shorthand holds do not survive the trip back down without an instrument:

- **Which anchor reading is unupdating.** The shorthand says "the person's response." The concrete case must decide whether a blind judgment, an unprompted behaviour, or a prompted rating qualifies once the system can see it. Fig 2 :33 governs the property, not the operationalisation.
- **How independence is measured.** The shorthand says "separate evidence." The concrete case must specify disjoint sources and show that overlap has no residual effect. Fig 2 :31-32 gives the quantity, not a protocol.
- **What counts as a real habitat.** Fig 11 enumerates actors, conditions and relations; the concrete case must list them for a given person before claiming a habitat was reproduced. A persona prompt is a substituted habitat, and the gap between the two is exactly what the transfer test measures.

Conversely, one thing survives cleanly: the residual must be indexed per person. That condition is stated in the figure at the same level of specificity at which it would be measured. R_T was **not** measured numerically, because no ground reading exists. It is recorded qualitatively as the enumerated set of dropped constraints. Treating "the formula is a condensed version of the metalaws" as identity would set R_T to zero and would be the dead-reckoning error Fig 4 :30 names.
