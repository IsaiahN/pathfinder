## 1. A shape is a constraint, not a filler

The figures README states the limit: the framework returns "what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)." Fig 9 :32: "you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself." Fig 13 :133: "what it returns is a shape rather than a name."

From *The Library of Babel*: "you can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."

So for this question a **shape** is the set of properties that any interaction must have if it is to reduce a person-slotted residual. It constrains; it does not select a product. "Contact with a differing closure, per-person attribution, an unupdating anchor, carried history, possible refusal, a real habitat" is a shape. "Use multi-turn role-play with a persona prompt" is a name. The first is derived; the second is a candidate that still owes a ground reading.

The *Society of Agents* essay adds the discipline note: a design sketch is not a measurement, and "where those fields and the figures disagree, the figures govern." That is why the protocol ranking is declared a `gap` rather than filled with a plausible-sounding method.

## 2. How this shape was obtained

- *Library of Babel*, on vocabulary bootstrapping: "use the existing vocabulary you do know that is closest to the words that you wish to describe and grasp the shape of." Recurse on overlapping terms. Here the starting vocabulary was `frame`, `closure`, `residual`, `anchor`, `import`, `w`, `habitat`.
- Fig 6 :30: "The description travels. Fails by being sharpened into terms only we can read. Cast it in effect terms." Fig 9 :30-31: "your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect rather than the cause." So the residual was stated as a per-person prediction gap, not as "lack of empathy" or "missing theory of mind."
- *Decoding Tacit Knowledge*: "When contact isn't possible, the best you can send is a plain description of what you'd expect to see, because anyone can check that against the world." This is the operational form of the two figure lines above and it is why the refuters in `01` are stated as observable outcomes.
- *Library of Babel*, on stopping: "you will know that you are done because the finish line will also be the starting place." Here the loop stops when the conditions are stated in a form a stranger can refute, not when a preferred protocol has been named.

## 3. What would license a name

Only two things, both outside the shape itself:

1. **The ground settles it.** Formula step 5: "the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays. A gate is not the ground." The figures README: the framework "stops at the limit of what the available instruments can distinguish, and records the lineage so the stop is honest."
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs** (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8 :50-52), with a source gate and a shadow test. A person's history can be imported this way; a protocol's superiority cannot be asserted this way without a ground reading.

*The Hard Problem is About Source* gives the reason a benchmark score cannot do the licensing: "If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information." The instrument "cannot comment on the source." It was the spectrometer, not an argument, that resolved the green-light case. The analogous instrument for REA-0065 is characterised in effect terms in audit §4 and is not held.

*They're Scanning Books When They Should* sharpens the import rule: "The real import is not a new term in the existing space. It is a new dimension of the space." No new dimension was imported here; the answer is a composition of figure rules already held.

## 4. Why the question's own vocabulary is the wrong starting point

"Perspectives and expectations" invites a search for a mental module, a theory-of-mind component, or a named empathy mechanism. That is frame-first, not residual-first. Fig 9 :53: "Residual first, frame second." *Decoding Tacit Knowledge* names the failure as projection: "a reading with no encounter is projection." The essay's pretext/context/subtext distinction is the precise vocabulary for what is missing. Text carries context and some subtext, "but it almost never carries the pretext, the 'you had to be there' part." The system's error is not that it lacks a module; it is that it reads a compressed message without the history that makes it mean something.

The same essay supplies the constructive side: understanding "gets rebuilt by the learner, and the way it gets rebuilt is contact: trying, being corrected, and trying again, in both directions." It also supplies the guard: "Long, close contact pulls the frames together, so the process that makes communication easy slowly uses up that extra reach." That is why independence (F4) is a condition and not a nice-to-have.

## 5. Q: Does the shape forbid naming any interaction at all? ANSWERED, with a correction to my own earlier reading

**First reading (now narrowed).** I initially treated "the framework returns a shape, not a name" as a blanket prohibition on asserting anything specific about interactions, and nearly recorded the whole answer as a gesture toward "some kind of contact."

**Correction.** The README's phrase governs "what a **missing** thing would have to look like." In this question nothing missing is being named. Contact with a differing closure, per-slot residuals, an anchor that does not update, carried history, refusal, and a real habitat are all already figure-derived constraints on a known quantity, not inventions of a seventh primitive. Asserting them is derivation, not naming. What remains forbidden is (a) ranking named protocols as the winner and (b) claiming a ground result without a ground reading. Both claims are kept visible so neither the first over-correction nor the original reading is silently lost. Triangulated (README + Fig 6 + Fig 9).

## 6. Two consequences for outreach

**Prestige is not action.** *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*: "Prestige must never be allowed to validate an action directly... Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it. The Ground Maintainer must be blind to prestige." A list of well-known researchers who agree with the shape is the named collapse. The legitimate ask is to give the claim a budget to be tried and let the ground, a person's held-out response, settle it.

**My own audit is a simulation, not a ground reading.** Same essay: "LLMs can quickly simulate an approximate answer/output... However, the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself." So `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` is a simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures. It is **not** a test of any AI system and must be labelled as such. It cannot settle anything it reports on. The same applies to `06`, `07`, and `09`.
