## QUESTION

What kinds of interaction enable an AI system to learn the perspectives and expectations of different people? Restated as an effect: which interactions drive `R_s = |Γ(b,a) − o′|` down for each person-slot `s` separately, across situations? Option A: name a winning protocol. Option B: derive the necessary shape and declare the ranking a `gap`. What each leads to: A sends a groundless uniqueness claim and invites a pooled benchmark that hides the residual; B sends a falsifiable conjunction of conditions and an honest stop. The figures govern the choice. Fig 1 :19: "The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated."

## DOMAIN CENSUS

R by arity, symmetry, scale; one line per domain with actors / currency / anchor; union or partition; order by dependency.

- **R (arity / symmetry / scale).** Three-place, system, person, situation, indexed by person-slot. Not symmetric: swapping people changes the term, because each has a history and a weighting `w`. Scale: single interaction loop, social habitat, population membrane. The transform between scales is lossy (Fig 4).
- **D1, the person as a frame.** Actors: the person, belief `b`, weighting `w`, history. Currency: expectations and predictions. Anchor: the person's revealed behaviour and judgment, legitimate here because the question is about people (Fig 2 :37). Admitted.
- **D2, the system's interaction loop.** Actors: system, that person, situation. Currency: per-slot residual and corrections. Anchor: a reading the system did not author. Admitted.
- **D3, the social habitat.** Actors: many people, conditions, relations. Currency: contact and independence. Anchor: each person's ground separately; the habitat is enumerated, not composed. Admitted.
- **D4, the import channel.** Actors: peer frames, corpus, instruments. Currency: provenanced atoms and head starts. Anchor: the ground plus the provenance record. Admitted.
- **X1, a generic model of people.** Actors: an average. Currency: one pooled score. Anchor: none at person-slot level. Not a domain yet (Fig 1 :22).
- **Union, not partition.** C1 contact and import, C2 attribution and unupdating anchor. Fig 9 :54: "And when the split lands on a union rather than a partition, unbundle before you search again." Dependency order: residual described, contact made, history imported, anchor maintained, generic term refused (Fig 3; Formula step 2).

Strain test: two-way contact (meaning) versus an unupdating anchor (accuracy) initially looked contradictory. Re-census assigns them to different figures and different jobs; no strain remains. The Kosinski conflict is handled in §4 of audit `01`: task accuracy is a reading that cannot comment on source, so the verdict is unchanged.

## VERDICT (from the figures)

The figure-derived verdict is the shape, not a protocol. Six conditions are necessary for an interaction to let a system learn the perspectives and expectations of different people: contact with a differing closure; per-person attribution; an anchor the system cannot author; independence between the people; carried history with the possibility of refusal; and a real, enumerated habitat. They form a union of two compounds and a molecule with an ordered interior, not a menu. The ranking of named protocols is a declared `gap`. The Kosinski conflict was returned to the census and subordinated to the ground/reading distinction; it did not flip the verdict. Mode `specified`, ungrounded, candidate.

- **cost if wrong:** bounded and locatable. Each condition carries a refuter and a mode, so a failure is findable rather than diffuse. The real cost is spending the independence of outside readers by sending a recording, a named protocol and a benchmark score, instead of a generator with a refuter. Fig 2 :42 prices that: "Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong."
- **undoable:** yes. Nothing is deleted if a condition falls. Fig 6 amendment: "What is recorded only grows. What is reachable is derived, and it can fall." The reach falls because something was added (a refusal), and the lineage in `01` and `11` lets a later reader locate the error.
- **reversed by:** any of the six pre-registered refuters in audit `01` §5, in particular a per-person residual that drops with no differing-frame contact; pooled scoring that matches attributed scoring; system-authored summaries that match an unupdating anchor; removal of history that changes nothing; a model that never refuses a held generic term; or a substituted habitat that transfers without loss. A source-discriminating instrument would move the shape toward `grounded`; a ground measurement ranking protocols would close the `gap` without changing the shape. Agreement, prestige, and benchmark scores are not such instruments.
