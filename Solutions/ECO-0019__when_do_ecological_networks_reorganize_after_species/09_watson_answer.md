## DOMAIN CENSUS

R by arity, symmetry and scale, then one line per domain with actors / currency / anchor, partition or union, and order by dependency.

- **R (the residual).** Post-loss change in function, indexed per function slot. Arity: many slots; the perturbation also has arity (one species versus a selective set). Symmetry: loss is a subtraction, not a symmetric join, and which actor is removed changes the outcome. Scale: from direct partners to cascades three steps out, and from immediate to delayed, hysteretic loss. Fig 1 :19; Fig 11 :49, :52.
- **Domain 1, the unmaintained habitat.** Actors: species, conditions, relations. Currency: contact and cascade. Anchor: measured persistence of function, which does not update on what frames think. No maintainer, no seat. Verdict is descriptive, not prescriptive.
- **Domain 2, the maintained habitat.** Actors: a seat plus the habitat. Currency: introduction, not exclusion. Anchor: the ground still decides. Verdict is about permanent work and production of new roles.
- **Domain 3, the network as composition.** Actors: species as atoms, interaction types as bonds. Currency: composition inside the closure of remaining actors. Anchor: a function measurement. Network is a structure, not a state. Verdict is a mint/reach rule per slot.
- **Domain 4, the measurement and verification frame.** Actors: ecologists, assays, network metrics. Currency: agreement versus residual. Anchor: the ground, never agreement. In this simulated run all four domains draw on one evidence pool, so their agreement is one pool talking, not corroboration.
- **Partition or union.** A union, not a partition. Two conditions bind: reachability of a substitute, and discrimination by the assay. A single lever clears only the region where one condition binds. A partition by function slot also applies. Fig 9 :4, :55.
- **Order by dependency.** Enumerate contact before scoring topology; measure per slot before aggregating; settle which atoms remain before judging what can compose; describe the residual before naming a frame. Fig 11 :44; Fig 1 :19; Fig 12 :104; Fig 9 :27.

## WORLD (secondary sources read)

World sources are corroboration only and never overrule a figure. All were opened 2026-11-23. The eight essays are by the framework's author and are high-overlap provenance recorded in `05_what_shape_means.md`; they are not listed here as independent witnesses.

- **Field: ecological networks / mutualism.** Memmott, Waser and Price, Proc R Soc B, 2004. Quote: "This relative tolerance to extinction derives from redundancy in pollinators per plant and from nested topology of the networks." Prediction: functions persist when a substitute composition is reachable. Corroborates Domain 3.
- **Field: ecological networks / complex systems.** Solé and Montoya, Proc R Soc B, 2001. Quote: "ecological networks are very robust against random removals but can be extremely fragile when selective attacks are used." Prediction: loss pattern discriminates, and valence is not intrinsic. Corroborates Domains 1 and 3.
- **Field: ecology / critical transitions.** Lever, van Nes, Scheffer and Bascompte, Ecology Letters, 2014. Quote: "once a tipping point is reached, pollinator populations collapse simultaneously. Recovering from this single community-wide collapse requires a relatively large improvement of conditions." Prediction: persistence can precede simultaneous loss, and recovery is costlier than prevention. Corroborates Domain 2 on permanent work; single-source for the threshold itself.
- **Field: frugivory networks / function.** Rojas et al., Journal of Animal Ecology, 2025. Quote: "we should remain cautious when inferring the functional consequences of plant species loss based solely on their position within networks." Prediction: a network metric cannot witness a function. Corroborates Domain 4 and the metamerism limit.
- **Field: ecological network modelling.** Kusch and Ordonez, bioRxiv preprint, 2023. Quote: "link loss sensitivity and realisation of rewiring potential" plus "bidirectional extinction cascades". Prediction: resilience has at least two dimensions and cascades run in both directions, so enumeration must be outward and not assume direction. Corroborates Domains 1 and 3.
- **Field: frugivory network structure.** Zhang et al., Proc R Soc B, 2025. Quote: "Interaction turnover is almost entirely due to species turnover rather than interaction rewiring among widely distributed species." Prediction: turnover and rewiring are distinct and must be separated before attributing function change. Corroborates the enumeration requirement; conflicts with no figure line, and the re-census treats it as a scope condition on which mechanism is acting.
- **Field: reference synthesis.** Wikipedia contributors, "Ecological network", Wikipedia, accessed 2026. Quote: "cascading extinctions are less likely in compartmentalized networks, as effects of species losses are limited to the original compartment." Prediction: contact structure bounds cascade spread, supporting outward enumeration. Corroborates Domain 1. Used as a mapping aid and lead to primary papers, not as a verdict.

## VERDICT (from the figures)

The shape survives; the question's unconditional phrasing does not. There is no single "when". Reorganization is a mint that pays only if it lowers the leftover residual in a function slot; function loss is a reach fall when no remaining composition delivers that function until an import arrives. Which actor matters is relative to the function, so selective loss of connectors is the dangerous case. Contact must be enumerated to cascade depth, and a network metric is a filter rather than a witness. In an unmaintained habitat nothing selects for function; in a managed habitat introduction is available and exclusion is not, so management is permanent work. This is a `specified`, ungrounded result. The threshold and hysteresis detail is world-sourced and single-source, not a figure-derived constant.

- **cost if wrong:** bounded and locatable. The packet states mode, refuters and provenance before the conclusion, so an error is findable rather than invisible. The real cost is spending the independence of outside readers by sending an averaged answer instead of a split one, which Fig 2 :42 prices: "Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong." That cost is avoidable by leading with the per-slot shape claim and its refuter.
- **undoable:** yes. The unconditional phrasing is removed without touching the derivation; `07_the_tether_answer.md` and `08_expert_verification_packet.md` already do this. A sent claim can be superseded by the corrected packet, and no primitive has to be withdrawn. The world-sourced threshold detail can be dropped without affecting the figure-derived branches.
- **reversed by:** a ground reading, not agreement. A per-slot assay showing that a global metric leaves no live slot residual refutes the split; insensitivity of function to selective connector loss refutes valence relativity; a recovery with no substitute and no import refutes reachability; linear decline with full recovery removes the world-sourced threshold; a demonstrated subtraction-based, permanently solved restoration refutes the managed-domain rule. Until one of these fires, the verdict stands as `candidate`.
