## What is being decided, in effect terms

A network of species and interactions loses one or more species. Two outcomes are asked about: (a) the remaining species re-wire and the functions the network delivers persist, or (b) a function the network delivered is lost. The decision is the discriminating condition between (a) and (b), stated per function, not per network.

The sought-for answer is bound as an ordinary term before the run, per the formula's Direction rule: `C*` = a rule that, given the pre-loss habitat and the identity of the removed species, predicts for each function slot whether a substitute composition is reachable in the remaining habitat (reorganize) or the function is out of reach until an import (lose function), and predicts the cascade order by which the loss propagates. Binding it makes the preference refusable by the same test as any other claim.
