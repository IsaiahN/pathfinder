# The Tether's answer: when do ecological networks reorganize after species loss, and when do they lose functions?

**Mode: `specified` — not `grounded`.** This is a shape and a route, not a result. Simulated derivation checked against the figures (`05_what_shape_means.md` §7), not a ground reading.

## 1. The residual it answers

A species loss changes a network. "Reorganize" names a change in bonds; "lose an important function" names a change in contact with the ground. These are different quantities and must not be averaged. The residual is indexed per function slot (Fig 1 :19 "The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated."). The question "when A and when B" is a union of overlapping conditions, not one hard question (Fig 9 :4). It is answered by splitting it, not by searching it as one thing.

## 2. The shape, one loop per domain, verdicts kept separate

### Domain 1: the unmaintained habitat

Actors are species, conditions and relations (Fig 11 :47 "relations: pollination, predation, competition, dependency"). Currency is contact and cascade. Anchor is the ground, that is what actually persists. There is no seat, Fig 11 :56 "Unmaintained. No maintainer and no live frame in the seat. The habitat is pure contact, nothing selects, and what survives is what the ground kept."

**Verdict (`general`, structural):** in this domain "the network reorganizes to preserve function" is a category error. Nobody is pursuing the function. Reorganization is a label applied afterwards to the arrangement that met the ground. Function loss is a reach fall measured per slot. These are descriptions, not goals. Triangulated: Fig 11 :56 and Fig 11 :36, plus the four essays on selection without a maintainer.

### Domain 2: the maintained habitat

A seat exists. Currency is introduction; exclusion is not available. Fig 11 :61 "The seat's power is asymmetric. Introduction is available; exclusion is not." Fig 11 :63 "Which is why a managed ecosystem is permanent work rather than a solved state."

**Verdict (`specified`, ungrounded):** management can restore a lost composition by adding an actor, but cannot subtract the fact of contact, and cannot treat a restored function as permanently solved. Recovery of a role that did not previously exist is `⇒` production, not `→` sequence. A one-off intervention that is not maintained is a channel failure, not a solved state. Triangulated: Fig 11 :61, :63 and `06_isomers_and_order.md` Q4, with Lever et al. 2014 supplying the world fact of hysteresis.

### Domain 3: the network as composition

Actors are species as atoms and interaction types as bonds. Currency is composition inside the closure of what the remaining actors can form. Anchor is a function measurement. Fig 13 :24 "that entities relate, and influence travels". Fig 13 :115 "The set is closed. The arrangements are not. That gap is why import exists."

**Verdict (`specified`, falsifiable):** a function is maintained when a substitute composition is reachable among the remaining actors and the resulting mint pays, that is when `cost(φ) + left(R,φ) < cost(R)`. `THE_FORMULA.md` step 3. A function is lost when no remaining composition delivers it; the reach for that slot has fallen. Re-wiring that does not lower `left(R,φ)` is not success (Fig 11 :40). New roles require an import. Triangulated: Fig 6 :23, :42, Fig 13 :115, with Memmott et al. 2004 and Solé and Montoya 2001 corroborating from an independent evidence pool.

### Domain 4: the measurement and verification frame

Actors are ecologists, functional assays and network metrics. Currency is agreement versus residual. Anchor is the ground, never agreement. Fig 2 :29 "An anchor is whatever settles the question and does not change its answer because of what any frame thinks. A measurement, an experiment, a counter, a market, a patient outcome. If it moves when you push on it, it is not an anchor."

**Verdict (`specified`):** a network metric is a filter, not a witness. Fig 9 :60 "Use filters for the budget and witnesses for the verdict. Never let a filter hand you a verdict." Connectivity, nestedness and redundancy price where to look; the witness is the per-slot functional observation after the loss. Agreement among researchers who share one dataset is one witness (Fig 2 :40). Single-source for the figure rule; world corroboration from Rojas et al. 2025 that network position does not determine nutritional importance.

## 3. The answer in one line per branch

- **Reorganize** when the lost actor's role is still composable from the remaining habitat, the mint pays, and the assay confirms the function in its slot.
- **Lose function** when no remaining composition delivers the function, so the reach for that slot has fallen until an import arrives.
- **Both can happen at once**, in different slots. That is why the question is a union (Fig 9 :55).
- **The dangerous case is selective loss of highly connected actors**, because valence is relative to the residual (Fig 11 :49) and random-loss robustness does not transfer (Solé and Montoya 2001).
- **The late case is threshold collapse**: persistence under increasing pressure can be followed by simultaneous loss and costly recovery (Lever et al. 2014), figure-consistent with permanent work but not figure-derived.

## 4. What it explicitly does not say

- Not one unconditional "when". The union is not averaged into a single rule.
- Not a universal keystone list, and not that highly connected species are intrinsically more important. Valence is relative to the residual (Fig 11 :49, :51).
- Not that reorganization implies safety. A re-wire with positive leftover residual carries a functional cost (Rojas et al. 2025).
- Not that a species count or a connectivity score predicts function. Same atoms, different bond, different substance (Fig 12 :117).
- Not that the framework's "habitat" is ecology's "niche". That mapping is explicitly declined in *Arrival of the Fittest*; see `05_what_shape_means.md` §5.
- Not grounded. No measurement at a ground was taken in this folder.

## 5. What would settle it

A per-slot functional assay run before and after a specified loss, with the habitat enumerated outward until the cascade stops mattering (Fig 11 :44). The discriminating test is whether a substitute composition is reachable and whether the leftover residual falls after re-wiring. A threshold claim needs repeated pressure levels and reversal, not a single before/after.

Standing falsifiers, written before the run (`01_figure_grounded_audit.md` §4): a global metric with no live slot residual; a rewiring that restores every slot or a loss with unchanged bonds; a recovery with no substitute and no import; random loss being no worse than selective loss; all change explained by direct partners; linear decline with full recovery; managed restoration by subtraction; a single unconditional rule with zero residual.

## 6. The honest stop

**Unreached, not unreachable.** The answer stops where the available instruments stop discriminating: a metric that reads topology cannot comment on the source of a function (the metamerism limit in `05_what_shape_means.md` §4). The six-term substrate is "a human's careful guess at reality's compression axes" (*They're Scanning Books*), not nature's own list. The lineage is recorded so the stop is checkable. "Indiscernibility is not identity" (*How Intelligence Actually Works*).

## 7. One-paragraph version for outreach

> The Tether does not give one unconditional "when". It splits the question. After a species loss, a function is maintained when a substitute composition is reachable among the remaining actors and the new bonds actually lower the measured gap in that function's slot; the function is lost when no such composition exists and the reach for that slot has fallen until a new actor arrives. Reorganization is a change in arrangement, function loss is a change in contact with the ground, so both can happen in the same network at once. Which species matters is relative to the function at stake, not a property of the species, so the dangerous case is selective loss of highly connected actors rather than random loss. A network metric is a filter for where to look, not a witness for what happened; the witness is the per-slot measurement after the loss, with the habitat enumerated out to the cascade depth. In an unmaintained system nobody is trying to preserve function, and in a managed system introduction is available but exclusion is not, so management is permanent work rather than a solved state. What the framework offers is that shape, its falsifiers written in advance, and an honest stop where the instruments no longer discriminate.
