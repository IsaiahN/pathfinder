2. **Stage 2:** only if curious, read the theory that produced it. The verdict on stage 1 is recorded *before* any theory is shown, and later agreement is never pooled as corroboration (Fig 2 :40).
3. **Stage 3:** optionally use the method recursively on other problems, with each user writing their own refuter and labelling their mode.
