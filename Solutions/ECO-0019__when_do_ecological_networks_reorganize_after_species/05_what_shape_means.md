## 1. A shape is a constraint you can state before you can name the answer

The canonical README says the framework returns "what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)". Fig 9 :32 "you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself". Fig 13 states that a system with a closure "can answer that before it searches, and what it returns is a shape rather than a name" and "Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence".

From *The Library of Babel*: "you can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question." And: "With each new term gained, you are able to narrow the space by definition and deepen your vocabulary."

For ECO-0019 the shape is: **per function slot, a substitute composition is reachable in the enumerated habitat, or it is not; if it is not, an import is required.** That is a constraint on any answer. Naming a threshold value of connectance, or naming which species will compensate, would be a name, not a shape. `SPACE(???)TIME` in the exemplar is the same kind of move: the slot stays open.

## 2. How the shape is obtained

- *Library of Babel* prescribes proximity: use the vocabulary you already hold that is closest to the thing, then correlate it with recurring unfamiliar terms. The result is a narrowing, not a filling.
- The same essay names the failure this question is prone to: "Perhaps your problem was a bundle or conflation of several problems masquerading as one? You will need to search all of those problems individually to find their answers and then combine them in a satisfactory way." This is the essay-form of Fig 9 :4 and of F9 in `01_figure_grounded_audit.md`.
- Fig 6 :30 "The description travels. Fails by being sharpened into terms only we can read. Cast it in effect terms." Fig 9 :30 "your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect rather than the cause". So the residual is described as *function delivered or not*, not as "topology changed".
- *Decoding Tacit Knowledge*: "When contact isn't possible, the best you can send is a plain description of what you'd expect to see, because anyone can check that against the world." This is why the outreach email leads with what an ecologist would observe per slot.

## 3. What licenses a name

Two things, both outside the shape:

1. **The ground settles it.** `THE_FORMULA.md` step 5: "SETTLE the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays. A gate is not the ground." For ECO-0019 the ground is the measured post-loss function, not a network metric and not expert agreement.
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs** (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8), with a source gate and a shadow test.

*They're Scanning Books* sharpens the limit: "A minimum viable set of six terms is a human's careful guess at reality's compression axes; another sensorium's minimum set need not be those six." And: "Declaring the primitives does not make them neutral. It makes the choice visible, which is worth a great deal, because a visible choice can be revised and an emergent one cannot." So the six-term substrate is a visible guess; that is a reason to state it, not a reason to treat it as nature's own list.

### Correction to my own reading of "shape, not a name"

I initially wrote that the framework cannot name the missing thing in this question at all. That overstates it. The canonical phrase governs a **missing primitive**, one not held in Γ. Here the lost actor's identity is known and the remaining atoms are known. What the framework can say is: *a substitute composition is reachable, or it is not; if not, something must be imported.* It cannot say **which** species should be introduced. That is a shape, not a name, and it is the correct limit. The parallel correction in the exemplar (`05_what_shape_means.md` §5b) applies here too.

## 4. The specific forbidden error: a source named from a projection

*The Hard Problem is About Source* gives the mechanism. Metamerism: a color instrument reads green and "it cannot comment on the source". One green light, or yellow plus blue, or RGB, or CMYK, or a scheme not yet discovered can all produce the same reading. "If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information (it cannot define consciousness in full)." And: "An instrument that can't distinguish these cases demonstrates its own inadequacy, it cannot claim anything substantial about what is being measured or what causes it." It was "proven wrong not by argument, but by the arrival of a better instrument", the spectrometer.

Applied here: a network metric is a projection. It reads connectivity or nestedness and cannot comment on the source of a function. Several network arrangements can deliver the same function, and one arrangement can deliver different functions in different contexts. Rojas et al. 2025 is the low-overlap ecological corroboration: plants important for network cohesiveness are not the plants that determine nutritional intake, and functional consequences should not be inferred "based solely on their position within networks". What is owed is a better instrument (a per-slot functional assay), not a name read off the projection.

*How Intelligence Actually Works* makes the same point about lineage: "The recursion converges toward indiscernibility, and indiscernibility is not identity." A rewired network that is indistinguishable from the old one by topology may have a different function; the record of the route is what keeps the claim checkable.

## 5. Three mapping limits that matter here

From *Arrival of the Fittest*, the section "Three mappings offered and declined" explicitly declines **the habitat as an ecological niche**: "A niche is defined by an organism's relation to its environment. A habitat here is defined by contact with a residual, and the residual moves so the same actors constitute a different habitat when the question changes." 

**I honor that refusal in this solution.** Figure 11's "habitat" is used as a rule set (enumerate contact, valence relative to the residual, introduce, never subtract, ground not habitat), not as ecology's "niche" and not as a synonym for the physical ecosystem. Where this folder writes "habitat" it means the figure's field of contact with R. This is a retraction of my first draft, which used "ecological habitat" loosely in `01`; the corrected wording is in `01` §1 and `09`.

The other two declined mappings matter less here but are recorded: Bayesian updating is declined ("there is no prior, no likelihood and no posterior anywhere in the mechanism") and the seat as a Popperian critic is declined because the seat "cannot reconstruct the claim".

*How a Society of Agents Would Work*: "The group's agreement is never the verdict." Ten ecologists who share one dataset are one witness. That rules out consensus as the check on this answer.

## 6. Prestige is not action

From *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*: "Prestige must never be allowed to validate an action directly." And: "Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it. The Ground Maintainer must be blind to prestige." The ask in `02_outreach_email.md` gives the claim a budget to be tried; it does not ask anyone to endorse it, and the contact list in `03` contains institutions and opt-in venues, not famous names selected for likely agreement.

## 7. My own audit is a simulation, not a ground reading

Same essay: "LLMs can quickly simulate an approximate answer/output with the formula found in the architecture post." And: "the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself." *Arrival of the Fittest* adds that LLM outputs "must be seen as simulacra" and "could never be verified if the solutions presented were not contrived to fit the formula."

So `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` and this file are simulated derivations checked for internal consistency against the figures. They are **not** tests of the ecological claim and must be labelled as such. Nothing here is `grounded`.

## 8. One ecology-specific consequence

*How Intelligence Actually Works* notes that "when altering a habitat by unintentionally introducing a predatory threat into the ecosystem, or disturbing the balance by adding or removing vital resource points, you can inadvertently create major unpredictable consequences downstream." *Arrival of the Fittest* cites Carson's *Silent Spring* as the instance behind Fig 11's cascade warning, and Jones, Lawton and Shachak 1994 as the maintained-mode instance for ecosystem engineering. These are sources the framework itself records, so they are figure-adjacent, not independent world corroboration. The independent corroboration in this solution is Memmott et al. 2004, Solé and Montoya 2001, Lever et al. 2014, Rojas et al. 2025 and Kusch and Ordonez 2023, none of whom draw on the framework.
