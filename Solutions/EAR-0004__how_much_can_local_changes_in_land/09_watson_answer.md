## DOMAIN CENSUS

R by arity, symmetry and scale; one line per domain with actors, currency and anchor; partition or union; order by dependency.

- **R (the residual).** The unmapped magnitude from local land or water change to regional and global climate. Arity: four or more (change, flux, regional state, global state, plus a feedback loop). Symmetry: place and time are not interchangeable; the land-atmosphere interface is a seam, not a symmetric join; local, regional and global are different scales. Scale: step (surface flux), cycle (diurnal and seasonal), level (regional circulation and teleconnection), lineage (cumulative land-use change). Fig 9 names a union compound: a case in two conditions is not a harder case of either.
- **Domain 1, the land surface as habitat.** Actors: vegetation, soil, water, albedo, roughness, terrain, land manager. Currency: fluxes of water, energy, momentum, carbon. Anchor: flux towers, soil-moisture probes and remote sensing, instruments that do not update on model output. Question type: habitat enumeration, never composition.
- **Domain 2, the atmospheric and climate frame.** Actors: temperature, precipitation, circulation, moisture, clouds. Currency: climate state variables and their changes. Anchor: observational record, reanalysis, radiosondes. Question type: a separate closure, ranged against its own ground.
- **Domain 3, the land-atmosphere seam.** Actors: coupled land and atmosphere models, observations. Currency: coupled prediction versus measured flux. Anchor: measurements neither side can produce alone. Question type: a seam, a convention that makes the disagreement statable, not a third frame.
- **Domain 4, the scale-up cascade.** Actors: regional circulation, teleconnections, cumulative forcing. Currency: per-slot residuals in each currency. Anchor: per-region attribution, not a global mean. Question type: union compound with its own loop.
- **Order by dependency.** Contact change first (Fig 11 :40), then regional transport, then global composition, then source discrimination (Fig 3 :20-21). Describe R before choosing a frame (Fig 9 :27-28). Refuse before mint (Formula step 2).

## VERDICT (from the figures)

The shape survives; the single number is refused. A local land or water change affects climate only by changing surface contact, so a change with no flux change registers nowhere. The effect is a contact cascade enumerated outward from the point of change, reported per region and per currency, with no standing sign for any land use. Water, energy, momentum and carbon effects do not add, and a global mean near zero is compatible with a live regional slot. The land-atmosphere interface is a seam between two closures, so coupled-model agreement is a convention rather than corroboration. The magnitude is `gap`, unreached rather than silence, until an instrument separates the land contribution from other forcings. Verdicts were kept per domain and never averaged. Mode: `general` on the shape, `specified` on the refusable parts, `gap` on the number, nothing `grounded`.

- **cost if wrong:** bounded and locatable. The packet is mode-labelled, states refuters before the conclusion and records the lineage, so an error is findable rather than invisible. The real cost is spending the independence of outside readers by sending a single-number claim instead of a shape with refuters. Fig 2 :42 prices that: "Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong." That cost is avoidable by leading with the shape and its refuter. A second cost is policy misuse: a wrong single number would be applied to land and water decisions at a scale it was never measured for.
- **undoable:** yes. The single-number framing is removable without touching the shape; `07_the_tether_answer.md` and `08_expert_verification_packet.md` already remove it. A sent claim can be superseded by the corrected packet, and no primitive has to be withdrawn.
- **reversed by:** a ground reading, not agreement. Four tests would move the verdict: a contact test showing fluxes unchanged yet a climate response (breaks the contact condition); a cascade test showing the residual confined to the direct footprint (breaks reach); a per-slot test showing the aggregate fully determines each slot (breaks the per-slot rule); a source test with two low-overlap instruments separating the land contribution (closes the gap). A demonstration that the currencies add would also break the shape. Until one of these fires, the verdict stands as `candidate`.
