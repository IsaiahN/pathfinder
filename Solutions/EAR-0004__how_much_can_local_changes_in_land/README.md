## Restating the question as an effect

The question asks for "how much": a single magnitude carrying a local land or water change up to a regional and then a global climate response. Two readings are available.

- **A (the naive reading):** there is one number, a global mean climate response per unit of local land change, and reporting it answers the question.
- **B (the figure-grounded reading):** the residual is indexed per place, per currency and per scale, the chain from local change to global climate is a union of conditions rather than one lever, and "how much" is only answerable as a per-slot distribution whose magnitude is settled by instruments, not by composition.

What each leads to: A hides live regional slots inside a global average (Fig 1 :19, :22) and sums incommensurable currencies, the water, energy, momentum and carbon effects of a land change (Fig 12 :125, :129). B yields a checkable shape, a dependency order, refuters written in advance, and an honest declared gap where the ground has not settled the number.

An answer would look like: for a named land change at a named place, the change in surface fluxes, the per-region residual in the relevant currency, the reach of the cascade, and the instrument that discriminates the land contribution from other forcings. That shape is bound here as an ordinary term so it can accrue a residual and be refused (Formula, "Direction").

## What is deliberately not here

- No personal emails of named individuals. No scraped contact lists. No bulk-send plan.
- No ranking of people by how likely they are to agree. Selectivity for agreement is the disqualifying correlation (Seat Map :163; Fig 2 :40).
- No claim that a coupled model ensemble agreeing with itself is corroboration. Agreement is the pool talking unless two low-overlap instruments range separately against the ground (Fig 2 :31, :40).

Three figure-grounded reasons:

1. **A room does not upgrade the label.** Seat Map :283: "A room does not upgrade the label." Adding readers raises the vocabulary and does not add an anchor.
2. **A verifier must not hold the interiors.** Fig 10 :42: "A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it." The first contact carries the residual and its refuter, never the framework's record.
3. **A falsifier written first is a design instrument.** Seat Map :287: "And a falsifier written before the run is a design instrument, not only an honesty device."

Legally: bulk unsolicited email breaches GDPR and CAN-SPAM style rules in many jurisdictions. Public opt-in submission routes avoid that failure as well as the structural one.
