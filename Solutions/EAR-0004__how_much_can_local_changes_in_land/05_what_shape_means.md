## 1. A shape is a constraint you can state before you can name the number

The figures README states the limit: the framework returns *"what a missing thing would have to look like (**a shape, not a name**)."* Fig 9 :32: *"→ you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself"*. Fig 13 :135: *"It cannot say which primitive is missing, because naming it would mean already holding it."*

From *The Library of Babel*: "You can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."

For EAR-0004 the shape is: **a land change affects climate only through changed surface contact; the effect is a per-region, per-currency contact cascade whose reach is empirical; the magnitude is unavailable until a source-discriminating instrument pays.** A single number, for example "global mean warming of X per unit of land change", is a name, not a shape. The shape says what any acceptable number would have to be a reading *of*: the flux change, the regional residual, the cascade reach, and the instrument. It does not supply the number.

## 2. How the shape is obtained, and what that means for "how much"

- *Library of Babel*: use the vocabulary already held that is closest to the thing, then "With each new term gained, you narrow the space and deepen your vocabulary." For this question the held terms are contact, cascade, slot, currency, seam and ground, taken from the figures, and the closest field terms are flux, evapotranspiration, coupling strength and teleconnection, taken from the sources read.
- Fig 6 :30: *"outward: does another frame already hold it? The description travels. Fails by being sharpened into terms only we can read. Cast it in effect terms."* Fig 9 :30-31: *"your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect rather than the cause"*. So the question was described in effect terms (what fails to resolve) before Koster et al. 2004 was opened. That order is why the paper was used as corroboration for a shape already derived, not as the source of the shape.
- *Decoding Tacit Knowledge*: "When contact isn't possible, the best you can send is a plain description of what you'd expect to see, because anyone can check that against the world." A shape is precisely that description. The outreach in `02` sends it before the theory.
- *Library of Babel* on stopping: "You will know that you are done because the finish line will also be the starting place, as there is nowhere else to search and nothing else that can describe the answer better." Here the stop is the declared `gap`: the residual stops registering at the limit of the available instruments, and the lineage is recorded so the stop is checkable.

## 3. What licenses a name (a number) in this question

Only two things, both outside the shape itself:

1. **The ground settles it.** Formula step 5: *"SETTLE the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays. A gate is not the ground."* The figures README says the framework *"stops at the limit of what the available instruments can distinguish, and records the lineage so the stop is honest."* For this question that means an attribution instrument separating land change from other forcings, reported per region and per currency.
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs** (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8 :50-52), with a source gate and a shadow test. A number imported from a coupled model that shares its evidence pool with the claim is not such an import.

*They're Scanning Books When They Should Be Studying Nature* sharpens the instrument point: "Every instrument is a crystallized hypothesis about what is worth measuring, and the hypothesis comes from human awareness." And: "The real import is not a new term in the existing space. It is a new dimension of the space." What is owed here is a new discriminating dimension in the observations, not a new adjective for land use.

## 4. Why the single global number is the forbidden error

*The Hard Problem is About Source* gives the mechanism. Metamerism: "the instrument is speaking only about what it measured, it cannot comment on the source." A global temperature reading can be produced by greenhouse forcing, aerosols, internal variability, land change, or several at once. "If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information." The passage that decides the honest stop: "The answer is unreached until a better instrument exists." And: "It was proven wrong not by argument, but by the arrival of a better instrument."

This is not a claim that land use has no climate effect. It is a claim that the *source* of a shared reading cannot be named from the reading alone. The figures say the same in their own vocabulary: Fig 1 :22 *"Aggregating across slots is how a live signal disappears into an average"* and Fig 2 :40 *"agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool"*.

*The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap* adds the self-validation limit: "The catch is that the output is only as good as the validator. The agent, or the tool, or the frame, or the theory, cannot validate itself." A coupled model ensemble cannot be the validator of a claim about its own interface.

## 5. Q1: Does "shape, not a name" forbid naming the land flux or the region?

**No, and this is a correction to a first reading I made.** The figures README says the phrase is about **what a missing thing would have to look like**. For EAR-0004, the fluxes (water, energy, momentum, carbon), the regions, and the instruments are **not missing primitives**; they are already in the held vocabulary of earth system science. Naming them is a binding from that frame onto held terms, not the invention of a new one. What remains forbidden is:

- inventing a seventh kind of climate effect to force a fit (Fig 13 :116-118, the set is closed, the arrangements are not);
- claiming that a binding moves the wall as an import would (Fig 6 :25: *"An import adds no atom to the world."*);
- naming the *source* of a shared reading without an instrument that discriminates among sources (§4).

So `05` §1's wording "a name, not a shape" applies to the single magnitude, not to the ordinary terms used to describe the shape. Both statements are kept here so the narrowing stays visible.

## 6. Q2: What exactly is the shape for "how much can local land or water change affect climate?"

1. **Contact condition.** A land or water change registers on climate only by changing the fluxes across the interface (Fig 11 :40). If those do not change, nothing downstream can register it.
2. **Per-slot, per-currency condition.** The answer is a distribution indexed by place and by currency, not one number (Fig 1 :19, :22; Fig 12 :125, :129). A global mean near zero with a live regional slot is a legal state (Fig 1 :13).
3. **Cascade condition.** The habitat must be enumerated outward until the cascade stops mattering; the residual that matters can be three steps out (Fig 11 :44, :52-53). Direct-footprint scoping is the named failure.
4. **No-standing-valence condition.** No land use is intrinsically good or bad for climate; valence is relative to the residual and the currency (Fig 11 :49-51).
5. **Seam condition.** The land-atmosphere interface is a seam between two closures; agreement within one coupled system is a convention that makes the disagreement statable, not triangulation (Fig 10 :13-18; Fig 2 :31, :40).
6. **Gap condition.** The magnitude is unreached until a source-discriminating instrument pays (Fig 8 :39; Fig 13 :134; source essay §4). Reported as a verdict, not as silence.

That six-point list is the sought-for shape bound as an ordinary term. It can be refused point by point by the refuters in `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` §4.

## 7. Two consequences for this solution

**Prestige is not action.** *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*: "Prestige must never be allowed to validate an action directly... Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it. The Ground Maintainer must be blind to prestige." A campaign gathering famous names to agree with a single-number claim is the named collapse. The legitimate ask is the opposite: give the shape a budget to be tried against measurement.

**This audit is a simulation, not a ground reading.** Same essay: "LLMs can quickly simulate an approximate answer/output with the formula found in the architecture post... However, the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself." The canonical article says LLM output with the formula attached is *"simulacra"* and that outputs *"could never be verified if the solutions presented were not contrived to fit the formula"*. So `01`, `06`, `07`, `08` and `09` are simulated derivations checked for internal consistency against the figures. They are **not** a test of the climate claim and must be labelled as such. They cannot settle anything they report on.
