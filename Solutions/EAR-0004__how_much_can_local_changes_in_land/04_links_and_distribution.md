## 6. Distribution: offer, do not push

Figure-grounded reasons:

- **Fig 10 :52** - *"Bring a characterised residual, never the source."* Sending the full architecture before anyone has described a residual sends the source, not the check. Lead with the shape and the refuter.
- **Fig 10 :42** - *"A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it."* A recipient who has absorbed the framework before being asked for a verdict has joined the frame being checked.
- **Fig 9 :27-28** - *"describe it before you go looking, or any frame you pick will seem to fit"*. Ask what result they would expect if the shape claim were true, before giving them the article.
- **Fig 2 :40** - *"disqualifying for a verdict: agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool"*. A send selected for people who already agree produces a convention, not corroboration.
- **Fig 11 :61** - *"Introduction is available; exclusion is not."* Sending a claim is an introduction into a habitat; you cannot take back the effect it has. Enumerate the cascade before sending.

**Recommended order in a reply:** (1) the shape claim with its mode; (2) the four refuters; (3) ask what they would count as a failure; (4) only then, if curious, the three links, with the note that the figures, not the summary, are the authority.

## 7. The two-stage offer

- **Stage 1 before stage 2** is Fig 10 :52 and Fig 2 :45 - *"Frames range against the ground. They do not negotiate with it."* The recipient checks the output without holding the framework's interior.
- **Stage 2 is opt-in by construction.** Record the verdict on stage 1 before showing any theory. Never pool later agreement as corroboration (Fig 2 :40).
- **Stage 3 sends a method, which is permitted.** Fig 4 :22 - *"Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down."* The formula is a generator, so giving it away does not give away an answer.
- **Stage 3 still needs a ground per problem.** Formula, "Direction": a frame cannot check the direction of its own question. Each user must write down what would refute their result before running it and state the mode of what they get.

**Hard precondition:** stages 1-3 only work if the stage-1 output survives. The output in this folder is a shape with a declared `gap` on the magnitude. If a checker demands a number, the honest answer is that the number is owed and the instrument is named, not that a number has been smuggled in (Fig 13 :134).
