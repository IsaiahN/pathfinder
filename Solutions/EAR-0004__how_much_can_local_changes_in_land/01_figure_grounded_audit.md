## 0. The residual, in effect terms

The question asks to move a local perturbation (land or water use change) through a chain: surface fluxes, regional climate, global climate, and to report "how much" at the end. In effect terms the residual is:

> Given a change in land or water use at a place and time, what fails to resolve is how much of that change arrives at the regional and global climate, through which channels, at which scale, and with which instrument separating the land contribution from every other forcing.

This is not one question. It is a union of at least four conditions (Fig 9 :54, :57): (1) does the land change alter the surface fluxes, the contact; (2) does the altered flux reach a regional response; (3) does the regional response compose into a global one; (4) can the ground discriminate the land contribution from the background. A single lever cannot clear the intersections (Fig 9 :55-56).

Stating the sought-for shape as an ordinary term, before the run (Formula, "Direction"): *an answer would name a land change, a place, the changed fluxes, the per-region residual in each currency, the reach of the cascade, and the instrument that discriminates the source*. This shape is bound now so it can accrue a residual and be refused.

## 1. DOMAIN CENSUS (before choosing any figure line)

### 1a. R by arity, symmetry and scale (Fig 9 :22, :27-28)

- **Arity.** Four or more things relate: a local change `A` at place `p` and time `t`; a set of surface fluxes `f` (water, energy, momentum, carbon); a regional climate state `C_r`; a global climate state `C_g`; plus feedbacks back from `C_r` to `f`. The question's "how much" is the magnitude of the map `A -> f -> C_r -> C_g`. Four arity, with a loop.
- **Symmetry.** Place and time are not interchangeable, so the residual cannot be pooled over them without loss (Fig 1 :19). The land-atmosphere interface is a **seam**, not a symmetric join: the two sides exchange fluxes but have different closures (Fig 10 :13-14, "two frames, one field, no shared reading"). "Local", "regional" and "global" are different scales, not three readings of one quantity (Fig 13 :35-36: adjacency is not duration, an ordering is not a pathway).
- **Scale.** Step (a surface flux at an instant), cycle (diurnal and seasonal), level (regional circulation, teleconnection), lineage (cumulative land-use change and long-term climate state). A number valid at one scale is not valid at another (Fig 4 :27-29, the transform between scales loses information).

### 1b. One line per domain: actors / currency / anchor

No nameable anchor means it is not yet a domain. Each domain below has one.

| domain | actors | currency | anchor (must not move when frames push on it) | question type |
|---|---|---|---|---|

| D2 The atmospheric / climate frame | temperature, precipitation, circulation, moisture, clouds | climate state variables and their changes | observational record, reanalysis, radiosondes | separate frame with its own closure |

### 1c. Partition or union?

**Union.** D1 through D4 overlap: the flux belongs to both the land and the atmosphere; the seam is their intersection; the scale-up uses both frames' outputs. Fig 9 :54: *"And when the split lands on a union rather than a partition, unbundle before you search again."* Fig 9 :57: *"a case in two conditions is not a harder case of either."* The union is a specific compound with its own loop, not a harder instance of "land use change" or of "climate".

A shared field between D1 and D2 is Fig 10's seam (line 13: "two frames, one field, no shared reading"), not a third frame that can be averaged with them.

### 1d. Order by dependency (Fig 3)

Fig 3 :20-21: *"The chain is ordered by dependency, not by importance. Each link consumes what the one above produces, so effort spent above the break arrives at a step that cannot use it."*

1. **Does the change alter contact?** If the surface fluxes do not change, nothing downstream can register it (Fig 11 :40). Upstream first.
2. **Does the flux reach a regional response?** Enumeration of direct contact is insufficient; the residual can be three steps out (Fig 11 :52-53).
3. **Does the regional response compose into a global one?** This is a union question, and R stays indexed per slot (Fig 1 :19).
4. **Can the ground discriminate the source?** An instrument that reads a temperature change cannot comment on whether land use caused it (source essay, §7 below).

Effort spent on step 4 when step 1 is unmeasured is work above the break arriving at a step that cannot use it (Fig 3 :19: "Optimising a link that already works cannot rescue one that does not.").

## 3. Findings

Each verdict is kept per domain and never averaged. Mode is stated on each. Refuters are attached before the conclusion.

### F1 - A single "how much" is the aggregation error, not the answer (D4; decisive, structural)

**Mode: `general`.**

Fig 1 :19: *"The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated."*
Fig 1 :22: *"Aggregating across slots is how a live signal disappears into an average."*
Fig 1 :13: *"a global R near zero with one live slot residual is a legal state"*
Symbols table, "slot": *"One object, region or entity being tracked. R is measured per slot, never as a single global number."*
Fig 1 :28: *"The gap is measured per object, not per step. A system that averages across objects has thrown away the only signal that says where to look."*

A global mean response near zero is compatible with a strong regional effect. The two are not alternative answers; one is the wrong instrument applied to the residual. So the honest answer to "how much" is a *distribution* per region and per currency, with the global mean reported only as a separate, labelled reading.

**Low-overlap corroboration (mathematics/statistics).** Wikipedia, *Simpson's paradox*: "a trend appears in several groups of data but disappears or reverses when the groups are combined." This is a different field from earth systems and from the framework, and it predicts exactly the failure Fig 1 names: pooling reverses or erases a per-group signal. This corroborates the figure verdict; it does not decide it.

**Refuter.** Show a land-change case in which the global aggregate fully determines each regional slot, with no information loss under pooling, on two instruments with low overlap. If that holds, F1 fails and a single number is legitimate.

### F2 - The mechanism is a contact cascade; direct-footprint scoping is how the effect is missed (D1, D4; load-bearing)

**Mode: `general` on the shape, `specified` on the claim that the response reaches beyond the direct footprint.**

Fig 11 :52-53: *"And contact cascades. Scoping the habitat to direct contact is how DDT reached the eagles: first-order enumeration, second-order effect, and the residual that mattered was three steps out."*
Fig 11 :44: *"List everything in contact with the residual, then what is in contact with those, and outward until the cascade stops mattering."*
Fig 11 :48: *"You do not invent the list. You read it off the world, and what you cannot perceive or measure yet is the residual."*
Fig 11 :73: *"The habitat is not the ground. The habitat is where actors come from and it updates; the ground is what settles, and it does not."*

So the upper bound on "how much" is the reach of the cascade in the habitat, which is an empirical question read off the world, not derivable from composition. Land change alters the surface exchange; the exchange alters boundary-layer structure; the response can reorganise regional circulation; regional anomalies can compose into a global forcing. Each step is a link in the chain and must be measured.

**World corroboration (earth system science).** Wikipedia, *Land use*: "human changes to land use and land cover have a profound impact on climate at a local and regional level, which in turn contributes to climate change." The same page: "The rapid decline of the Aral Sea is an example how local-scale land use and land change can have compounded impacts on regional climate systems" (a water-diversion case three steps out). Wikipedia, *Teleconnection*: "Teleconnection in atmospheric science refers to climate anomalies being related to each other at large distances (typically thousands of kilometers)." These predict reach beyond the footprint. All three are one encyclopedia pool, so they are one witness, not three (Fig 2 :40).

**Refuter.** A controlled land-change perturbation whose measured regional and global residual stops at the direct surface footprint at every instrument resolution, with the enumeration extended at least three steps out. If found, the contact-cascade claim narrows to the footprint.

### F3 - There is no standing valence: no fixed list of good or bad land uses (D1; general)

**Mode: `general`.**

Fig 11 :49: *"An actor's valence is relative to the residual, not a property it carries."*
Fig 11 :50: *"The same actor is a benefactor to one residual and a malfactor to another, so mutualism and parasitism are relations rather than kinds."*
Fig 11 :51: *"No standing list of good actors survives a change of residual."*

So "which land use helps the climate" is not answerable in the abstract. The same irrigation scheme can be a local cooling influence through evapotranspiration and a different sign in another currency or at another scale. Which dominates depends on the residual and the currency. Any ranking of land uses by a fixed climate sign is a naming error, not a result.

**World corroboration (hydrology, physical geography).** Wikipedia, *Evapotranspiration*: "Evapotranspiration (ET) refers to the combined processes that move water from the Earth's surface into the atmosphere, including both evaporation and transpiration." and "Evapotranspiration is an important part of the local water cycle and climate". Wikipedia, *Terrain*: "Terrain affects surface water flow and distribution. Over a large area, it can affect weather and climate patterns." Both predict sign and magnitude that depend on which flux and which scale are read. One encyclopedia pool, so one witness.

**Refuter.** A land-change class whose sign is invariant across residuals, currencies, places and instruments. That would reintroduce a carried valence and break Fig 11 :49-51.

### F4 - The land-atmosphere interface is a seam between two closures; coupled-model agreement is not triangulation (D2, D3; specified)

**Mode: `specified` on the claim that the ground is hard to reach here; `general` on the seam structure.**

Fig 10 :13-18: *"two frames, one field, no shared reading"* ... *"a disagreement nothing can state is not yet a defect"* ... *"the convention does not fix the bug"* ... *"it makes the bug statable"* ... *"a declared meaning is not a grounded one"*.
Fig 2 :31: *"Independence, between two frames, is how little they draw on the same evidence."*
Fig 2 :40: *"disqualifying for a verdict: agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool"*
Fig 2 :35: *"Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you."*
Fig 7 :13: *"agreement compounds, evidence does not"*

A land-surface model and an atmospheric model are two closures over one field. Coupling them produces a convention that makes the mismatch statable; it does not by itself ground the flux. An ensemble of coupled models that share parameterisations, initial datasets and tuning targets is one pool wearing many names (Fig 8 :48-49: "Two frames that overlap almost completely are one frame wearing two names.").

**World corroboration (the core field, low overlap with the framework essays).** Koster et al., "Regions of Strong Coupling Between Soil Moisture and Precipitation", *Science*, 2004, abstract read via Semantic Scholar API on 2026-11-22 (full paper not opened, recorded as such in the ledger): "Previous estimates of land-atmosphere interaction (the impact of soil moisture on precipitation) have been limited by a lack of observational data and by the model dependence of computational estimates." And: "This allows a multimodel estimation of the regions on Earth where precipitation is affected by soil moisture anomalies during Northern Hemisphere summer." This predicts precisely the shape F4 and F1 assert: per-region coupling strength, and a known deficit of observational data and model independence. It corroborates, at maximum independence from the essays. It does not settle the magnitude.

**Refuter.** An independently instrumented attribution in which two observations with genuinely different evidence pools (for example, flux-tower networks and a separate satellite retrieval with independent calibration) return the same land signal. Under Fig 2 :31 those would be two instruments pointed at the same thing, so their agreement would be triangulation. F4 would narrow to the remaining links.

### F5 - A change that does not alter contact changes nothing (D1; general constraint)

**Mode: `general` as a constraint on any causal story; not a claim that any particular small effect is negligible.**

Fig 11 :40: *"A system cannot be made more capable by improving the agent in isolation. An improvement that does not change contact changes nothing, however much it improves."* Read at this domain: a land-use change that leaves the surface fluxes of water, energy, momentum and carbon unchanged cannot move the climate. The magnitude is a function of the flux change, not of the area, the label, or the money spent on the land.

**World corroboration (earth observation).** Wikipedia, *Urban heat island*: "The main cause of the UHI effect is from the modification of land surfaces, while waste heat generated by energy usage is a secondary contributor." and "Urban areas occupy about 0.5% of the Earth's land surface but host more than half of the world's population." Wikipedia, *Soil moisture*: "Soil moisture measurement can be based on in situ probes or remote sensing methods." These predict that the operative quantity is the changed surface state, measurable by instruments. One encyclopedia pool, one witness.

**Refuter.** A measured regional or global climate response to a land-use change for which no change in any surface flux can be detected at the resolution of the best available instrument. If reliably repeated, the contact constraint fails or the instrument is the wrong one (Fig 6 :34-35: no edge to extend from).

### F6 - The currencies do not add, so "how much" has no single unit (D4; general)

**Mode: `general`.**

Fig 12 :125: *"A composition costs in two currencies, and they do not add."*
Fig 12 :126: *"Bits price the description. Actions price finding out whether it holds. They are in different units, so a system holding one accounting cannot compare them and will not notice the shortfall."*
Fig 12 :129: *"Summing them into one figure is the error this names, not the fix for it."*
Fig 12 :130: *"A price in bits and a price in actions are two readings, held apart."*

For this question the land change produces changes in water flux, energy flux, momentum flux and carbon flux. These operate on different state variables and are not commensurable in a single "how much". A blended index or a single equivalent number is the named error. "Some combination" is legitimate only as several separately read measures with declared scopes, never as a sum.

**Refuter.** A demonstrated conversion in which a unit of evapotranspiration change and a unit of carbon flux change add without loss to a common quantity that the ground pays for. No such conversion is held here. Single-source (Fig 12) until that is shown.

### F7 - The magnitude is a declared gap pending a source-discriminating instrument (D4; gap)

**Mode: `gap` on the magnitude; `general` on the shape and on what would close it.**

Fig 8 :39: *"So a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence."*
Fig 9 :44: *"Only a sealed room can be searched to the end, and no frame is a sealed room. So searching and finding nothing proves nothing."*
Fig 13 :134: *"Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence,"*
Fig 13 :135: *"It cannot say which primitive is missing, because naming it would mean already holding it."*
Fig 9 :32: *"→ you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself"*
Fig 6 :30: *"outward: does another frame already hold it? The description travels. Fails by being sharpened into terms only we can read. Cast it in effect terms."*

A temperature trend reads the combined effect of greenhouse forcing, aerosols, internal variability and land change. The reading cannot comment on the source. What is owed is an instrument (a design of observations and attribution) whose closure discriminates among sources, not another argument about the sign or size of the effect. Until such an instrument exists and pays, the number is `unreached`, reported as a verdict.

**World corroboration (human cognition / instruments, low overlap).** Isaiah N, "The Hard Problem is About Source" (2026): "the instrument is speaking only about what it measured, it cannot comment on the source." and "The answer is unreached until a better instrument exists." This predicts the need for a source discriminator; it corroborates the figure verdict. Koster et al. 2004 independently predicts the same thing from inside the field ("a lack of observational data and ... model dependence"). Two low-overlap witnesses for the shape, still not the number.

**Refuter / closure condition.** A public, reproducible attribution result in which the land contribution is separated from other forcings by a documented instrument, with the residual reported per region and per currency. That would move the magnitude from `gap` toward `specified` and eventually `grounded`. "Unreachable is a verdict, not a silence": the gap is recorded, not hidden.

**Mode: `general`.**

## 4. Pre-registered refuter (written before the conclusion)

Required by Seat Map :287: *"And a falsifier written before the run is a design instrument, not only an honesty device."*

The shape claim in this folder is refused if any of the following holds:

1. **Aggregation loss fails.** A case is shown in which the global aggregate fully determines every regional slot in every currency, with no information loss under pooling, on two low-overlap instruments. (Breaks F1.)
2. **Contact cascade fails.** A controlled land-change perturbation has its regional and global residual confined to the direct surface footprint at every instrument resolution, after the habitat was enumerated three steps out. (Breaks F2.)
3. **Currencies add.** A common measure is demonstrated in which water, energy, momentum and carbon flux changes add without loss and the ground pays for the sum. (Breaks F6.)
4. **Source discrimination arrives.** A public, reproducible instrument separates the land contribution from other forcings per region and per currency. (Closes F7; this is a closure condition, not a refutation of the shape, and is recorded as such.)
5. **The union collapses.** The four conditions of §0 are reduced to one lever that settles all of them. (Breaks F8.)
6. **The seam is not a seam.** Land and atmosphere models are shown to draw on genuinely independent evidence at every link while remaining coupled, so their agreement is triangulation rather than one pool. (Narrows F4.)

If 1, 2, 3 or 5 fires, the shape is wrong and is withdrawn. If 4 or 6 fires, the gap narrows and the answer moves toward `specified`. Until one is run, nothing in this folder is `grounded`.

## 5. Recommendation

1. Do not answer "how much" with one number. Report a per-slot, per-currency distribution and label the global mean as a separate reading (F1, F6).
2. Enumerate the habitat outward three steps before estimating any magnitude; the residual that matters is often not at the point of change (F2).
3. Treat coupled model agreement as a convention that makes the mismatch statable, not as triangulation. Buy independence with measurements neither side can produce (F4).
4. State the mode on every sentence. `general` for the shape, `specified` for refusable claims, `gap` for the magnitude (F7).
5. Declare the union and unbundle it before further search (F8).
6. Use public opt-in venues as budget filters, not witnesses. Lead with the shape claim and its refuter (F9).
