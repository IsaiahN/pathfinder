**Use:** one-to-one, or as a cover note attached to a submission through a public venue in `03_public_contact_points.csv`. **Not** for bulk send. **Do not ask anyone to trust the framework or to agree with it.**

**Hard precondition:** do not send until the refuters in `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` §4 are understood and the mode labels are intact. The output must survive validation or the two-stage offer collapses on contact.

**Subject:** Check one claim about land-use and climate before you decide whether the method is worth reading

> Hello [name],
>
> I have one specific, checkable output about how local land or water use changes propagate to regional and global climate. I am not asking you to read a theory first, and I am not asking you to agree with anything. I am asking what you would count as a failure of the claim. If it does not survive, there is nothing else to look at.
>
> **The output (`general` on the shape, `specified` on the refusable parts, `gap` on the number).** A land-use change can affect climate only by changing the fluxes across the land-atmosphere interface: water, energy, momentum and carbon. If the interface does not change, nothing downstream can register the change. Once it does, the effect is a contact cascade that must be enumerated outward from the point of change, because the residual that matters is often several steps away rather than at the surface footprint. The consequence is that "how much" is not one number: the signal is indexed by place and by currency, and a global mean near zero is compatible with a strong regional effect. The interface between a land model and an atmosphere model is a seam between two different closures, so agreement between coupled models is not, by itself, triangulation. The magnitude stays marked `gap` until an instrument separates the land contribution from the other forcings.
>
> **What this explicitly does not claim.** It does not say that any particular land use is always good or always bad for climate. The sign depends on which residual and which currency are being read, so there is no standing list. It does not blend water, energy, momentum and carbon into one index; those currencies do not add. It does not claim that a small local temperature change is negligible globally, nor that a global mean is useless. It says a global mean is one labelled reading, not the answer.
>
> **What would refuse the shape (written down before this message):**
> 1. a case where the global aggregate fully determines every regional slot in every currency, with no information loss under pooling, on two independent instruments;
> 2. a controlled land-change perturbation whose regional and global residual stops at the direct surface footprint at every instrument resolution, after the surroundings were enumerated three steps out;
> 3. a demonstration that the water, energy, momentum and carbon effects add without loss into one quantity the ground pays for;
> 4. a public, reproducible instrument that separates the land contribution from the other forcings per region and per currency (that would close the gap rather than refute the shape, and I would record it as closure).
>
> A one-line "this fails because..." is more useful to me than agreement. If you would rather not engage, no reply is needed.
>
> **If it survives, and only then:** here are the source links. The figures, not my summary of them, are the authority.
> - Figures and formula for direct download (SVG, branch `general-agent`): https://github.com/IsaiahN/tether/tree/general-agent/figures
> - Canonical usage rule (figures README): https://github.com/IsaiahN/tether/blob/general-agent/figures/README.md

>

## Stage order after a reply

1. **Stage 1.** Send only the shape claim, the mode and the four refuters. Ask what the reader would count as a failure. Record that verdict before any theory is shown.
2. **Stage 2, opt-in only.** If curious, send the three links. A reader who has absorbed the framework before giving a verdict has joined the frame being checked, so their later agreement is never pooled as corroboration (Fig 2 :40; Fig 10 :42).
3. **Stage 3.** Offer the formula as a method to reuse, with the instruction that each user writes their own refuter and states their own mode.
