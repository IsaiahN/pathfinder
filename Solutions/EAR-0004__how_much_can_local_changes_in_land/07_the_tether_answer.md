# The Tether's answer on local land or water change and climate

**Mode: `general` on the structural shape, `specified` on the claims a measurement could refuse, `gap` on any single magnitude.** Not `grounded`. Simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures, not a ground reading (`05_what_shape_means.md` §7).

## 1. The residual it answers

A local land or water change must move through a surface flux, a regional response and a global composition before it can be called a climate effect. What fails to resolve is how much of the change arrives at each stage, in which currency, and with what instrument separating the land contribution from every other forcing. It is a **union compound**, not one hard question (Fig 9 :57: *"a case in two conditions is not a harder case of either."*), with its own loop and its own ground.

## 2. The shape, per domain (verdicts kept separate, never averaged)

**D1, the land surface as habitat. Verdict: contact condition and no standing valence.** A land change registers only if it changes the fluxes across the interface. Fig 11 :40: *"A system cannot be made more capable by improving the agent in isolation. An improvement that does not change contact changes nothing, however much it improves."* And the sign is not carried by the land use: Fig 11 :49 *"An actor's valence is relative to the residual, not a property it carries."* and Fig 11 :51 *"No standing list of good actors survives a change of residual."* Status: `general`, triangulated across Fig 11 and the land-atmosphere sources (Wikipedia land use and evapotranspiration pages, one pool). There is no fixed ranking of land uses by climate benefit.

**D2, the atmosphere and climate frame. Verdict: a separate closure.** The atmosphere has its own state variables and its own ground. It cannot be averaged with the land frame; the two meet at D3. Fig 2 :45: *"Frames range against the ground. They do not negotiate with it."* Status: `general`, single-source for the structural point (Fig 2), corroborated by the soil-moisture precipitation literature read as one witness (Koster et al. 2004).

**D3, the land-atmosphere seam. Verdict: a convention that makes the mismatch statable, not a third frame.** Fig 10 :13: *"two frames, one field, no shared reading"*. Coupled land-atmosphere models produce a reading neither side can produce alone, but if they share evidence their agreement is not triangulation. Fig 2 :40: *"disqualifying for a verdict: agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool"* and Fig 8 :49: *"Two frames that overlap almost completely are one frame wearing two names."* Status: `specified`, triangulated across Fig 10 and Fig 2 with Koster et al. 2004's "model dependence of computational estimates" as the low-overlap corroborator. It does not settle any magnitude.

**D4, the scale-up cascade. Verdict: a per-slot distribution, not a number.** Fig 1 :19: *"The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated."* Fig 1 :22: *"Aggregating across slots is how a live signal disappears into an average."* A global mean near zero with a live regional effect is a legal state (Fig 1 :13). The currencies also refuse a sum: Fig 12 :129: *"Summing them into one figure is the error this names, not the fix for it."* Status: `general` for the per-slot rule (triangulated with Fig 12 and Simpson's paradox), `gap` for the magnitude.

**D4, the magnitude. Verdict: unreached, reported as a verdict.** Fig 8 :39: *"So a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence."* Fig 13 :134: *"Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence,"*. A temperature reading cannot comment on which forcing produced it, so what is owed is a source-discriminating instrument, not another argument. Status: `gap` on the number; `general` on what would close it.

## 3. What it explicitly does not say

- Not that land use has no climate effect. The effect is real; the single number is what is refused.
- Not that any land use is always good or always bad. Valence is relative to the residual (Fig 11 :49-51); irrigation can have a different sign in a different currency or at a different scale.
- Not that global means are useless. They are one labelled reading, not the answer.
- Not that water, energy, momentum and carbon effects can be blended. They are held apart (Fig 12 :125-130).
- Not that coupled models are wrong because they agree. They are a convention that makes the mismatch statable; the anchor is measurement (Fig 10 :18-19: *"a declared meaning is not a grounded one"*).
- Not a derivation of one climate state from another, and no new primitive.
- Not `grounded`. Nothing here was settled by a measurement of the land contribution.

## 4. What would settle it, and where the chain currently stops

The ground is an instrument, not an argument. The chain (Fig 3 :20-21) runs: contact change, regional transport, global composition, source discrimination.

1. **Contact Test.** Does the land or water change alter the surface fluxes at the resolution of an instrument? If not, the chain stops at link 1 (Fig 11 :40).
2. **Cascade Test.** Enumerate outward three steps (Fig 11 :52-53). Does the regional residual persist beyond the direct footprint at each step, and does the feedback loop close with an amplifying or damping sign (Fig 12 :121)?
3. **Per-slot Test.** Is the effect reported per region and per currency, with the global mean as a separate labelled reading (Fig 1 :19, :22)? A single pooled number is the failure mode, not the result.
4. **Source Test.** Can an observation design separate the land contribution from greenhouse forcing, aerosols and internal variability, with two low-overlap instruments ranging against the same ground (Fig 2 :31)? This is the link most likely to have no instrument (Fig 3 :27: *"A link with no instrument is not a link that works. It is a link nobody has looked at."*).
5. **Standing falsifiers.** Derive one of the six substrate terms from the other five; show the global aggregate fully determines every regional slot; show the currencies add; show the residual stops at the direct footprint. Any of these moves the shape.

## 5. The honest stop

**Unreached, not unreachable in principle.** Fig 9 :44: *"Only a sealed room can be searched to the end, and no frame is a sealed room. So searching and finding nothing proves nothing."* Fig 6 :34-35: *"The question is not whether a sensor could exist. It is whether anything, at any resolution, is already returning something that fails to resolve."* A source-discriminating observation design is not described here, so a new instrument would be an import with a source gate and a shadow test, debited against independence (Fig 6 :24). The stop is recorded with its lineage so a later reader can locate the error rather than only feel it. From *How Intelligence Actually Works*: "The recursion converges toward indiscernibility, and indiscernibility is not identity."

## 6. One-paragraph version for outreach

> The Tether does not produce a single number for how much local land or water change affects climate, and it says why that number is the wrong instrument. A land change registers on climate only if it changes the fluxes across the land-atmosphere interface, and the resulting effect is a contact cascade whose reach must be enumerated outward from the point of change, because the residual that matters is often several steps away. That effect is indexed by region and by currency: water, energy, momentum and carbon do not add, and a global mean near zero is compatible with a strong regional signal. There is no standing list of good or bad land uses, because the sign depends on the residual being read. The land-atmosphere interface is a seam between two different closures, so coupled models agreeing is a convention, not corroboration. The magnitude stays marked `gap` until an instrument separates the land contribution from the other forcings. What is offered is the shape any answer must have, the falsifiers written in advance, and the tests that would close it. The number stays open until the ground settles it.
