# The Tether's answer on QM vs GR

**Mode: `specified` — not `grounded`.** This is a shape and a route, not a result. Simulated derivation
checked against the figures (`05_what_shape_means.md` §6), not a ground reading.

## 1. The residual it answers

GR and QM agree in their own domains and fail at their contact: perturbative gravity diverges;
unitary evolution does not yield definite outcomes; evaporation does not preserve unitarity. The
contact is a **union compound**, not one hard problem (Fig 9: *"a case in two conditions is not a
harder case of either"*). It has its own loop and its own ground.

## 2. The shape

1. **Space and time cannot bond directly.** Fig 13 :75: *"Two states never bond directly. It is not
   space-time but space, a structure, time."* So "spacetime" is not a primitive — it is a derivative
   with a structure in the slot. The slot stays marked `???`.
2. **Both theories are first-order derivatives of the six-term set.** A first-order molecule is
   `state → structure → state`, one crossing. GR reads `Space – structure – Time`; QM reads
   `Energy – structure – Time`. Both are legal under Rule 1. Fig 13 itself licenses more than one
   gradient: *"A gradient over space and one over time are different terms, because which state it is
   bonded to is part of what it is."*
3. **They are not one theory to be merged; they are two structures to be compared.** Over the shared
   atom set they are Fig 12's **isomers** — *"same atoms, different bond, different substance. The
   difference is invisible in the formula and decisive in the behaviour."* As the record currently
   writes them they share only Time, so the weaker claim is that they share a **functional group**
   (Fig 12 :124): *"the group says what kind; the qualifier says which member."*
4. **Their union is second-order.** Both first-order molecules are settled against the ground, so
   each becomes an atom for the next level: Fig 12 :104, *"A settled molecule becomes an atom for
   whatever composes over it... irreducible is a fact about a level."* Their comparison ⋛ is a
   structural relation at a threshold — **not a unified field**.
5. **The structure in the slot is not named by the framework.** It returns a shape, not a name.
   Selecting Gradient or Spectrum is a binding from the physics frame, provenanced as such — not a
   derivation and not an import that moves the wall. The specific binding `metric tensor = Gradient`
   remains unproven: the metric defines the interval (ordering, spectrum-like); the connection (∂g)
   is the gradient-like object. That mapping is still open.

## 3. What it explicitly does not say

- Not a ToE. No final room. The center of the Venn diagram remains a compound with its own loop.
- Not "gravity is not curvature." Curvature is the part of GR that cannot be transformed away; that is
  settled physics and the framework does not overwrite it.
- Not a derivation of one theory from the other, and not a new primitive.
- Not grounded. No measurement at the Planck scale settles `left(R,φ)`.

## 4. What would settle it — the ground

A source-discriminating instrument, not a further argument. *The Hard Problem is About Source*: an
instrument that reads green *"cannot comment on the source"*; the spectrometer resolved it. Here the
corresponding questions are:

- Does the metric behave as **ordering** (spectrum-like) or **directedness** (gradient-like) at the
  Planck scale? An instrument that discriminates these would name the slot.
- **Threshold Test:** does a ⋛ relation hold between the two first-order molecules at that scale —
  or demonstrably not?
- **Fundamental Metric, restated so it can fire:** a measurement in which the metric's role in the
  interval cannot be recovered from a cross-kind composition at all. (As written in the record, the
  falsifier is already satisfied by textbook GR and is therefore dead.)
- Standing falsifiers from the record: derive one of the six from the other five; admit a state–state
  or structure–structure composition that cannot be refused; measure a direct space–time bond.

## 5. The honest stop

**Unreached, not unreachable.** The six-term set is *"a human's careful guess at reality's
compression axes"* — declaring it makes the choice visible, not neutral. Another sensorium's minimum
set need not be these six. The answer stops at the limit of the available instruments and records the
lineage so the stop is checkable. *"Indiscernibility is not identity."*

## 6. One-paragraph version for outreach

> The Tether does not unify general relativity and quantum mechanics, and it does not claim the final
> answer. It says that space and time cannot bond directly, so spacetime is a derivative with a
> structure in the slot; that both theories are first-order derivatives of one six-term set and stand
> to each other as isomers — same atoms, arranged differently; and that their union is a second-order
> comparison at a threshold, not a new field. It refuses to name the structure in the slot, because
> the framework returns a shape, not a name. What it offers is the shape any candidate answer must
> have, the falsifiers written in advance, and the instruments that would close it. The center stays
> open until the ground settles it.
