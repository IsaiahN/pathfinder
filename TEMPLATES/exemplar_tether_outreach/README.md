## What is deliberately not here

- No personal emails of named individuals.
- No ranking of people by how likely they are to believe the claim.
- No bulk-send plan.

Three reasons, in the figures' own words:

1. **A room does not upgrade the label.** *"A room reaching a conclusion without the ground is agreement, and agreement among frames is a weighted average of influence. Adding people raises the vocabulary and does not add an anchor."* (Seat Map, line 283)
2. **Correlation disqualifies a verdict.** *"High correlation is disqualifying for a verdict and efficient for a convention."* (Seat Map, line 163) Selecting recipients by receptiveness manufactures the correlation that would make their agreement worthless.
3. **Asking people to trust before they check spends their independence.** *"A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it"* (Fig 10 :42); the outside frame *"must not hold the interiors"* (Seat Map, Seat 4).

Legally: scraping personal emails for unsolicited bulk outreach breaches GDPR/CAN-SPAM-style rules in many jurisdictions and will get the sending domain blacklisted. That is a practical failure on top of the structural one.

opted in to receiving claims and is positioned to refute them — not a purchased list of believers.
