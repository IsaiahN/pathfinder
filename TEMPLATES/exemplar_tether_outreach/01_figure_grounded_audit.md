### F2 — The glyph was wrong; `⋛ = ⋈` was declined (RESOLVED 2026-11-22)

The Operators table and Figure 12 define comparison as **⋛** — *"a threshold relation between two
quantities"* — with the test *"is it about which is larger, rather than both being present?"*

The draft wrote **⋈** (U+22C8 BOWTIE). Web check, 2026-11-22:

- `⋈` is the **natural join** of two relations in relational algebra and database theory (LaTeX
  `\bowtie`; the Unicode CLDR label is literally "natural join").
- In mathematical physics it is used in a few places for a **non-commuting action of two groups** —
  e.g. `SL(2,Z) ⋈ SO(d,d;Z)` — a semidirect-product-like notation, **not** a comparison.
- No source was found in which `⋈` means *"a threshold relation between two quantities."*

So physicists **do** use `⋈`, but not for comparison. Writing `⋛ = ⋈` "for clarity" would import a
glyph whose established meanings are a join and a group action — the opposite of clarity for the
exact audience this is aimed at. Figure 8 :50–52: *"Convergent derivation and adopted import look
identical in the contents. Only the record of where each came from separates them."* And the
Operators table :68: *"A relation that fails all [tests] is not an operator you are missing; it is a
claim that has not been made yet."*

**Resolution:** the shipping form uses `⋛`. The original `⋈` is retained above as evidence of the
error, so a reader can see the correction rather than only the corrected claim.

And the role is wrong even with the right glyph. Operators table :51 and Fig 12 :52:

Calling quantum gravity a comparison bond maps it to **a goal**, not to a unified field or a
